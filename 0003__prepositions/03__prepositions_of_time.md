# Prepositions of Time

> Words that tell us WHEN something happens.

---

## Quick Reference

| Preposition | Use With | Hindi | Example |
|-------------|----------|-------|---------|
| at | Exact time | baje / par | at 5 PM, at noon |
| on | Days, Dates | ko | on Monday, on 15th |
| in | Months, Years, Seasons | mein | in January, in 2024 |
| by | Deadline | tak | by 5 PM |
| before | Earlier | se pehle | before lunch |
| after | Later | ke baad | after dinner |
| during | Throughout | ke dauran | during the movie |
| since | Starting point | se | since morning |
| until/till | End point | tak | until 5 PM |

---

## AT - Exact Time

> Use with: clock time, night, noon, midnight, dawn, weekends

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. The meeting is at 10 AM.
   - Meeting 10 baje hai.

2. I wake up at 6 in the morning.
   - Main subah 6 baje uthta hoon.

3. She came at noon.
   - Wo dopahar ko aayi.

4. We sleep at midnight.
   - Hum aadhi raat ko sote hain.

5. The party is at night.
   - Party raat ko hai.

6. He works at dawn.
   - Wo subah subah kaam karta hai.

7. Let's meet at lunch.
   - Lunch par milte hain.

8. She left at sunset.
   - Wo suraj dhalne par gayi.

9. I'm free at weekends.
   - Main weekend par free hoon.

10. Call me at 9 o'clock.
    - Mujhe 9 baje call karo.

11. The train arrives at 3:30 PM.
    - Train 3:30 baje aati hai.

12. We have dinner at 8 every night.
    - Hum har raat 8 baje dinner karte hain.

13. The shop opens at 10 AM.
    - Dukaan 10 baje khulti hai.

14. I feel tired at the end of the day.
    - Din ke aakhir mein main thaka hua hota hoon.

15. She arrived at exactly 5 o'clock.
    - Wo theek 5 baje pahunchi.

---

## ON - Days and Dates

> Use with: days of week, specific dates, special days

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I'll call you on Monday.
   - Main Monday ko call karunga.

2. My birthday is on 15th March.
   - Mera birthday 15 March ko hai.

3. We have a meeting on Friday.
   - Friday ko meeting hai.

4. The exam is on 25th December.
   - Exam 25 December ko hai.

5. Let's meet on Sunday.
   - Sunday ko milte hain.

6. He came on the first day.
   - Wo pehle din aaya.

7. I work on weekdays.
   - Main weekdays ko kaam karta hoon.

8. We rest on holidays.
   - Hum chuttiyon par aaraam karte hain.

9. On that day, I was busy.
   - Us din main busy tha.

10. She was born on 1st January.
    - Wo 1 January ko paida hui.

11. We're closed on weekends.
    - Hum weekends par band rehte hain.

12. The party is on Saturday night.
    - Party Saturday raat ko hai.

13. I'll see you on Thursday.
    - Thursday ko milte hain.

14. He proposed on Valentine's Day.
    - Usne Valentine's Day par propose kiya.

15. We have a test on the last day of month.
    - Mahine ke aakhri din test hai.

## IN - Months, Years, Seasons, Long Periods

> Use with: months, years, centuries, seasons, morning/afternoon/evening

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She was born in 1995.
   - Wo 1995 mein paida hui.

2. We'll meet in January.
   - Hum January mein milenge.

3. It's hot in summer.
   - Garmiyon mein garmi hoti hai.

4. I study in the morning.
   - Main subah padhta hoon.

5. He sleeps in the afternoon.
   - Wo dopahar ko sota hai.

6. Let's go out in the evening.
   - Shaam ko bahar chalte hain.

7. I'll finish in 2 hours.
   - Main 2 ghante mein khatam karunga.

8. In winter, it gets cold.
   - Sardi mein thandi hoti hai.

9. Things will change in the future.
   - Bhavishya mein cheezein badlengi.

10. In the past, there were no phones.
    - Pehle phone nahi the.

11. I'll be back in 10 minutes.
    - Main 10 minute mein wapas aaunga.

12. The movie starts in an hour.
    - Movie ek ghante mein shuru hogi.

13. In my childhood, we played outside.
    - Bachpan mein hum bahar khelte the.

14. She always wakes up in the night.
    - Wo hamesha raat mein uth jaati hai.

15. In the beginning, it was difficult.
    - Shuru mein mushkil tha.

## BY - Deadline

> Use with: time by which something must happen

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I'll finish by 5 PM.
   - Main 5 baje tak khatam karunga.

2. Submit the report by Monday.
   - Monday tak report submit karo.

3. He'll arrive by noon.
   - Wo dopahar tak aa jayega.

4. Pay the bill by tomorrow.
   - Kal tak bill bharo.

5. The work should be done by next week.
   - Agle hafte tak kaam ho jana chahiye.

6. I'll be there by 8 o'clock.
   - Main 8 baje tak wahan hounga.

7. Complete this by the end of the day.
   - Din khatam hone tak ye pura karo.

8. She'll return by evening.
   - Wo shaam tak laaut aayegi.

9. By the time you come, I'll be ready.
   - Jab tum aaoge, main ready hounga.

10. Finish eating by 2 PM.
    - 2 baje tak khana khatam karo.

11. I need to leave by 6 at the latest.
    - Mujhe zyada se zyada 6 baje tak nikalna hai.

12. By next year, I'll learn driving.
    - Agle saal tak main driving seekh lunga.

13. Complete the course by March.
    - March tak course complete karo.

14. She'll be home by dinner time.
    - Wo dinner time tak ghar aa jayegi.

15. By the time we reached, the movie had started.
    - Jab tak hum pahunche, movie shuru ho chuki thi.

## BEFORE - Earlier Than

> Use when one thing happens first

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Wash hands before eating.
   - Khane se pehle haath dho.

2. I'll finish before lunch.
   - Main lunch se pehle khatam karunga.

3. He left before me.
   - Wo mujhse pehle gaya.

4. Before sleeping, brush your teeth.
   - Sone se pehle brush karo.

5. Think before you speak.
   - Bolne se pehle socho.

6. She arrived before the meeting started.
   - Meeting shuru hone se pehle wo aa gayi.

7. I've never been here before.
   - Main pehle yahaan nahi aaya.

8. Before 2020, things were different.
   - 2020 se pehle cheezein alag thi.

9. Call me before you leave.
   - Jaane se pehle mujhe call karo.

10. Finish homework before playing.
    - Khelne se pehle homework khatam karo.

---

## AFTER - Later Than

> Use when one thing happens second

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. We'll meet after lunch.
   - Hum lunch ke baad milenge.

2. What happened after that?
   - Uske baad kya hua?

3. She came after me.
   - Wo mere baad aayi.

4. Rest after work.
   - Kaam ke baad aaraam karo.

5. After the movie, we went home.
   - Movie ke baad hum ghar gaye.

6. I'll call you after 5 PM.
   - Main 5 baje ke baad call karunga.

7. After eating, wash the dishes.
   - Khana khane ke baad bartan dho.

8. Day after day, he improved.
   - Din ke baad din wo behtar hua.

9. After school, I go to coaching.
   - School ke baad main coaching jaata hoon.

10. She left after the party.
    - Party ke baad wo chali gayi.

---

## DURING - Throughout

> Use for things happening in a time period

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Don't talk during class.
   - Class ke dauran baat mat karo.

2. It rained during the match.
   - Match ke dauran baarish hui.

3. I fell asleep during the movie.
   - Movie ke dauran main so gaya.

4. She called during lunch.
   - Usne lunch ke dauran call kiya.

5. What did you do during vacation?
   - Vacation ke dauran tumne kya kiya?

6. Don't use phone during driving.
   - Driving ke dauran phone use mat karo.

7. He studied during the night.
   - Usne raat ke dauran padhai ki.

8. During summer, we travel.
   - Garmiyon ke dauran hum travel karte hain.

9. I met him during the conference.
   - Conference ke dauran maine usse mila.

10. She cried during the speech.
    - Speech ke dauran wo royi.

---

## SINCE - Starting Point (Past to Now)

> Use with a point in time when something started

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I've been waiting since morning.
   - Main subah se wait kar raha hoon.

2. She has lived here since 2010.
   - Wo 2010 se yahaan rehti hai.

3. I know him since childhood.
   - Main use bachpan se jaanta hoon.

4. It has been raining since yesterday.
   - Kal se baarish ho rahi hai.

5. He's been sick since Monday.
   - Wo Monday se bimaar hai.

6. Since then, I haven't seen her.
   - Tab se maine usse nahi dekha.

7. I've been studying since 6 AM.
   - Main subah 6 baje se padh raha hoon.

8. Since last year, prices have increased.
   - Pichle saal se prices badhe hain.

9. She's been working since morning.
   - Wo subah se kaam kar rahi hai.

10. Since when have you been here?
    - Tum kab se yahaan ho?

---

## UNTIL / TILL - End Point

> Use with the point when something stops

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Wait until I come back.
   - Jab tak main wapas nahi aata, ruko.

2. I'll work till 6 PM.
   - Main 6 baje tak kaam karunga.

3. She slept until noon.
   - Wo dopahar tak soyi.

4. Don't leave until the work is done.
   - Jab tak kaam nahi hota, mat jao.

5. The shop is open till 9 PM.
   - Dukaan 9 baje tak khuli hai.

6. Until yesterday, I didn't know.
   - Kal tak mujhe nahi pata tha.

7. Wait till the rain stops.
   - Jab tak baarish nahi rukti, ruko.

8. I'll be here until evening.
   - Main shaam tak yahaan hounga.

9. She studied till midnight.
   - Usne aadhi raat tak padhai ki.

10. Until next time, goodbye!
    - Agle baar tak, alvida!

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| at Monday | on Monday |
| in 5 PM | at 5 PM |
| on morning | in the morning |
| at January | in January |
| since 2 hours | for 2 hours |
| during 2 hours | for 2 hours |
