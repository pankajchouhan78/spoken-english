# Prepositions of Direction / Movement

> Words that tell us WHERE something is going.

---

## Quick Reference

| Preposition | Hindi         | Movement                 |
| ----------- | ------------- | ------------------------ |
| to          | ko / ki taraf | Destination              |
| toward      | ki taraf      | Direction (not reaching) |
| into        | ke andar      | Movement inside          |
| onto        | ke upar       | Movement to surface      |
| from        | se            | Origin / Source          |
| across      | paar          | Side to side             |
| along       | ke saath      | Following a path         |
| through     | se hokar      | From end to end          |
| up          | upar          | Higher direction         |
| down        | neeche        | Lower direction          |
| out of      | se bahar      | Movement outside         |

---

## TO - Destination

> Use when going somewhere (reaching the destination)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I'm going to school.

   - Main school ja raha hoon.
2. She went to the market.

   - Wo market gayi.
3. He traveled to London.

   - Wo London gaya.
4. Come to my house.

   - Mere ghar aao.
5. I walk to office every day.

   - Main roz office paidal jaata hoon.
6. She flew to Japan.

   - Wo hawai jahaz se Japan gayi.
7. Let's go to the park.

   - Chalo park chalte hain.
8. Send this to him.

   - Ye use bhej do.
9. I'm returning to home.

   - Main ghar wapas ja raha hoon.
10. He moved to a new city.

    - Wo nayi city shift ho gaya.
11. Give this letter to your father.

    - Ye chithi apne papa ko do.
12. I'll introduce you to my friends.

    - Main tumhe apne doston se milwaunga.
13. She pointed to the map.

    - Usne map ki taraf point kiya.
14. Add sugar to your tea.

    - Chai mein cheeni daalo.
15. We walked to the beach.

    - Hum beach tak paidal gaye.
16. Take me to the hospital.

    - Mujhe hospital le chalo.
17. I'm looking forward to meeting you.

    - Tumse milne ka intezaar hai.
18. Listen to me carefully.

    - Dhyan se meri baat suno.
19. What happened to your phone?

    - Tumhare phone ko kya hua?
20. This belongs to him.

    - Ye uska hai.

## TOWARD / TOWARDS - Direction (Not Reaching)

> Use when moving in a direction but not necessarily reaching

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Walk toward the station.

   - Station ki taraf chalo.
2. She ran toward him.

   - Wo uski taraf bhaagi.
3. The car is coming toward us.

   - Car hamari taraf aa rahi hai.
4. He looked toward the sky.

   - Usne aasmaan ki taraf dekha.
5. We're moving toward success.

   - Hum safalta ki taraf badh rahe hain.
6. The dog ran toward the gate.

   - Kutta gate ki taraf bhaaga.
7. Turn toward the left.

   - Baayein taraf mudo.
8. She leaned toward him.

   - Wo uski taraf jhuki.
9. We're heading toward the city.

   - Hum sheher ki taraf ja rahe hain.
10. His attitude changed toward me.

    - Uska mujhse bartaav badal gaya.
11. She moved toward the exit.

    - Wo exit ki taraf badhi.
12. We're making progress toward our goal.

    - Hum apne lakshya ki taraf badh rahe hain.
13. He walked toward the sunset.

    - Wo sunset ki taraf chala.
14. The ship sailed toward the island.

    - Jahaz dweep ki taraf chala.
15. She has a positive attitude toward life.

    - Uska zindagi ke prati positive nazariya hai.

## INTO - Movement Inside

> Use when going from outside to inside

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She went into the room.

   - Wo kamre ke andar gayi.
2. Jump into the pool.

   - Pool mein kood jao.
3. Put the book into the bag.

   - Kitaab bag mein daal do.
4. He fell into the trap.

   - Wo jaal mein phas gaya.
5. Pour water into the glass.

   - Paani glass mein daal do.
6. The cat ran into the house.

   - Billi ghar mein bhaag gayi.
7. He walked into the office.

   - Wo office mein gaya.
8. She got into the car.

   - Wo car mein baithi.
9. Don't look into my eyes.

   - Meri aankhon mein mat dekho.
10. Translate this into Hindi.

    - Ise Hindi mein translate karo.
11. She burst into tears.

    - Wo ro padi.
12. He ran into his old friend.

    - Uski purane dost se achanak mulakat ho gayi.
13. They broke into the house.

    - Unhone ghar mein saar maar ki.
14. We got into an argument.

    - Humari bahes ho gayi.
15. Cut the apple into pieces.

    - Seb ke tukde karo.
16. The frog jumped into the pond.

    - Mendhak talab mein kooda.
17. She changed into new clothes.

    - Usne naye kapde badal liye.
18. He turned the room into an office.

    - Usne kamre ko office mein badal diya.
19. The water turned into ice.

    - Paani baraf mein badal gaya.
20. Don't rush into decisions.

    - Jaldi mein faisla mat karo.

## ONTO - Movement to Surface

> Use when moving to a surface

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. The cat jumped onto the table.

   - Billi table par koodi.
2. Put the book onto the shelf.

   - Kitaab shelf par rakho.
3. He climbed onto the roof.

   - Wo chhat par chadha.
4. She stepped onto the stage.

   - Wo stage par chadhi.
5. The bird flew onto the branch.

   - Pakshi daali par uda.
6. He loaded the bags onto the truck.

   - Usne bags truck par lade.
7. She jumped onto the bed.

   - Wo bed par koodi.
8. Water spilled onto the floor.

   - Paani zameen par gir gaya.
9. The dog jumped onto the sofa.

   - Kutta sofa par kooda.
10. Copy this onto the document.

    - Ise document mein copy karo.

## FROM - Origin / Source

> Use when something starts from a place

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I'm from India.

   - Main India se hoon.
2. The train comes from Delhi.

   - Train Delhi se aati hai.
3. She took the book from the shelf.

   - Usne shelf se kitaab uthayi.
4. I got this from my friend.

   - Mujhe ye dost se mila.
5. He works from home.

   - Wo ghar se kaam karta hai.
6. The noise is coming from outside.

   - Shor bahar se aa raha hai.
7. She returned from the trip.

   - Wo trip se lauti.
8. I learned it from my teacher.

   - Maine apne teacher se seekha.
9. Take the pen from the desk.

   - Desk se pen lo.
10. The letter is from my mother.

    - Chithi meri maa ki hai.

## ACROSS - From One Side to Another

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She swam across the river.

   - Usne nadi paar ki.
2. Walk across the street.

   - Sadak paar karo.
3. He ran across the field.

   - Wo maidan paar kiya.
4. The bridge goes across the river.

   - Pul nadi ke paar jaata hai.
5. Look across the road.

   - Sadak ke paar dekho.
6. News spread across the country.

   - Khabar puri country mein faili.
7. They traveled across Europe.

   - Unhone Europe ghuma.
8. She shouted across the room.

   - Usne puri room mein chillaya.
9. The message spread across the world.

   - Message puri duniya mein faila.
10. He lives across the street.

    - Wo sadak ke uss paar rehta hai.

## ALONG - Following a Path

**Daily Use Sentences:**

   - Nadi ke kinare chalo.

   - Sadak ke kinare ped lagaye gaye hain.
3. We drove along the coast.

   - Hum samundar ke kinare drive kiye.
4. She walked along the corridor.

   - Wo corridor mein chali.
5. Run along the beach.

   - Beach ke kinare daudo.
6. Houses are built along the street.

   - Ghar sadak ke kinare bane hain.
7. The lights are along the wall.

   - Lights deewar ke saath hain.
8. He strolled along the path.

   - Wo raaste par ghuma.
9. Shops are located along the main road.

   - Dukaaanein main road par hain.
10. Follow the signs along the way.

    - Raaste mein signs follow karo.

## THROUGH - From End to End

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Walk through the door.

   - Darwaze se guzro.
2. He drove through the tunnel.

   - Wo tunnel se guzra.
3. Light passes through the glass.

   - Roshni sheeshe se guzarti hai.
4. She looked through the window.

   - Usne khidki se dekha.
5. We walked through the forest.

   - Hum jungle se guzre.
6. The river flows through the city.

   - Nadi sheher se guzarti hai.
7. He pushed through the crowd.

   - Wo bheed se guzra.
8. I read through the book.

   - Maine puri kitaab padhi.
9. The needle goes through the cloth.

   - Sui kapde se guzarti hai.
10. She went through a lot.

    - Usne bahut kuch jhela.

> Use for higher or lower movement

**Daily Use Sentences:**

   - Seedhiyon se upar jao.
2. Come down carefully.

   - Dhyan se neeche aao.
3. The balloon went up in the sky.

   - Gubbara aasmaan mein gaya.
4. He climbed up the mountain.

   - Wo pahad par chadha.
5. She fell down the stairs.

   - Wo seedhiyon se giri.
6. Walk down the road.

   - Sadak par aage chalo.
7. The price went up.

   - Daam badh gaye.
8. Temperature came down.

   - Temperature gir gaya.
9. Look up at the sky.

   - Aasmaan ki taraf dekho.
10. Put it down on the table.

    - Ise table par rakho.

## OUT OF - Movement Outside

> Use when going from inside to outside

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She ran out of the room.

   - Wo kamre se bahar bhaagi.
2. He jumped out of the window.

   - Wo khidki se bahar kooda.
3. Get out of the car.

   - Car se bahar niklo.
4. The cat came out of the box.

   - Billi dabbe se bahar aayi.
5. I'm out of money.

   - Mere paas paise nahi hain.
6. We ran out of time.

   - Humara time khatam ho gaya.
7. She pulled the keys out of her bag.

   - Usne bag se chaabi nikaali.
8. He walked out of the meeting.

   - Wo meeting se bahar chala gaya.
9. Take it out of the box.

   - Ise dabbe se nikaalo.
10. She looked out of the window.

    - Usne khidki se bahar dekha.

| on the table   | onto the table      |
| at the door    | to the door         |
| inside         | out of              |
