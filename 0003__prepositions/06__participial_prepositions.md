# Participial / Advanced Prepositions

> Prepositions derived from verbs (present/past participles) used in formal English.

---

## Quick Reference

| Preposition     | Hindi Meaning    | Use               |
| --------------- | ---------------- | ----------------- |
| according to    | ke anusar        | Based on source   |
| concerning      | ke baare mein    | About / Regarding |
| considering     | ko dekhte hue    | Given that        |
| including       | samet            | As part of group  |
| excluding       | ke alawa         | Not part of group |
| following       | ke baad          | After / Next      |
| regarding       | ke baare mein    | About             |
| respecting      | ke sambandh mein | About (formal)    |
| given           | ko dekhte hue    | Considering       |
| pending         | tak              | Until resolved    |
| during          | ke dauran        | Throughout        |
| barring         | ke alawa         | Except            |
| notwithstanding | ke bawajood      | Despite           |

## According to - "Ke Anusar"

> Based on information from a source

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. According to the report, sales increased.

   - Report ke anusar, sales badhi.
2. According to him, the meeting is at 5.

   - Uske anusar meeting 5 baje hai.
3. He acted according to the rules.

   - Usne niyamon ke anusar kaam kiya.
4. According to the weather forecast, it will rain.

   - Mausam ke anusar baarish hogi.
5. Everything went according to plan.

   - Sab kuch plan ke anusar hua.
6. According to experts, this is safe.

   - Experts ke anusar ye surakshit hai.
7. She dressed according to the dress code.

   - Usne dress code ke anusar kapde pahne.
8. According to tradition, we celebrate this day.

   - Parampara ke anusar hum ye din manate hain.
9. According to law, this is illegal.

   - Kanoon ke anusar ye galat hai.
10. According to sources, the news is true.

    - Sutron ke anusar khabar sahi hai.
11. According to the doctor, rest is needed.

    - Doctor ke anusar aaraam zaroori hai.
12. According to my experience, this won't work.

    - Mere anubhav ke anusar ye kaam nahi karega.
13. Work according to the schedule.

    - Schedule ke anusar kaam karo.
14. According to research, exercise is beneficial.

    - Research ke anusar exercise faydemand hai.
15. According to the recipe, add salt first.

    - Recipe ke anusar pehle namak daalo.

## Concerning - "Ke Baare Mein"

> About a particular matter

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I received a letter concerning my application.

   - Mujhe apni application ke baare mein chithi mili.
2. We have some questions concerning the project.

   - Humein project ke baare mein kuch sawaal hain.
3. The discussion was concerning budget.

   - Charcha budget ke baare mein thi.
4. There are concerns concerning safety.

   - Safety ke baare mein chintaein hain.
5. She spoke concerning the new policy.

   - Usne nayi policy ke baare mein baat ki.
6. I'm writing concerning your complaint.

   - Main tumhari shikayat ke baare mein likh raha hoon.
7. There's been a notice concerning the changes.

   - Badlav ke baare mein notice aayi hai.
8. He called concerning the order.

   - Usne order ke baare mein call kiya.
9. The email was concerning the deadline.

   - Email deadline ke baare mein thi.
10. We need clarification concerning this rule.

    - Humein is niyam ke baare mein spashteekaran chahiye.

## Considering - "Ko Dekhte Hue"

> Given a particular fact or circumstance

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Considering his age, he's very active.

   - Uski umar ko dekhte hue wo bahut active hai.
2. Considering the time, let's hurry.

   - Time ko dekhte hue jaldi karte hain.
3. Considering all factors, this is the best option.

   - Sab cheezein dekhte hue ye sabse accha option hai.
4. Considering the weather, we stayed home.

   - Mausam ko dekhte hue hum ghar rahe.
5. She did well, considering it was her first time.

   - Pehli baar ko dekhte hue usne accha kiya.
6. Considering the cost, it's worth it.

   - Keemat ko dekhte hue ye sahi hai.
7. Considering the situation, he acted wisely.

   - Sthiti ko dekhte hue usne samajhdari se kaam kiya.
8. Considering your experience, you should lead.

   - Tumhare experience ko dekhte hue tumhe lead karna chahiye.
9. Considering everything, I agree with you.

   - Sab kuch dekhte hue main tumse sehmat hoon.
10. Considering the risks, be careful.

    - Khatron ko dekhte hue savdhaan raho.
11. Considering her health, she should rest.

    - Uski sehat ko dekhte hue use aaraam karna chahiye.
12. Considering the budget, this is our best option.

    - Budget ko dekhte hue ye hamara sabse accha option hai.
13. Considering his background, he's done well.

    - Uski background ko dekhte hue usne accha kiya.
14. Considering everything, I'm happy.

    - Sab kuch dekhte hue main khush hoon.
15. Considering the demand, prices may rise.

    - Demand ko dekhte hue daam badh sakte hain.

## Including - "Samet" / "Shamil"

> As part of a group

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Everyone came, including the manager.

   - Sab aaye, manager samet.
2. The price is 500, including tax.

   - Daam 500 hai, tax samet.
3. I've visited 5 countries, including Japan.

   - Maine 5 desh ghume hain, Japan samet.
4. All members, including me, agreed.

   - Sabhi members, main samet, raazi the.
5. The package contains everything, including instructions.

   - Package mein sab hai, instructions samet.
6. There were 10 people, including the host.

   - Host samet 10 log the.
7. The trip cost 5000, including food.

   - Trip ka kharcha 5000 tha, khana samet.
8. I've read all his books, including the latest one.

   - Maine uski sabhi kitaabein padhi hain, nayi samet.
9. Everyone came, including the boss.

   - Sab aaye, boss samet.
10. The fee is 500, including materials.

    - Fee 500 hai, materials samet.

## Excluding - "Ke Alawa" / "Ke Bina"

> Not as part of a group

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. The price is 500, excluding tax.

   - Daam 500 hai, tax ke bina.
2. Everyone passed, excluding two students.

   - Do students ke alawa sab pass hue.
3. We work every day, excluding Sunday.

   - Hum roz kaam karte hain, Sunday ke alawa.
4. All fees are refundable, excluding registration.

   - Registration ke alawa sabhi fees refundable hain.
5. The offer is valid for all, excluding staff.

   - Staff ke alawa sabke liye offer valid hai.
6. The cost is 1000, excluding delivery.

   - Keemat 1000 hai, delivery ke bina.
7. Everyone agreed, excluding a few members.

   - Kuch members ke alawa sab agree the.
8. The museum is open daily, excluding Monday.

   - Museum roz khula hai, Monday ke alawa.
9. All items are on sale, excluding electronics.

   - Electronics ke alawa sabhi items sale par hain.
10. The price is final, excluding taxes.

    - Daam final hai, taxes ke bina.

## Following - "Ke Baad"

> After something / Next

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Following the meeting, we had lunch.

   - Meeting ke baad humne lunch kiya.
2. Following his advice, I applied for the job.

   - Uski salah ke baad maine job ke liye apply kiya.
3. The police arrived following the accident.

   - Accident ke baad police aayi.
4. Following the announcement, everyone cheered.

   - Announcement ke baad sab khush hue.
5. He became famous following the movie.

   - Film ke baad wo famous ho gaya.
6. Following the instructions, I completed the task.

   - Instructions follow karke maine kaam kiya.
7. Following the rain, it became cool.

   - Baarish ke baad thanda ho gaya.
8. She took rest following the surgery.

   - Surgery ke baad usne aaraam kiya.
9. Following years of practice, he became expert.

   - Saalon ki practice ke baad wo expert bana.
10. Following this, we'll discuss the next topic.

    - Iske baad hum agla topic discuss karenge.
11. Following the incident, security was increased.

    - Ghatna ke baad security badha di gayi.
12. Following the success, he became confident.

    - Safalta ke baad wo confident ho gaya.
13. Following your email, I'm writing to confirm.

    - Tumhari email ke baad main confirm karne likh raha hoon.
14. Following the storm, there was a power cut.

    - Toofan ke baad bijli gayi.
15. Following the rules, he completed the task.

    - Niyamon ka palan karte hue usne kaam kiya.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Given the situation, we should wait.

   - Mauka mile to main khud ko prove karunga.

   - Maujuda halaat ko dekhte hue humein rukna chahiye.

## Pending - "Tak" / "Lambitt"

> Until something is resolved

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. The decision is pending approval.

   - Faisla approval ke lambitt hai.
2. He's out on bail, pending trial.

   - Wo bail par hai, trial tak.
3. Payment is pending verification.

   - Payment verification tak ruki hui hai.
4. The project is pending review.

   - Project review ke intezaar mein hai.
5. Pending further notice, work from home.

   - Agli notice tak ghar se kaam karo.
6. The case is pending investigation.

   - Case jaanch ke lambitt hai.
7. His application is pending approval.

   - Uski application manjuri ke lambitt hai.
8. Pending the results, we can't proceed.

   - Nateeje tak hum aage nahi badh sakte.
9. The project is on hold, pending funding.

   - Project ruka hua hai, funding tak.
10. Pending confirmation, don't make plans.

## Barring - "Ke Alawa"

> Except / Unless

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Barring any delays, we'll finish on time.

   - Delay na ho to hum time par khatam karenge.
2. Everyone came, barring two people.

   - Do logon ke alawa sab aaye.
3. Barring accidents, we should arrive early.

   - Accident na ho to hum jaldi pahunchenge.
4. She works every day, barring Sunday.

   - Sunday ke alawa wo roz kaam karti hai.
5. Barring any objections, the plan is approved.

   - Koi aapatti na ho to plan approved hai.
6. Barring rain, we'll have the match.

   - Baarish na ho to match hoga.
7. Barring unforeseen circumstances, I'll be there.

   - Koi unexpected baat na ho to main wahan hounga.
8. Everyone passed, barring one student.

   - Ek student ke alawa sab pass hue.
9. Barring any issues, we'll launch tomorrow.

   - Koi dikkat na ho to kal launch karenge.
10. The project will be done, barring delays.

    - Delay na ho to project ho jayega.

## Notwithstanding - "Ke Bawajood"

> Despite / In spite of

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Notwithstanding the rain, we went out.

   - Baarish ke bawajood hum bahar gaye.
2. He succeeded, notwithstanding all obstacles.

   - Sabhi rukaavaton ke bawajood wo safal hua.
3. Notwithstanding the cost, we bought it.

   - Keemat ke bawajood humne khareed liya.
4. She continued, notwithstanding the difficulties.

   - Mushkilon ke bawajood wo jari rahi.
5. Notwithstanding my advice, he took the risk.

   - Meri salah ke bawajood usne risk liya.
6. Notwithstanding the opposition, the bill passed.

   - Virodh ke bawajood bill pass hua.
7. She succeeded, notwithstanding all challenges.

   - Sabhi chunautiyon ke bawajood wo safal hui.
8. Notwithstanding his age, he's very active.

   - Umar ke bawajood wo bahut active hai.
9. The event continued, notwithstanding the weather.

   - Mausam ke bawajood event jari raha.
10. Notwithstanding the error, the system worked.

    - Galti ke bawajood system kaam kiya.
