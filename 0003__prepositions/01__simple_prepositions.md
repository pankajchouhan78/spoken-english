## Detailed Usage with Examples

### About - "Ke Baare Mein"

1. Tell me about yourself.
   - Apne baare mein batao.

2. The movie is about love.
   - Film pyaar ke baare mein hai.

3. It costs about 500 rupees.
   - Lagbhag 500 rupaye hai.

4. I'm worried about the exam.
   - Mujhe exam ki chinta hai.

5. What are you thinking about?
   - Tum kya soch rahe ho?

6. We're talking about something else.
   - Hum kisi aur cheez ke baare mein baat kar rahe hain.

7. I forgot about the meeting.
   - Main meeting ke baare mein bhool gaya.

8. How about going for a walk?
   - Chalne ke baare mein kya khyal hai?

9. She cares about her family.
   - Use apni family ki fikar hai.

10. Don't worry about it.
    - Iske baare mein tension mat lo.

---

### Above - "Ke Upar"

1. The fan is above my head.
   - Pankha mere sar ke upar hai.

2. The temperature is above 40 degrees.
   - Temperature 40 degree se upar hai.

3. He is above average.
   - Wo average se upar hai.

4. The plane flew above the clouds.
   - Plane baadlon ke upar uda.

5. This is above my understanding.
   - Ye meri samajh se upar hai.

6. Hang the painting above the sofa.
   - Painting sofa ke upar lagao.

7. The apartment above ours is noisy.
   - Hamare upar wala flat noisy hai.

8. She values honesty above everything.
   - Wo imaandari ko sab se upar rakhti hai.

9. The mountain rises above the clouds.
   - Pahad baadlon se upar uthta hai.

10. Keep your head above water.
    - Apna sar paani ke upar rakho.

---

### Across - "Paar"

1. He lives across the road.
   - Wo sadak ke paar rehta hai.

2. She swam across the river.
   - Usne nadi paar ki.

3. The shop is across the street.
   - Dukaan sadak ke uss paar hai.

4. News spread across the country.
   - Khabar puri country mein faili.

5. Walk across the bridge.
   - Pul paar karo.

6. The hotel is just across the park.
   - Hotel park ke just paar hai.

7. She waved at me from across the room.
   - Usne room ke dusri taraf se haath hilaya.

8. Put the line across the page.
   - Page par line khicho.

9. He threw the ball across the yard.
   - Usne ball yard ke paar fenki.

10. The news spread across social media.
    - Khabar social media par faili.

---

### After - "Ke Baad"

1. I'll call you after lunch.
   - Main lunch ke baad call karunga.

2. She came after me.
   - Wo mere baad aayi.

3. What happened after that?
   - Uske baad kya hua?

4. He ran after the bus.
   - Wo bus ke peeche bhaga.

5. Day after day, he worked hard.
   - Din ke baad din wo mehnat karta raha.

6. I'll do it after finishing this.
   - Ye khatam karne ke baad karunga.

7. She named the baby after her mother.
   - Usne bachche ka naam apni maa ke naam par rakha.

8. After all, he's just a child.
   - Aakhir wo bachcha hi hai.

9. Look after yourself.
   - Apna khayal rakhna.

10. The movie starts after the ads.
    - Ads ke baad movie shuru hogi.

---

### Against - "Ke Khilaf"

1. Don't lean against the wall.
   - Deewar ke sahare mat lagao.

2. We played against them.
   - Hum unke khilaf khele.

3. I'm against this idea.
   - Main is idea ke khilaf hoon.

4. He fought against injustice.
   - Usne anyay ke khilaf ladai ki.

5. The boat sailed against the wind.
   - Naav hawa ke khilaf chali.

6. He pushed against the door.
   - Usne darwaze ko dhakka diya.

7. My parents are against this marriage.
   - Mere parents is shaadi ke khilaf hain.

8. We're racing against time.
   - Hum waqt ke khilaf daur rahe hain.

9. The picture looks good against the white wall.
   - Tasveer safed deewar ke saath acchi lagti hai.

10. Save money against bad times.
    - Bure waqt ke liye paise bachao.

---

### Along - "Ke Saath / Kinare"

1. Walk along the river.
   - Nadi ke kinare chalo.

2. Trees are planted along the road.
   - Sadak ke kinare ped lagaye gaye hain.

3. He sang along with me.
   - Wo mere saath gaya.

4. Come along with us.
   - Hamare saath aao.

5. We drove along the coast.
   - Hum samundar ke kinare drive kiye.

---

### Among - "Ke Beech Mein" (Group of 3+)

1. She sat among her friends.
   - Wo apne doston ke beech mein baithi.

2. Divide it among yourselves.
   - Apne aap mein baant lo.

3. He is popular among students.
   - Wo students mein popular hai.

4. This is a secret among us.
   - Ye hamare beech ka raaz hai.

5. Choose one among these.
   - Inme se ek chuno.

---

### Around - "Ke Aas Paas"

1. There are trees around the house.
   - Ghar ke aas paas ped hain.

2. Turn around and look.
   - Mud kar dekho.

3. He'll come around 5 PM.
   - Wo lagbhag 5 baje aayega.

4. Don't fool around.
   - Bakwaas mat karo.

5. The earth moves around the sun.
   - Dharti suraj ke chakkar lagati hai.

---

### Before - "Se Pehle"

1. Wash your hands before eating.
   - Khane se pehle haath dho.

2. I arrived before him.
   - Main usse pehle pahuncha.

3. Think before you speak.
   - Bolne se pehle socho.

4. The day before yesterday.
   - Kal se pehle wala din (parso).

5. I've never seen this before.
   - Maine ye pehle kabhi nahi dekha.

---

1. The garden is behind the house.
   - Baagh ghar ke peeche hai.

2. Don't talk behind my back.
   - Meri peeth peeche baat mat karo.

3. He is behind in studies.
   - Wo padhai mein peeche hai.

4. What's behind this decision?
   - Is faisale ke peeche kya hai?

5. The sun went behind the clouds.
   - Suraj baadlon ke peeche chala gaya.

---

### Below - "Neeche"

1. My room is below yours.

3. Sign below the line.
   - Line ke neeche sign karo.

4. He is below average.
   - Wo average se neeche hai.

5. See the notes below.
   - Neeche notes dekho.

### Beside - "Ke Bagal Mein"

1. Sit beside me.
   - Mere bagal mein baitho.

2. The park is beside the school.
   - Park school ke bagal mein hai.

3. She walked beside him.
   - Wo uske saath chali.

4. Keep your phone beside you.
   - Apna phone paas rakho.

5. Beside this, there's nothing else.
   - Iske alawa kuch nahi hai.

---

### Between - "Ke Beech" (Two things)

1. Sit between Ram and Shyam.
   - Ram aur Shyam ke beech baitho.

2. Choose between tea and coffee.
   - Chai aur coffee mein se chuno.

3. The match is between India and Pakistan.
   - Match India aur Pakistan ke beech hai.

4. Keep this between us.
   - Ye hamare beech rahe.

5. What's the difference between them?
   - Unme kya fark hai?

---

### By - "Dwara / Se / Tak"

1. This book is written by him.
   - Ye kitaab usne likhi hai.

2. Sit by the window.
   - Khidki ke paas baitho.

3. I'll finish by 5 PM.
   - Main 5 baje tak khatam karunga.

4. Travel by bus.
   - Bus se safar karo.

5. Day by day, he improved.
   - Din ba din wo sudhra.

---

### During - "Ke Dauran"

1. Don't talk during the class.
   - Class ke dauran baat mat karo.

2. It rained during the match.
   - Match ke dauran baarish hui.

3. I slept during the movie.
   - Movie ke dauran main so gaya.

4. She called during lunch.
   - Usne lunch ke dauran call kiya.

5. What did you do during vacation?
   - Vacation ke dauran tumne kya kiya?

---

### For - "Ke Liye"

1. This gift is for you.
   - Ye gift tumhare liye hai.

2. I've been waiting for an hour.
   - Main ek ghante se wait kar raha hoon.

3. He works for a company.
   - Wo ek company ke liye kaam karta hai.

4. What is this for?
   - Ye kis liye hai?

5. Thanks for your help.
   - Madad ke liye shukriya.

---

### From - "Se"

1. I'm from India.
   - Main India se hoon.

2. The train leaves from platform 5.
   - Train platform 5 se nikalti hai.

3. He works from 9 to 5.
   - Wo 9 se 5 tak kaam karta hai.

4. Take it from the table.
   - Table se utha lo.

5. I heard it from him.
   - Maine usse suna.

---

### Like - "Jaisa"

1. He looks like his father.
   - Wo apne papa jaisa dikhta hai.

2. It tastes like honey.
   - Ye shahad jaisa lagta hai.

3. Don't behave like a child.
   - Bachche jaisa behave mat karo.

4. I feel like sleeping.
   - Mujhe sone ka mann hai.

5. What is she like?
   - Wo kaisi hai?

---

### Near - "Paas"

1. My house is near the station.
   - Mera ghar station ke paas hai.

2. Don't go near the fire.
   - Aag ke paas mat jao.

3. Sit near me.
   - Mere paas baitho.

4. The end is near.
   - Ant paas hai.

5. Is there a hospital near here?
   - Kya yahaan paas mein hospital hai?

---

### Of - "Ka / Ki / Ke"

1. The color of the sky is blue.
   - Aasmaan ka rang neela hai.

2. A cup of tea, please.
   - Ek cup chai dena.

3. He is a friend of mine.
   - Wo mera dost hai.

4. The capital of India is Delhi.
   - Bharat ki rajdhani Delhi hai.

5. Most of them agreed.
   - Unme se zyada log raazi the.

---

### Over - "Ke Upar / Paar"

1. The bridge is over the river.
   - Pul nadi ke upar hai.

2. The bird flew over the house.
   - Pakshi ghar ke upar uda.

3. It's over now.
   - Ab khatam ho gaya.

4. She is over 30 years old.
   - Wo 30 saal se upar ki hai.

5. Think it over.
   - Iske baare mein socho.

---

### Through - "Se Hokar"

1. Walk through the door.
   - Darwaze se guzro.

2. He drove through the tunnel.
   - Wo tunnel se guzra.

3. I got the job through a friend.
   - Mujhe dost ke zariye job mili.

4. Read through the document.
   - Document pura padho.

5. She went through a lot.
   - Usne bahut kuch jhela.

---

### With - "Ke Saath"

1. Come with me.
   - Mere saath aao.

2. She lives with her parents.
   - Wo apne parents ke saath rehti hai.

3. Cut it with a knife.
   - Chaku se kaato.

4. I agree with you.
   - Main tumse sehmat hoon.

5. He was angry with me.
   - Wo mujhse naraz tha.

---

### Without - "Ke Bina"

1. Don't go without me.
   - Mere bina mat jao.

2. I can't live without you.
   - Main tumhare bina nahi reh sakta.

3. He left without saying goodbye.
   - Wo bina bye bole chala gaya.

4. Coffee without sugar, please.
   - Bina cheeni ki coffee dena.

5. You can't enter without a ticket.
   - Ticket ke bina andar nahi aa sakte.
