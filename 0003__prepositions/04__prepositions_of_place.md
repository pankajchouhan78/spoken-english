# Prepositions of Place / Position

> Words that tell us WHERE something is.

---

## Quick Reference

| Preposition | Hindi | Position |
|-------------|-------|----------|
| in | mein / andar | Inside enclosed space |
| on | par / upar | On surface |
| at | par / pe | Exact point |
| above | ke upar | Higher (not touching) |
| over | ke upar | Higher / Covering |
| below | neeche | Lower position |
| under | ke neeche | Directly below |
| beneath | ke neeche | Under (formal) |
| behind | peeche | At the back |
| in front of | saamne | At the front |
| beside | bagal mein | Next to |
| between | beech mein | In middle of two |
| among | beech mein | In middle of group (3+) |
| near | paas | Close to |
| far from | door | Not close |
| inside | andar | Interior |
| outside | bahar | Exterior |

---

## IN - Inside Enclosed Space

> Use for: rooms, buildings, cities, countries, containers

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I'm in the room.
   - Main kamre mein hoon.

2. She lives in Delhi.
   - Wo Delhi mein rehti hai.

3. The keys are in my pocket.
   - Chaabi meri jeb mein hai.

4. He works in a bank.
   - Wo bank mein kaam karta hai.

5. There's water in the bottle.
   - Bottle mein paani hai.

6. I'm in India.
   - Main India mein hoon.

7. The cat is in the box.
   - Billi dabba mein hai.

8. She's in the kitchen.
   - Wo kitchen mein hai.

9. I saw it in the newspaper.
   - Maine ise newspaper mein dekha.

10. The money is in the drawer.
    - Paise drawer mein hain.

11. I'm stuck in traffic.
    - Main traffic mein fansa hoon.

12. She's in the shower.
    - Wo nahane mein hai.

13. Put it in the fridge.
    - Ise fridge mein rakho.

14. He's in a meeting right now.
    - Wo abhi meeting mein hai.

15. I read it in a book.
    - Maine ise ek kitaab mein padha.

16. There's a fly in my soup.
    - Mere soup mein makhi hai.

17. I live in a small town.
    - Main ek chhote sheher mein rehta hoon.

18. She works in the IT department.
    - Wo IT department mein kaam karti hai.

19. In my opinion, this is wrong.
    - Mere khayal se ye galat hai.

20. The answer is in the book.
    - Jawab kitaab mein hai.

## ON - On Surface

> Use for: surfaces, floors, tables, walls

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. The book is on the table.
   - Kitaab table par hai.

2. Sit on the chair.
   - Kursi par baitho.

3. Hang the picture on the wall.
   - Tasveer deewar par lagao.

4. She's standing on the stage.
   - Wo stage par khadi hai.

5. The cat is on the roof.
   - Billi chhat par hai.

6. There's dust on the floor.
   - Zameen par dhool hai.

7. He put the glass on the table.
   - Usne glass table par rakha.

8. Your name is on the list.
   - Tumhara naam list mein hai.

9. I'm on the bus.
   - Main bus mein hoon.

10. She's on the phone.
    - Wo phone par hai.

11. The painting is on the wall.
    - Painting deewar par hai.

12. There's a sticker on my laptop.
    - Mere laptop par sticker hai.

13. The cat is sleeping on the sofa.
    - Billi sofa par so rahi hai.

14. I saw it on TV.
    - Maine TV par dekha.

15. He put his hand on my shoulder.
    - Usne meri kandhe par haath rakha.

16. There's a spider on the ceiling.
    - Chhat par makdi hai.

17. Write it on the board.
    - Board par likho.

18. She has a ring on her finger.
    - Uski ungli par ring hai.

19. The laptop is on my desk.
    - Laptop meri desk par hai.

20. I'm working on a new project.
    - Main naye project par kaam kar raha hoon.

## AT - Exact Point / Location

> Use for: specific places, addresses, events

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I'm at the bus stop.
   - Main bus stop par hoon.

2. She's waiting at the door.
   - Wo darwaze par wait kar rahi hai.

3. We met at the restaurant.
   - Hum restaurant mein mile.

4. He's at home.
   - Wo ghar par hai.

5. I'm at work.
   - Main office mein hoon.

6. She's at school.
   - Wo school mein hai.

7. Meet me at the station.
   - Mujhse station par milo.

8. Look at the board.
   - Board ki taraf dekho.

9. He's at the airport.
   - Wo airport par hai.

10. I'm sitting at my desk.
    - Main apni desk par baitha hoon.

11. Turn left at the traffic light.
    - Traffic light par left mudo.

12. She's waiting at the entrance.
    - Wo entrance par wait kar rahi hai.

13. We arrived at the destination.
    - Hum manzil par pahunch gaye.

14. I'll pick you up at the corner.
    - Main tumhe corner par pick karunga.

15. She looked at me and smiled.
    - Usne mujhe dekha aur muskurayi.

16. The meeting is at the conference room.
    - Meeting conference room mein hai.

17. I'm good at cooking.
    - Main cooking mein accha hoon.

18. He's angry at me.
    - Wo mujhse naraz hai.

19. We stopped at the gas station.
    - Hum petrol pump par ruke.

20. She's at the gym right now.
    - Wo abhi gym mein hai.

## ABOVE - Higher Position (Not Touching)

> Use when something is higher but not directly over

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. The fan is above my head.
   - Pankha mere sar ke upar hai.

2. The plane flew above the clouds.
   - Hawai jahaz baadlon ke upar uda.

3. Hang it above the door.
   - Ise darwaze ke upar lagao.

4. The temperature is above 40 degrees.
   - Temperature 40 degree se upar hai.

5. There's a light above the mirror.
   - Sheeshe ke upar light hai.

---

## BELOW - Lower Position

> Use when something is at a lower level

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. My room is below yours.
   - Mera kamra tumhare neeche hai.

2. The temperature dropped below zero.
   - Temperature zero se neeche gira.

3. Sign below the line.
   - Line ke neeche sign karo.

4. The valley is below the mountain.
   - Ghati pahad ke neeche hai.

5. See the notes below.
   - Neeche notes dekho.

---

## UNDER - Directly Below

> Use when something is directly underneath

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. The cat is under the table.
   - Billi table ke neeche hai.

2. He hid under the bed.
   - Wo bed ke neeche chhupa.

3. Children under 12 get free entry.
   - 12 saal se kam umar ke bachche free hain.

4. The book fell under the sofa.
   - Kitaab sofa ke neeche giri.

5. She's under pressure.
   - Wo pressure mein hai.

---

## BEHIND - At the Back

> Use for things at the back of something

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. The garden is behind the house.
   - Baag ghar ke peeche hai.

2. He's hiding behind the door.
   - Wo darwaze ke peeche chhupa hai.

3. The sun went behind the clouds.
   - Suraj baadlon ke peeche chala gaya.

4. Who's behind this plan?
   - Is plan ke peeche kaun hai?

5. Don't talk behind my back.
   - Meri peeth peeche baat mat karo.

---

## IN FRONT OF - At the Front

> Use for things at the front

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Stand in front of the class.
   - Class ke saamne khade ho.

2. The car is parked in front of the house.
   - Car ghar ke saamne khadi hai.

3. He stood in front of the mirror.
   - Wo sheeshe ke saamne khada tha.

4. There's a garden in front of the hotel.
   - Hotel ke saamne garden hai.

5. Don't stand in front of the TV.
   - TV ke saamne mat khade ho.

---

## BESIDE / NEXT TO - Side by Side

> Use for things that are at the side

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Sit beside me.
   - Mere bagal mein baitho.

2. The bank is next to the post office.
   - Bank post office ke bagal mein hai.

3. She walked beside him.
   - Wo uske saath chali.

4. Put this next to that.
   - Ise uske bagal mein rakho.

5. The hospital is beside the school.
   - Hospital school ke bagal mein hai.

---

## BETWEEN - In Middle of Two

> Use for position in the middle of TWO things

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Sit between Ram and Shyam.
   - Ram aur Shyam ke beech baitho.

2. The shop is between the bank and the hotel.
   - Dukaan bank aur hotel ke beech hai.

3. Choose between tea and coffee.
   - Chai aur coffee mein se chuno.

4. The river flows between the two cities.
   - Nadi dono shaharon ke beech behti hai.

5. Keep this between us.
   - Ye hamare beech rahe.

---

## AMONG - In Middle of Group (3+)

> Use for position among THREE or more things

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She sat among her friends.
   - Wo apne doston ke beech mein baithi.

2. He is popular among students.
   - Wo students mein popular hai.

3. Divide it among yourselves.
   - Apne aap mein baant lo.

4. This is a secret among us.
   - Ye hamare beech ka raaz hai.

5. Among all, he's the best.
   - Sab mein wo sabse accha hai.

---

## NEAR / FAR FROM - Distance

> Use for describing how close or far something is

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. My house is near the station.
   - Mera ghar station ke paas hai.

2. Don't go near the fire.
   - Aag ke paas mat jao.

3. The airport is far from here.
   - Airport yahaan se door hai.

4. Is there a hospital near here?
   - Kya yahaan paas mein hospital hai?

5. Stay far from trouble.
   - Mushkil se door raho.

---

## INSIDE / OUTSIDE - Interior / Exterior

> Use for describing if something is in or out

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Come inside the house.
   - Ghar ke andar aao.

2. Wait outside the office.
   - Office ke bahar ruko.

3. It's cold outside.
   - Bahar thandi hai.

4. The dog is inside.
   - Kutta andar hai.

5. Don't go outside alone.
   - Akele bahar mat jao.

## Quick Comparison

|-------------|---------|----------|
| in | in the box | Enclosed |
| on | on the table | Surface |
| at | at the door | Point |
| above | above the head | Higher (gap) |
| over | over the bridge | Higher/covering |
| below | below average | Lower |
| under | under the table | Directly below |
| behind | behind the house | At back |
| in front of | in front of the class | At front |
| between | between A and B | Middle of 2 |
| among | among friends | Middle of 3+ |

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| at the room | in the room |
| in the table | on the table |
| in the door | at the door |
| between 3 people | among 3 people |
| on India | in India |
