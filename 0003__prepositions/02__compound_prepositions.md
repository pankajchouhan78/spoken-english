## Detailed Usage with Examples

### According to - "Ke Anusar"

1. According to the news, it will rain.
   - Khabar ke anusar baarish hogi.

2. He did everything according to the plan.
   - Usne sab kuch plan ke anusar kiya.

3. According to me, this is wrong.
   - Mere hisaab se ye galat hai.

4. According to the doctor, take rest.
   - Doctor ke anusar aaraam karo.

5. We worked according to rules.
   - Humne niyamon ke anusar kaam kiya.

---

### Ahead of - "Se Aage"

1. He is ahead of his class.
   - Wo apni class se aage hai.

2. Walk ahead of me.
   - Mere aage chalo.

3. We finished ahead of time.
   - Humne waqt se pehle khatam kiya.

4. Think ahead of problems.
   - Problems se pehle socho.

5. India is ahead of Pakistan in the match.
   - Match mein India Pakistan se aage hai.

---

### Along with - "Ke Saath"

1. Come along with your friends.
   - Apne doston ke saath aao.

2. He brought food along with drinks.
   - Wo khana aur drinks saath laya.

3. She works along with her husband.
   - Wo apne husband ke saath kaam karti hai.

4. I'll go along with your plan.
   - Main tumhare plan ke saath hoon.

5. Along with studies, do exercise.
   - Padhai ke saath exercise bhi karo.

---

### Apart from - "Ke Alawa"

1. Apart from English, I speak Hindi.
   - English ke alawa main Hindi bolta hoon.

2. Apart from you, no one helps me.
   - Tumhare alawa koi madad nahi karta.

3. Apart from this, there's nothing else.
   - Iske alawa kuch nahi hai.

4. Apart from salary, I get bonus.
   - Salary ke alawa mujhe bonus milta hai.

5. Apart from cooking, she loves gardening.
   - Khana banana ke alawa use gardening pasand hai.

---

### Because of - "Ki Wajah Se"

1. I'm late because of traffic.
   - Traffic ki wajah se late hoon.

2. He failed because of laziness.
   - Aalas ki wajah se wo fail hua.

3. Because of you, I'm happy.
   - Tumhari wajah se main khush hoon.

4. The match was cancelled because of rain.
   - Baarish ki wajah se match cancel hua.

5. I couldn't sleep because of noise.
   - Shor ki wajah se main so nahi paaya.

---

### Due to - "Ki Wajah Se"

1. The flight is delayed due to fog.
   - Kohra ki wajah se flight late hai.

2. He's absent due to illness.
   - Bimari ki wajah se wo absent hai.

3. Success is due to hard work.
   - Safalta mehnat ki wajah se hai.

4. Due to lack of time, I couldn't come.
   - Time ki kami ki wajah se nahi aa paaya.

5. The shop is closed due to holiday.
   - Chutti ki wajah se dukaan band hai.

---

### In front of - "Ke Saamne"

1. Stand in front of the class.
   - Class ke saamne khade ho.

2. The car is parked in front of the house.
   - Car ghar ke saamne khadi hai.

3. Don't talk in front of elders.
   - Badon ke saamne baat mat karo.

4. He stood in front of the mirror.
   - Wo sheeshe ke saamne khada tha.

5. The temple is in front of the market.
   - Mandir market ke saamne hai.

6. Don't stand in front of the TV.
   - TV ke saamne mat khade raho.

7. In front of everyone, he apologized.
   - Sabke saamne usne maafi maangi.

8. The garden is in front of the hotel.
   - Hotel ke saamne garden hai.

9. She parked right in front of my door.
   - Usne mere darwaze ke saamne park kiya.

10. In front of his boss, he was very polite.
    - Boss ke saamne wo bahut polite tha.

---

### In spite of - "Ke Bawajood"

1. In spite of the rain, we went out.
   - Baarish ke bawajood hum bahar gaye.

2. He passed in spite of difficulties.
   - Mushkilon ke bawajood wo pass hua.

3. In spite of being tired, she worked.
   - Thaki hone ke bawajood usne kaam kiya.

4. In spite of warnings, he ignored.
   - Chetawani ke bawajood usne ignore kiya.

5. She smiled in spite of pain.
   - Dard ke bawajood wo muskurai.

6. In spite of his efforts, he failed.
   - Uski koshish ke bawajood wo fail hua.

7. In spite of the traffic, we arrived on time.
   - Traffic ke bawajood hum time par pahunche.

8. In spite of having money, he's unhappy.
   - Paise hone ke bawajood wo khush nahi hai.

9. In spite of my request, he left.
   - Meri request ke bawajood wo chala gaya.

10. In spite of all odds, they won.
    - Sabhi mushkilon ke bawajood unhone jeeta.

---

### Instead of - "Ki Jagah"

1. Use honey instead of sugar.
   - Cheeni ki jagah shahad use karo.

2. He came instead of his brother.
   - Wo apne bhai ki jagah aaya.

3. Let's walk instead of driving.
   - Drive ki jagah paidal chalte hain.

4. Study instead of playing.
   - Khelne ki jagah padho.

5. Instead of complaining, take action.
   - Shikayat ki jagah action lo.

6. He went instead of his brother.
   - Wo apne bhai ki jagah gaya.

7. Instead of waiting, I called him.
   - Wait karne ki jagah maine use call kiya.

8. Use this pen instead of that.
   - Us ki jagah ye pen use karo.

9. Instead of sleeping late, wake up early.
   - Der tak sone ki jagah jaldi utho.

10. Instead of arguing, let's discuss.
    - Bahas ki jagah baat karte hain.

5. The shop next to the restaurant.

6. The ATM is next to the bank.
   - ATM bank ke bagal mein hai.

7. My seat was next to the window.
   - Meri seat window ke bagal mein thi.

8. The hotel is next to the station.
   - Hotel station ke bagal mein hai.

9. He was standing right next to me.

10. The coffee shop is next to the bookstore.
    - Coffee shop bookstore ke bagal mein hai.

### On behalf of - "Ki Taraf Se"

1. I'm speaking on behalf of my team.
   - Main apni team ki taraf se bol raha hoon.

2. He accepted the award on behalf of his father.
   - Usne apne father ki taraf se award liya.

3. On behalf of everyone, thank you.
   - Sabki taraf se dhanyavaad.

4. She signed on behalf of the company.
   - Usne company ki taraf se sign kiye.

5. I apologize on behalf of my friend.
   - Main apne dost ki taraf se maafi maangta hoon.

6. On behalf of the company, we welcome you.
   - Company ki taraf se hum aapka swagat karte hain.

7. He collected the cheque on behalf of his father.
   - Usne apne papa ki taraf se cheque liya.

8. On behalf of all of us, thank you!
   - Hum sabki taraf se dhanyavad!

9. She represented on behalf of her country.
   - Usne apne desh ki taraf se pratinidhitva kiya.

10. On behalf of the group, I accept this honor.
    - Group ki taraf se main ye samman sweekaar karta hoon.

### Out of - "Se Bahar / Mein Se"

1. He jumped out of the window.
   - Wo khidki se bahar kooda.

2. Nine out of ten people agree.
   - Dus mein se nau log maante hain.

3. We ran out of time.
   - Humara time khatam ho gaya.

4. She did it out of love.
   - Usne ye pyaar se kiya.

5. I'm out of money.
   - Mere paas paise nahi hain.

6. She made a dress out of old curtains.
   - Usne purane pardon se dress banayi.

7. 9 out of 10 students passed.
   - 10 mein se 9 students pass hue.

8. Get out of my way!
   - Mere raaste se hato!

9. I'm out of ideas.
   - Mere paas ideas nahi rahe.

10. We're running out of time.
    - Humara time khatam ho raha hai.

### Prior to - "Se Pehle"

1. Prior to the meeting, review the documents.
   - Meeting se pehle documents dekh lo.

2. He worked there prior to joining us.
   - Humse judne se pehle wo wahan kaam karta tha.

3. Prior to 2020, things were different.
   - 2020 se pehle cheezein alag thi.

4. Please arrive prior to 9 AM.
   - 9 baje se pehle aa jana.

5. Prior to this, I had no experience.
   - Isse pehle mujhe koi experience nahi tha.

6. Prior to his arrival, we prepared the room.
   - Uske aane se pehle humne kamra taiyar kiya.

7. Prior to the exam, study well.
   - Exam se pehle acche se padho.

8. Prior to cooking, wash the vegetables.
   - Khana banane se pehle sabzi dho lo.

9. Prior to leaving, check everything.
   - Jaane se pehle sab check karo.

10. Prior to the surgery, follow these rules.
    - Surgery se pehle in niyamon ka palan karo.

### Thanks to - "Ki Wajah Se" (Positive)

1. Thanks to you, we succeeded.
   - Tumhari wajah se hum safal hue.

2. Thanks to technology, life is easier.
   - Technology ki wajah se zindagi aasan hai.

3. Thanks to his help, I finished early.
   - Uski madad ki wajah se maine jaldi khatam kiya.

4. Thanks to the doctor, he recovered.
   - Doctor ki wajah se wo theek hua.

5. Thanks to hard work, she got promoted.
   - Mehnat ki wajah se uski promotion hui.

6. Thanks to the rain, it's cool now.
   - Baarish ki wajah se ab thanda hai.

7. Thanks to technology, life is easier.
   - Technology ki wajah se zindagi aasaan hai.

8. Thanks to my teacher, I learned this.
   - Mere teacher ki wajah se maine ye seekha.

9. Thanks to good planning, we finished early.
   - Acchi planning ki wajah se humne jaldi khatam kiya.

10. Thanks to your help, I passed.
    - Tumhari madad ki wajah se main pass hua.

### With regard to - "Ke Baare Mein"

1. With regard to your question, I'll explain.
   - Tumhare sawaal ke baare mein main samjhaunga.

2. I'm calling with regard to the job.
   - Main job ke baare mein call kar raha hoon.

3. With regard to this matter, we'll discuss.
   - Is maamle ke baare mein hum baat karenge.

4. With regard to your complaint, we apologize.
   - Tumhari shikayat ke baare mein hum maafi chahte hain.

5. Nothing to say with regard to this.
   - Iske baare mein kuch nahi kehna.

6. With regard to the policy, here's the update.
   - Policy ke baare mein ye update hai.

7. We contacted you with regard to your application.
   - Tumhari application ke baare mein humne contact kiya.

8. With regard to the deadline, it's been extended.
   - Deadline ke baare mein, ise badha diya gaya hai.

9. With regard to safety, we're very strict.
   - Safety ke baare mein hum bahut strict hain.

10. With regard to this project, let's talk tomorrow.
    - Is project ke baare mein kal baat karte hain.
