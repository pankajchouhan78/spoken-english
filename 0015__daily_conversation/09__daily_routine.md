# Daily Routine

> How to describe your daily routine in English.

---

## Morning Routine

| English | Hindi |
|---------|-------|
| I usually wake up around 6:30 AM. | Main usually 6:30 baje uthta hoon. |
| After waking up, I stretch and make my bed. | Uthne ke baad, main stretching karta hoon aur bed theek karta hoon. |
| I brush my teeth and take a quick shower. | Main brush karta hoon aur jaldi se shower leta hoon. |
| Then, I get dressed for the day. | Phir, main kapde pehenta hoon. |
| I prepare and eat a healthy breakfast. | Main healthy breakfast banata hoon aur khata hoon. |
| Sometimes, I listen to music while eating. | Kabhi kabhi khate waqt music sunta hoon. |
| I leave the house by 8:00 AM. | Main 8 baje ghar se nikalta hoon. |
| On my way, I like to listen to a podcast. | Raaste mein, main podcast sunna pasand karta hoon. |

## Evening Routine

| English | Hindi |

## Sample Daily Routine Paragraph

**My Daily Routine:**

I wake up at **6:30 AM**. After that, I do all the necessary tasks like freshening up, brushing my teeth, taking a shower, and getting ready for the day. Then I have **breakfast**.

I leave the house at around **8:00 AM**. When I reach the office, I greet everyone and shake hands with my colleagues. After that, we start our work.

At some point in the morning, we have **tea** in the office. Then, between **1:00 and 2:00 PM**, we take our **lunch break**. After lunch, I take a **short break to refresh**, and then get back to work.

Around **6:00 PM**, we take another short break. I usually leave the office between **8:00 and 9:00 PM**.

When I reach home, I **wash my face and hands**, and **change into comfortable clothes**. After that, I have **dinner**, and then spend some **time with my family**. I do **10 minutes of meditation** before going to bed at around **11:30 PM**.
