# Interview Phrases

> Common phrases for job interviews.

---

## Introducing Yourself

| English | Hindi |
|---------|-------|
| Let me introduce myself. | Main apna introduction deta hoon. |
| I have X years of experience in... | Mere paas X saal ka experience hai ... mein. |
| My strength is... | Meri strength hai... |
| I'm passionate about... | Mujhe ... mein passion hai. |
| I'm a quick learner. | Main jaldi seekhta hoon. |
| I work well under pressure. | Main pressure mein achha kaam karta hoon. |
| I'm looking for new challenges. | Main naye challenges dhundh raha hoon. |
| I'm a team player. | Main team ke saath achha kaam karta hoon. |

---

## Talking About Experience

| English | Hindi |
|---------|-------|
| In my previous role, I... | Apni pichli job mein, main... |
| I was responsible for... | Main ... ke liye responsible tha. |
| I managed a team of... | Maine ... logon ki team manage ki. |
| I successfully completed... | Maine successfully complete kiya... |
| I learned a lot about... | Maine ... ke baare mein bahut seekha. |
| My biggest achievement was... | Meri sabse badi achievement thi... |

---

## Asking Questions in Interview

| English | Hindi |
|---------|-------|
| What does a typical day look like? | Ek normal din kaisa hota hai? |
| What are the growth opportunities? | Growth ke kya opportunities hain? |
| Can you tell me about the team? | Team ke baare mein bataiye? |
| What's the company culture like? | Company culture kaisa hai? |
| When can I expect to hear back? | Kab tak jawab milega? |
| What are the next steps? | Agle steps kya hain? |
| Is there anything else you need from me? | Kya aapko mujhse kuch aur chahiye? |

---

## Answering Tough Questions

| English | Hindi |
|---------|-------|
| That's a great question. | Yeh bahut achha sawaal hai. |
| Let me think about that. | Mujhe sochne dijiye. |
| I haven't faced that situation, but... | Maine aisi situation face nahi ki, lekin... |
| I would handle it by... | Main isse handle karta... |
| In my experience... | Mere experience mein... |
| I'm not sure, but I would... | Mujhe sure nahi, lekin main... |

---

## Ending the Interview

| English | Hindi |
|---------|-------|
| Thank you for your time. | Aapke time ke liye shukriya. |
| It was great meeting you. | Aapse milkar achha laga. |
| I'm very interested in this role. | Mujhe is role mein bahut interest hai. |
| I look forward to hearing from you. | Main aapke jawab ka intezaar karunga. |
| Please let me know if you need anything else. | Agar kuch aur chahiye toh batana. |
| I hope to work with you soon. | Umeed hai jaldi saath kaam karenge. |

---

## Common Interview Questions

| Question | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| Tell me about yourself. | Apne baare mein batao. |
| Why do you want this job? | Tum yeh job kyu chahte ho? |
| What are your strengths? | Tumhari strengths kya hain? |
| What are your weaknesses? | Tumhari weaknesses kya hain? |
| Where do you see yourself in 5 years? | 5 saal mein khud ko kahan dekhte ho? |
| Why should we hire you? | Hum tumhe kyu hire karein? |
| Do you have any questions for us? | Tumhara koi sawaal hai? |
