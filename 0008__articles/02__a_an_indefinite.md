# A and AN - Indefinite Articles

> A/AN = "one" or "any one" (koi ek)
> Used for NON-SPECIFIC, SINGULAR, COUNTABLE nouns.

---

## A vs AN - The Sound Rule

| Use A | Use AN |
|-------|--------|
| Before CONSONANT sound | Before VOWEL sound |
| a book, a car | an apple, an egg |
| a university (yoo-) | an umbrella |
| a European (yoo-) | an hour (silent h) |

---

## Uses of A/AN

### 1. First Time Mention (Pehli Baar)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I saw a dog in the park.
   - Maine park mein ek kutta dekha.

2. She bought an umbrella.
   - Usne ek chhata kharida.

3. There's a problem with the car.
   - Car mein ek problem hai.

4. I met a friend yesterday.
   - Kal main ek dost se mila.

5. He found a wallet on the road.
   - Use road par ek wallet mila.

---

### 2. Profession / Job (Kaam/Pesa)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. He is a doctor.
   - Wo ek doctor hai.

2. She is an engineer.
   - Wo ek engineer hai.

3. I want to be a teacher.
   - Main ek teacher banna chahta hoon.

4. My father is a businessman.
   - Mere papa ek businessman hain.

5. She works as an accountant.
   - Wo ek accountant ki tarah kaam karti hai.

6. He became a pilot.
   - Wo ek pilot ban gaya.

7. I'm a student.
   - Main ek student hoon.

8. She is an artist.
   - Wo ek artist hai.

9. My brother is a lawyer.
   - Mera bhai ek vakeel hai.

10. He is an honest politician.
    - Wo ek imaandar neta hai.

---

### 3. "One" Meaning (Ek ka Matlab)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I'll be back in an hour.
   - Main ek ghante mein wapas aaunga.

2. She ate an apple.
   - Usne ek seb khaya.

3. Give me a minute.
   - Ek minute do.

4. I need a pen.
   - Mujhe ek pen chahiye.

5. Wait a moment.
   - Ek pal ruko.

6. He earns a lakh per month.
   - Wo ek lakh per month kamata hai.

7. I have a question.
   - Mera ek sawaal hai.

8. Can I have a glass of water?
   - Ek glass paani mil sakta hai?

9. It costs a hundred rupees.
   - Ye sau rupaye ka hai.

10. I'll call you in a week.
    - Main ek hafte mein call karunga.

---

### 4. "Per" or "Each" Meaning (Har/Prati)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I go to gym twice a week.
   - Main hafte mein do baar gym jaata hoon.

2. This costs fifty rupees a kilo.
   - Ye pachaas rupaye kilo hai.

3. He drives 100 km an hour.
   - Wo 100 km per hour drive karta hai.

4. She earns 500 rupees an hour.
   - Wo 500 rupaye ghante kamaati hai.

5. We meet once a month.
   - Hum mahine mein ek baar milte hain.

6. Take this medicine twice a day.
   - Ye dawai din mein do baar lo.

7. The car runs 15 km a liter.
   - Car 15 km per liter chalti hai.

8. I read a book a month.
   - Main ek kitaab mahine mein padhta hoon.

9. They pay a thousand a day.
   - Wo hazaar rupaye din dete hain.

10. She teaches three days a week.
    - Wo hafte mein teen din padhati hai.

---

### 5. Type/Kind (Prakar)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. A mango is a fruit.
   - Aam ek phal hai.

2. A dog is a loyal animal.
   - Kutta ek wafadar jaanwar hai.

3. An elephant is a large animal.
   - Haathi ek bada jaanwar hai.

4. A laptop is a useful device.
   - Laptop ek useful device hai.

5. An apple is healthy.
   - Seb healthy hota hai.

---

### 6. Exclamations with "What"

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. What a beautiful day!
   - Kitna sundar din!

2. What an idea!
   - Kya idea hai!

3. What a shame!
   - Kitni sharam ki baat!

4. What a surprise!
   - Kya surprise hai!

5. What an amazing view!
   - Kitna amazing nazaara!

6. What a waste!
   - Kitna waste!

7. What a relief!
   - Kitni rahat!

8. What a coincidence!
   - Kya ittefaaq hai!

9. What an honor!
   - Kitne sammaan ki baat!

10. What a mess!
    - Kya gandagi hai!

## Tricky Cases - A or AN?

| Word | Article | Reason |
|------|---------|--------|
| university | **a** university | "yoo" sound (consonant) |
| umbrella | **an** umbrella | "uh" sound (vowel) |
| European | **a** European | "yoo" sound (consonant) |
| hour | **an** hour | Silent 'h', "ow" sound |
| honest | **an** honest man | Silent 'h', "o" sound |
| one | **a** one-way street | "wun" sound (consonant) |
| uniform | **a** uniform | "yoo" sound (consonant) |
| uncle | **an** uncle | "uh" sound (vowel) |
| useful | **a** useful tool | "yoo" sound (consonant) |
| MBA | **an** MBA | "em" sound (vowel) |
| hotel | **a** hotel | 'h' is pronounced |
| heir | **an** heir | Silent 'h' |

**Sentences:**

1. He is a European tourist.
   - Wo ek European tourist hai.

2. I'll be there in an hour.
   - Main ek ghante mein wahaan hounga.

3. She is an honest person.
   - Wo ek imaandar insaan hai.

4. It was a one-time offer.
   - Ye ek baar ki offer thi.

5. He is wearing a uniform.
   - Wo uniform pehne hai.

6. My uncle is an MBA.
   - Mere chacha MBA hain.

7. This is a useful tip.
   - Ye ek useful tip hai.

8. I stayed at a hotel.
   - Main ek hotel mein ruka.

9. He is an heir to the throne.
   - Wo gaddi ka waarish hai.

10. She attends a university.
    - Wo ek university mein padhti hai.

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| He is doctor. | He is **a** doctor. |
| She bought umbrella. | She bought **an** umbrella. |
| I need an one-way ticket. | I need **a** one-way ticket. |
| He is a honest man. | He is **an** honest man. |
| I have an uniform. | I have **a** uniform. |
| What beautiful day! | What **a** beautiful day! |

## Summary

| Use A/AN When | Example |
|---------------|---------|
| First mention | I saw **a** cat. |
| Profession | She is **an** engineer. |
| Meaning "one" | Give me **a** minute. |
| Meaning "per" | Twice **a** week. |
| Type/Kind | **A** lion is dangerous. |
| Exclamations | What **a** day! |
