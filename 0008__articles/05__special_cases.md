# Special Cases & Common Mistakes in Articles

> Tricky situations where article usage can be confusing.

---

## Same Word, Different Meaning

### Hospital, School, College, Prison, Church, Bed

| With THE | Without Article |
|----------|-----------------|
| Purpose: visit/work | Purpose: main function |
| go to **the** hospital (to visit someone) | go to hospital (as patient) |
| go to **the** school (to meet teacher) | go to school (to study) |

**Sentences:**

1. He is in hospital. (as a patient)
   - Wo hospital mein hai (bimar hai).

2. I went to the hospital to visit him. (visitor)
   - Main usse milne hospital gaya.

3. Children go to school. (to study)
   - Bachche school jaate hain.

4. Parents went to the school. (for meeting)
   - Parents school gaye (meeting ke liye).

5. He is in prison. (as a prisoner)
   - Wo jail mein hai (kaidi hai).

6. The lawyer went to the prison. (to meet client)
   - Vakeel jail gaya (client se milne).

7. I go to bed at 10 PM. (to sleep)
   - Main 10 baje sone jaata hoon.

8. The cat is on the bed. (furniture)
   - Billi bed par hai.

9. She goes to church on Sundays. (to pray)
   - Wo Sunday ko church jaati hai.

10. The wedding is at the church. (building)
    - Shaadi church mein hai.

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Missing Article with Professions

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| He is doctor. | He is **a** doctor. |
| She is engineer. | She is **an** engineer. |
| I want to be teacher. | I want to be **a** teacher. |

### Mistake 2: Using Article with Uncountables (General)

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| I like the music. | I like music. |
| The water is important. | Water is important. |
| I need the money. | I need money. |

### Mistake 3: Using THE with Countries/Languages

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| I live in the India. | I live in India. |
| I speak the English. | I speak English. |
| She went to the Japan. | She went to Japan. |

### Mistake 4: Wrong A/AN Based on Sound

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| a honest man | **an** honest man |
| an university | **a** university |
| a MBA | **an** MBA |
| an European | **a** European |

### Mistake 5: Missing THE with Unique Things

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| Sun rises in east. | **The** sun rises in **the** east. |
| Moon is beautiful. | **The** moon is beautiful. |
| Internet is useful. | **The** internet is useful. |

### Mistake 6: Missing THE with Superlatives

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| She is best student. | She is **the** best student. |
| This is most expensive. | This is **the** most expensive. |
| He is tallest in class. | He is **the** tallest in class. |
