# Zero Article - When NOT to Use Articles

> Sometimes we use NO article at all.
> This is called "Zero Article" (Ø).

---

## When to Use Zero Article

### 1. General Plural Nouns (Saamanya Bahuvachan)

> Talking about ALL of a category, not specific ones.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Dogs are loyal animals.
   - Kutte wafadar jaanwar hote hain.

2. Books are our best friends.
   - Kitaabein humare sabse acche dost hain.

3. Cars are expensive these days.
   - Aaj kal cars mehengi hain.

4. Children love to play.
   - Bachche khelna pasand karte hain.

5. Phones have become necessary.
   - Phone zaroori ho gaye hain.

6. Teachers deserve respect.
   - Teachers izzat ke haqdaar hain.

7. Doctors help patients.
   - Doctors marizo ki madad karte hain.

8. Students should study hard.
   - Students ko mehnat se padhna chahiye.

9. Flowers are beautiful.
   - Phool sundar hote hain.

10. Birds can fly.
    - Pakshi ud sakte hain.

---

### 2. Uncountable Nouns (Jo Gine Nahi Ja Sakte)

> General concepts, materials, abstract ideas.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Water is essential for life.
   - Paani jeevan ke liye zaroori hai.

2. I love music.
   - Mujhe sangeet pasand hai.

3. Money can't buy happiness.
   - Paise se khushi nahi kharid sakte.

4. Time is precious.
   - Samay keemati hai.

5. Sugar is sweet.
   - Cheeni meethi hoti hai.

6. Knowledge is power.
   - Gyan hi shakti hai.

7. Love conquers all.
   - Pyaar sab jeet leta hai.

8. Rice is a staple food in India.
   - Chawal Bharat ka mukhya bhojan hai.

9. Patience is important.
   - Sabr zaroori hai.

10. Air is getting polluted.
    - Hawa praduusit ho rahi hai.

11. Coffee keeps me awake.
    - Coffee mujhe jaaga rakhti hai.

12. Gold is expensive.
    - Sona mehenga hai.

13. Information is free on the internet.
    - Internet par jaankari free hai.

14. Honesty is the best policy.
    - Imaandari sabse acchi neeti hai.

15. Education changes lives.
    - Shiksha zindagi badal deti hai.

### 3. Meals (Bhojan)

3. Let's have dinner together.
   - Saath mein dinner karte hain.

4. I skipped breakfast today.
   - Aaj naashta chhod diya.

5. We met over lunch.
   - Hum lunch par mile.

6. Dinner was delicious.
   - Dinner swaadist tha.

7. I don't eat much for lunch.

   - Humne raat 8 baje dinner kiya.
