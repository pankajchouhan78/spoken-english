# THE - Definite Article

> THE = Specific one (wo wala / khaas)
> Used when both speaker and listener KNOW which one.

---

## Uses of THE

### 1. Second Mention (Doosri Baar)

> First time: a/an → Second time: the

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I bought a phone. The phone is very fast.
   - Maine ek phone liya. Wo phone bahut fast hai.

2. I saw a dog. The dog was barking.
   - Maine ek kutta dekha. Wo kutta bhaunk raha tha.

3. She has a car. The car is red.
   - Uske paas ek car hai. Wo car laal hai.

4. I met a man. The man was helpful.
   - Main ek aadmi se mila. Wo aadmi helpful tha.

5. We visited a temple. The temple was beautiful.
   - Hum ek mandir gaye. Wo mandir sundar tha.

---

### 2. Unique Things (Ek Hi Hai)

> Only one exists.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. The sun rises in the east.
   - Suraj purab mein ugta hai.

2. The moon is bright tonight.
   - Aaj raat chaand chamak raha hai.

3. The earth rotates on its axis.
   - Dharti apni dhuri par ghoomti hai.

4. The sky is clear today.
   - Aaj aasmaan saaf hai.

5. The internet has changed everything.
   - Internet ne sab badal diya.

6. The government announced new rules.
   - Sarkar ne naye niyam announce kiye.

7. The Prime Minister gave a speech.
   - PM ne bhashan diya.

8. The world is changing fast.
   - Duniya tezi se badal rahi hai.

9. The universe is infinite.
   - Brahmand anant hai.

10. The Taj Mahal is in Agra.
    - Taj Mahal Agra mein hai.

---

### 3. Known from Context (Pata Hai Kaunsa)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Close the door.
   - Darwaza band karo. (we know which door)

2. Where is the bathroom?
   - Bathroom kahaan hai?

3. The food is ready.
   - Khana taiyaar hai.

4. Can you pass the salt?
   - Namak dena.

5. Turn off the light.
   - Light band karo.

6. The boss is calling you.
   - Boss bula rahe hain.

7. I'll meet you at the office.
   - Main office mein milunga.

8. The children are playing outside.
   - Bachche bahar khel rahe hain.

9. Did you finish the work?
   - Kaam khatam kiya?

10. The meeting is at 3 PM.
    - Meeting 3 baje hai.

---

### 4. Superlatives (Sabse + Adjective)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She is the best student.
   - Wo sabse acchi student hai.

2. This is the tallest building.
   - Ye sabse lambi building hai.

3. He is the fastest runner.
   - Wo sabse tez daudne wala hai.

4. This is the most expensive one.
   - Ye sabse mehenga hai.

5. She is the smartest in class.
   - Wo class mein sabse smart hai.

6. That was the worst day.
   - Wo sabse bura din tha.

7. This is the easiest way.
   - Ye sabse aasaan tarika hai.

8. He is the richest man in town.
   - Wo sheher ka sabse ameer aadmi hai.

9. It's the hottest day of the year.
   - Ye saal ka sabse garam din hai.

10. She is the most beautiful.
    - Wo sabse sundar hai.

---

### 5. Ordinal Numbers (First, Second, Third...)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She won the first prize.
   - Usne pehla prize jeeta.

2. I live on the second floor.
   - Main doosri manzil par rehta hoon.

3. He came the third time.
   - Wo teesri baar aaya.

4. It's the tenth day of the month.
   - Ye mahine ka dasvan din hai.

5. This is the last chance.
   - Ye aakhri mauka hai.

6. He is the only child.
   - Wo ikauta bachcha hai.

7. It's the next step.
   - Ye agla kadam hai.

8. This is the same problem.
   - Ye wahi problem hai.

9. She was the first to arrive.
   - Wo sabse pehle aayi.

10. It's the hundredth time I'm telling you.
    - Main sauvi baar bol raha hoon.

### 6. Musical Instruments (Bajaana)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She plays the piano.
   - Wo piano bajati hai.

2. He plays the guitar.
   - Wo guitar bajata hai.

3. I'm learning the violin.
   - Main violin seekh raha hoon.

4. She is good at the flute.
   - Wo bansuri mein acchi hai.

5. He plays the drums.
   - Wo drums bajata hai.

---

### 7. Geographical Names

**Use THE:**
- Rivers: the Ganges, the Yamuna
- Oceans/Seas: the Indian Ocean, the Arabian Sea
- Mountain Ranges: the Himalayas
- Deserts: the Sahara, the Thar
- Groups of Islands: the Andamans
- Countries with Republic/Kingdom/States: the USA, the UK

**NO THE:**
- Countries: India, Japan (not "the India")
- Single Mountains: Mount Everest
- Lakes: Lake Baikal
- Continents: Asia, Europe

**Sentences:**

1. The Ganges is a holy river.
   - Ganga pavitra nadi hai.

2. The Himalayas are beautiful.
   - Himalaya sundar hai.

3. We crossed the Arabian Sea.
   - Humne Arabian Sea paar ki.

4. The Thar desert is in Rajasthan.
   - Thar Rajasthan mein hai.

5. She traveled across the USA.
   - Usne USA ghuma.

---

### 8. Newspapers, Holy Books, Famous Buildings

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I read the Times of India.
   - Main Times of India padhta hoon.

2. The Quran is the holy book of Islam.
   - Quran Islam ki pavitra kitaab hai.

3. The Bible contains many teachings.
   - Bible mein bahut sikhayein hain.

4. The Ramayana is an epic.
   - Ramayana ek mahakavya hai.

5. The Eiffel Tower is in Paris.
   - Eiffel Tower Paris mein hai.

---

### 9. Directions (Dishayein)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. The sun rises in the east.
   - Suraj purab mein ugta hai.

2. The sun sets in the west.
   - Suraj paschim mein doobta hai.

3. We traveled to the north.
   - Hum uttar ki taraf gaye.

4. The wind is blowing from the south.
   - Hawa dakshin se aa rahi hai.

5. India is in the south of Asia.
   - Bharat Asia ke dakshin mein hai.

---

### 10. Groups of People (Samuh)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. The rich should help the poor.
   - Ameer ko gareeb ki madad karni chahiye.

2. The young have more energy.
   - Jawaan mein zyada energy hoti hai.

3. The old deserve respect.
   - Buzurg izzat ke haqdaar hain.

4. The blind face many challenges.
   - Andhe ko bahut mushkilein aati hain.

5. The unemployed need support.
   - Berozgaar ko support chahiye.

---

## Summary - When to Use THE

| Use Case | Example |
|----------|---------|
| Second mention | I saw a cat. **The** cat was white. |
| Unique things | **The** sun, **the** moon |
| Known from context | Close **the** door. |
| Superlatives | **The** best, **the** tallest |
| Ordinals | **The** first, **the** last |
| Instruments | Play **the** guitar |
| Rivers, Oceans | **The** Ganges, **the** Pacific |
| Holy books | **The** Quran, **the** Bible |
| Directions | In **the** east |
| Groups | **The** rich, **the** poor |
