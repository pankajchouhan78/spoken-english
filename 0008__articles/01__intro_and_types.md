# Articles - Introduction & Types

> Articles are small words (a, an, the) that come BEFORE nouns.
> They tell us if the noun is specific or general.

---

## What are Articles?

| Type | Article | Use | Hindi Concept |
|------|---------|-----|---------------|
| Indefinite | a, an | Any one (general) | Koi ek |
| Definite | the | Specific one | Wo wala (specific) |
| Zero | (no article) | General/uncountable | Koi article nahi |

---

## The Three Types

### 1. Indefinite Articles (A / AN)

> Used for NON-SPECIFIC, SINGULAR, COUNTABLE nouns.

| Article | Rule | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| A | Before consonant SOUND | a book, a university |
| AN | Before vowel SOUND | an apple, an hour |

**Key:** It's about SOUND, not spelling!

1. I saw a dog. (any dog, not specific)
   - Maine ek kutta dekha.

2. She is an engineer. (one of many engineers)
   - Wo ek engineer hai.

3. Give me a pen. (any pen)
   - Mujhe ek pen do.

---

### 2. Definite Article (THE)

> Used for SPECIFIC nouns that both speaker and listener know.

1. The dog is barking. (that specific dog)
   - Wo kutta bhaunk raha hai.

2. Open the door. (the door we both know)
   - Darwaza kholo.

3. The sun rises in the east. (only one sun)
   - Suraj purab mein ugta hai.

---

### 3. Zero Article (No Article)

> No article needed in some cases.

1. I love music. (general concept)
   - Mujhe sangeet pasand hai.

2. Water is essential. (uncountable, general)
   - Paani zaroori hai.

3. Dogs are loyal. (all dogs, general)
   - Kutte wafadar hote hain.

---

## Quick Comparison

| Situation | Example | Why |
|-----------|---------|-----|
| Any one | I need **a** pen. | Non-specific |
| Specific one | Give me **the** pen. | Both know which |
| All/General | **Ø** Pens are useful. | Plural, general |
| Uncountable | **Ø** Water is cold. | Can't count |

---

## A vs AN - Sound Rule

| SOUND | Article | Examples |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Consonant sound | A | a dog, a cat, a **u**niversity (yoo-), a **E**uropean |
| Vowel sound | AN | an apple, an **h**our (silent h), an **M**BA, an **h**onest man |

**Tricky Examples:**

| Word | Article | Why |
|------|---------|-----|
| university | a | Starts with "yoo" sound |
| hour | an | Silent 'h', starts with "ow" sound |
| honest | an | Silent 'h', starts with "o" sound |
| one | a | Starts with "w" sound |
| MBA | an | Starts with "em" sound |
| European | a | Starts with "yoo" sound |

## When to Use What?

```
Is the noun COUNTABLE?
│
├── YES (book, pen, dog)
│   │
│   ├── SINGULAR?
│   │   │
│   │   ├── SPECIFIC? → THE book
│   │   │
│   │   └── NOT SPECIFIC? → A/AN book
│   │
│   └── PLURAL?
│       │
│       ├── SPECIFIC? → THE books
│       │
│       └── NOT SPECIFIC? → (no article) books
│
└── NO (water, music, love)
    │
    ├── SPECIFIC? → THE water (in this glass)
    │
    └── GENERAL? → (no article) Water is essential.
```

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Why |
|---------|---------|-----|
| He is engineer. | He is **an** engineer. | Profession needs article |
| I love the music. | I love music. | General concept, no article |
| Sun is bright. | **The** sun is bright. | Only one sun |
| I have an uniform. | I have **a** uniform. | "yoo" sound |
| Give me a honest answer. | Give me **an** honest answer. | Silent 'h' |

## Summary Table

| Article | Use | Example |
|---------|-----|---------|
| A | Consonant sound, singular, non-specific | a book, a user |
| AN | Vowel sound, singular, non-specific | an apple, an hour |
| THE | Specific (singular or plural) | the book, the books |
| (none) | General plural or uncountable | books, water |
