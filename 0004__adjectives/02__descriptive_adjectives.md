# Descriptive Adjectives - Daily Use

> Words that describe the QUALITY of nouns.

---

## Appearance Adjectives

### Beautiful / Pretty / Handsome / Ugly

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She is very beautiful.
   - Wo bahut sundar hai.

2. What a pretty dress!
   - Kitni pyari dress hai!

3. He is a handsome man.
   - Wo ek sundar aadmi hai.

4. The ugly truth is hard to accept.
   - Kadvi sachai maanna mushkil hai.

5. Your handwriting is beautiful.
   - Tumhari likhawat sundar hai.

6. This is a pretty good idea.
   - Ye kaafi accha idea hai.

7. She looks beautiful in that saree.
   - Wo us saree mein sundar lagti hai.

8. He is handsome and smart.
   - Wo sundar aur smart hai.

9. That was an ugly fight.
   - Wo ek buri ladai thi.

10. The sunset is beautiful today.
    - Aaj ka suryast sundar hai.

### Tall / Short / Fat / Thin

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. My brother is very tall.
   - Mera bhai bahut lamba hai.

2. She is short but strong.
   - Wo chhoti hai par mazboot hai.

3. I'm getting fat these days.
   - Aaj kal main mota ho raha hoon.

4. He became very thin after illness.
   - Bimari ke baad wo bahut patla ho gaya.

5. The tall building is our office.
   - Wo lambi building hamara office hai.

6. This rope is too short.
   - Ye rassi bahut chhoti hai.

7. The fat cat sleeps all day.
   - Moti billi din bhar soti hai.

8. She has thin hair.
   - Uske baal patle hain.

9. He is the tallest in the class.
   - Wo class mein sabse lamba hai.

10. Short people can also be successful.
    - Chhote log bhi safal ho sakte hain.

11. Don't eat too much or you'll get fat.
    - Zyada mat khao nahi to mote ho jaoge.

12. This thin wire is dangerous.
    - Ye patli taar khatarnak hai.

13. Tall trees are good for shade.
    - Lambe ped chhaon ke liye acche hain.

14. Keep the answer short and simple.
    - Jawab chhota aur simple rakho.

15. The thin man ran fast.
    - Patla aadmi tez bhaaga.

## Condition Adjectives

### Clean / Dirty / Wet / Dry

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Keep your room clean.
   - Apna kamra saaf rakho.

2. The street is very dirty.
   - Sadak bahut gandi hai.

3. My clothes are wet from the rain.
   - Baarish se mere kapde geele hain.

4. Wait until the floor is dry.
   - Jab tak floor sukha na ho, ruko.

5. I like to drink clean water.
   - Mujhe saaf paani peena pasand hai.

6. His hands are dirty.
   - Uske haath gande hain.

7. The towel is still wet.
   - Towel abhi bhi geela hai.

8. The paint is not dry yet.
   - Paint abhi sukha nahi hai.

9. A clean environment is important.
   - Saaf mahaul zaroori hai.

10. Don't touch dirty things.
    - Gandi cheezein mat chuo.

11. Wet floor is slippery.
    - Geela floor fislan wala hota hai.

12. Dry leaves fall in autumn.
    - Patjhad mein sukhe patte girte hain.

13. She always keeps the house clean.
    - Wo hamesha ghar saaf rakhti hai.

14. The dirty dishes are in the sink.
    - Gande bartan sink mein hain.

15. My hair is still wet.
    - Mere baal abhi bhi geele hain.

   - Wo sabse acchi student hai.

   - Suprabhat! Kaise ho?

7. I had a bad dream.
   - Mujhe bura sapna aaya.

8. This phone is better than that one.
   - Ye phone us se behtar hai.

9. He is my best friend.
   - Wo mera sabse accha dost hai.

10. That was his worst performance.
    - Wo uski sabse kharab performance thi.

11. Good things come to those who wait.
    - Jo wait karte hain unhe acchi cheezein milti hain.

12. Bad habits are hard to break.
    - Buri aadatein todna mushkil hai.

13. Practice makes you better.
    - Practice tumhe behtar banati hai.

14. This is the best restaurant in town.
    - Ye sheher ka sabse accha restaurant hai.

15. The worst is over now.
    - Sabse bura waqt guzar gaya.
