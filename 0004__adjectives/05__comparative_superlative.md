# Comparative & Superlative Adjectives

> Comparing two or more things.

---

## Rules Overview

| Type | Rule | Example |
|------|------|---------|
| Positive | Base form | tall, big, beautiful |
| Comparative | Compare 2 things | taller, bigger, more beautiful |
| Superlative | Compare 3+ things | tallest, biggest, most beautiful |

---

## Rule 1: Short Adjectives (1 syllable)

**Add -er / -est**

| Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| tall | taller | tallest |
| short | shorter | shortest |
| fast | faster | fastest |
| slow | slower | slowest |
| old | older | oldest |
| young | younger | youngest |
| long | longer | longest |
| small | smaller | smallest |
| cheap | cheaper | cheapest |
| clean | cleaner | cleanest |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. He is taller than me.
   - Wo mujhse lamba hai.

2. She is the tallest in the class.
   - Wo class mein sabse lambi hai.

3. This car is faster than that one.
   - Ye car us se tez hai.

4. He is the fastest runner.
   - Wo sabse tez dhavak hai.

5. My brother is older than me.
   - Mera bhai mujhse bada hai.

6. She is the oldest in the family.
   - Wo parivaar mein sabse badi hai.

7. This road is longer than that one.
   - Ye sadak us se lambi hai.

8. This is the longest river in India.
   - Ye Bharat ki sabse lambi nadi hai.

9. Your house is smaller than mine.
   - Tumhara ghar mere se chhota hai.

10. This is the smallest room.
    - Ye sabse chhota kamra hai.

11. This shop is cheaper than that one.
    - Ye dukaan us se sasti hai.

12. This is the cheapest option.
    - Ye sabse sasta option hai.

13. My room is cleaner than yours.
    - Mera kamra tumhare se saaf hai.

14. This is the cleanest restaurant.
    - Ye sabse saaf restaurant hai.

15. She runs slower than him.
    - Wo usse dheere daurti hai.

## Rule 2: Adjectives ending in -e

**Add -r / -st**

| Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| large | larger | largest |
| nice | nicer | nicest |
| safe | safer | safest |
| wide | wider | widest |
| simple | simpler | simplest |
| late | later | latest |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. This room is larger than that one.
   - Ye kamra us se bada hai.

2. She is nicer than her sister.
   - Wo apni behen se zyada acchi hai.

3. This area is safer than the old one.
   - Ye jagah purani se zyada safe hai.

4. This road is wider than before.
   - Ye sadak pehle se chaudi hai.

5. The latest model is expensive.
   - Naya model mehenga hai.

6. She is the nicest person I know.
   - Wo sabse acchi insaan hai jo main jaanta hoon.

7. This is the largest mall in the city.
   - Ye sheher ka sabse bada mall hai.

8. This is the simplest solution.
   - Ye sabse simple solution hai.

9. Which is the safest route?
   - Kaun sa raasta sabse safe hai?

10. The street is wider now.
    - Sadak ab chaudi hai.

## Rule 3: Adjectives ending in consonant + y

**Change -y to -ier / -iest**

| Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| happy | happier | happiest |
| busy | busier | busiest |
| easy | easier | easiest |
| heavy | heavier | heaviest |
| angry | angrier | angriest |
| dirty | dirtier | dirtiest |
| funny | funnier | funniest |
| pretty | prettier | prettiest |
| lazy | lazier | laziest |
| early | earlier | earliest |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She is happier than before.
   - Wo pehle se zyada khush hai.

2. This is the happiest day of my life.
   - Ye meri zindagi ka sabse khush din hai.

3. I'm busier than last week.
   - Main pichle hafte se zyada busy hoon.

4. He is the busiest person I know.
   - Wo sabse busy insaan hai jo main jaanta hoon.

5. This sum is easier than that one.
   - Ye sawaal us se aasaan hai.

6. This is the easiest way.
   - Ye sabse aasaan tarika hai.

7. This bag is heavier than that one.
   - Ye bag us se bhaari hai.

8. This is the heaviest box.
   - Ye sabse bhaari dabba hai.

9. She arrived earlier than me.
   - Wo mujhse pehle pahunchi.

10. He came the earliest.
    - Wo sabse pehle aaya.

11. Your room is dirtier than mine.
    - Tumhara kamra mere se zyada ganda hai.

12. He is lazier than his brother.
    - Wo apne bhai se zyada aalsi hai.

13. She is the prettiest girl in school.
    - Wo school mein sabse sundar ladki hai.

14. Today I'm angrier than yesterday.
    - Aaj main kal se zyada naraz hoon.

15. This movie is funnier than the last one.
    - Ye film pichli se zyada funny hai.

## Rule 4: Double the consonant

**For short adjectives ending in vowel + consonant**

| Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| big | bigger | biggest |
| hot | hotter | hottest |
| thin | thinner | thinnest |
| fat | fatter | fattest |
| sad | sadder | saddest |
| wet | wetter | wettest |
| fit | fitter | fittest |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. This box is bigger than that one.
   - Ye dabba us se bada hai.

2. This is the biggest house in the area.
   - Ye is ilaake ka sabse bada ghar hai.

3. Today is hotter than yesterday.
   - Aaj kal se zyada garam hai.

4. This is the hottest day of summer.
   - Ye garmi ka sabse garam din hai.

5. She is thinner than before.
   - Wo pehle se patli hai.

6. He is the thinnest in the group.
   - Wo group mein sabse patla hai.

7. He looks fatter now.
   - Wo ab zyada mota lag raha hai.

8. This is the wettest month.
   - Ye sabse baarish wala mahina hai.

9. I feel sadder today.
   - Aaj mujhe zyada dukh ho raha hai.

10. She is fitter than me.
    - Wo mujhse zyada fit hai.

## Rule 5: Long Adjectives (2+ syllables)

**Use more / most**

| Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| beautiful | more beautiful | most beautiful |
| expensive | more expensive | most expensive |
| comfortable | more comfortable | most comfortable |
| intelligent | more intelligent | most intelligent |
| important | more important | most important |
| interesting | more interesting | most interesting |
| difficult | more difficult | most difficult |
| dangerous | more dangerous | most dangerous |
| popular | more popular | most popular |
| careful | more careful | most careful |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She is more beautiful than her sister.
   - Wo apni behen se zyada sundar hai.

2. She is the most beautiful girl I've seen.
   - Wo sabse sundar ladki hai jo maine dekhi hai.

3. This phone is more expensive than that one.
   - Ye phone us se zyada mehenga hai.

4. This is the most expensive watch.
   - Ye sabse mehengi ghadi hai.

5. This chair is more comfortable.
   - Ye kursi zyada aaraamdaayak hai.

6. This is the most comfortable bed.
   - Ye sabse aaraamdaayak bistar hai.

7. He is more intelligent than his brother.
   - Wo apne bhai se zyada buddhiman hai.

8. She is the most intelligent student.
   - Wo sabse buddhiman student hai.

9. Health is more important than money.
   - Sehat paise se zyada zaroori hai.

10. This is the most important lesson.
    - Ye sabse zaroori sabak hai.

11. This book is more interesting than that one.
    - Ye kitaab us se zyada interesting hai.

12. This is the most interesting story.
    - Ye sabse dilchasp kahani hai.

13. Math is more difficult than English.
    - Math English se zyada mushkil hai.

14. This is the most difficult question.
    - Ye sabse mushkil sawaal hai.

15. Be more careful next time.
    - Agli baar zyada savdhaan raho.

## Rule 6: Irregular Adjectives

**No rule - must memorize**

| Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| good | better | best |
| bad | worse | worst |
| far | farther/further | farthest/furthest |
| little | less | least |
| much/many | more | most |
| old | older/elder | oldest/eldest |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. This is better than that.
   - Ye us se behtar hai.

2. This is the best restaurant.
   - Ye sabse accha restaurant hai.

3. Today was worse than yesterday.
   - Aaj kal se bura tha.

4. This is the worst day ever.
   - Ye ab tak ka sabse bura din hai.

5. He walked farther than me.
   - Wo mujhse zyada door tak chala.

6. This is the farthest I've traveled.
   - Ye sabse door tak maine safar kiya hai.

7. I have less time than before.
   - Mere paas pehle se kam time hai.

8. This has the least sugar.
   - Isme sabse kam cheeni hai.

9. I need more water.
   - Mujhe zyada paani chahiye.

10. She has the most books.
    - Uske paas sabse zyada kitaabein hain.

11. My elder brother is a doctor.
    - Mera bada bhai doctor hai.

12. He is the eldest in the family.
    - Wo parivaar mein sabse bada hai.

13. This plan is better than the previous one.
    - Ye plan pehle se behtar hai.

14. Your situation is worse than mine.
    - Tumhari sthiti meri se buri hai.

15. Need further information.
    - Aur jaankaari chahiye.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|----------|-----------|
| more taller | taller |
| most biggest | biggest |
| more better | better |
| more easy | easier |
| beautifuler | more beautiful |
| gooder | better |
| badder | worse |
