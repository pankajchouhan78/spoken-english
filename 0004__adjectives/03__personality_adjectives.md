# Personality & Character Adjectives

> Words that describe a person's nature, behavior, and character.

---

## Positive Personality Traits

### Kind / Helpful / Generous

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She is very kind to everyone.
   - Wo sabke saath bahut acchi hai.

2. My neighbor is very helpful.
   - Mera padosi bahut madad karne wala hai.

3. He is generous with his money.
   - Wo paison ke maamle mein dildar hai.

4. Kind words can change someone's life.
   - Pyare shabd kisi ki zindagi badal sakte hain.

5. Be helpful to others.
   - Dusron ki madad karo.

6. A generous donation was made.
   - Ek dildar daan kiya gaya.

7. She has a kind heart.
   - Uska dil dayalu hai.

8. Thank you for being so helpful.
   - Itni madad ke liye dhanyavaad.

9. He was generous enough to pay for everyone.
   - Wo itna dildar tha ki sabka bill bhara.

10. Kind people are always respected.
    - Dayalu logon ki hamesha izzat hoti hai.

11. Your helpful nature is appreciated.
    - Tumhari madadgaar nature ki qadr hai.

12. A generous person never lacks anything.
    - Dildar insaan ko kabhi kuch ki kami nahi hoti.

13. Be kind to animals.
    - Jaanwaron ke saath daya karo.

14. She is always helpful in difficult times.
    - Wo mushkil waqt mein hamesha madad karti hai.

15. His generous gift surprised me.
    - Uske dildar gift ne mujhe hairan kiya.

## Negative Personality Traits

**Daily Use Sentences:**

   - Wo bahut aalsi hai.

2. Don't be careless with your belongings.
   - Apni cheezon ke baare mein laparwah mat raho.

3. Selfish people have no friends.
   - Khudgarz logon ke dost nahi hote.

4. Lazy people never succeed.
   - Aalsi log kabhi safal nahi hote.

5. It was a careless mistake.
   - Ye ek laparwahi ki galti thi.

6. Don't be so selfish.
   - Itna khudgarz mat bano.

7. The lazy student failed.
   - Aalsi student fail ho gaya.

8. Careless driving is dangerous.
   - Laparwah driving khatarnak hai.

9. His selfish behavior hurt everyone.
   - Uski khudgarzi ne sabko dukh pahunchaya.

10. Stop being lazy and start working.
    - Aalsi hona band karo aur kaam shuru karo.

11. Careless handling broke the vase.
    - Laparwahi se uthane se vase toot gaya.

12. Selfish decisions harm relationships.
    - Khudgarz faislay rishton ko nuksaan pahunchate hain.

13. A lazy morning leads to a busy evening.
    - Aalsi subah se shaam mein bhagdaud hoti hai.

14. Don't be careless with your health.
    - Apni sehat ke baare mein laparwah mat raho.

15. Selfish love is not true love.
    - Khudgarz pyaar sachcha pyaar nahi hai.
