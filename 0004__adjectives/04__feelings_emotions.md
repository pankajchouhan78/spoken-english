8. She was pleased to hear the news.
   - Wo khabar sunkar khush hui.

    - Khushi ke mauke ne sabko ikattha kiya.

11. I'm excited about starting my new job.
    - Nayi job shuru karne ke liye excited hoon.

12. He looked pleased with his performance.
    - Wo apni performance se khush dikh raha tha.

14. The children were joyful on Diwali.

15. I'm so excited I can't sleep.
    - Main itna excited hoon ki so nahi pa raha.
