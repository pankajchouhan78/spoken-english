# Opposite Adjectives (Antonyms)

> Learning adjectives in pairs helps remember them better.

---

## Size & Measurement

| Adjective | Opposite | Hindi (Adj) | Hindi (Opp) |
|-----------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| big | small | bada | chhota |
| large | tiny | bada | bahut chhota |
| long | short | lamba | chhota |
| wide | narrow | chauda | sakra |
| thick | thin | mota | patla |
| deep | shallow | gehra | uthla |
| high | low | uncha | neecha |
| heavy | light | bhaari | halka |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. This box is big, that one is small.
   - Ye dabba bada hai, wo chhota hai.

2. The road is wide but the lane is narrow.
   - Sadak chaudi hai par gali sakri hai.

3. This book is thick, that one is thin.
   - Ye kitaab moti hai, wo patli hai.

4. The water is deep here and shallow there.
   - Yahaan paani gehra hai aur wahan uthla.

5. High mountains and low valleys.
   - Unche pahad aur neichi ghatiyaan.

6. Heavy bag vs light bag.
   - Bhaari bag aur halka bag.

7. Long hair or short hair?
   - Lambe baal ya chhote baal?

8. A large pizza is better than a tiny one.
   - Bada pizza chhote se behtar hai.

9. The box is too heavy for me.
   - Dabba mere liye bahut bhaari hai.

10. This wire is too thin.
    - Ye taar bahut patli hai.

## Temperature & Weather

| Adjective | Opposite | Hindi (Adj) | Hindi (Opp) |
|-----------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| hot | cold | garam | thanda |
| warm | cool | naram garam | thanda |
| wet | dry | geela | sukha |
| sunny | cloudy | dhoop wala | badli wala |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. The coffee is hot, the juice is cold.
   - Coffee garam hai, juice thanda hai.

2. I like warm weather, not cold.
   - Mujhe garam mausam pasand hai, thanda nahi.

3. The clothes are still wet, not dry.
   - Kapde abhi bhi geele hain, sukhe nahi.

4. Today is sunny but tomorrow will be cloudy.
   - Aaj dhoop hai par kal badal honge.

5. Hot tea in cold weather is perfect.
   - Thande mausam mein garam chai perfect hai.

6. The road is wet after the rain.
   - Baarish ke baad sadak geeli hai.

7. I prefer cool evenings to hot afternoons.
   - Mujhe thandi shaam garam dopahar se zyada pasand hai.

8. Wait for the clothes to get dry.
   - Kapdon ke sukhne ka intezaar karo.

9. It was sunny in the morning but cloudy now.
   - Subah dhoop thi par ab badal hain.

10. Drink something cold, it's too hot outside.
    - Kuch thanda piyo, bahar bahut garam hai.

## Quality & Condition

| Adjective | Opposite | Hindi (Adj) | Hindi (Opp) |
|-----------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| good | bad | accha | bura |
| new | old | naya | purana |
| clean | dirty | saaf | ganda |
| fresh | stale | taaza | baasi |
| right | wrong | sahi | galat |
| true | false | sach | jhooth |
| safe | dangerous | surakshit | khatarnak |
| strong | weak | mazboot | kamzor |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Good people and bad people exist everywhere.
   - Acche aur bure log har jagah hote hain.

2. I sold my old phone and bought a new one.
   - Maine purana phone becha aur naya liya.

3. Keep your room clean, not dirty.
   - Apna kamra saaf rakho, ganda nahi.

4. Eat fresh food, avoid stale food.
   - Taaza khana khao, baasi se bachcho.

5. Your answer is right, his is wrong.
   - Tumhara jawab sahi hai, uska galat.

6. Is this statement true or false?
   - Kya ye baat sach hai ya jhooth?

7. This area is safe, that one is dangerous.
   - Ye jagah safe hai, wo khatarnak.

8. He is strong, but his brother is weak.
   - Wo mazboot hai, par uska bhai kamzor.

9. New clothes feel better than old ones.
   - Naye kapde purane se acche lagte hain.

10. A strong body needs fresh food.
    - Mazboot shareer ko taaza khana chahiye.

## Personality

| Adjective | Opposite | Hindi (Adj) | Hindi (Opp) |
|-----------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| happy | sad | khush | dukhi |
| kind | cruel | dayalu | nirdayi |
| brave | coward | bahadur | kaayar |
| honest | dishonest | imaandar | beimaani |
| polite | rude | vinay | badtameez |
| generous | selfish | dildar | khudgarz |
| humble | proud | namr | ghamandi |
| patient | impatient | sabr wala | besabra |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She was happy yesterday, but sad today.
   - Wo kal khush thi, par aaj dukhi hai.

2. Be kind, not cruel to animals.
   - Jaanwaron se dayalu bano, nirdayi nahi.

3. Brave soldiers defeat coward enemies.
   - Bahadur sainik kaayar dushmano ko harate hain.

4. Honest people succeed, dishonest people fail.
   - Imaandar log safal hote hain, beimaan fail.

5. Be polite, don't be rude.
   - Polite bano, badtameez mat bano.

6. Generous people give, selfish people take.
   - Dildar log dete hain, khudgarz lete hain.

7. Stay humble even when successful, never proud.
   - Safal hone par bhi namr raho, ghamandi nahi.

8. Patient people handle stress, impatient ones don't.
   - Sabr wale log stress handle karte hain, besabre nahi.

9. A kind word is better than cruel action.
   - Ek pyara shabd nirdayi kaam se behtar hai.

10. He became dishonest after becoming rich.
    - Ameer hone ke baad wo beimaan ho gaya.

## Appearance

| Adjective | Opposite | Hindi (Adj) | Hindi (Opp) |
|-----------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| beautiful | ugly | sundar | badsurat |
| fat | thin | mota | patla |
| tall | short | lamba | nata |
| young | old | jawan | budha |
| rich | poor | ameer | gareeb |
| dark | light/fair | kaala | gora |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She is beautiful, not ugly.
   - Wo sundar hai, badsurat nahi.

2. He used to be fat, now he is thin.
   - Wo pehle mota tha, ab patla hai.

3. My father is tall, my mother is short.
   - Mere papa lambe hain, mummy chhoti hain.

4. The young man helped the old woman.
   - Jawan aadmi ne budhi aurat ki madad ki.

5. Rich or poor, everyone deserves respect.
   - Ameer ho ya gareeb, sabko izzat milni chahiye.

6. Dark nights and bright days.
   - Kaali raatein aur ujle din.

7. She was young once, now she is old.
   - Wo kabhi jawan thi, ab budhi hai.

8. From poor to rich, his journey is inspiring.
   - Gareeb se ameer, uski yatra prerit karti hai.

9. Tall buildings block the view.
   - Lambi buildings nazara rok leti hain.

10. He became thin after exercising.
    - Exercise ke baad wo patla ho gaya.

## Speed & Time

| Adjective | Opposite | Hindi (Adj) | Hindi (Opp) |
|-----------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| fast | slow | tez | dheema |
| quick | slow | jaldi | dheere |
| early | late | jaldi | deri |
| first | last | pehla | aakhri |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. He is fast, she is slow.
   - Wo tez hai, wo dheemi hai.

2. Give me a quick reply, not slow.
   - Jaldi jawab do, dheere nahi.

3. Come early, don't be late.
   - Jaldi aao, der mat karo.

4. He came first, I came last.
   - Wo pehla aaya, main aakhri.

5. Fast cars are dangerous on slow roads.
   - Tez gaadiyaan dheemi sadakon par khatarnak hain.

6. I need a quick solution.
   - Mujhe jaldi solution chahiye.

7. Early birds catch the worm.
   - Jaldi uthne wale safal hote hain.

8. The first bus was full, I took the last one.
   - Pehli bus bhari thi, maine aakhri li.

9. Don't be slow, be quick!
   - Dheere mat bano, jaldi karo!

10. She arrived late, he arrived early.
    - Wo deri se aayi, wo jaldi aaya.

## Position & Distance

| Adjective | Opposite | Hindi (Adj) | Hindi (Opp) |
|-----------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| near | far | paas | door |
| front | back | saamne | peeche |
| top | bottom | upar | neeche |
| inside | outside | andar | bahar |
| left | right | baayein | daayein |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. The shop is near, the mall is far.
   - Dukaan paas hai, mall door hai.

2. Sit in the front, not at the back.
   - Saamne baitho, peeche nahi.

3. Put it on top, not at the bottom.
   - Upar rakho, neeche nahi.

4. Stay inside, don't go outside.
   - Andar raho, bahar mat jao.

5. Turn left, then right.
   - Baayein mudo, phir daayein.

6. My house is near the station.
   - Mera ghar station ke paas hai.

7. Front seats are expensive, back seats are cheap.
   - Aage ki seats mehengi hain, peeche ki sasti.

8. The book is at the top of the pile.
   - Kitaab dheri ke upar hai.

9. The kids are playing outside.
   - Bachche bahar khel rahe hain.

10. The pharmacy is on the left, bank on the right.
    - Dawai ki dukaan baayein hai, bank daayein.
