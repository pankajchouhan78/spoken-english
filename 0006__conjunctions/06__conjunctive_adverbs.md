# Conjunctive Adverbs

> Connect independent clauses with a specific relationship.
> Usually followed by a comma.

---

## HOWEVER (lekin/halaanki) - Contrast

> Shows opposite or unexpected information.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I tried hard. However, I failed.
   - Maine bahut koshish ki. Lekin, fail ho gaya.

2. The hotel was expensive. However, the service was poor.
   - Hotel mehenga tha. Halaanki, service kharab thi.

3. She is young. However, she is very mature.
   - Wo jawan hai. Lekin, bahut mature hai.

4. I wanted to help. However, I couldn't.
   - Main madad karna chahta tha. Lekin, nahi kar saka.

5. The job is difficult. However, I'll try.
   - Kaam mushkil hai. Lekin, koshish karunga.

6. He apologized. However, I'm still angry.
   - Usne maafi maangi. Lekin, main abhi bhi gussa hoon.

7. It's raining. However, we'll still go.
   - Baarish ho rahi hai. Lekin, hum phir bhi jayenge.

8. I don't agree. However, I respect your opinion.
   - Main agree nahi karta. Lekin, tumhari raye ki izzat karta hoon.

9. He is rich. However, he is not happy.
   - Wo ameer hai. Lekin, khush nahi.

10. The exam was hard. However, I passed.
    - Exam mushkil tha. Lekin, pass ho gaya.

11. She said no. However, I'll ask again.
    - Usne na kaha. Lekin, phir poochunga.

12. I was tired. However, I finished the work.
    - Main thaka tha. Lekin, kaam khatam kiya.

13. The movie got bad reviews. However, I liked it.
    - Film ko buri reviews mili. Lekin, mujhe pasand aayi.

14. I'm busy. However, I'll make time for you.
    - Main busy hoon. Lekin, tumhare liye time nikaalunga.

15. The price increased. However, the quality improved.
    - Price badh gayi. Lekin, quality sudhar gayi.

## THEREFORE / THUS (isliye) - Result

> Shows the result or conclusion.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. He was sick. Therefore, he didn't come.
   - Wo bimar tha. Isliye, nahi aaya.

2. She studied hard. Therefore, she passed.
   - Usne mehnat ki. Isliye, pass hui.

3. It was late. Therefore, we went home.
   - Der ho gayi thi. Isliye, ghar chale gaye.

4. I saved money. Therefore, I can buy a car now.
   - Maine paise bachaye. Isliye, ab car kharid sakta hoon.

5. He lied. Therefore, I don't trust him.
   - Usne jhooth bola. Isliye, main uspe bharosa nahi karta.

6. The product failed. Therefore, we redesigned it.
   - Product fail hua. Isliye, humne redesign kiya.

7. She was the best candidate. Therefore, she got the job.
   - Wo sabse acchi candidate thi. Isliye, job mili.

8. We ran out of time. Therefore, we postponed the meeting.
   - Time khatam ho gaya. Isliye, meeting postpone ki.

9. He didn't study. Therefore, he failed.
   - Usne padhai nahi ki. Isliye, fail hua.

10. The train was delayed. Therefore, I was late.
    - Train late thi. Isliye, main late tha.

11. I forgot my wallet. Thus, I couldn't pay.
    - Wallet bhool gaya. Isliye, pay nahi kar saka.

12. She worked overtime. Thus, she finished early.
    - Usne overtime kiya. Isliye, jaldi khatam hua.

13. He is honest. Therefore, everyone respects him.
    - Wo imaandar hai. Isliye, sab uski izzat karte hain.

14. The weather was bad. Thus, the flight was cancelled.
    - Mausam kharab tha. Isliye, flight cancel hui.

15. I exercise daily. Therefore, I'm healthy.
    - Main roz exercise karta hoon. Isliye, healthy hoon.

## MOREOVER / FURTHERMORE (iske alaawa) - Addition

> Adds more information to support the point.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She is smart. Moreover, she is hardworking.
   - Wo smart hai. Iske alaawa, mehanti bhi hai.

2. The house is beautiful. Furthermore, it's affordable.
   - Ghar sundar hai. Iske alaawa, sasti bhi hai.

3. He speaks English. Moreover, he knows French.
   - Wo English bolta hai. Iske alaawa, French bhi aati hai.

4. The food was delicious. Furthermore, the service was excellent.
   - Khana swaadist tha. Iske alaawa, service bhi behtareen thi.

5. I'm tired. Moreover, I'm hungry.
   - Main thaka hoon. Iske alaawa, bhukha bhi.

6. The product is good. Furthermore, it's eco-friendly.
   - Product accha hai. Iske alaawa, eco-friendly bhi.

7. She is talented. Moreover, she is humble.
   - Wo talented hai. Iske alaawa, namr bhi.

8. The job pays well. Furthermore, the benefits are great.
   - Job mein salary acchi hai. Iske alaawa, benefits bhi acche.

9. He is rich. Moreover, he is generous.
   - Wo ameer hai. Iske alaawa, dildar bhi.

10. The hotel is clean. Furthermore, the location is perfect.
    - Hotel saaf hai. Iske alaawa, location bhi perfect.

11. I don't have time. Moreover, I don't have interest.
    - Mere paas time nahi. Iske alaawa, interest bhi nahi.

12. She cooks well. Furthermore, she bakes beautifully.
    - Wo accha khana banati hai. Iske alaawa, baking bhi sundar.

13. The deal is profitable. Moreover, it's low risk.
    - Deal profitable hai. Iske alaawa, risk bhi kam.

14. He is intelligent. Furthermore, he is creative.
    - Wo intelligent hai. Iske alaawa, creative bhi.

15. The weather is nice. Moreover, it's a holiday.
    - Mausam accha hai. Iske alaawa, chhuti bhi hai.

## INSTEAD (iske bajay) - Replacement

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I didn't go to work. Instead, I stayed home.
   - Main kaam nahi gaya. Iske bajay, ghar raha.

2. He didn't call. Instead, he sent a message.
   - Usne call nahi kiya. Iske bajay, message bheja.

3. Don't complain. Instead, find a solution.
   - Shikayat mat karo. Iske bajay, solution dhundho.

4. She didn't cry. Instead, she smiled.
   - Wo royi nahi. Iske bajay, muskurayi.

5. I didn't sleep. Instead, I worked all night.
   - Main soya nahi. Iske bajay, poori raat kaam kiya.

6. He didn't shout. Instead, he spoke calmly.
   - Wo chillaya nahi. Iske bajay, shaanti se bola.

7. Don't worry. Instead, focus on the solution.
   - Chinta mat karo. Iske bajay, solution par focus karo.

8. She didn't quit. Instead, she tried harder.
   - Usne chhoda nahi. Iske bajay, aur mehnat ki.

9. I didn't eat rice. Instead, I had roti.
   - Maine chawal nahi khaye. Iske bajay, roti khayi.

10. He didn't take a taxi. Instead, he walked.
    - Usne taxi nahi li. Iske bajay, paidal gaya.

## OTHERWISE (nahi to / warna) - Condition/Consequence

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Study hard. Otherwise, you'll fail.
   - Mehnat se padho. Warna, fail ho jaoge.

2. Leave now. Otherwise, you'll be late.
   - Abhi niklo. Warna, late ho jaoge.

3. Take an umbrella. Otherwise, you'll get wet.
   - Chhata le lo. Warna, bheeg jaoge.

4. Be careful. Otherwise, you might get hurt.
   - Savdhan raho. Warna, chot lag sakti hai.

5. Save money. Otherwise, you'll regret later.
   - Paise bachao. Warna, baad mein pachtaoge.

6. Eat healthy. Otherwise, you'll fall sick.
   - Healthy khao. Warna, bimar padoge.

7. Listen carefully. Otherwise, you'll make mistakes.
   - Dhyan se suno. Warna, galti hogi.

8. Follow the rules. Otherwise, you'll be punished.
   - Niyam maano. Warna, saza milegi.

9. Hurry up. Otherwise, the bus will leave.
   - Jaldi karo. Warna, bus chali jayegi.

10. Pay the bill. Otherwise, they'll cut the power.
    - Bill bharo. Warna, bijli kat jayegi.

11. Be honest. Otherwise, people won't trust you.
    - Imaandar raho. Warna, log bharosa nahi karenge.

12. Rest now. Otherwise, you'll be too tired.
    - Abhi aaram karo. Warna, bahut thak jaoge.

13. Keep practicing. Otherwise, you'll forget.
    - Practice karte raho. Warna, bhool jaoge.

14. Book early. Otherwise, you won't get tickets.
    - Jaldi book karo. Warna, tickets nahi milenge.

15. Apologize now. Otherwise, she won't forgive you.
    - Abhi maafi maango. Warna, wo maaf nahi karegi.

## MEANWHILE / IN THE MEANTIME (is beech mein) - Time

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. The food is cooking. Meanwhile, let's set the table.
   - Khana ban raha hai. Is beech mein, table set karte hain.

2. She was shopping. Meanwhile, I was waiting in the car.
   - Wo shopping kar rahi thi. Is beech mein, main car mein wait kar raha tha.

3. The boss is busy. In the meantime, please wait.
   - Boss busy hain. Is beech mein, please wait karo.

4. I was studying. Meanwhile, my brother was playing.
   - Main padh raha tha. Is beech mein, mera bhai khel raha tha.

5. The train was delayed. Meanwhile, we had coffee.
   - Train late thi. Is beech mein, humne coffee pee.

6. She was on the phone. Meanwhile, I finished the work.
   - Wo phone par thi. Is beech mein, maine kaam khatam kiya.

7. He went to buy groceries. In the meantime, I cleaned the house.
   - Wo groceries lene gaya. Is beech mein, maine ghar saaf kiya.

8. The report isn't ready. Meanwhile, work on other tasks.
   - Report taiyaar nahi hai. Is beech mein, doosre kaam karo.

9. I was cooking. Meanwhile, the guests arrived.
   - Main khana bana raha tha. Is beech mein, mehman aa gaye.

10. The movie was downloading. In the meantime, I read a book.
    - Movie download ho rahi thi. Is beech mein, maine kitaab padhi.

## Summary Table

| Conjunctive Adverb | Use | Hindi |
|--------------------|-----|-------|
| however | contrast | lekin/halaanki |
| therefore/thus | result | isliye |
| moreover/furthermore | addition | iske alaawa |
| instead | replacement | iske bajay |
| otherwise | consequence | warna |
| meanwhile | same time | is beech mein |
| nevertheless | despite that | phir bhi |
| consequently | result | natijatan |
| similarly | comparison | isi tarah |
| finally | conclusion | aakhir mein |

## Punctuation Rules

| Position | Punctuation | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| Between sentences | Period or semicolon before | He was tired. However, he worked. |
| Start of sentence | Comma after | However, he still worked. |
| Mid sentence | Commas around | He, however, still worked. |
