# Subordinating Conjunctions - Cause & Contrast

> Join a DEPENDENT clause to an INDEPENDENT clause.
> The dependent clause cannot stand alone.

---

## BECAUSE / SINCE / AS (kyunki) - Cause/Reason

> Explain WHY something happens.

### Because (kyunki)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I stayed home because it was raining.
   - Main ghar raha kyunki baarish ho rahi thi.

2. She is happy because she passed the exam.
   - Wo khush hai kyunki wo pass ho gayi.

3. I'm tired because I didn't sleep well.
   - Main thaka hoon kyunki acchi neend nahi aayi.

4. He was late because of traffic.
   - Wo late tha kyunki traffic thi.

5. I bought this book because it was on sale.
   - Maine ye kitaab kharidi kyunki sale thi.

6. She cried because she was hurt.
   - Wo royi kyunki use dard hua.

7. I can't come because I'm busy.
   - Main nahi aa sakta kyunki busy hoon.

8. He failed because he didn't study.
   - Wo fail hua kyunki usne padhai nahi ki.

9. I trust him because he's honest.
   - Main uspe bharosa karta hoon kyunki wo imaandar hai.

10. She left early because she was unwell.
    - Wo jaldi gayi kyunki tabiyat theek nahi thi.

11. I'm learning English because I need it for my job.
    - Main English seekh raha hoon kyunki job ke liye chahiye.

12. He was punished because he broke the rules.
    - Use saza mili kyunki usne niyam tode.

13. They moved here because of the school.
    - Wo yahaan aaye kyunki school ki wajah se.

14. I said no because I don't agree.
    - Maine na kaha kyunki main agree nahi karta.

15. She's upset because you lied to her.
    - Wo upset hai kyunki tumne jhooth bola.

---

### Since (jab se / kyunki)

> ⚠️ "Since" has TWO meanings:
> - Cause: kyunki (because)
> - Time: jab se (from that time)

**Daily Use Sentences (Cause):**

1. Since you're here, let's start.
   - Kyunki tum aa gaye, shuru karte hain.

2. Since it's late, we should leave.
   - Kyunki der ho gayi, humein jaana chahiye.

3. Since you asked, I'll tell you.
   - Kyunki tumne poocha, main batata hoon.

4. Since he's the boss, we must listen.
   - Kyunki wo boss hai, humein sunna padega.

5. Since I have no choice, I'll accept.
   - Kyunki mere paas choice nahi, main maan lunga.

**Daily Use Sentences (Time):**

6. I've been waiting since morning.
   - Main subah se intezaar kar raha hoon.

7. She has lived here since 2010.
   - Wo 2010 se yahaan reh rahi hai.

8. I haven't eaten since yesterday.
   - Maine kal se kuch nahi khaya.

9. Since when have you been here?
   - Tum kab se yahaan ho?

10. We've been friends since childhood.
    - Hum bachpan se dost hain.

## ALTHOUGH / THOUGH / EVEN THOUGH (halaanki) - Contrast

> Show something unexpected or opposite.

### Although / Though (halaanki / par)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Although he is rich, he is not happy.
   - Halaanki wo ameer hai, wo khush nahi.

2. Though it was raining, we went out.
   - Halaanki baarish ho rahi thi, hum bahar gaye.

3. Although she tried hard, she failed.
   - Halaanki usne bahut koshish ki, wo fail hui.

4. I helped him, although he didn't deserve it.
   - Maine uski madad ki, halaanki wo deserve nahi karta tha.

5. Though tired, he finished the work.
   - Halaanki thaka tha, usne kaam khatam kiya.

6. Although I don't like it, I'll do it.
   - Halaanki mujhe pasand nahi, main karunga.

7. Though he apologized, I'm still angry.
   - Halaanki usne maafi maangi, main abhi bhi gussa hoon.

8. Although it's expensive, it's worth it.
   - Halaanki mehenga hai, ye worth it hai.

9. She went to work, though she was sick.
   - Wo kaam gayi, halaanki bimar thi.

10. Although late, he still came.
    - Halaanki late tha, phir bhi aaya.

11. Though I warned him, he didn't listen.
    - Halaanki maine chetaaya, usne nahi suna.

12. Although young, she is very mature.
    - Halaanki chhoti hai, bahut mature hai.

13. I accepted, though I had doubts.
    - Maine maan liya, halaanki shaq tha.

14. Although busy, she always calls her parents.
    - Halaanki busy hai, wo parents ko hamesha call karti hai.

15. Though simple, this dish is delicious.
    - Halaanki simple hai, ye dish swaadist hai.

---

### Even Though (halaanki / phir bhi)

> Stronger than "although" - shows bigger surprise.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Even though I was tired, I went to the gym.
   - Halaanki main thaka tha, phir bhi gym gaya.

2. Even though she knew the truth, she lied.
   - Halaanki wo sach jaanti thi, usne jhooth bola.

3. He failed even though he studied hard.
   - Wo fail hua halaanki usne bahut padha.

4. Even though it's free, I don't want it.
   - Halaanki ye free hai, mujhe nahi chahiye.

5. She forgave him even though he hurt her.
   - Usne maaf kar diya halaanki usne dard diya.

6. Even though I explained, he didn't understand.
   - Halaanki maine samjhaya, wo nahi samjha.

7. He keeps trying even though he always fails.
   - Wo koshish karta hai halaanki hamesha fail hota hai.

8. Even though the movie was boring, I watched it.
   - Halaanki film boring thi, maine dekhi.

9. She smiled even though she was sad.
   - Wo muskurayi halaanki dukhi thi.

10. Even though we won, I'm not satisfied.
    - Halaanki hum jeete, main santusht nahi.

---

## WHILE / WHEREAS (jabki) - Contrast

> Show difference between two things.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. While I prefer tea, she likes coffee.
   - Jabki mujhe chai pasand hai, use coffee.

2. He is tall, whereas his brother is short.
   - Wo lamba hai, jabki uska bhai chhota.

3. While some people work, others rest.
   - Jabki kuch log kaam karte hain, doosre aaram.

4. She is hardworking, whereas he is lazy.
   - Wo mehanti hai, jabki wo aalsi.

5. While I agree partly, I have concerns.
   - Jabki main thoda agree karta hoon, chinta hai.

6. He succeeded, whereas his friend failed.
   - Wo safal hua, jabki uska dost fail.

7. While it's not perfect, it's good enough.
   - Jabki perfect nahi hai, kaafi accha hai.

8. She is patient, whereas I get angry quickly.
   - Wo sabr wali hai, jabki mujhe jaldi gussa aata hai.

9. While some supported him, others opposed.
   - Jabki kuch support kiye, doosre oppose.

10. He is rich, whereas she is poor.
    - Wo ameer hai, jabki wo gareeb.
