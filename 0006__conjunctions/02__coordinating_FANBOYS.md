# Coordinating Conjunctions - FANBOYS

> Join words, phrases, or clauses of EQUAL importance.
> Remember: **F**or, **A**nd, **N**or, **B**ut, **O**r, **Y**et, **S**o

---

## AND (aur) - Addition

> Joins similar ideas together.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I like tea and coffee.
   - Mujhe chai aur coffee pasand hai.

2. She is smart and beautiful.
   - Wo smart aur sundar hai.

3. I went to the market and bought vegetables.
   - Main bazaar gaya aur sabzi kharidi.

4. He works hard and earns well.
   - Wo mehnat karta hai aur accha kamata hai.

5. My mother and father are doctors.
   - Meri maa aur papa doctors hain.

6. Read the book and answer the questions.
   - Kitaab padho aur sawaalon ke jawab do.

7. Wake up early and exercise daily.
   - Jaldi utho aur roz exercise karo.

8. I studied hard and passed the exam.
   - Maine mehnat ki aur exam pass kiya.

9. She cooks and cleans the house.
   - Wo khana banati hai aur ghar saaf karti hai.

10. Add salt and pepper to taste.
    - Swad ke liye namak aur mirch dalo.

11. I need a pen and paper.
    - Mujhe pen aur paper chahiye.

12. He is honest and hardworking.
    - Wo imaandar aur mehanti hai.

13. We ate dinner and watched a movie.
    - Humne khana khaya aur movie dekhi.

14. She smiled and waved at me.
    - Usne muskuraya aur haath hilaya.

15. Learn and grow every day.
    - Har din seekho aur badho.

---

## BUT (lekin/par) - Contrast

> Shows opposite or unexpected ideas.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. He is rich but unhappy.
   - Wo ameer hai par khush nahi.

2. I tried hard but failed.
   - Maine bahut koshish ki par fail ho gaya.

3. She is old but active.
   - Wo budhi hai par active hai.

4. It's expensive but worth it.
   - Ye mehenga hai par worth it hai.

5. I want to help but I can't.
   - Main madad karna chahta hoon par nahi kar sakta.

6. The food is tasty but spicy.
   - Khana swaadist hai par teekha hai.

7. He is poor but honest.
   - Wo gareeb hai par imaandar hai.

8. I called him but he didn't answer.
   - Maine use call kiya par usne nahi uthaya.

9. She is strict but fair.
   - Wo sakht hai par fair hai.

10. The movie was long but interesting.
    - Film lambi thi par interesting thi.

11. I like him but don't trust him.
    - Mujhe wo pasand hai par bharosa nahi.

12. It's difficult but not impossible.
    - Ye mushkil hai par impossible nahi.

13. She wants to go but can't.
    - Wo jaana chahti hai par nahi ja sakti.

14. I'm tired but I'll finish the work.
    - Main thaka hoon par kaam khatam karunga.

15. He apologized but I didn't forgive him.
    - Usne maafi maangi par maine maaf nahi kiya.

---

## OR (ya) - Choice/Alternative

> Gives options or alternatives.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Do you want tea or coffee?
   - Tumhe chai chahiye ya coffee?

2. Study hard or you will fail.
   - Mehnat se padho ya fail ho jaoge.

3. Is it true or false?
   - Ye sach hai ya jhooth?

4. We can go today or tomorrow.
   - Hum aaj ja sakte hain ya kal.

5. Take it or leave it.
   - Lo ya chhod do.

6. Should I call you or message you?
   - Main tumhe call karun ya message?

7. You can pay by cash or card.
   - Cash se ya card se pay kar sakte ho.

8. Is he coming or not?
   - Wo aa raha hai ya nahi?

9. Hurry up or we'll be late.
   - Jaldi karo ya late ho jayenge.

10. Would you like rice or roti?
    - Tumhe chawal chahiye ya roti?

11. You can stay here or come with us.
    - Tum yahaan reh sakte ho ya humare saath aa sakte ho.

12. Is this yours or mine?
    - Ye tumhara hai ya mera?

13. Speak now or forever hold your peace.
    - Abhi bolo ya hamesha chup raho.

14. Do it properly or don't do it at all.
    - Sahi se karo ya bilkul mat karo.

15. Which one - red or blue?
    - Kaun sa - laal ya neela?

---

## SO (isliye) - Result/Consequence

> Shows the result of something.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. It was raining, so I stayed home.
   - Baarish ho rahi thi, isliye ghar raha.

2. I was tired, so I went to sleep.
   - Main thaka tha, isliye so gaya.

3. He studied hard, so he passed.
   - Usne mehnat ki, isliye pass hua.

4. She was late, so she missed the bus.
   - Wo late thi, isliye bus miss ho gayi.

5. I was hungry, so I ordered food.
   - Mujhe bhook lagi thi, isliye khana order kiya.

6. The traffic was heavy, so I was late.
   - Traffic heavy thi, isliye late ho gaya.

7. He lied to me, so I don't trust him.
   - Usne mujhse jhooth bola, isliye bharosa nahi karta.

8. It's cold outside, so wear a jacket.
   - Bahar thandi hai, isliye jacket pehno.

9. She saved money, so she could travel.
   - Usne paise bachaye, isliye travel kar saki.

10. I forgot my wallet, so I couldn't pay.
    - Wallet bhool gaya, isliye pay nahi kar saka.

11. He was sick, so he didn't come to work.
    - Wo bimar tha, isliye kaam par nahi aaya.

12. The movie was boring, so I left early.
    - Film boring thi, isliye jaldi chala gaya.

13. I had no cash, so I paid by card.
    - Cash nahi tha, isliye card se pay kiya.

14. She works hard, so she deserves success.
    - Wo mehnat karti hai, isliye safalta ki haqdaar hai.

15. It's getting late, so let's go.
    - Der ho rahi hai, isliye chalo.

---

## YET (phir bhi) - Contrast/Surprise

> Similar to "but", shows unexpected contrast.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She is young yet wise.
   - Wo jawan hai phir bhi samajhdar.

2. He tried hard, yet he failed.
   - Usne bahut koshish ki, phir bhi fail hua.

3. It's simple yet effective.
   - Ye simple hai phir bhi effective.

4. The task is difficult, yet achievable.
   - Kaam mushkil hai, phir bhi ho sakta hai.

5. She is tired, yet she keeps working.
   - Wo thaki hai, phir bhi kaam kar rahi hai.

6. It was risky, yet I took the chance.
   - Ye risky tha, phir bhi maine chance liya.

7. He is poor, yet generous.
   - Wo gareeb hai, phir bhi dildar.

8. The movie was long, yet engaging.
   - Film lambi thi, phir bhi engaging.

9. I warned him, yet he didn't listen.
   - Maine use chetaaya, phir bhi nahi suna.

10. It's old yet still functional.
    - Ye purana hai phir bhi kaam karta hai.

11. She is busy, yet always finds time for family.
    - Wo busy hai, phir bhi parivaar ke liye time nikaali hai.

12. The food was cold, yet tasty.
    - Khana thanda tha, phir bhi swaadist.

13. He is quiet, yet very observant.
    - Wo chup hai, phir bhi bahut dhyan rakhta hai.

14. I was scared, yet I spoke up.
    - Main dara hua tha, phir bhi bola.

15. It's expensive, yet I'll buy it.
    - Ye mehenga hai, phir bhi khareedunga.

---

## FOR (kyunki) - Reason

> Gives the reason (more formal, less common).

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. He was punished, for he was late.
   - Use saza mili, kyunki wo late tha.

2. She left early, for she was unwell.
   - Wo jaldi gayi, kyunki wo theek nahi thi.

3. I stayed home, for it was raining.
   - Main ghar raha, kyunki baarish ho rahi thi.

4. He succeeded, for he worked hard.
   - Wo safal hua, kyunki usne mehnat ki.

5. We hurried, for the train was about to leave.
   - Hum jaldi kiye, kyunki train jaane wali thi.

6. She cried, for she lost her wallet.
   - Wo royi, kyunki uska wallet kho gaya.

7. I couldn't sleep, for it was too noisy.
   - Main so nahi saka, kyunki bahut shor tha.

8. He apologized, for he had made a mistake.
   - Usne maafi maangi, kyunki usne galti ki thi.

9. They celebrated, for they had won.
   - Unhone celebrate kiya, kyunki wo jeet gaye the.

10. I'm happy, for my friend is coming.
    - Main khush hoon, kyunki mera dost aa raha hai.

---

## NOR (na hi) - Negative Addition

> Adds another negative element.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. He doesn't eat meat, nor does he drink.
   - Wo meat nahi khata, na hi peeta hai.

2. She neither called nor messaged.
   - Usne na call kiya na message.

3. I have no time, nor do I have money.
   - Mere paas time nahi, na hi paise hain.

4. He is not smart, nor is he hardworking.
   - Wo smart nahi, na hi mehanti hai.

5. I don't like coffee, nor do I like tea.
   - Mujhe coffee pasand nahi, na hi chai.

6. She didn't apologize, nor did she explain.
   - Usne maafi nahi maangi, na hi samjhaya.

7. He won't come, nor will he call.
   - Wo nahi aayega, na hi call karega.

8. I can't swim, nor can I dive.
   - Main tair nahi sakta, na hi dive kar sakta.

9. She doesn't speak English, nor does her brother.
   - Wo English nahi bolti, na hi uska bhai.

10. They didn't help, nor did they offer.
    - Unhone madad nahi ki, na hi offer kiya.

---

## FANBOYS Summary

| Conjunction | Hindi | Use | Example |
|-------------|-------|-----|---------|
| For | kyunki | reason | He left, for he was tired. |
| And | aur | addition | tea and coffee |
| Nor | na hi | negative | not this, nor that |
| But | lekin/par | contrast | poor but honest |
| Or | ya | choice | this or that |
| Yet | phir bhi | surprise | tired yet working |
| So | isliye | result | rained, so stayed |
