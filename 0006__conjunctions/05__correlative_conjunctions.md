# Correlative Conjunctions - Pairs

> Work in PAIRS to join equal elements.
> Both parts must be used together.

---

## BOTH...AND (dono...aur)

> Adds emphasis when including two things.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Both tea and coffee are available.
   - Chai aur coffee dono available hain.

2. She is both smart and beautiful.
   - Wo smart aur sundar dono hai.

3. I like both pizza and pasta.
   - Mujhe pizza aur pasta dono pasand hain.

4. Both my parents are teachers.
   - Mere dono parents teachers hain.

5. He can speak both Hindi and English.
   - Wo Hindi aur English dono bol sakta hai.

6. Both the movie and the book were excellent.
   - Film aur kitaab dono behtareen thi.

7. I'm both tired and hungry.
   - Main thaka aur bhukha dono hoon.

8. She is both hardworking and talented.
   - Wo mehanti aur talented dono hai.

9. Both boys and girls can participate.
   - Ladke aur ladkiyan dono hissa le sakte hain.

10. He is both my friend and mentor.
    - Wo mera dost aur mentor dono hai.

11. I need both time and money.
    - Mujhe time aur paisa dono chahiye.

12. Both options are good.
    - Dono options acche hain.

13. She works both day and night.
    - Wo din aur raat dono kaam karti hai.

14. I visited both Delhi and Mumbai.
    - Main Delhi aur Mumbai dono gaya.

15. Both he and she agreed.
    - Wo aur wo dono agree hue.

---

## EITHER...OR (ya...ya)

> Choose ONE from two options.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Either come now or don't come at all.
   - Ya abhi aao ya mat aao.

2. You can have either tea or coffee.
   - Tumhe ya chai mil sakti hai ya coffee.

3. Either you apologize or I'm leaving.
   - Ya tum maafi maango ya main jaa raha hoon.

4. I'll either call you or message you.
   - Main ya tumhe call karunga ya message.

5. Either study hard or fail.
   - Ya mehnat se padho ya fail ho jao.

6. You can sit either here or there.
   - Tum ya yahaan baith sakte ho ya wahaan.

7. Either today or tomorrow, I'll finish it.
   - Ya aaj ya kal, main khatam kar dunga.

8. Either he comes or I go.
   - Ya wo aaye ya main jaaun.

9. Either accept it or reject it.
   - Ya maan lo ya chhod do.

10. You're either with us or against us.
    - Ya tum humare saath ho ya khilaf.

11. Either take a taxi or walk.
    - Ya taxi lo ya paidal jao.

12. I'll buy either the red one or the blue one.
    - Main ya laal lega ya neela.

13. Either she comes first or her brother.
    - Ya wo pehle aayegi ya uska bhai.

14. You can pay either by cash or card.
    - Ya cash se pay karo ya card se.

15. Either way is fine with me.
    - Dono mein se koi bhi tarika mujhe chalega.

---

## NEITHER...NOR (na...na)

> Reject BOTH options (negative).

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Neither he nor she came.
   - Na wo aaya na wo.

2. I have neither time nor money.
   - Mere paas na time hai na paisa.

3. She neither called nor messaged.
   - Usne na call kiya na message.

4. Neither the food nor the service was good.
   - Na khana accha tha na service.

5. I'm neither happy nor sad.
   - Main na khush hoon na dukhi.

6. He neither agreed nor disagreed.
   - Usne na agree kiya na disagree.

7. Neither option is good.
   - Dono mein se koi option accha nahi.

8. She speaks neither English nor Hindi.
   - Wo na English bolti hai na Hindi.

9. I have neither seen nor heard about it.
   - Maine na dekha na suna iske baare mein.

10. Neither my father nor my mother was home.
    - Na papa ghar the na mummy.

11. He is neither rich nor famous.
    - Wo na ameer hai na famous.

12. The movie was neither interesting nor boring.
    - Film na interesting thi na boring.

13. I neither like nor dislike him.
    - Mujhe na pasand hai na napasand.

14. Neither the train nor the bus was on time.
    - Na train time par thi na bus.

15. She ate neither breakfast nor lunch.
    - Usne na nashta khaya na lunch.

## NOT ONLY...BUT ALSO (sirf nahi...balki bhi)

> Emphasizes ADDITION of something extra.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She is not only smart but also kind.
   - Wo sirf smart nahi balki dayalu bhi hai.

2. Not only did he come, but he also helped.
   - Wo sirf aaya nahi balki madad bhi ki.

3. He is not only my friend but also my mentor.
   - Wo sirf mera dost nahi balki mentor bhi hai.

4. Not only was it expensive, but it was also broken.
   - Ye sirf mehenga nahi tha balki toota bhi tha.

5. She not only works hard but also works smart.
   - Wo sirf mehnat nahi karti balki smart bhi karti hai.

6. Not only students but also teachers attended.
   - Sirf students nahi balki teachers bhi aaye.

7. He not only passed but also topped the class.
   - Wo sirf pass nahi hua balki class top bhi kiya.

8. Not only is it beautiful but also practical.
   - Ye sirf sundar nahi balki practical bhi hai.

9. She is not only my sister but also my best friend.
   - Wo sirf meri behen nahi balki meri sabse acchi dost bhi hai.

10. Not only did I finish, but I finished early.
    - Main sirf khatam nahi kiya balki jaldi bhi kiya.

11. He speaks not only English but also French.
    - Wo sirf English nahi bolata balki French bhi.

12. Not only was he late, but he also forgot the documents.
    - Wo sirf late nahi tha balki documents bhi bhool gaya.

13. She not only apologized but also corrected her mistake.
    - Usne sirf maafi nahi maangi balki galti bhi sudhari.

14. Not only did they win, but they won easily.
    - Wo sirf jeete nahi balki aasani se jeete.

15. The food is not only cheap but also delicious.
    - Khana sirf sasta nahi balki swaadist bhi hai.

## WHETHER...OR (chahe...ya)

> Presents two alternatives/possibilities.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I don't know whether he will come or not.
   - Mujhe nahi pata chahe wo aayega ya nahi.

2. Tell me whether you agree or disagree.
   - Bata do chahe agree ho ya disagree.

3. Whether you like it or not, you have to do it.
   - Chahe pasand ho ya na ho, karna padega.

4. I'm going whether it rains or not.
   - Main jaunga chahe baarish ho ya na ho.

5. Whether by bus or train, I'll reach on time.
   - Chahe bus se ya train se, time par pahunch jaunga.

6. I can't decide whether to go or stay.
   - Main decide nahi kar pa raha chahe jaaun ya rukun.

7. Whether you believe it or not, it's true.
   - Chahe maano ya na maano, ye sach hai.

8. I'll support you whether you win or lose.
   - Main tumhara saath dunga chahe jeeto ya haaro.

9. Whether it's hot or cold, I have to go.
   - Chahe garam ho ya thanda, mujhe jaana hai.

10. I don't care whether he agrees or not.
    - Mujhe farak nahi padta chahe wo agree kare ya na kare.

11. Whether rich or poor, everyone is welcome.
    - Chahe ameer ho ya gareeb, sab ka swagat hai.

12. I'll finish this whether easy or difficult.
    - Main ye khatam karunga chahe aasaan ho ya mushkil.

13. Whether today or tomorrow, I need an answer.
    - Chahe aaj ya kal, mujhe jawab chahiye.

14. She'll succeed whether she takes this job or that one.
    - Wo safal hogi chahe ye job le ya wo.

15. I'll call you whether I get the news or not.
    - Main call karunga chahe news mile ya na mile.

## AS...AS (jitna...utna) - Comparison

> Compares two things as EQUAL.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She is as tall as her brother.
   - Wo apne bhai jitni lambi hai.

2. This is as good as that.
   - Ye utna hi accha hai jitna wo.

3. He works as hard as anyone.
   - Wo utni mehnat karta hai jitna koi bhi.

4. I'm not as fast as you.
   - Main tumhare jitna tez nahi.

5. She is as beautiful as a flower.
   - Wo phool jitni sundar hai.

6. The new phone is as expensive as the old one.
   - Naya phone purane jitna mehenga hai.

7. He runs as fast as a cheetah.
   - Wo cheetah jitna tez daudta hai.

8. This task is not as difficult as it seems.
   - Ye kaam utna mushkil nahi jitna lagta hai.

9. She speaks English as fluently as a native.
   - Wo English utni acchi bolti hai jitni native.

10. I'll come as soon as possible.
    - Main jitni jaldi ho sake aaunga.

11. As far as I know, he's honest.
    - Jahan tak mujhe pata hai, wo imaandar hai.

12. As long as you try, I'll support you.
    - Jab tak tum koshish karo, main support karunga.

13. He is as strong as an ox.
    - Wo bailya jitna mazboot hai.

14. She is as patient as a saint.
    - Wo sant jitni sabr wali hai.

15. The test was not as easy as I expected.
    - Test utna aasaan nahi tha jitna socha tha.
