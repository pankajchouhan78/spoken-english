# Subordinating Conjunctions - Condition & Time

> Join a DEPENDENT clause to an INDEPENDENT clause.

---

## IF (agar) - Condition

> Shows what will happen UNDER A CONDITION.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. If you work hard, you will succeed.
   - Agar mehnat karoge, safal hoge.

2. If it rains, I'll stay home.
   - Agar baarish hui, main ghar rahunga.

3. Call me if you need help.
   - Mujhe call karo agar madad chahiye.

4. If you don't hurry, you'll be late.
   - Agar jaldi nahi kiye, late ho jaoge.

5. I'll go if you come with me.
   - Main jaunga agar tum saath aao.

6. If I had money, I would buy a car.
   - Agar mere paas paise hote, car kharidta.

7. Ask me if you have any questions.
   - Mujhse poocho agar koi sawaal ho.

8. If he comes, tell him to wait.
   - Agar wo aaye, use wait karne bolo.

9. I'll be happy if you visit.
   - Main khush hounga agar tum aao.

10. If you study now, you can play later.
    - Agar abhi padho, baad mein khel sakte ho.

11. If I were you, I wouldn't go.
    - Agar main tumhari jagah hota, nahi jaata.

12. What will you do if you fail?
    - Agar fail ho gaye to kya karoge?

13. If you don't understand, ask again.
    - Agar samajh na aaye, phir poocho.

14. I'll help you if I can.
    - Agar kar saka to madad karunga.

15. If possible, please come early.
    - Agar ho sake, jaldi aana.

## UNLESS (jab tak nahi) - Negative Condition

> Means "if not" - action won't happen without the condition.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Unless you try, you won't succeed.
   - Jab tak koshish nahi karoge, safal nahi hoge.

2. I won't go unless you come with me.
   - Main nahi jaunga jab tak tum saath nahi aao.

3. Unless it stops raining, we can't go.
   - Jab tak baarish nahi ruki, hum nahi ja sakte.

4. He won't understand unless you explain.
   - Wo nahi samjhega jab tak samjhao nahi.

5. Unless you work hard, you'll fail.
   - Jab tak mehnat nahi karoge, fail hoge.

6. I won't believe unless I see proof.
   - Main nahi maanunga jab tak proof na dekh lun.

7. Unless there's an emergency, don't call at night.
   - Jab tak emergency na ho, raat ko call mat karo.

8. You can't enter unless you have a ticket.
   - Jab tak ticket na ho, andar nahi ja sakte.

9. Unless he apologizes, I won't forgive him.
   - Jab tak wo maafi na maange, main maaf nahi karunga.

10. We won't start unless everyone is here.
    - Jab tak sab nahi aa jaate, shuru nahi karenge.

11. Unless you ask, you won't get.
    - Jab tak maangoge nahi, milega nahi.

12. I won't sign unless I read it first.
    - Main sign nahi karunga jab tak pehle padh na lun.

13. Unless the price drops, I won't buy.
    - Jab tak price na gire, main nahi khareedunga.

14. You won't know unless you try.
    - Jab tak koshish nahi karoge, pata nahi chalega.

15. Unless you tell me, I won't know.
    - Jab tak tum nahi bataoge, mujhe nahi pata chalega.

## WHEN (jab) - Time

> Shows the time something happens.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Call me when you arrive.
   - Jab pahuncho, call karo.

2. When I was young, I played a lot.
   - Jab main chhota tha, bahut khelta tha.

3. I'll help you when I'm free.
   - Jab free hounga, madad karunga.

4. When he comes, we'll start the meeting.
   - Jab wo aayega, meeting shuru karenge.

5. I was sleeping when you called.
   - Main so raha tha jab tumne call kiya.

6. When it rains, the roads get flooded.
   - Jab baarish hoti hai, sadakein doob jaati hain.

7. Tell me when you're ready.
   - Bata do jab taiyaar ho jao.

8. When I grow up, I want to be a doctor.
   - Jab bada hounga, doctor banna chahta hoon.

9. I feel happy when I see you.
   - Jab tumhe dekhta hoon, khush hota hoon.

10. When she sings, everyone listens.
    - Jab wo gaati hai, sab sunte hain.

11. I'll call you when I reach home.
    - Jab ghar pahunch jaunga, call karunga.

12. When the sun sets, it gets cold.
    - Jab suraj doobta hai, thandi hoti hai.

13. I understood when he explained.
    - Jab usne samjhaya, tab samjha.

14. When you finish, let me know.
    - Jab khatam ho jaye, bata dena.

15. I cry when I watch sad movies.
    - Jab sad movies dekhta hoon, rota hoon.

## WHILE (jab / jabki) - During

> Shows two actions happening at the SAME TIME.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Don't talk while eating.
   - Khana khate waqt baat mat karo.

2. She fell asleep while reading.
   - Wo padhte padhte so gayi.

3. I listen to music while working.
   - Main kaam karte waqt music sunta hoon.

4. While I cook, you clean.
   - Jab main khana banaun, tum saaf karo.

5. He called while I was in a meeting.
   - Usne call kiya jab main meeting mein tha.

6. While you wait, have some tea.
   - Jab tak wait karo, chai piyo.

7. Don't use your phone while driving.
   - Gaadi chalate waqt phone mat use karo.

8. While studying, I got a message.
   - Padhte waqt mujhe message aaya.

9. She watches TV while doing housework.
   - Wo ghar ka kaam karte waqt TV dekhti hai.

10. While I agree, I have some concerns.
    - Jab main agree karta hoon, kuch chintaayein hain.

11. I fell down while running.
    - Daudte waqt gir gaya.

12. While he was talking, I left.
    - Jab wo baat kar raha tha, main chala gaya.

13. Don't disturb me while I'm working.
    - Jab kaam kar raha hoon, disturb mat karo.

14. While waiting for the bus, I met a friend.
    - Bus ka wait karte waqt ek dost mila.

15. She sings while she cooks.
    - Wo khana banate waqt gaati hai.

## BEFORE / AFTER (pehle / baad mein) - Time Sequence

### Before (pehle)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Wash your hands before eating.
   - Khane se pehle haath dho.

2. Think before you speak.
   - Bolne se pehle socho.

3. I want to finish this before I leave.
   - Jaane se pehle ye khatam karna hai.

4. Call me before you come.
   - Aane se pehle call karo.

5. He left before I arrived.
   - Meri aane se pehle wo chala gaya.

6. Read the instructions before starting.
   - Shuru karne se pehle instructions padho.

7. I've heard this story before.
   - Maine ye kahani pehle suni hai.

8. Lock the door before you sleep.
   - Sone se pehle darwaza band karo.

9. Check your work before submitting.
   - Submit karne se pehle kaam check karo.

10. Eat something before taking medicine.
    - Dawai lene se pehle kuch kha lo.

### After (baad mein)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I'll call you after the meeting.
   - Meeting ke baad call karunga.

2. Let's go out after dinner.
   - Khane ke baad bahar chalte hain.

3. She felt better after resting.
   - Aaram ke baad wo behtar feel hui.

4. After you finish, we can talk.
   - Jab tum khatam kar lo, baat karte hain.

5. He went home after work.
   - Kaam ke baad wo ghar gaya.

6. I understood after he explained.
   - Uske samjhane ke baad samjha.

7. After the rain, the weather was cool.
   - Baarish ke baad mausam thanda tha.

8. Clean up after you eat.
   - Khana khane ke baad saaf karo.

9. We'll celebrate after we win.
   - Jeetne ke baad celebrate karenge.

10. I felt tired after the workout.
    - Workout ke baad thakan lagi.

## UNTIL / TILL (jab tak) - Duration

> Action continues UP TO a point in time.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Wait here until I come back.
   - Jab tak main wapas na aaun, yahaan ruko.

2. I'll work until 6 PM.
   - Main 6 baje tak kaam karunga.

3. Don't leave until the movie ends.
   - Jab tak movie khatam na ho, mat jao.

4. We waited until the rain stopped.
   - Humne jab tak baarish nahi ruki, wait kiya.

5. Stay in bed until you feel better.
   - Jab tak better feel na ho, bed mein raho.

6. I won't give up until I succeed.
   - Jab tak safal na ho jaaun, haar nahi maanunga.

7. Keep trying until you get it right.
   - Jab tak sahi na ho, koshish karte raho.

8. She studied until midnight.
   - Wo aadhi raat tak padhi.

9. Wait until your name is called.
   - Jab tak naam na bulaya jaye, ruko.

10. I'll stay here until tomorrow.
    - Main kal tak yahaan rahunga.

---

## AS SOON AS (jaise hi) - Immediate Time

> Immediately after something happens.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Call me as soon as you reach.
   - Jaise hi pahuncho, call karo.

2. As soon as I saw him, I recognized him.
   - Jaise hi dekha, pehchaan liya.

3. I'll leave as soon as it stops raining.
   - Jaise hi baarish ruki, chala jaunga.

4. As soon as the bell rang, we left.
   - Jaise hi ghanti baji, hum nikal gaye.

5. Tell me as soon as you know.
   - Jaise hi pata chale, bata dena.

6. He started working as soon as he arrived.
   - Jaise hi pahuncha, kaam shuru kar diya.

7. As soon as I finish, I'll call you.
   - Jaise hi khatam ho, call karunga.

8. She smiled as soon as she saw me.
   - Jaise hi mujhe dekha, muskurayi.

9. I knew the answer as soon as I heard the question.
   - Jaise hi sawaal suna, jawab pata tha.

10. As soon as possible, please respond.
    - Jitni jaldi ho sake, jawab do.
