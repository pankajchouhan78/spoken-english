# Conjunctions - Introduction & Types

> Conjunctions JOIN words, phrases, or clauses together.
> They are the "glue" of sentences.

---

## What is a Conjunction?

| Type | Function | Example |
|------|----------|---------|
| Word + Word | tea **and** coffee | chai **aur** coffee |
| Phrase + Phrase | in the morning **or** at night | subah mein **ya** raat ko |
| Clause + Clause | I came **but** he left | Main aaya **par** wo chala gaya |

---

## Types of Conjunctions

| Type | Function | Examples |
|------|----------|----------|
| Coordinating | Join equal parts | and, but, or, so, yet |
| Subordinating | Join unequal parts | because, although, if, when |
| Correlative | Work in pairs | either...or, neither...nor |

---

## 1. Coordinating Conjunctions (FANBOYS)

> Join words, phrases, or clauses of EQUAL importance.

| Letter | Conjunction | Hindi | Use |
|--------|-------------|-------|-----|
| F | for | kyunki | reason |
| A | and | aur | addition |
| N | nor | na hi | negative addition |
| B | but | lekin/par | contrast |
| O | or | ya | choice |
| Y | yet | phir bhi | contrast |
| S | so | isliye | result |

**Quick Examples:**

1. I like tea **and** coffee.
   - Mujhe chai **aur** coffee pasand hai.

2. He is poor **but** honest.
   - Wo gareeb hai **par** imaandar hai.

3. Study hard **or** you will fail.
   - Mehnat se padho **ya** fail ho jaoge.

4. It was raining, **so** I stayed home.
   - Baarish ho rahi thi, **isliye** main ghar raha.

5. She is tired, **yet** she keeps working.
   - Wo thaki hai, **phir bhi** kaam kar rahi hai.

---

## 2. Subordinating Conjunctions

> Join a DEPENDENT clause to an INDEPENDENT clause.

| Category | Conjunctions |
|----------|--------------|
| Cause/Reason | because, since, as |
| Contrast | although, though, even though, while |
| Condition | if, unless, provided, in case |
| Time | when, while, before, after, until, as soon as |
| Purpose | so that, in order that |
| Comparison | than, as...as |

**Quick Examples:**

1. I stayed home **because** it was raining.
   - Main ghar raha **kyunki** baarish ho rahi thi.

2. **Although** he is rich, he is not happy.
   - **Halaanki** wo ameer hai, wo khush nahi hai.

3. **If** you work hard, you will succeed.
   - **Agar** mehnat karoge, safal hoge.

4. Call me **when** you arrive.
   - Jab pahuncho **tab** call karo.

5. Wait here **until** I return.
   - Yahaan ruko **jab tak** main lautun.

---

## 3. Correlative Conjunctions

> Work in PAIRS to join equal elements.

| Pair | Hindi | Use |
|------|-------|-----|
| both...and | dono...aur | addition |
| either...or | ya...ya | choice |
| neither...nor | na...na | negative |
| not only...but also | sirf...nahi balki | emphasis |
| whether...or | chahe...ya | alternatives |

**Quick Examples:**

1. **Both** tea **and** coffee are available.
   - Chai **aur** coffee **dono** available hain.

2. **Either** come now **or** don't come at all.
   - **Ya** abhi aao **ya** mat aao.

3. He is **neither** rich **nor** famous.
   - Wo **na** ameer hai **na** famous.

4. She is **not only** smart **but also** kind.
   - Wo **sirf** smart **nahi balki** dayalu bhi hai.

5. I don't know **whether** he will come **or** not.
   - Mujhe nahi pata **chahe** wo aayega **ya** nahi.

## Position Rules

| Type | Position | Example |
|------|----------|---------|
| Coordinating | Between items | A **and** B |
| Subordinating | Start of dependent clause | **Because** it rained, I stayed. |
| Correlative | Before each item | **Both** A **and** B |

---
