# HERE, THERE, NOW, THEN - Demonstrative Adverbs

> Point to PLACE or TIME.
> HERE/THERE = Place | NOW/THEN = Time

---

## HERE (yahaan) - Near Place

> Points to a place CLOSE to the speaker.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Come here, please.
   - Yahaan aao, please.

2. I live here.
   - Main yahaan rehta hoon.

3. Put the bag here.
   - Bag yahaan rakho.

4. Here is your coffee.
   - Ye lo tumhari coffee.

5. I've been here before.
   - Main yahaan pehle aa chuka hoon.

6. Sign here at the bottom.
   - Yahaan neeche sign karo.

7. The bus stops here.
   - Bus yahaan rukti hai.

8. What are you doing here?
   - Tum yahaan kya kar rahe ho?

9. Stay here until I come back.
   - Jab tak main wapas na aaun, yahaan ruko.

10. Here we go!
    - Chalo shuru karte hain!

11. I'm new here.
    - Main yahaan naya hoon.

12. It's very hot here.
    - Yahaan bahut garam hai.

13. Here is the money I owe you.
    - Ye lo jo paise dene the.

14. Nobody is here.
    - Yahaan koi nahi hai.

15. The problem lies here.
    - Problem yahaan hai.

---

## THERE (wahaan) - Far Place

> Points to a place FAR from the speaker.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Go there and wait.
   - Wahaan jao aur wait karo.

2. The restaurant is over there.
   - Restaurant wahaan hai.

3. I went there yesterday.
   - Main kal wahaan gaya tha.

4. Put it there on the table.
   - Use wahaan table par rakho.

5. Who lives there?
   - Wahaan kaun rehta hai?

6. There is the bus stop.
   - Wahaan bus stop hai.

7. I'll meet you there.
   - Main tumse wahaan milunga.

8. There he comes!
   - Wo aa gaya!

9. Stay there, don't move.
   - Wahaan ruko, hilo mat.

10. How long have you been there?
    - Tum wahaan kitni der se ho?

11. The bank is right there.
    - Bank bilkul wahaan hai.

12. There is no one there.
    - Wahaan koi nahi hai.

13. I saw him standing there.
    - Maine use wahaan khade dekha.

14. We'll reach there by evening.
    - Hum shaam tak wahaan pahunch jayenge.

15. There goes my bus!
    - Meri bus chali gayi!

---

## HERE vs THERE - Comparison

| HERE (Near) | THERE (Far) |
|-------------|-------------|
| Come here | Go there |
| I'm here | I was there |
| Here is... | There is... |
| Over here | Over there |
| Right here | Right there |
| Around here | Around there |

**Contrast Sentences:**

1. I'm here, but he's there.
   - Main yahaan hoon, par wo wahaan hai.

2. Put this here and that there.
   - Ye yahaan rakho aur wo wahaan.

3. Here it's cold, but there it's hot.
   - Yahaan thanda hai, par wahaan garam.

4. The shop is here, the mall is there.
   - Dukaan yahaan hai, mall wahaan.

5. I live here, I work there.
   - Main yahaan rehta hoon, wahaan kaam karta hoon.

---

## NOW (abhi) - Present Time

> Points to the CURRENT moment.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I'm busy now.
   - Main abhi busy hoon.

2. Do it now!
   - Abhi karo!

3. What are you doing now?
   - Abhi kya kar rahe ho?

4. Now I understand.
   - Ab samajh aa gaya.

5. Call me now.
   - Abhi call karo.

6. The time is now.
   - Waqt abhi hai.

7. Right now, I'm eating.
   - Abhi main kha raha hoon.

8. Now or never!
   - Abhi ya kabhi nahi!

9. I have to go now.
   - Mujhe abhi jaana hai.

10. From now on, I'll work harder.
    - Ab se, main zyada mehnat karunga.

11. By now, he must have reached.
    - Ab tak wo pahunch gaya hoga.

12. Until now, I didn't know.
    - Ab tak mujhe nahi pata tha.

13. Just now, I got the message.
    - Abhi abhi message aaya.

14. I'm free now, let's talk.
    - Main abhi free hoon, baat karte hain.

15. Now is the right time.
    - Ab sahi waqt hai.

---

## THEN (tab/phir) - Past/Future Time

> Points to ANOTHER time (past or future).

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I was young then.
   - Main tab chhota tha.

2. We'll meet, and then we'll decide.
   - Milenge, phir decide karenge.

3. First eat, then talk.
   - Pehle khao, phir baat karo.

4. If you study, then you'll pass.
   - Agar padhoge, to pass hoge.

5. Since then, I haven't seen him.
   - Tab se maine use nahi dekha.

6. Until then, wait here.
   - Tab tak, yahaan ruko.

7. Back then, life was simple.
   - Tab zindagi simple thi.

8. Study now, play then.
   - Abhi padho, baad mein khelo.

9. If not now, then when?
   - Agar abhi nahi, to kab?

10. Then what happened?
    - Phir kya hua?

11. By then, it was too late.
    - Tab tak bahut der ho gayi thi.

12. Every now and then, I visit them.
    - Kabhi kabhi main unse milne jaata hoon.

13. We were happy then.
    - Hum tab khush the.

14. I didn't know the truth then.
    - Tab mujhe sach nahi pata tha.

15. Now and then, I think about it.
    - Kabhi kabhi iske baare mein sochta hoon.

---

## NOW vs THEN - Comparison

| NOW (Present) | THEN (Past/Future) |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Right now | Back then |
| Just now | Since then |
| By now | By then |
| Until now | Until then |
| From now | From then |

**Contrast Sentences:**

1. I'm busy now, I'll be free then.
   - Main abhi busy hoon, tab free hounga.

2. Now I know, then I didn't.
   - Ab pata hai, tab nahi tha.

3. Do it now, not then.
   - Abhi karo, tab nahi.

4. Now it's easy, then it was difficult.
   - Ab aasaan hai, tab mushkil tha.

5. I'm here now, I was there then.
   - Main abhi yahaan hoon, tab wahaan tha.

## Common Expressions

| Expression | Hindi | Meaning |
|------------|-------|---------|
| Here and there | Yahaan wahaan | In different places |
| Now and then | Kabhi kabhi | Sometimes |
| Here we go | Chalo shuru | Let's begin |
| There you go | Ye lo | Here it is |
| Right here | Bilkul yahaan | Exactly this place |
| Right now | Abhi | Immediately |
| From here | Yahaan se | Starting from this place |
| From there | Wahaan se | Starting from that place |
| From now on | Ab se | Starting from this time |
| Since then | Tab se | From that time |

**Sentences:**

1. I search here and there for my keys.
   - Main chaabi yahaan wahaan dhundhta hoon.

2. Now and then, I miss my hometown.
   - Kabhi kabhi mujhe apne gaon ki yaad aati hai.

3. Here we go again!
   - Phir se wahi ho raha hai!

4. There you go, it's done.
   - Ye lo, ho gaya.

5. From now on, no more excuses.
   - Ab se, koi bahana nahi.
