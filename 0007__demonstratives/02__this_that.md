# THIS vs THAT - Singular Demonstratives

> THIS = Near (paas) | THAT = Far (door)
> Both are used for SINGULAR nouns.

---

## THIS (ye/yeh) - Near

> Points to something CLOSE to the speaker.

### As Pronoun (replaces noun)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. This is my book.
   - Ye meri kitaab hai.

2. This is very important.
   - Ye bahut zaroori hai.

3. What is this?
   - Ye kya hai?

4. This is delicious!
   - Ye bahut swaadist hai!

5. I don't understand this.
   - Mujhe ye samajh nahi aa raha.

6. This is the right answer.
   - Ye sahi jawab hai.

7. Is this yours?
   - Kya ye tumhara hai?

8. This is my first time here.
   - Yahaan meri pehli baar hai.

9. Take this.
   - Ye lo.

10. Who made this?
    - Ye kisne banaya?

11. This is amazing!
    - Ye kamaal hai!

12. I need this urgently.
    - Mujhe ye urgently chahiye.

13. This is the problem.
    - Ye problem hai.

14. How much is this?
    - Ye kitne ka hai?

15. This is my favorite.
    - Ye mera favorite hai.

## THAT (wo/woh) - Far

> Points to something FAR from the speaker.

### As Pronoun (replaces noun)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. That is his car.
   - Wo uski car hai.

2. What is that?
   - Wo kya hai?

3. That was delicious!
   - Wo bahut swaadist tha!

4. I don't like that.
   - Mujhe wo pasand nahi.

5. That is a good idea.
   - Wo accha idea hai.

6. Is that your house?
   - Kya wo tumhara ghar hai?

7. That was a mistake.
   - Wo galti thi.

8. Who said that?
   - Wo kisne kaha?

9. That is the reason.
   - Wo wajah hai.

10. Don't do that.
    - Wo mat karo.

11. That is correct.
    - Wo sahi hai.

12. I remember that.
    - Mujhe wo yaad hai.

13. That was unexpected.
    - Wo unexpected tha.

14. That is my dream.
    - Wo mera sapna hai.

15. I can't forget that.
    - Main wo bhool nahi sakta.

---

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. That building is tall.
   - Wo building lambi hai.

2. I want that one.
   - Mujhe wo wala chahiye.

3. That man is my uncle.
   - Wo aadmi mere chacha hain.

4. That movie was great.
   - Wo film behtareen thi.

5. That day was special.
   - Wo din khaas tha.

6. That road is dangerous.
   - Wo raasta khatarnak hai.

7. I met him that year.
   - Main usse us saal mila.

8. That place is far.
   - Wo jagah door hai.

9. That restaurant is expensive.
   - Wo restaurant mehenga hai.

10. That song reminds me of you.
    - Wo gaana tumhari yaad dilaata hai.

11. That decision was wrong.
    - Wo faisla galat tha.

## THIS vs THAT - Comparison

| Aspect | THIS | THAT |
|--------|------|------|
| Distance | Near | Far |
| Time | Present/Current | Past |
| Phone | Introducing self | Asking who |
| Preference | What I prefer | What I don't prefer |

---

## Special Uses

### 1. On Phone

**Introducing yourself:**
- Hello, **this** is Rahul speaking.
- Namaste, **ye** Rahul bol raha hoon.

**Asking who:**
- Who is **that**?
- **Wo** kaun hai?

### 2. Introducing People

**Face to face:**
- **This** is my wife, Priya.
- **Ye** meri patni Priya hai.

**Talking about someone not present:**
- **That** man I told you about...
- **Wo** aadmi jiske baare mein bataya tha...

### 3. Referring to Time

| THIS (current/near) | THAT (past/far) |
|---------------------|-----------------|
| this morning (aaj subah) | that morning (us subah) |
| this week (is hafte) | that week (us hafte) |
| this year (is saal) | that year (us saal) |
| this time (is baar) | that time (us waqt) |

**Examples:**

1. This year, I'll work harder.
   - Is saal, main zyada mehnat karunga.

2. That was a difficult year.
   - Wo mushkil saal tha.

3. This moment is precious.
   - Ye pal keemati hai.

4. I'll never forget that day.
   - Wo din kabhi nahi bhulunga.

5. At this point, I'm confused.
   - Is waqt, main confused hoon.

## Common Expressions

| Expression | Hindi | Meaning |
|------------|-------|---------|
| This is it! | Ye raha! / Bas ye hai! | Found it / Final decision |
| That's it! | Bas! / Ho gaya! | Finished / No more |
| Is that so? | Kya sach? | Really? |
| Like this | Aise | In this manner |
| Like that | Waise | In that manner |
| This way | Is taraf | This direction |
| That way | Us taraf | That direction |
| This much | Itna | This amount |
| That much | Utna | That amount |

**Sentences:**

1. Do it like this.
   - Aise karo.

2. Don't talk like that.
   - Waise baat mat karo.

3. Come this way.
   - Is taraf aao.

4. The shop is that way.
   - Dukaan us taraf hai.

5. I can only eat this much.
   - Main itna hi kha sakta hoon.
