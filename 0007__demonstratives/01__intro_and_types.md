# Demonstrative Words - Introduction & Types

> Demonstratives POINT to specific things, people, or ideas.
> They answer: WHICH ONE? or WHERE?

---

## What are Demonstratives?

| Type | Words | Hindi |
|------|-------|-------|
| Near (singular) | this | ye/yeh |
| Near (plural) | these | ye sab |
| Far (singular) | that | wo/woh |
| Far (plural) | those | wo sab |

---

## Types of Demonstratives

### 1. Demonstrative Pronouns

> Replace a noun completely.

| Word | Example | Hindi |
|------|---------|-------|
| this | **This** is my book. | **Ye** meri kitaab hai. |
| that | **That** is your car. | **Wo** tumhari car hai. |
| these | **These** are my friends. | **Ye** mere dost hain. |
| those | **Those** are expensive. | **Wo** mehnge hain. |

### 2. Demonstrative Adjectives

> Describe a noun (come BEFORE the noun).

| Word | Example | Hindi |
|------|---------|-------|
| this | **This book** is good. | **Ye kitaab** acchi hai. |
| that | **That car** is fast. | **Wo car** tez hai. |
| these | **These apples** are fresh. | **Ye seb** taaze hain. |
| those | **Those people** are kind. | **Wo log** dayalu hain. |

### 3. Demonstrative Adverbs

> Show location or manner.

| Word | Example | Hindi |
|------|---------|-------|
| here | Come **here**. | **Yahaan** aao. |
| there | Go **there**. | **Wahaan** jao. |
| now | Do it **now**. | **Abhi** karo. |
| then | I was young **then**. | **Tab** main chhota tha. |

## Distance Rule

| Distance | Singular | Plural | Place |
|----------|----------|--------|-------|
| NEAR (paas) | this | these | here |
| FAR (door) | that | those | there |

## Common Uses

### 1. Pointing to Objects

1. This is my phone.
   - Ye mera phone hai.

2. That is his car.
   - Wo uski car hai.

3. These are fresh fruits.
   - Ye taaze phal hain.

4. Those are my books.
   - Wo meri kitaabein hain.

5. Look at this!
   - Ye dekho!

### 2. Pointing to People

1. This is my brother.
   - Ye mera bhai hai.

2. That is our teacher.
   - Wo humare teacher hain.

3. These are my colleagues.
   - Ye mere colleagues hain.

4. Those are the guests.
   - Wo mehman hain.

5. Who is that?
   - Wo kaun hai?

### 3. Pointing to Ideas/Situations

1. This is a good idea.
   - Ye accha idea hai.

2. That was a mistake.
   - Wo galti thi.

3. I didn't expect this.
   - Mujhe ye ummeed nahi thi.

4. Is that clear?
   - Kya wo samajh aa gaya?

5. What does this mean?
   - Iska kya matlab hai?

### 4. Pointing to Time

1. This morning was cold.
   - Aaj subah thandi thi.

2. That day was special.
   - Wo din khaas tha.

3. These days, I'm busy.
   - Aaj kal, main busy hoon.

4. Those were the good old days.
   - Wo acche purane din the.

5. At this moment, I'm happy.
   - Is waqt, main khush hoon.

## Demonstrative vs. Article

| Demonstrative | Article |
|---------------|---------|
| **This** book (specific, pointing) | **A** book (any book) |
| **That** car (specific, far) | **The** car (known car) |
