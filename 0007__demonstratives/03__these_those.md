# THESE vs THOSE - Plural Demonstratives

> THESE = Near (paas) | THOSE = Far (door)
> Both are used for PLURAL nouns.

---

## THESE (ye / ye sab) - Near Plural

> Points to multiple things CLOSE to the speaker.

### As Pronoun (replaces noun)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. These are my books.
   - Ye meri kitaabein hain.

2. These are delicious!
   - Ye bahut swaadist hain!

3. What are these?
   - Ye kya hain?

4. These are for you.
   - Ye tumhare liye hain.

5. I don't need these.
   - Mujhe ye nahi chahiye.

6. These are my friends.
   - Ye mere dost hain.

7. Are these yours?
   - Kya ye tumhare hain?

8. These belong to me.
   - Ye mere hain.

9. Take these with you.
   - Ye apne saath le jao.

10. Who made these?
    - Ye kisne banaye?

11. These are expensive.
    - Ye mehnge hain.

12. How much are these?
    - Ye kitne ke hain?

13. I want to buy these.
    - Main ye kharidna chahta hoon.

14. These are the best options.
    - Ye sabse acche options hain.

15. These are the documents you asked for.
    - Ye wo documents hain jo tumne maange.

---

   - Ye phool sundar hain.

   - Ye problems serious hain.

    - Ye bachche talented hain.

## THOSE (wo / wo sab) - Far Plural

> Points to multiple things FAR from the speaker.

### As Pronoun (replaces noun)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Those are his books.
   - Wo uski kitaabein hain.

2. Those look expensive.
   - Wo mehnge lagte hain.

3. What are those?
   - Wo kya hain?

4. I don't like those.
   - Mujhe wo pasand nahi.

5. Those are not mine.
   - Wo mere nahi hain.

6. Who are those people?
   - Wo log kaun hain?

7. Those were the good old days.
   - Wo acche purane din the.

8. I remember those.
   - Mujhe wo yaad hain.

9. Those are the ones I want.
   - Wo wahi hain jo mujhe chahiye.

10. Don't buy those.
    - Wo mat khareedo.

11. Those were difficult times.
    - Wo mushkil waqt tha.

12. I can't forget those.
    - Main wo bhool nahi sakta.

13. Those are my relatives.
    - Wo mere rishtedaar hain.

14. Those are the rules.
    - Wo niyam hain.

15. Look at those!
    - Wo dekho!

---

### As Adjective (before noun)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Those books are old.
   - Wo kitaabein purani hain.

2. Those people are kind.
   - Wo log dayalu hain.

3. Those days were special.
   - Wo din khaas the.

4. I remember those times.
   - Mujhe wo waqt yaad hai.

5. Those buildings are tall.
   - Wo buildings lambi hain.

6. Those children are playing.
   - Wo bachche khel rahe hain.

7. I want those shoes.
   - Mujhe wo joote chahiye.

8. Those flowers smell nice.
   - Wo phool acche mahakte hain.

9. Those cars are expensive.
   - Wo cars mehengi hain.

10. I met those guys last year.
    - Main un ladkon se pichle saal mila.

11. Those memories are precious.
    - Wo yaadein keemati hain.

12. Those students are hardworking.
    - Wo students mehanti hain.

13. Those mistakes taught me a lot.
    - Un galtiyon ne mujhe bahut sikhaya.

14. I miss those moments.
    - Mujhe un palon ki yaad aati hai.

15. Those stories were inspiring.
    - Wo kahaniyaan inspiring thi.

## THESE vs THOSE - Comparison

| Aspect | THESE | THOSE |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Distance | Near | Far |
| Time | Current | Past |
| Preference | What I like | What I don't prefer |
| Singular form | this | that |

---

## Common Expressions

| Expression | Hindi | Meaning |
|------------|-------|---------|
| These days | Aaj kal | Nowadays |
| Those days | Un dino | In those times |
| One of these | In mein se ek | One from this group |
| One of those | Un mein se ek | One from that group |
| All of these | Ye sab | Every one of these |
| All of those | Wo sab | Every one of those |
| Like these | Aise | Similar to these |
| Like those | Waise | Similar to those |

---

## Time Expressions

| THESE (Present/Near) | THOSE (Past/Far) |
|---------------------|------------------|
| these days (aaj kal) | those days (un dino) |
| these years (ye saal) | those years (wo saal) |
| these moments (ye pal) | those moments (wo pal) |
| these times (ye waqt) | those times (wo waqt) |

**Sentences:**

1. These days, everyone uses smartphones.
   - Aaj kal, sab smartphone use karte hain.

2. Those were difficult days.
   - Wo mushkil din the.

3. I'm enjoying these moments.
   - Main in palon ka maza le raha hoon.

4. I miss those carefree days.
   - Mujhe un befikri ke dino ki yaad aati hai.

5. These are trying times for everyone.
   - Ye sab ke liye mushkil waqt hai.

---

## Comparing Things

**Sentences:**

1. These are better than those.
   - Ye un se behtar hain.

2. I prefer these over those.
   - Mujhe wo nahi, ye pasand hain.

3. Those are expensive, these are cheap.
   - Wo mehnge hain, ye saste hain.

4. These are new, those are old.
   - Ye naye hain, wo purane hain.

5. Do you want these or those?
   - Tumhe ye chahiye ya wo?

6. These look fresher than those.
   - Ye un se zyada taaze lagte hain.

7. I'll take these, not those.
   - Main ye lunga, wo nahi.

8. These fit me, those don't.
   - Ye mujhe fit hain, wo nahi.

9. Those are mine, these are yours.
   - Wo mere hain, ye tumhare.

10. These taste better than those.
    - Ye un se zyada swaadist hain.
