# SUCH, SAME, OTHER - Related Demonstratives

> Words that point to similarity, sameness, or difference.

---

## SUCH (aisa/aise) - Of This Kind

> Points to a TYPE or QUALITY of something.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I've never seen such beauty.
   - Maine aisi sundarti kabhi nahi dekhi.

2. Don't say such things.
   - Aisi baatein mat kaho.

3. Such people are rare.
   - Aise log kam hain.

4. I didn't expect such behavior.
   - Mujhe aisa behave ummeed nahi thi.

5. Such is life.
   - Zindagi aisi hi hai.

6. Why are you in such a hurry?
   - Itni jaldi mein kyun ho?

7. There's no such thing as free lunch.
   - Free mein kuch nahi milta.

8. I don't like such movies.
   - Mujhe aisi films pasand nahi.

9. Such a beautiful day!
   - Kitna sundar din!

10. She is such a kind person.
    - Wo kitni dayalu hai.

11. Don't make such excuses.
    - Aise bahane mat do.

12. I've never heard such nonsense.
    - Maine aisi bakwaas kabhi nahi suni.

13. Such things happen in life.
    - Zindagi mein aisi cheezein hoti hain.

14. He is such a hard worker.
    - Wo kitna mehanti hai.

15. There's no such person here.
    - Yahaan aisa koi nahi hai.

## SUCH A / SUCH AN (itna/kitna)

> Used before singular countable nouns.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. He is such a good friend.
   - Wo kitna accha dost hai.

2. It was such a long day.
   - Kitna lamba din tha.

3. She has such a sweet voice.
   - Uski awaaz kitni meethi hai.

4. This is such a difficult question.
   - Ye kitna mushkil sawaal hai.

5. I've never met such a person.
   - Maine aisa insaan kabhi nahi dekha.

6. Why are you making such a fuss?
   - Itna shor kyun macha rahe ho?

7. It's such a pleasure to meet you.
   - Aapse milkar bahut khushi hui.

8. She told such a funny joke.
   - Usne kitna funny joke sunaya.

9. I've never been to such a place.
   - Main aisi jagah kabhi nahi gaya.

10. What such a waste of time!
    - Kitna time waste hua!

## SAME (wahi/same) - Identical

> Points to something that is NOT different.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. We wear the same uniform.
   - Hum same uniform pehnte hain.

2. We have the same birthday.
   - Humare birthday same hain.

3. It's the same story again.
   - Wahi kahani phir se.

4. I feel the same way.
   - Mujhe bhi aisa hi lagta hai.

5. We're in the same class.
   - Hum same class mein hain.

6. The same thing happened to me.
   - Mere saath bhi yahi hua.

7. Don't make the same mistake again.
   - Wahi galti phir mat karo.

8. She said the same thing.
   - Usne bhi yahi kaha.

9. They look the same to me.
   - Mujhe dono same lagte hain.

10. At the same time, I was there.
    - Usi waqt, main wahaan tha.

11. We have the same problem.
    - Humari same problem hai.

12. It's not the same without you.
    - Tumhare bina same nahi hai.

13. The same rules apply to everyone.
    - Same niyam sabpar laagu hain.

14. I'll have the same as him.
    - Mujhe bhi wahi do jo usko.

15. History repeats itself; it's the same pattern.
    - Itihaas repeat hota hai; wahi pattern.

## OTHER / ANOTHER (doosra/aur)

### OTHER (doosra/aur) - Different One(s)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I'll try the other one.
   - Main doosra try karunga.

2. Some people work, others rest.
   - Kuch log kaam karte hain, doosre aaram.

3. Where is the other shoe?
   - Doosra joota kahaan hai?

4. Give me the other option.
   - Mujhe doosra option do.

5. The other day, I met him.
   - Kuch din pehle, main usse mila.

6. On the other hand, it's expensive.
   - Doosri taraf, ye mehenga hai.

7. Other students are waiting.
   - Doosre students wait kar rahe hain.

8. I have other plans.
   - Mere doosre plans hain.

9. Let's go to some other place.
   - Kisi aur jagah chalte hain.

10. Each other means together.
    - Ek doosre ka matlab saath.

11. The other side is better.
    - Doosri taraf behtar hai.

12. Some like tea, others prefer coffee.
    - Kuch chai pasand karte hain, kuch coffee.

13. We help each other.
    - Hum ek doosre ki madad karte hain.

14. There must be some other way.
    - Koi aur raasta hoga.

15. What do others think about this?
    - Doosre is baare mein kya sochte hain?

---

### ANOTHER (ek aur) - One More / Different One

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Give me another chance.
   - Mujhe ek aur mauka do.

2. I want another cup of tea.
   - Mujhe ek aur chai chahiye.

3. That's another story.
   - Ye alag kahani hai.

4. One way or another, I'll do it.
   - Kisi na kisi tarah, main karunga.

5. Let's meet another day.
   - Kisi aur din milte hain.

6. Another problem has come up.
   - Ek aur problem aa gayi.

7. Can I have another piece?
   - Ek aur tukda mil sakta hai?

8. He's found another job.
   - Use ek aur job mil gayi.

9. Try another method.
   - Koi aur tarika try karo.

10. Another five minutes, please.
    - Paanch minute aur, please.

11. She moved to another city.
    - Wo doosre sheher chali gayi.

12. Another thing I want to mention...
    - Ek aur baat batani hai...

13. Ask another person.
    - Kisi aur se poocho.

14. I need another opinion.
    - Mujhe aur raye chahiye.

15. One after another, they arrived.
    - Ek ke baad ek, sab aaye.

## OTHER vs ANOTHER - Difference

| OTHER | ANOTHER |
|-------|---------|
| With plural nouns | With singular nouns |
| The other books | Another book |
| Specific different one | Any additional one |
| "The other" = definite | "Another" = indefinite |

**Examples:**

1. I have other options. (plural)
   - Mere paas aur options hain.

2. I need another option. (singular)
   - Mujhe ek aur option chahiye.

3. The other students left. (specific group)
   - Doosre students chale gaye.

4. Another student came. (one more)
   - Ek aur student aaya.

5. Where are the other files? (specific files)
   - Doosri files kahaan hain?

## Common Expressions

| Expression | Hindi | Meaning |
|------------|-------|---------|
| Such as | jaise ki | For example |
| As such | is tarah se | In that capacity |
| The same to you | Aapko bhi | Return the wish |
| All the same | Phir bhi | Nevertheless |
| One after another | Ek ke baad ek | In sequence |
| Each other | Ek doosre ko | Mutual |
| One another | Ek doosre ko | Mutual (3+) |
| Other than | Iske alaawa | Except |
| The other day | Kuch din pehle | Recently |
| On the other hand | Doosri taraf | Alternatively |

**Sentences:**

1. Fruits such as apples and oranges are healthy.
   - Phal jaise seb aur santra healthy hain.

2. Happy New Year! - The same to you!
   - Naya saal mubarak! - Aapko bhi!

3. We love each other.
   - Hum ek doosre se pyaar karte hain.

4. Other than this, I have nothing.
   - Iske alaawa mere paas kuch nahi.

5. I saw her the other day.
   - Maine use kuch din pehle dekha.
