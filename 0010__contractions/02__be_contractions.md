# BE Contractions - am, is, are

> Contractions with the verb "to be" (am, is, are).
> Most common in everyday speech.

---

## Overview

| Full Form | Contraction | Hindi |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| I am | I'm | Main hoon |
| you are | you're | Tum ho |
| he is | he's | Wo hai |
| she is | she's | Wo hai |
| it is | it's | Ye hai |
| we are | we're | Hum hain |
| they are | they're | Wo hain |

---

## I'M (I am)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I'm fine, thank you.
   - Main theek hoon, shukriya.

2. I'm going to the market.
   - Main market ja raha hoon.

3. I'm tired today.
   - Main aaj thaka hua hoon.

4. I'm not sure about this.
   - Mujhe is baare mein pakka nahi pata.

5. I'm happy to help.
   - Main khushi se madad karunga.

6. I'm learning English.
   - Main English seekh raha hoon.

7. I'm from India.
   - Main India se hoon.

8. I'm busy right now.
   - Main abhi busy hoon.

9. I'm sorry for the delay.
   - Der ke liye maafi chahta hoon.

10. I'm looking for a job.
    - Main job dhundh raha hoon.

1. You're welcome.

   - Tum sabse acche ho.

4. You're late again.
   - Tum phir se late ho.

5. You're not listening.
   - Tum sun nahi rahe.

   - Tum mere sabse acche dost ho.

7. You're right about that.
   - Tum sahi keh rahe ho.

8. You're always so kind.
   - Tum hamesha itne dayalu ho.

9. You're making a mistake.
   - Tum galti kar rahe ho.

10. You're invited to the party.
    - Tumhe party mein bulaya hai.

## Negative BE Contractions

| Full Form | Contraction |
|-----------|-------------|
| is not | isn't |
| are not | aren't |
| was not | wasn't |
| were not | weren't |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. He isn't here right now.
   - Wo abhi yahaan nahi hai.

2. They aren't coming today.
   - Wo aaj nahi aa rahe.

3. It wasn't my idea.
   - Ye mera idea nahi tha.

4. We weren't informed.
   - Humein bataya nahi gaya.

5. She isn't feeling well.
   - Uski tabiyat theek nahi.

## Common Confusion: IT'S vs ITS

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **It's** | It is / It has | It's raining. |
| **Its** | Possessive (uska) | The dog wagged its tail. |

**Remember:** It's = It is (contraction has apostrophe)

---

## Common Confusion: YOU'RE vs YOUR

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **You're** | You are | You're smart. |
| **Your** | Possessive (tumhara) | Your book is here. |

---

## Common Confusion: THEY'RE vs THEIR vs THERE

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **They're** | They are | They're coming. |
| **Their** | Possessive (unka) | Their house is big. |
| **There** | Place (wahaan) | Go there. |
