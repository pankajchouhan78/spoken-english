## DON'T / DOESN'T / DIDN'T

### DON'T (Do not)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I don't know.
   - Mujhe nahi pata.

2. I don't understand.
   - Mujhe samajh nahi aa raha.

3. I don't like coffee.
   - Mujhe coffee pasand nahi.

4. Don't worry about it.
   - Iske baare mein chinta mat karo.

5. I don't think so.
   - Mujhe nahi lagta.

6. We don't have time.
   - Humare paas time nahi.

7. They don't live here.
   - Wo yahaan nahi rehte.

8. I don't want to go.
   - Main jaana nahi chahta.

9. Don't tell anyone.
   - Kisi ko mat batana.

10. I don't remember.
    - Mujhe yaad nahi.

---

### DOESN'T (Does not)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. He doesn't work here.
   - Wo yahaan kaam nahi karta.

2. She doesn't like spicy food.
   - Use teekha khana pasand nahi.

3. It doesn't matter.
   - Koi farak nahi padta.

4. He doesn't know the answer.
   - Use jawab nahi pata.

5. She doesn't speak English.
   - Wo English nahi bolti.

6. It doesn't work.
   - Ye kaam nahi kar raha.

7. He doesn't eat meat.
   - Wo meat nahi khata.

8. She doesn't live far.
   - Wo door nahi rehti.

9. It doesn't make sense.
   - Iska koi matlab nahi.

10. He doesn't listen to anyone.
    - Wo kisi ki nahi sunta.

---

### DIDN'T (Did not)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I didn't know that.
   - Mujhe ye nahi pata tha.

2. She didn't come yesterday.
   - Wo kal nahi aayi.

3. We didn't go anywhere.
   - Hum kahin nahi gaye.

4. He didn't call me.
   - Usne mujhe call nahi kiya.

5. I didn't see it.
   - Maine nahi dekha.

6. They didn't understand.
   - Wo nahi samjhe.

7. She didn't say anything.
   - Usne kuch nahi kaha.

8. I didn't mean to hurt you.
   - Maine tumhe hurt karne ka matlab nahi tha.

9. We didn't expect this.
   - Humne ye expect nahi kiya tha.

10. He didn't finish the work.
    - Usne kaam khatam nahi kiya.

## CAN'T / COULDN'T

### CAN'T (Cannot)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I can't hear you.
   - Mujhe sun nahi raha.

2. I can't wait anymore.
   - Main aur wait nahi kar sakta.

3. She can't come today.
   - Wo aaj nahi aa sakti.

4. I can't believe it.
   - Mujhe yakeen nahi ho raha.

5. He can't swim.
   - Wo tair nahi sakta.

6. We can't afford this.
   - Hum ye afford nahi kar sakte.

7. I can't find my keys.
   - Mujhe chaabi nahi mil rahi.

8. You can't be serious.
   - Tum serious nahi ho sakte.

9. I can't remember his name.
   - Mujhe uska naam yaad nahi.

10. She can't drive at night.
    - Wo raat ko drive nahi kar sakti.

---

### COULDN'T (Could not)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I couldn't sleep last night.
   - Kal raat neend nahi aayi.

2. She couldn't find the way.
   - Use raasta nahi mila.

3. We couldn't understand him.
   - Hum use samajh nahi paye.

4. He couldn't come due to work.
   - Wo kaam ki wajah se nahi aa saka.

5. I couldn't believe my eyes.
   - Mujhe apni aankhon par yakeen nahi hua.

6. They couldn't finish on time.
   - Wo time par khatam nahi kar paye.

7. I couldn't help laughing.
   - Main hasi rok nahi paya.

8. She couldn't speak properly.
   - Wo theek se bol nahi payi.

9. We couldn't afford it then.
   - Tab hum afford nahi kar sakte the.

10. He couldn't remember anything.
    - Use kuch yaad nahi tha.

## SHOULDN'T / WOULDN'T / MUSTN'T

### SHOULDN'T (Should not)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. You shouldn't eat so much.
   - Tumhe itna nahi khana chahiye.

2. He shouldn't be late.
   - Use late nahi hona chahiye.

3. We shouldn't waste time.
   - Humein time waste nahi karna chahiye.

4. She shouldn't worry.
   - Use chinta nahi karni chahiye.

5. You shouldn't talk like that.
   - Tumhe aise nahi bolna chahiye.

---

### WOULDN'T (Would not)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I wouldn't do that if I were you.
   - Tumhari jagah hota to aisa nahi karta.

2. He wouldn't listen to me.
   - Wo meri nahi sunta.

3. She wouldn't tell anyone.
   - Wo kisi ko nahi batayegi.

4. I wouldn't worry about it.
   - Main iske baare mein chinta nahi karta.

5. They wouldn't agree.
   - Wo nahi maante.

---

### MUSTN'T (Must not)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. You mustn't be late.
   - Tumhe late nahi hona chahiye.

2. You mustn't tell anyone.
   - Tum kisi ko mat batana.

3. We mustn't forget this.
   - Humein ye bhoolna nahi chahiye.

4. You mustn't give up.
   - Tum haar mat mano.

5. He mustn't know about this.
   - Use iske baare mein pata nahi chalna chahiye.
