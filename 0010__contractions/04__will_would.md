# WILL & WOULD Contractions

> Contractions with "will" and "would".
> Used for future tense and conditional sentences.

---

## Overview

| Full Form | Contraction | Use |
|-----------|-------------|-----|
| I will | I'll | Future |
| you will | you'll | Future |
| he will | he'll | Future |
| she will | she'll | Future |
| it will | it'll | Future |
| we will | we'll | Future |
| they will | they'll | Future |
| I would | I'd | Conditional/Polite |
| you would | you'd | Conditional/Polite |

---

## I'LL (I will)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I'll call you later.
   - Main tumhe baad mein call karunga.

2. I'll be there in 10 minutes.
   - Main 10 minute mein wahaan hounga.

3. I'll help you with that.
   - Main tumhari madad karunga.

4. I'll see you tomorrow.
   - Kal milte hain.

5. I'll think about it.
   - Main sochta hoon.

6. I'll do my best.
   - Main poori koshish karunga.

7. I'll let you know.
   - Main tumhe bata dunga.

8. I'll take care of it.
   - Main iska dhyan rakhunga.

9. I'll be right back.
   - Main abhi aata hoon.

10. I'll never forget this.
    - Main ye kabhi nahi bhulunga.

## I'D / YOU'D / HE'D (I would / You would / He would)

> Used for polite requests, preferences, and conditionals.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I'd like a cup of tea.
   - Mujhe ek cup chai chahiye.

2. I'd prefer to stay home.
   - Main ghar rehna pasand karunga.

3. I'd love to come.
   - Main zaroor aaunga.

4. You'd better leave now.
   - Tumhe abhi nikalna chahiye.

5. He'd rather not go.
   - Wo jaana nahi chahega.

6. She'd be happy to help.
   - Wo khushi se madad karegi.

7. I'd appreciate your help.
   - Tumhari madad ki kadr karunga.

8. You'd enjoy this book.
   - Tumhe ye kitaab pasand aayegi.

9. We'd like to book a table.
   - Hum table book karna chahte hain.

10. They'd understand if you explained.
    - Agar tum samjhao to wo samjhenge.

## Negative Contractions

| Full Form | Contraction |
|-----------|-------------|
| will not | won't |
| would not | wouldn't |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I won't forget this.
   - Main ye nahi bhulunga.

2. She won't come today.
   - Wo aaj nahi aayegi.

3. It won't take long.
   - Zyada time nahi lagega.

4. They won't agree.
   - Wo nahi maanenge.

5. I wouldn't do that.
   - Main aisa nahi karta.

6. He wouldn't listen.
   - Wo nahi sunta.

7. We won't be late.
   - Humein der nahi hogi.

8. She wouldn't tell anyone.
   - Wo kisi ko nahi batayegi.

9. I won't let you down.
   - Main tumhe nirash nahi karunga.

10. They wouldn't understand.
    - Wo nahi samjhenge.

## WON'T - Special Note

> "Won't" is irregular!
> Will + not = Won't (not "willn't")

**Why?** Historical reasons - "wol not" → "won't"

**Common Uses:**

1. I won't give up.
   - Main haar nahi manunga.

2. He won't listen to anyone.
   - Wo kisi ki nahi sunta.

3. It won't work.
   - Ye kaam nahi karega.

4. We won't be there.
   - Hum wahaan nahi honge.

5. She won't stop trying.
   - Wo koshish karna nahi chhodegi.

---

## 'D Reminder: WOULD vs HAD

| Context | Meaning | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| + V1 (base verb) | would | I'd go if I could. |
| + V3 (past participle) | had | I'd already gone. |
| + like/love/prefer/rather | would | I'd like coffee. |
