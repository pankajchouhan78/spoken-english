# Question Words & Other Contractions

> Contractions with question words, "let's", and other common words.
> Very common in everyday speech.

---

## Question Word Contractions

| Full Form | Contraction | Hindi |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| what is | what's | Kya hai |
| what has | what's | Kya hua |
| who is | who's | Kaun hai |
| who has | who's | Kisne |
| where is | where's | Kahaan hai |
| when is | when's | Kab hai |
| how is | how's | Kaisa hai |
| why is | why's | Kyun hai |

## WHAT'S (What is / What has)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. What's your name?
   - Tumhara naam kya hai?

2. What's the time?
   - Kya time hua?

3. What's happening?
   - Kya ho raha hai?

4. What's the problem?
   - Problem kya hai?

5. What's for dinner?
   - Dinner mein kya hai?

6. What's that noise?
   - Ye awaaz kya hai?

7. What's the matter?
   - Kya baat hai?

8. What's going on?
   - Kya chal raha hai?

9. What's the weather like?
   - Mausam kaisa hai?

10. What's happened? (what has)
    - Kya hua?

## WHO'S vs WHOSE

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Who's** | Who is / Who has | Who's there? |
| **Whose** | Possessive (kiska) | Whose book is this? |

**Examples:**

1. Who's calling? (Who is)
   - Kaun call kar raha hai?

2. Whose phone is ringing? (Possessive)
   - Kiska phone baj raha hai?

3. Who's coming tonight? (Who is)
   - Aaj raat kaun aa raha hai?

4. Whose turn is it? (Possessive)
   - Kiski baari hai?

---

| Word | Meaning | Example |
