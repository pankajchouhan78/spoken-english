# Contractions - Introduction & Types

> Contractions are SHORTENED forms of words.
> Two words joined with an APOSTROPHE (').
> Very common in spoken English and informal writing.

---

## What are Contractions?

| Full Form | Contraction | Hindi Meaning |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| I am | I'm | Main hoon |
| do not | don't | Nahi karta |
| will not | won't | Nahi karega |
| he is / he has | he's | Wo hai / Uske paas hai |

## Types of Contractions

| Full Form | Contraction |
|-----------|-------------|
| I am | I'm |
| you are | you're |
| he is | he's |
| she is | she's |
| it is | it's |
| we are | we're |
| they are | they're |

|-----------|-------------|

## Apostrophe Rule

> The apostrophe (') replaces the MISSING letters.

| Full | Contraction | Missing |
|------|-------------|---------|
| can**no**t | can't | n, o |
| do **no**t | don't | o |
| I a**m** | I'm | a |
| I wi**ll** | I'll | w, i |
| wi**ll** not | won't | (irregular) |

## When to Use Contractions

| Use ✅ | Avoid ❌ |
|--------|----------|
| Spoken English | Formal writing |
| Casual texts/emails | Academic papers |
| Friendly conversations | Business letters |
| Informal writing | Legal documents |

---

## Confusing Contractions

| Contraction | Meaning 1 | Meaning 2 |
|-------------|-----------|-----------|
| he's | he is | he has |
| she's | she is | she has |
| it's | it is | it has |
| I'd | I would | I had |
| you'd | you would | you had |
| they'd | they would | they had |

**Context tells the meaning:**
- He's tall. (he is)
- He's gone. (he has)
- I'd like tea. (I would)
- I'd finished. (I had)

---

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Reason |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Your late. | You're late. | You're = you are |
| Its raining. | It's raining. | It's = it is |
| Whose coming? | Who's coming? | Who's = who is |
| Their happy. | They're happy. | They're = they are |
