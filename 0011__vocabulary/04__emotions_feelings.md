## Using Emotions in Sentences

### I feel...

| Sentence | Hindi |
|----------|-------|
| I feel happy today. | Aaj mujhe khushi ho rahi hai. |
| I feel sad about this. | Is baare mein dukh ho raha hai. |
| I feel angry at him. | Mujhe us par gussa aa raha hai. |
| I feel scared of the dark. | Mujhe andhera se darr lagta hai. |
| I feel tired after work. | Kaam ke baad thakan lagti hai. |

### I'm feeling...

| Sentence | Hindi |
|----------|-------|
| I'm feeling excited. | Main excited feel kar raha hoon. |
| I'm feeling nervous. | Mujhe ghabrahat ho rahi hai. |
| I'm feeling better now. | Ab better feel ho raha hai. |
| I'm not feeling well. | Tabiyat theek nahi. |

### That makes me feel...

| Sentence | Hindi |
|----------|-------|
| That makes me feel happy. | Isse mujhe khushi hoti hai. |
| That makes me feel uncomfortable. | Isse ajeeb lagta hai. |
| That makes me feel proud. | Isse garv hota hai. |
