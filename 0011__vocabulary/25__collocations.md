# Collocations

> Words that naturally go together in English.
> Learning collocations makes you sound more natural.

---

## What are Collocations?

**Wrong:** Make homework ❌
**Right:** Do homework ✅

**Wrong:** Strong rain ❌
**Right:** Heavy rain ✅

> Native speakers use these word combinations naturally!
