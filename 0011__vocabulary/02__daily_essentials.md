# Daily Essential Words

> Most commonly used words in everyday English.
> Learn these FIRST for basic communication.

---

## Basic Pronouns

| English | Hindi | Example |
|---------|-------|---------|
| I | Main | I am happy. |
| You | Tum/Aap | You are smart. |
| He | Wo (male) | He is my friend. |
| She | Wo (female) | She is a teacher. |
| It | Ye/Wo (thing) | It is a book. |
| We | Hum | We are ready. |
| They | Wo (plural) | They are coming. |
| This | Ye | This is mine. |
| That | Wo | That is yours. |
