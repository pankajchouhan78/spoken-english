# Confusing Words

> Words that look or sound similar but have different meanings.
> Master these to avoid common mistakes.

---

## Their / There / They're

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| Their | Possessive | Unka | Their house is big. |
| There | Place | Wahaan | Go there. |
| They're | Contraction | Wo hain | They're coming. |

**Memory Tip:** 
- Their = ownership (heir = inheritor)
- There = place (here/there)
- They're = they are

---

## Your / You're

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| Your | Possessive | Tumhara | Your book is here. |
| You're | Contraction | Tum ho | You're smart. |

**Memory Tip:** You're = You are (replace and check)

---

## Its / It's

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| Its | Possessive | Uska | The dog wagged its tail. |
| It's | Contraction | Ye hai | It's raining. |

**Memory Tip:** It's = It is or It has

---

## Then / Than

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| Then | Time | Phir/Tab | Then we went home. |
| Than | Comparison | Se | He's taller than me. |

**Examples:**
- I ate, **then** I slept. (time order)
- He is better **than** me. (comparison)

---

## Affect / Effect

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| Affect | Verb | Asar dalna | The rain affected the match. |
| Effect | Noun | Asar | The effect was immediate. |

**Memory Tip:** 
- **A**ffect = **A**ction (verb)
- **E**ffect = **E**nd result (noun)

## Advice / Advise

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| Advice | Noun | Salah | Give me advice. |
| Advise | Verb | Salah dena | I advise you to study. |

**Memory Tip:** Advice (noun - like ice), Advise (verb - like rise)

## Lay / Lie

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| Lay | Rakhna (needs object) | Lay the book on the table. |
| Lie | Letna (no object) | Lie down on the bed. |

**Note:** Lay needs an object, Lie doesn't.

---

## Borrow / Lend

| Word | Type | Meaning | Example |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| Borrow | Verb | Udhar lena | Can I borrow your pen? |
| Lend | Verb | Udhar dena | Can you lend me money? |

**Memory Tip:** 
- Borrow = you receive
- Lend = you give

---

## Bring / Take

| Word | Direction | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Bring | Toward speaker | Bring it here. |
| Take | Away from speaker | Take it there. |

---

## Say / Tell

| Word | Usage | Example |
|------|-------|---------|
| Say | + words (no person) | He said hello. |
| Tell | + person | He told me hello. |

**Correct:**
- He **said** "I'm coming."
- He **told** me that he's coming.

## Learn / Teach

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| Learn | Seekhna | I'm learning English. |
| Teach | Padhana | She teaches English. |

**Correct:**
- He **taught** me (NOT learned me).
- I **learned** from him.
