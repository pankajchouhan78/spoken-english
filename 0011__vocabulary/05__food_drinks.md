### Ordering Phrases

| English | Hindi |
|---------|-------|
| I'd like to order... | Mujhe ... chahiye |
| Can I have...? | Kya mujhe ... mil sakta hai? |
| What do you recommend? | Aap kya recommend karenge? |
| Is this spicy? | Ye teekha hai? |
| Less spicy, please. | Kam teekha please. |
| The bill, please. | Bill please. |
| Keep the change. | Chutte rakh lo. |
