# Vocabulary - Introduction & Learning Tips

> Building vocabulary is KEY to fluency in English.
> Learn words in CONTEXT, not in isolation.

---

## Why Vocabulary Matters

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Better Speaking | More words = More expression |
| Better Listening | Understand more conversations |
| Better Reading | Comprehend books, articles easily |
| Better Writing | Express ideas clearly |
| Confidence | Speak without hesitation |

---

## How to Learn Vocabulary Effectively

### 1. Learn in Context
> Don't just memorize words. Learn how they're used.

| Word | Context | Better Understanding |
|------|---------|---------------------|
| "Upset" | "I'm upset about the news" | Emotional disturbance |
| "Upset" | "He upset the glass" | Knocked over |

### 2. Group Related Words
> Learn words in categories/themes.

**Example: Kitchen**
- Stove, pan, spatula, ladle, pot, lid

**Example: Emotions**
- Happy, sad, angry, excited, nervous, calm

### 3. Use Word Families

| Root Word | Noun | Verb | Adjective | Adverb |
|-----------|------|------|-----------|--------|
| Create | Creation | Create | Creative | Creatively |
| Success | Success | Succeed | Successful | Successfully |
| Beauty | Beauty | Beautify | Beautiful | Beautifully |

### 4. Learn Collocations
> Words that go TOGETHER naturally.

| Verb | + Noun |
|------|--------|
| Make | a decision, a mistake, progress |
| Do | homework, the dishes, your best |
| Take | a break, a photo, a chance |
| Have | a meeting, lunch, a conversation |

### 5. Practice Daily
- Learn 5-10 new words daily
- Review previous words
- Use new words in sentences
- Speak them out loud

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake ❌ | Better Approach ✅ |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Learning random words | Learn by themes |
| Only reading | Speaking + Writing |
| Ignoring pronunciation | Learn pronunciation too |
| Not reviewing | Review every week |
| Learning alone | Practice with someone |
