# Question Words (WH Words)

> Words used to ask questions in English.
> Master these to ask any question!

---

## Basic Question Words

| Word | Hindi | Asks About |
|------|-------|------------|
| What | Kya | Thing/Action |
| Who | Kaun | Person |
| Where | Kahaan | Place |
| When | Kab | Time |
| Why | Kyun | Reason |
| How | Kaise | Manner/Way |
| Which | Kaunsa | Choice |
| Whose | Kiska | Possession |
| Whom | Kisko | Object person |
