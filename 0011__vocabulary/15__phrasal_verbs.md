# Phrasal Verbs

> Verb + Preposition/Adverb = New Meaning
> Very common in everyday English speech.

---

## What are Phrasal Verbs?

**Definition:** A verb combined with a preposition or adverb that creates a new meaning different from the original verb.

| Verb | + Particle | = Phrasal Verb | Meaning |
|------|------------|----------------|---------|
| Look | + up | Look up | Search/Dhundhna |
| Give | + up | Give up | Quit/Chhod dena |
| Take | + off | Take off | Remove/Utarna |
