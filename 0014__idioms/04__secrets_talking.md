# Idioms - Secrets & Talking

> Idioms related to secrets, conversations, and communication.

---

## 1. Spill the beans

**Meaning:** Secret bata dena / Raaz khol dena.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Who told her about the plan? | Someone must have spilled the beans. |
| Why is she upset? | He spilled the beans about her surprise party. |
| Can you keep a secret? | Don't worry. I won't spill the beans. |

---

## 2. Let the cat out of the bag

**Meaning:** Galti se ya jaldi secret bata dena.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Who told them about the surprise? | Someone let the cat out of the bag. |
| Why was the surprise ruined? | Someone let the cat out of the bag. |
| Did you tell anyone? | No! I didn't let the cat out of the bag. |

---

## 3. Break the ice

**Meaning:** Awkwardness door karna / Baat shuru karna.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| How did you start talking to her? | I told a joke to break the ice. |
| The meeting felt awkward at first. | Yeah, but his funny story broke the ice. |
| How do you handle new clients? | I try to break the ice with small talk. |

---

## 4. Pull someone's leg

**Meaning:** Masti mein mazaak karna ya chhedna.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Were you serious? | No, I was just pulling your leg. |
| Did he really say that? | No, he was just pulling my leg. |
| Are you telling the truth? | Relax! I'm just pulling your leg. |

---

## 5. Speak of the devil

**Meaning:** Jis ki baat kar rahe the woh saamne aa gaya.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Oh look, there's Rahul! | Speak of the devil! We were just talking about you. |
| Is that Priya at the door? | Speak of the devil! |
| Hey, you're here! | Speak of the devil – we just mentioned your name. |

---

## 6. Under the table

**Meaning:** Chhupke ya illegal tareeke se paisa dena ya lena.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| How did he get the contract? | He paid under the table. |
| Why was it so easy? | They took money under the table. |
| Is it legal? | No, it was all under the table. |
