# Idioms - Health & Feelings

> Idioms related to health, emotions, and feelings.

---

## 1. Under the weather

**Meaning:** Thoda bimaar ya weak feel karna.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Why didn't you come? | I was feeling under the weather. |
| How is she today? | She's a bit under the weather. |
| Are you okay? | I'm just under the weather. |

---

## 2. On cloud nine

**Meaning:** Bahut khush hona ya khushi se jhoom uthna.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| How did he react to the news? | He was on cloud nine. |
| How's she feeling today? | She's on cloud nine after the promotion. |
| Why are they celebrating? | They're on cloud nine because they won. |

---

## 3. In hot water

**Meaning:** Musibat mein hona ya dikkat mein hona.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Why is he so worried? | He's in hot water with his boss. |
| What happened after that? | She got in hot water for missing the deadline. |
| Is she safe? | She was in hot water but it's fine now. |

---

## 4. In the same boat

**Meaning:** Ek jaise mushkil mein hona ya same problem mein hona.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Are you worried too? | Yes, we're all in the same boat. |
| Why did you help them? | We're in the same boat. |
| Are you affected as well? | Yes, I'm in the same boat. |

---

## 5. Fond of

**Meaning:** Kisi cheez ya insaan ko bahut pasand karna.

**Examples:**
- I'm fond of music.
- She's fond of reading novels.
- He's very fond of his dog.
- They're fond of travelling.

---

## 6. Cry over spilt milk

**Meaning:** Jo ho gaya uske liye bekar mein pareshaan hona.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Are you still upset about it? | No point crying over spilt milk. |
| Why is she sad about that loss? | She's just crying over spilt milk. |
| Should we worry about it now? | No, it's just crying over spilt milk. |

---

## 7. A blessing in disguise

**Meaning:** Pehle bura lage par baad mein achha ho jaye.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| How was losing that job? | It was a blessing in disguise. |
| Why did you move cities? | It turned out to be a blessing in disguise. |
| Was the failure helpful? | Yes, it was a blessing in disguise. |
