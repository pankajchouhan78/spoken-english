# Idioms - Money & Success

> Idioms related to money, cost, success, and progress.

---

## 1. Cost an arm and a leg

**Meaning:** Bahut mehenga hona.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| How much was the new car? | It cost an arm and a leg. |
| Why didn't you buy it? | It costs an arm and a leg. |
| Is that hotel cheap? | No, it costs an arm and a leg. |

---

## 2. Break the bank

**Meaning:** Bahut mehenga hona ya zyada paisa lagna.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new phone break the bank? | No, it was affordable. |
| Can we afford this trip? | Don't worry, it won't break the bank. |
| Why didn't you buy it? | It would have broken the bank. |

---

## 3. Born with a silver spoon

**Meaning:** Ameer ghar mein paida hona / Privilege ke saath.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Why doesn't he worry about money? | He was born with a silver spoon. |
| How did he afford such a big house? | He was born with a silver spoon. |
| Is he self-made? | No, he was born with a silver spoon. |

---

## 4. Going great guns

**Meaning:** Bohot badhiya chal raha hai / Full speed mein progress.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| How's the project coming along? | It's going great guns! We're ahead of schedule. |
| How's your career going? | It's going great guns! I got a promotion. |
| How's the business doing? | It's going great guns! Sales increased by 30%. |

---

## 5. A piece of cake

**Meaning:** Bilkul aasaan kaam.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Was the exam difficult? | No, it was a piece of cake! |
| How was the interview? | It was a piece of cake. |
| Can you solve this problem? | Sure! It's a piece of cake for me. |

---

## 6. It's a matter of...

**Meaning:** Yeh baat hai... ki / Yeh mamla hai.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Why didn't he agree? | It's a matter of trust. |
| Why are they arguing? | It's a matter of money. |
| Will this take a lot of time? | No, it's just a matter of five minutes. |
| Will he succeed? | Yes, it's just a matter of time. |

---

## 7. Hit the nail on the head

**Meaning:** Bilkul sahi point bolna.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| What did you think of his answer? | He really hit the nail on the head. |
| How did she explain the problem? | She hit the nail on the head. |
| Was his suggestion helpful? | Yes, it hit the nail on the head. |

---

## 8. Back to square one

**Meaning:** Shuruwat se dobara start karna, pehle wala kaam fail hone ke baad.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the project work out? | No, we're back to square one. |
| What happened to the deal? | It fell through, so we're back to square one. |
| Why are you starting again? | Because we're back to square one. |
