# Idioms - Work & Time

> Idioms related to work, effort, time, and daily activities.

---

## 1. Burn the midnight oil

**Meaning:** Raat ko der tak kaam karna ya padhna.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| How did you finish the report so fast? | I burned the midnight oil last night. |
| Why are you so tired? | I was burning the midnight oil to study. |
| How did she get such good marks? | She burned the midnight oil before exams. |

---

## 2. Call it a day

**Meaning:** Kaam bandh karna ya din ka kaam khatam samajhna.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Are you still working? | No, I'm going to call it a day. |
| Should we continue? | Let's call it a day and finish tomorrow. |
| Why are you leaving? | I think it's time to call it a day. |

---

## 3. Hit the sack

**Meaning:** Sone jaana ya bed par jaana.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Why did you leave early? | I was tired and wanted to hit the sack. |
| What are you doing now? | I'm going to hit the sack. |
| Did you stay up late? | No, I hit the sack early. |

---

## 4. Once in a blue moon

**Meaning:** Bahut hi kam ya kabhi-kabhi.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Do you see him often? | No, only once in a blue moon. |
| How often do you eat out? | Once in a blue moon. |
| Do they visit you regularly? | No, it's once in a blue moon. |

---

## 5. Better late than never

**Meaning:** Der se karna, na karne se behtar hai.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Sorry I'm late with the gift. | That's okay, better late than never! |
| He finally apologized after 5 years. | Well, better late than never. |
| Should I still submit my report? | Yes! Better late than never. |
| You finally came to the party! | Better late than never! |

---

## 6. The ball is in your court

**Meaning:** Ab faisla tumhare haath mein hai.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Should we wait for them? | No, the ball is in their court. |
| Will you accept the offer? | The ball is in my court now. |
| What happens next? | The ball is in your court. |

---

## 7. Bite the bullet

**Meaning:** Mushkil cheez ko himmat se karna ya sehna.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Why did you finally tell him? | I had to bite the bullet and be honest. |
| How did she handle the surgery? | She just bit the bullet and went through with it. |
| Did you talk to the boss? | Yes, I bit the bullet and told him the truth. |
