# Adverbs - Introduction & Types

> Adverbs modify VERBS, ADJECTIVES, or OTHER ADVERBS.
> They tell us HOW, WHEN, WHERE, HOW OFTEN, or HOW MUCH.

---

## What is an Adverb?

| Modifies | Example |
|----------|---------|
| Verb | She runs **quickly**. |
| Adjective | He is **very** tall. |
| Adverb | She runs **very** quickly. |

---

## Types of Adverbs

| Type | Question | Examples |
|------|----------|----------|
| Manner | How? | slowly, quickly, carefully |
| Time | When? | now, today, yesterday, soon |
| Place | Where? | here, there, everywhere |
| Frequency | How often? | always, never, sometimes |
| Degree | How much? | very, too, enough, quite |

---

## 1. Adverbs of Manner (How?)

> Describe HOW an action is performed.

| Adverb | Hindi | Example |
|--------|-------|---------|
| slowly | dheere se | Walk slowly. |
| quickly | jaldi se | Come quickly. |
| carefully | dhyan se | Drive carefully. |
| loudly | zor se | Speak loudly. |
| quietly | chupchap | Sit quietly. |
| politely | vinay se | Ask politely. |
| beautifully | sundar tarike se | She dances beautifully. |
| badly | buri tarah | He failed badly. |
| well | acchi tarah | She speaks well. |
| hard | mehnat se | Work hard. |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Please speak slowly.
   - Dheere bolo please.

2. He ran quickly to catch the bus.
   - Wo bus pakadne ke liye jaldi bhaaga.

3. Drive carefully on wet roads.
   - Geeli sadakon par dhyan se chalao.

4. Don't talk loudly in the library.
   - Library mein zor se mat bolo.

5. The baby is sleeping, walk quietly.
   - Bachcha so raha hai, chupchap chalo.

6. Always ask politely.
   - Hamesha vinay se poocho.

7. She sings beautifully.
   - Wo sundar gaati hai.

8. He performed badly in the exam.
   - Wo exam mein bura kiya.

9. She speaks English well.
   - Wo English acchi bolti hai.

10. You must work hard to succeed.
    - Safal hone ke liye mehnat karni padegi.

## 2. Adverbs of Time (When?)

> Tell us WHEN something happens.

| Adverb | Hindi | Example |
|--------|-------|---------|
| now | abhi | Come now. |
| today | aaj | I'm busy today. |
| tomorrow | kal | See you tomorrow. |
| yesterday | kal (beeta) | I met him yesterday. |
| soon | jaldi | I'll be there soon. |
| later | baad mein | Call me later. |
| already | pehle se | I already ate. |
| still | abhi bhi | He's still working. |
| yet | abhi tak | Not finished yet. |
| just | abhi abhi | I just arrived. |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I need to leave now.
   - Mujhe abhi jaana hai.

2. What are you doing today?
   - Aaj kya kar rahe ho?

3. I'll come tomorrow.
   - Main kal aaunga.

4. I saw her yesterday.
   - Maine use kal dekha.

5. The train will arrive soon.
   - Train jaldi aayegi.

6. Let's discuss this later.
   - Iske baare mein baad mein baat karte hain.

7. I have already finished my work.
   - Main pehle hi apna kaam khatam kar chuka hoon.

8. He is still sleeping.
   - Wo abhi bhi so raha hai.

9. The food is not ready yet.
   - Khana abhi taiyaar nahi hua.

10. I just woke up.
    - Main abhi abhi utha.

## 3. Adverbs of Place (Where?)

> Tell us WHERE something happens.

| Adverb | Hindi | Example |
|--------|-------|---------|
| here | yahaan | Come here. |
| there | wahaan | Go there. |
| everywhere | har jagah | I searched everywhere. |
| nowhere | kahin nahi | He went nowhere. |
| somewhere | kahin | Let's go somewhere. |
| anywhere | kahin bhi | Sit anywhere. |
| inside | andar | Come inside. |
| outside | bahar | Wait outside. |
| upstairs | upar | Go upstairs. |
| downstairs | neeche | Come downstairs. |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Please come here.
   - Yahaan aao please.

2. Put the bag there.
   - Bag wahaan rakho.

3. I looked everywhere for my keys.
   - Maine chaabi har jagah dhundhi.

4. He went nowhere yesterday.
   - Wo kal kahin nahi gaya.

5. Let's go somewhere tonight.
   - Aaj raat kahin chalte hain.

6. You can sit anywhere.
   - Tum kahin bhi baith sakte ho.

7. It's cold outside, come inside.
   - Bahar thandi hai, andar aao.

8. Wait outside for a minute.
   - Ek minute bahar ruko.

9. The bedroom is upstairs.
   - Bedroom upar hai.

10. The kitchen is downstairs.
    - Kitchen neeche hai.

## 4. Adverbs of Frequency (How Often?)

> Tell us HOW OFTEN something happens.

| Adverb | Hindi | Frequency |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| always | hamesha | 100% |
| usually | aam taur par | 80% |
| often | aksar | 70% |
| sometimes | kabhi kabhi | 50% |
| occasionally | kabhi kabhi | 30% |
| rarely | bahut kam | 10% |
| seldom | kam hi | 10% |
| never | kabhi nahi | 0% |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I always wake up early.
   - Main hamesha jaldi uthta hoon.

2. She usually walks to work.
   - Wo aam taur par kaam par paidal jaati hai.

3. We often eat out on weekends.
   - Hum aksar weekend par bahar khate hain.

4. I sometimes watch TV at night.
   - Main kabhi kabhi raat ko TV dekhta hoon.

5. He occasionally visits his parents.
   - Wo kabhi kabhi apne parents se milne jaata hai.

6. She rarely gets angry.
   - Wo bahut kam gussa hoti hai.

7. He seldom makes mistakes.
   - Wo kam hi galti karta hai.

8. I never drink alcohol.
   - Main kabhi sharab nahi peeta.

9. She is always on time.
   - Wo hamesha time par hoti hai.

10. They never forget my birthday.
    - Wo mera birthday kabhi nahi bhoolte.

## 5. Adverbs of Degree (How Much?)

> Tell us the INTENSITY or DEGREE of something.

| Adverb | Hindi | Example |
|--------|-------|---------|
| very | bahut | Very good. |
| too | bahut zyada | Too hot. |
| quite | kaafi | Quite nice. |
| rather | kuch | Rather cold. |
| enough | kaafi | Good enough. |
| almost | lagbhag | Almost done. |
| completely | poori tarah | Completely wrong. |
| absolutely | bilkul | Absolutely right. |
| extremely | bahut zyada | Extremely tired. |
| hardly | mushkil se | Hardly any time. |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. This is very important.
   - Ye bahut zaroori hai.

2. The coffee is too hot.
   - Coffee bahut zyada garam hai.

3. The movie was quite interesting.
   - Film kaafi interesting thi.

4. It's rather cold today.
   - Aaj kuch thanda hai.

5. Is this big enough?
   - Kya ye kaafi bada hai?

6. I'm almost finished.
   - Main lagbhag khatam ho gaya.

7. You are completely wrong.
   - Tum poori tarah galat ho.

8. You are absolutely right.
   - Tum bilkul sahi ho.

9. I'm extremely tired today.
   - Main aaj bahut thaka hua hoon.

10. I can hardly hear you.
    - Main tumhe mushkil se sun pa raha hoon.

## Formation of Adverbs

### Rule 1: Adjective + -ly

| Adjective | Adverb |
|-----------|--------|
| slow | slowly |
| quick | quickly |
| careful | carefully |
| beautiful | beautifully |
| polite | politely |

### Rule 2: Adjectives ending in -y → -ily

| Adjective | Adverb |
|-----------|--------|
| happy | happily |
| easy | easily |
| angry | angrily |
| lucky | luckily |
| heavy | heavily |

### Rule 3: Adjectives ending in -le → -ly

| Adjective | Adverb |
|-----------|--------|
| simple | simply |
| gentle | gently |
| terrible | terribly |
| possible | possibly |

### Rule 4: Same form (No change)

| Adjective | Adverb |
|-----------|--------|
| fast | fast |
| hard | hard |
| late | late |
| early | early |
| daily | daily |

### Rule 5: Irregular Adverbs

| Adjective | Adverb |
|-----------|--------|
| good | well |
| bad | badly |

---

## Position of Adverbs

| Type | Position | Example |
|------|----------|---------|
| Manner | After verb/object | She sings **beautifully**. |
| Time | End of sentence | I'll come **tomorrow**. |
| Place | After verb | Come **here**. |
| Frequency | Before main verb | I **always** eat breakfast. |
| Degree | Before adjective/adverb | It's **very** hot. |
