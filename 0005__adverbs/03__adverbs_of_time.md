# Adverbs of Time - Daily Use

> Answer the question: WHEN does it happen?

---

## Now / Then / Soon / Later

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I need to leave now.
   - Mujhe abhi jaana hai.

2. Do it now, don't delay.
   - Abhi karo, der mat karo.

3. I was young then.
   - Main tab jawan tha.

4. We'll meet then.
   - Hum tab milenge.

5. The food will be ready soon.
   - Khana jaldi taiyaar ho jayega.

6. I'll call you soon.
   - Main tumhe jaldi call karunga.

7. Let's discuss this later.
   - Iske baare mein baad mein baat karte hain.

8. I'll finish it later.
   - Main ise baad mein khatam karunga.

9. Are you free now?
   - Kya tum abhi free ho?

10. Things were different then.
    - Tab cheezein alag thi.

11. See you soon!
    - Jaldi milte hain!

12. I'll reply later.
    - Main baad mein reply karunga.

13. What are you doing right now?
    - Abhi kya kar rahe ho?

14. We were just kids then.
    - Hum tab bachche the.

15. Don't worry, help will come soon.
    - Chinta mat karo, madad jaldi aayegi.

## Today / Yesterday / Tomorrow

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I'm very busy today.
   - Main aaj bahut busy hoon.

2. What are your plans for today?
   - Aaj ke kya plan hain?

3. I saw him yesterday.
   - Maine use kal dekha.

4. Yesterday was a holiday.
   - Kal chhuti thi.

5. I'll finish this tomorrow.
   - Main ye kal khatam karunga.

6. Tomorrow is my birthday.
   - Kal mera birthday hai.

7. The meeting is today at 3 PM.
   - Meeting aaj 3 baje hai.

8. I didn't go to work yesterday.
   - Main kal kaam par nahi gaya.

9. What will you do tomorrow?
   - Kal kya karoge?

10. Today is a beautiful day.
    - Aaj sundar din hai.

11. I was sick yesterday.
    - Main kal bimar tha.

12. We'll travel tomorrow morning.
    - Hum kal subah travel karenge.

13. Let's start today itself.
    - Aaj hi shuru karte hain.

14. Yesterday's match was exciting.
    - Kal ka match exciting tha.

15. Tomorrow never comes for lazy people.
    - Aalsi logon ke liye kal kabhi nahi aata.

## Already / Yet / Still / Just

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I have already eaten.
   - Main khana kha chuka hoon.

2. She has already left.
   - Wo ja chuki hai.

3. Have you finished yet?
   - Kya tumne abhi tak khatam kiya?

4. The train hasn't arrived yet.
   - Train abhi tak nahi aayi.

5. He is still sleeping.
   - Wo abhi bhi so raha hai.

6. Are you still working?
   - Kya tum abhi bhi kaam kar rahe ho?

7. I just woke up.
   - Main abhi abhi utha.

8. She just left five minutes ago.
   - Wo abhi paanch minute pehle gayi.

9. I already know the answer.
   - Main jawab pehle se jaanta hoon.

10. Is it not ready yet?
    - Kya abhi tak taiyaar nahi hua?

11. They are still waiting for you.
    - Wo abhi bhi tumhara intezaar kar rahe hain.

12. I just received your message.
    - Mujhe abhi abhi tumhara message mila.

13. We've already discussed this.
    - Humne ye pehle hi discuss kar liya.

14. The work is not done yet.
    - Kaam abhi tak nahi hua.

15. He still loves her.
    - Wo abhi bhi usse pyaar karta hai.

## Before / After / During

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Wash your hands before eating.
   - Khane se pehle haath dho.

2. I've seen this movie before.
   - Maine ye film pehle dekhi hai.

3. Call me after 6 PM.
   - 6 baje ke baad call karo.

4. I'll rest after work.
   - Kaam ke baad aaram karunga.

5. Don't talk during the meeting.
   - Meeting ke dauran baat mat karo.

6. He fell asleep during the lecture.
   - Wo lecture ke dauran so gaya.

7. Think before you speak.
   - Bolne se pehle socho.

8. What did you do before this job?
   - Is job se pehle kya karte the?

9. I feel fresh after a shower.
   - Nahane ke baad fresh feel hota hai.

10. Life was difficult before technology.
    - Technology se pehle zindagi mushkil thi.

11. We ate dinner after the movie.
    - Film ke baad humne khana khaya.

12. Don't use your phone during class.
    - Class ke dauran phone mat chalao.

13. I always exercise before breakfast.
    - Main hamesha breakfast se pehle exercise karta hoon.

14. She cried during the sad scene.
    - Wo sad scene ke dauran royi.

15. Study now, play after.
    - Abhi padho, baad mein khelo.

## Early / Late

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I wake up early every day.
   - Main roz jaldi uthta hoon.

2. Come early tomorrow.
   - Kal jaldi aana.

3. He always arrives late.
   - Wo hamesha late aata hai.

4. Don't be late for the interview.
   - Interview ke liye late mat hona.

5. The early bird catches the worm.
   - Jo jaldi uthta hai wo safal hota hai.

6. It's getting late, let's go.
   - Der ho rahi hai, chalo.

7. I reached the station early.
   - Main station jaldi pahunch gaya.

8. She came late to the party.
   - Wo party mein late aayi.

9. Sleep early, wake up early.
   - Jaldi so jao, jaldi utho.

10. Better late than never.
    - Der aaye durust aaye.

11. I finished my work early today.
    - Maine aaj kaam jaldi khatam kiya.

12. The train was 30 minutes late.
    - Train 30 minute late thi.

13. Start early to avoid traffic.
    - Traffic se bachne ke liye jaldi niklo.

14. It's too late to apologize.
    - Maafi maangne mein bahut der ho gayi.

15. He wakes up early to exercise.
    - Wo exercise ke liye jaldi uthta hai.
