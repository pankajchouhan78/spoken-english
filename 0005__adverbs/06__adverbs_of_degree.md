# Adverbs of Degree - Daily Use

> Answer the question: HOW MUCH or TO WHAT EXTENT?

---

## Very / Too / So

> ⚠️ Important Difference:
> - very = bahut (neutral)
> - too = bahut zyada (negative, excess)
> - so = itna (for emphasis)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. This is very important.
   - Ye bahut zaroori hai.

2. She is very beautiful.
   - Wo bahut sundar hai.

3. The tea is too hot.
   - Chai bahut zyada garam hai.

4. This bag is too heavy.
   - Ye bag bahut zyada bhaari hai.

5. I'm so tired today.
   - Main aaj itna thaka hoon.

6. She was so happy!
   - Wo itni khush thi!

7. He runs very fast.
   - Wo bahut tez daudta hai.

8. It's too late to go.
   - Jaane mein bahut der ho gayi.

9. I love you so much.
   - Main tumse itna pyaar karta hoon.

10. The movie was very interesting.
    - Film bahut interesting thi.

11. Don't eat too much.
    - Bahut zyada mat khao.

12. He is so kind to everyone.
    - Wo sabke saath itna accha hai.

13. I'm very happy with my job.
    - Main apni job se bahut khush hoon.

14. This is too difficult for me.
    - Ye mere liye bahut mushkil hai.

15. Why are you so quiet today?
    - Aaj tum itne chup kyun ho?

## Quite / Rather / Fairly

> These show MODERATE degree:
> - quite = kaafi (fairly positive)
> - rather = kuch (can be negative)
> - fairly = kaafi (more neutral)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. The food was quite good.
   - Khana kaafi accha tha.

2. She is quite intelligent.
   - Wo kaafi buddhiman hai.

3. It's rather cold today.
   - Aaj kuch thanda hai.

4. He was rather rude to me.
   - Wo mujhse kuch badtameez tha.

5. The movie was fairly interesting.
   - Film kaafi interesting thi.

6. This is fairly easy.
   - Ye kaafi aasaan hai.

7. I'm quite busy this week.
   - Main is hafte kaafi busy hoon.

8. The journey was rather long.
   - Safar kuch lamba tha.

9. She speaks English fairly well.
   - Wo kaafi acchi English bolti hai.

10. I quite like this place.
    - Mujhe ye jagah kaafi pasand hai.

11. The test was rather difficult.
    - Test kuch mushkil tha.

12. His performance was fairly good.
    - Uski performance kaafi acchi thi.

13. I'm quite sure about this.
    - Main iske baare mein kaafi sure hoon.

14. It was rather disappointing.
    - Ye kuch disappointing tha.

15. The restaurant is fairly cheap.
    - Restaurant kaafi sasta hai.

## Enough / Almost / Completely

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Is this big enough?
   - Kya ye kaafi bada hai?

2. I don't have enough time.
   - Mere paas kaafi time nahi hai.

3. I'm almost done.
   - Main lagbhag khatam ho gaya.

4. We're almost there.
   - Hum lagbhag pahunch gaye.

5. You're completely wrong.
   - Tum bilkul galat ho.

6. The work is completely finished.
   - Kaam poori tarah khatam hai.

7. Is the food hot enough?
   - Kya khana kaafi garam hai?

8. I've almost forgotten about it.
   - Main ise lagbhag bhool gaya.

9. I completely agree with you.
   - Main tumse poori tarah agree karta hoon.

10. Do you have enough money?
    - Kya tumhare paas kaafi paise hain?

11. The jar is almost empty.
    - Jar lagbhag khaali hai.

12. The project was completely successful.
    - Project poori tarah safal raha.

13. He's old enough to decide.
    - Wo faisla lene ke liye kaafi bada hai.

14. I'm almost ready.
    - Main lagbhag taiyaar hoon.

15. She completely ignored me.
    - Usne mujhe poori tarah ignore kiya.

## Extremely / Absolutely / Totally

> These show MAXIMUM degree:

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I'm extremely tired.
   - Main bahut zyada thaka hoon.

2. This is extremely important.
   - Ye bahut zyada zaroori hai.

3. You're absolutely right.
   - Tum bilkul sahi ho.

4. That's absolutely amazing!
   - Ye bilkul amazing hai!

5. I totally agree with you.
   - Main tumse poori tarah agree karta hoon.

6. He totally forgot about the meeting.
   - Wo meeting poori tarah bhool gaya.

7. The view is extremely beautiful.
   - Nazara bahut zyada sundar hai.

8. This is absolutely necessary.
   - Ye bilkul zaroori hai.

9. I'm totally confused.
   - Main poori tarah confused hoon.

10. She was extremely angry.
    - Wo bahut zyada gussa thi.

11. That's absolutely wrong.
    - Ye bilkul galat hai.

12. He was totally surprised.
    - Wo poori tarah hairan tha.

13. The food was extremely delicious.
    - Khana bahut zyada swaadist tha.

14. I'm absolutely certain.
    - Main bilkul yakeen hoon.

15. This is totally unacceptable.
    - Ye bilkul manzoor nahi hai.

## Hardly / Barely / Scarcely

> These show VERY LOW degree (almost nothing):

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I can hardly hear you.
   - Main tumhe mushkil se sun pa raha hoon.

2. There's hardly any food left.
   - Khana mushkil se bacha hai.

3. I barely escaped the accident.
   - Main mushkil se accident se bacha.

4. She barely passed the exam.
   - Wo mushkil se exam pass hui.

5. I scarcely knew anyone there.
   - Main wahaan mushkil se kisi ko jaanta tha.

6. There was scarcely any water.
   - Paani mushkil se tha.

7. I can hardly believe it.
   - Mujhe yakeen karna mushkil hai.

8. He barely spoke a word.
   - Usne mushkil se ek shabd bola.

9. I could scarcely breathe.
   - Main mushkil se saans le pa raha tha.

10. There's hardly any time left.
    - Time mushkil se bacha hai.

11. She barely noticed me.
    - Usne mujhe mushkil se notice kiya.

12. I scarcely remember his face.
    - Mujhe uska chehra mushkil se yaad hai.

13. He can hardly walk after the injury.
    - Chot ke baad wo mushkil se chal pa raha hai.

14. I barely have enough money.
    - Mere paas mushkil se kaafi paisa hai.

15. She scarcely smiled.
    - Wo mushkil se muskurayi.

## Comparison Quick Reference

| Level | Adverbs | Hindi |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Maximum | extremely, absolutely, totally, completely | bahut zyada, bilkul |
| High | very, really, quite | bahut, sach mein |
| Moderate | fairly, rather, pretty | kaafi, kuch |
| Low | slightly, somewhat | thoda sa |
| Minimum | hardly, barely, scarcely | mushkil se |

---

## Common Mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Explanation |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| very much important | very important | Don't use "much" with adjectives |
| too much cold | too cold | Use "too" directly |
| enough big | big enough | "Enough" comes AFTER adjective |
| very delicious | delicious / absolutely delicious | Some words are already strong |
| I am very agree | I completely agree | "Agree" is a verb, not adjective |
