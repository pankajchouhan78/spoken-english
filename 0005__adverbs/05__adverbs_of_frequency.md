# Adverbs of Frequency - Daily Use

> Answer the question: HOW OFTEN does it happen?

---

## Frequency Scale

| Adverb | Hindi | Percentage |
|--------|-------|------------|
| always | hamesha | 100% |
| usually | aam taur par | 80-90% |
| normally | aam taur par | 80% |
| frequently | aksar | 70% |
| often | aksar | 60-70% |
| sometimes | kabhi kabhi | 40-50% |
| occasionally | kabhi kabhi | 20-30% |
| rarely | bahut kam | 10% |
| seldom | kam hi | 5-10% |
| hardly ever | mushkil se kabhi | 2-5% |
| never | kabhi nahi | 0% |

---

## Always / Never

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I always wake up at 6 AM.
   - Main hamesha 6 baje uthta hoon.

2. She is always on time.
   - Wo hamesha time par hoti hai.

3. He always helps others.
   - Wo hamesha dusron ki madad karta hai.

4. I never eat fast food.
   - Main kabhi fast food nahi khata.

5. She never lies.
   - Wo kabhi jhooth nahi bolti.

6. He never gives up.
   - Wo kabhi haar nahi maanta.

7. Always speak the truth.
   - Hamesha sach bolo.

8. Never trust strangers.
   - Ajnabiyon par kabhi bharosa mat karo.

9. I always carry an umbrella.
   - Main hamesha chhata rakhta hoon.

10. She never forgets birthdays.
    - Wo kabhi birthday nahi bhoolti.

11. Always be polite to elders.
    - Badon ke saath hamesha polite raho.

12. Never drink and drive.
    - Kabhi drink aur drive mat karo.

13. He is always busy with work.
    - Wo hamesha kaam mein busy rehta hai.

14. I never miss my morning walk.
    - Main kabhi apni subah ki sair nahi miss karta.

15. Always check before crossing the road.
    - Sadak paar karne se pehle hamesha dekho.

## Usually / Often / Sometimes

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I usually have tea in the morning.
   - Main aam taur par subah chai peeta hoon.

2. She usually walks to work.
   - Wo aam taur par kaam par paidal jaati hai.

3. We often go to that restaurant.
   - Hum aksar us restaurant mein jaate hain.

4. He often visits his parents.
   - Wo aksar apne parents se milne jaata hai.

5. Sometimes I feel lonely.
   - Kabhi kabhi mujhe akela lagta hai.

6. I sometimes eat out on weekends.
   - Main kabhi kabhi weekend par bahar khata hoon.

7. He usually sleeps early.
   - Wo aam taur par jaldi sota hai.

8. We often watch movies together.
   - Hum aksar saath mein movies dekhte hain.

9. Sometimes things don't go as planned.
   - Kabhi kabhi cheezein plan ke mutabiq nahi hoti.

10. I usually read before sleeping.
    - Main aam taur par sone se pehle padhta hoon.

11. She often makes mistakes.
    - Wo aksar galtiyan karti hai.

12. Sometimes life gets difficult.
    - Kabhi kabhi zindagi mushkil ho jaati hai.

13. He is usually happy.
    - Wo aam taur par khush rehta hai.

14. We often disagree on things.
    - Hum aksar baaton par agree nahi hote.

15. Sometimes I just need a break.
    - Kabhi kabhi mujhe bas ek break chahiye.

## Rarely / Seldom / Hardly Ever

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I rarely watch TV.
   - Main bahut kam TV dekhta hoon.

2. She rarely gets angry.
   - Wo bahut kam gussa hoti hai.

3. He seldom makes mistakes.
   - Wo kam hi galti karta hai.

4. They seldom visit us.
   - Wo kam hi humare yahaan aate hain.

5. I hardly ever eat outside.
   - Main mushkil se kabhi bahar khata hoon.

6. She hardly ever complains.
   - Wo mushkil se kabhi shikayat karti hai.

7. He rarely takes a day off.
   - Wo bahut kam chhuti leta hai.

8. We seldom travel by train.
   - Hum kam hi train se travel karte hain.

9. I hardly ever drink coffee.
   - Main mushkil se kabhi coffee peeta hoon.

10. She rarely speaks in meetings.
    - Wo meetings mein bahut kam bolti hai.

11. He seldom eats junk food.
    - Wo kam hi junk food khata hai.

12. I hardly ever go to parties.
    - Main mushkil se kabhi parties mein jaata hoon.

13. She rarely cries.
    - Wo bahut kam roti hai.

14. They seldom agree with each other.
    - Wo kam hi ek dusre se agree karte hain.

15. I hardly ever watch news.
    - Main mushkil se kabhi news dekhta hoon.

## Position of Frequency Adverbs

### Rule: Before main verb, after "be"

| Sentence Type | Position | Example |
|---------------|----------|---------|
| With main verb | Before verb | I **always eat** breakfast. |
| With "be" verb | After "be" | She **is always** late. |
| With helping verb | After helping verb | I have **never seen** this. |

**Examples:**

1. ✅ I always wake up early.
   - Main hamesha jaldi uthta hoon.

2. ✅ She is always happy.
   - Wo hamesha khush rehti hai.

3. ✅ He has never been to Paris.
   - Wo kabhi Paris nahi gaya.

4. ✅ They usually go together.
   - Wo aam taur par saath jaate hain.

5. ✅ I am often tired after work.
   - Main kaam ke baad aksar thaka hota hoon.

6. ✅ We have sometimes disagreed.
   - Hum kabhi kabhi disagree hue hain.

7. ✅ She is rarely sick.
   - Wo bahut kam bimar hoti hai.

8. ✅ He never arrives on time.
   - Wo kabhi time par nahi aata.

9. ✅ I sometimes feel stressed.
   - Main kabhi kabhi stressed feel karta hoon.

10. ✅ They are usually polite.
    - Wo aam taur par polite hote hain.
