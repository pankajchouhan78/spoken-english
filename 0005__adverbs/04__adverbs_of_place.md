# Adverbs of Place - Daily Use

> Answer the question: WHERE does it happen?

---

## Here / There

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Come here, please.
   - Yahaan aao please.

2. I live here.
   - Main yahaan rehta hoon.

3. Put the bag there.
   - Bag wahaan rakho.

4. I went there yesterday.
   - Main kal wahaan gaya tha.

5. Sign here at the bottom.
   - Yahaan neeche sign karo.

6. The restaurant is right there.
   - Restaurant wahaan hai.

7. Stay here, I'll be back.
   - Yahaan ruko, main aata hoon.

8. Don't go there alone.
   - Wahaan akele mat jao.

9. Here is your coffee.
   - Ye lo tumhari coffee.

10. I was there for two hours.
    - Main wahaan do ghante tha.

11. What are you doing here?
    - Tum yahaan kya kar rahe ho?

12. Who lives there?
    - Wahaan kaun rehta hai?

13. The problem lies here.
    - Problem yahaan hai.

14. I'll meet you there.
    - Main tumse wahaan milunga.

15. Here is the document you needed.
    - Ye raha document jo tumhe chahiye tha.

## Inside / Outside

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Come inside, it's cold.
   - Andar aao, thandi hai.

2. The keys are inside my bag.
   - Chaabi mere bag ke andar hai.

3. Wait outside for a moment.
   - Ek pal bahar ruko.

4. The children are playing outside.
   - Bachche bahar khel rahe hain.

5. It's warm inside the house.
   - Ghar ke andar garam hai.

6. There's a garden outside.
   - Bahar ek garden hai.

7. Don't go inside without permission.
   - Bina ijazat andar mat jao.

8. It's raining outside.
   - Bahar baarish ho rahi hai.

9. Let's eat inside today.
   - Aaj andar khana khate hain.

10. The dog is barking outside.
    - Kutta bahar bhaunk raha hai.

11. Stay inside during the storm.
    - Toofan ke dauran andar raho.

12. Let's sit outside in the sun.
    - Dhoop mein bahar baithe hain.

13. Is anyone inside?
    - Kya andar koi hai?

14. The car is parked outside.
    - Car bahar parked hai.

15. Keep the food inside the fridge.
    - Khana fridge ke andar rakho.

## Upstairs / Downstairs

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. My room is upstairs.
   - Mera kamra upar hai.

2. Go upstairs and rest.
   - Upar jao aur aaram karo.

3. The kitchen is downstairs.
   - Kitchen neeche hai.

4. Come downstairs for dinner.
   - Khane ke liye neeche aao.

5. He went upstairs to sleep.
   - Wo sone ke liye upar gaya.

6. The guests are waiting downstairs.
   - Mehman neeche intezaar kar rahe hain.

7. Take the bags upstairs.
   - Bags upar le jao.

8. I heard a noise downstairs.
   - Mujhe neeche awaaz aayi.

9. The bathroom is upstairs.
   - Bathroom upar hai.

10. Let's go downstairs and check.
    - Chalo neeche jaake dekhte hain.

11. She ran upstairs in a hurry.
    - Wo jaldi mein upar bhaagi.

12. The shop is downstairs.
    - Dukaan neeche hai.

13. My grandfather can't climb upstairs.
    - Mere dada upar nahi chadh sakte.

14. We eat dinner downstairs.
    - Hum raat ka khana neeche khate hain.

15. Go upstairs and call your brother.
    - Upar jao aur bhai ko bulao.

## Everywhere / Somewhere / Anywhere / Nowhere

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I searched everywhere for my keys.
   - Maine chaabi har jagah dhundhi.

2. He travels everywhere for work.
   - Wo kaam ke liye har jagah jaata hai.

3. Let's go somewhere tonight.
   - Aaj raat kahin chalte hain.

4. I want to go somewhere peaceful.
   - Main kahin shant jagah jaana chahta hoon.

5. You can sit anywhere you like.
   - Tum kahin bhi baith sakte ho.

6. Is there a pharmacy anywhere nearby?
   - Kya paas mein kahin pharmacy hai?

7. There's nowhere to park.
   - Kahin park karne ki jagah nahi hai.

8. He went nowhere yesterday.
   - Wo kal kahin nahi gaya.

9. I see his influence everywhere.
   - Mujhe uska asar har jagah dikhta hai.

10. Put it somewhere safe.
    - Ise kahin safe jagah rakho.

11. I can't find it anywhere.
    - Mujhe ye kahin nahi mil raha.

12. There's nowhere to hide.
    - Kahin chhupne ki jagah nahi hai.

13. Good people are everywhere.
    - Acche log har jagah hote hain.

14. I need to go somewhere urgently.
    - Mujhe kahin jaldi jaana hai.

15. This road leads nowhere.
    - Ye raasta kahin nahi jaata.

## Forward / Backward / Away

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Move forward in the queue.
   - Line mein aage badho.

2. Take one step forward.
   - Ek kadam aage badho.

3. Don't look backward.
   - Peeche mat dekho.

4. The car is moving backward.
   - Car peeche ja rahi hai.

5. Go away! Leave me alone.
   - Dur jao! Mujhe akela chhodo.

6. He walked away without saying anything.
   - Wo bina kuch kahe chala gaya.

7. We are moving forward with the plan.
   - Hum plan ke saath aage badh rahe hain.

8. She stepped backward in surprise.
   - Wo hairan hokar peeche hatni.

9. The problems won't go away on their own.
   - Problems apne aap dur nahi jayengi.

10. Look forward, not backward.
    - Aage dekho, peeche nahi.

11. He ran away from the scene.
    - Wo jagah se bhaag gaya.

12. Push the button to move forward.
    - Aage jaane ke liye button dabao.

13. The train is going backward.
    - Train peeche ja rahi hai.

14. Stay away from negative people.
    - Negative logon se dur raho.

15. Keep moving forward in life.
    - Zindagi mein aage badhte raho.
