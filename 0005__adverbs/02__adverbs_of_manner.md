# Adverbs of Manner - Daily Use

> Answer the question: HOW is the action done?

---

## Slowly / Quickly / Fast

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Please speak slowly, I can't understand.
   - Dheere bolo please, samajh nahi aa raha.

2. Walk slowly, the floor is wet.
   - Dheere chalo, floor geela hai.

3. He eats very slowly.
   - Wo bahut dheere khata hai.

4. Come quickly, we're getting late!
   - Jaldi aao, der ho rahi hai!

5. She finished her work quickly.
   - Usne jaldi se kaam khatam kiya.

6. Don't drive so fast.
   - Itni tez mat chalao.

7. Time goes by fast.
   - Waqt tez guzarta hai.

8. The train is moving slowly.
   - Train dheere chal rahi hai.

9. Think quickly and give me an answer.
   - Jaldi socho aur jawab do.

10. He runs fast like a cheetah.
    - Wo cheetah ki tarah tez daudta hai.

11. Read slowly and understand every word.
    - Dheere padho aur har shabd samjho.

12. The project was completed quickly.
    - Project jaldi complete ho gaya.

13. The internet is working slowly today.
    - Aaj internet dheere chal raha hai.

14. Respond quickly to customer queries.
    - Customer ke sawaalon ka jaldi jawab do.

15. My heart is beating fast.
    - Mera dil tez dhadak raha hai.

## Carefully / Carelessly

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Drive carefully at night.
   - Raat ko dhyan se chalao.

2. Handle this glass carefully.
   - Is glass ko dhyan se pakdo.

3. Listen carefully to the instructions.
   - Instructions dhyan se suno.

4. He carelessly left the door open.
   - Usne laparwahi se darwaza khula chhod diya.

5. Read the question carefully before answering.
   - Jawab dene se pehle sawaal dhyan se padho.

6. She carefully wrapped the gift.
   - Usne dhyan se gift pack kiya.

7. Don't handle money carelessly.
   - Paise laparwahi se mat handle karo.

8. Cross the road carefully.
   - Sadak dhyan se paar karo.

9. He carelessly forgot his wallet.
   - Usne laparwahi se apna wallet bhool gaya.

10. Check your work carefully.
    - Apna kaam dhyan se check karo.

11. She writes very carefully.
    - Wo bahut dhyan se likhti hai.

12. He carelessly made many mistakes.
    - Usne laparwahi se bahut galtiyan ki.

13. Pack the fragile items carefully.
    - Naauk cheezon ko dhyan se pack karo.

14. Don't act carelessly in important matters.
    - Zaroori maamlon mein laparwahi mat karo.

15. Carefully choose your words.
    - Apne shabd dhyan se chuno.

## Loudly / Quietly / Softly

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Please speak more loudly.
   - Thoda zor se bolo please.

2. Don't talk so loudly in public.
   - Public mein itna zor se mat bolo.

3. The baby is sleeping, walk quietly.
   - Bachcha so raha hai, chupchap chalo.

4. She quietly left the room.
   - Wo chupchap kamre se nikal gayi.

5. Speak softly, don't shout.
   - Naram awaaz mein bolo, chillao mat.

6. He laughed loudly at the joke.
   - Wo joke par zor se hansa.

7. Close the door quietly.
   - Darwaza chupchap band karo.

8. She sang softly to the baby.
   - Usne bachche ko dheere gaana gaaya.

9. The music is playing too loudly.
   - Music bahut zor se chal raha hai.

10. Work quietly and don't disturb others.
    - Chupchap kaam karo aur dusron ko disturb mat karo.

11. Touch softly, it's delicate.
    - Dheere se chhuo, ye nazuk hai.

12. He knocked loudly on the door.
    - Usne zor se darwaze par knock kiya.

13. They talked quietly in the library.
    - Unhone library mein dheere baat ki.

14. Speak softly, I can hear you.
    - Dheere bolo, main sun sakta hoon.

15. The thunder roared loudly.
    - Bijli zor se garji.

## Well / Badly / Poorly

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She speaks English very well.
   - Wo bahut acchi English bolti hai.

2. He performed well in the exam.
   - Usne exam mein accha kiya.

3. I slept well last night.
   - Maine kal raat acchi neend li.

4. The team played badly today.
   - Team ne aaj bura khela.

5. He behaved badly at the party.
   - Usne party mein bura behave kiya.

6. The business is doing poorly.
   - Business bura chal raha hai.

7. Well done! You passed the test.
   - Shabash! Tum pass ho gaye.

8. Things went badly for him.
   - Uske liye cheezein buri ho gayin.

9. She cooks really well.
   - Wo sach mein accha khana banati hai.

10. The patient is responding well.
    - Patient acchi tarah respond kar raha hai.

11. He treated her badly.
    - Usne uske saath bura behave kiya.

12. I didn't sleep well.
    - Meri neend acchi nahi aayi.

13. She managed the situation well.
    - Usne situation acchi tarah manage kiya.

14. The project was managed poorly.
    - Project buri tarah manage hua.

15. He plays guitar very well.
    - Wo bahut accha guitar bajata hai.

## Hard / Hardly

> ⚠️ These have DIFFERENT meanings!
> - hard = with effort (mehnat se)
> - hardly = barely, almost not (mushkil se)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She works very hard.
   - Wo bahut mehnat karti hai.

2. I can hardly see anything.
   - Mujhe mushkil se kuch dikh raha hai.

3. Study hard for the exam.
   - Exam ke liye mehnat se padho.

4. I hardly know him.
   - Main use mushkil se jaanta hoon.

5. He trains hard every day.
   - Wo roz mehnat se practice karta hai.

6. There's hardly any food left.
   - Khana mushkil se bacha hai.

7. Push hard, the door is stuck.
   - Zor se dhakko, darwaza atka hai.

8. I could hardly believe it.
   - Mujhe yakeen karna mushkil tha.

9. Work hard and success will follow.
   - Mehnat karo, safalta aayegi.

10. She hardly speaks in meetings.
    - Wo meetings mein mushkil se bolti hai.

11. Hit the ball hard.
    - Ball ko zor se maaro.

12. I hardly slept last night.
    - Main kal raat mushkil se soya.

13. They worked hard to finish on time.
    - Unhone time par khatam karne ke liye mehnat ki.

14. I can hardly walk after the exercise.
    - Exercise ke baad main mushkil se chal pa raha hoon.

15. Try hard and you will succeed.
    - Koshish karo aur safal hoge.
