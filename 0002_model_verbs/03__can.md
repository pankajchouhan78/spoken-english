# Can - Ability, Permission, Possibility

---

## 1. Can (Ability - Present)

> **Usage:** Jab kuch karne ki ability/capability batani ho
> **Structure:** Subject + can + V1 (base verb)

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I can speak English.

   - Main English bol sakta hoon.
2. She can cook very well.

   - Wo bahut accha khana bana sakti hai.
3. He can drive a car.

   - Wo car chala sakta hai.
4. I can swim.

   - Main tair sakta hoon.
5. She can play guitar.

   - Wo guitar baja sakti hai.
6. We can finish this today.

   - Hum yeh aaj khatam kar sakte hain.
7. He can solve this problem.

   - Wo yeh problem solve kar sakta hai.
8. I can understand Hindi.

   - Main Hindi samajh sakta hoon.
9. She can run very fast.

   - Wo bahut tez daur sakti hai.
10. They can help you.

    - Wo tumhari madad kar sakte hain.

---

## 2. Can (Permission - Informal)

> **Usage:** Jab informally permission maangni ho
> **Structure:** Can + subject + V1?

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. Can I use your phone?

   - Kya main tumhara phone use kar sakta hoon?
2. Can I sit here?

   - Kya main yahaan baith sakta hoon?
3. Can I go now?

   - Kya main ab ja sakta hoon?
4. Can I ask you something?

   - Kya main tumse kuch pooch sakta hoon?
5. Can I borrow your pen?

   - Kya main tumhara pen le sakta hoon?
6. Can we start the meeting?

   - Kya hum meeting shuru kar sakte hain?
7. Can I have some water?

   - Kya mujhe thoda paani mil sakta hai?
8. Can I come in?

   - Kya main andar aa sakta hoon?
9. Can I talk to you for a minute?

   - Kya main tumse ek minute baat kar sakta hoon?
10. Can we leave early today?

    - Kya hum aaj jaldi ja sakte hain?

---

## 3. Can (Possibility)

> **Usage:** Jab koi general possibility batani ho
> **Structure:** Subject + can + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. It can get very hot in summer.

   - Garmi mein bahut garam ho sakta hai.
2. This road can be dangerous at night.

   - Yeh road raat ko dangerous ho sakti hai.
3. Anyone can make mistakes.

   - Koi bhi galti kar sakta hai.
4. Technology can change our lives.

   - Technology humari zindagi badal sakti hai.
5. Hard work can lead to success.

   - Mehnat se safalta mil sakti hai.

---

## 4. Can't (Negative - Inability/Not Allowed)

> **Usage:** Jab kuch nahi kar sakte ya allowed nahi ho
> **Structure:** Subject + can't + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I can't swim.

   - Main tair nahi sakta.
2. She can't come today.

   - Wo aaj nahi aa sakti.
3. I can't understand this.

   - Main yeh samajh nahi sakta.
4. You can't park here.

   - Tum yahaan park nahi kar sakte.
5. We can't wait any longer.

   - Hum aur intezaar nahi kar sakte.
6. I can't find my keys.

   - Mujhe apni keys nahi mil rahi.
7. He can't hear you.

   - Wo tumhe sun nahi sakta.
8. I can't believe this.

   - Mujhe yeh yakeen nahi ho raha.
9. She can't eat spicy food.

   - Wo teekha khana nahi kha sakti.
10. We can't afford this.

    - Hum yeh afford nahi kar sakte.

---

## 5. Can Be (State/Condition)

> **Usage:** Jab kisi cheez ki possible state batani ho
> **Structure:** Subject + can be + adjective/noun

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. He can be very rude sometimes.

   - Wo kabhi kabhi bahut rude ho sakta hai.
2. Life can be difficult.

   - Zindagi mushkil ho sakti hai.
3. She can be late sometimes.

   - Wo kabhi kabhi late ho sakti hai.
4. This job can be stressful.

   - Yeh job stressful ho sakti hai.
5. Weather can be unpredictable.

   - Mausam unpredictable ho sakta hai.

---

## 6. Can vs Could vs May (Permission Difference)

> **Formality Level:**
> Can = Informal | Could = Polite | May = Formal

**Examples:**

| Informal (Can)  | Polite (Could)    | Formal (May)    |
| --------------- | ----------------- | --------------- |
| Can I go?       | Could I go?       | May I go?       |
| Can I use this? | Could I use this? | May I use this? |
| Can I sit here? | Could I sit here? | May I sit here? |

---

## 7. Be Able To (Alternative to Can)

> **Usage:** Jab "can" use nahi ho sakta (future, past, perfect tenses)
> **Structure:** Subject + be able to + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I will be able to come tomorrow.

   - Main kal aa sakuunga.
2. She was able to finish the work.

   - Wo kaam khatam kar paayi.
3. I have been able to learn a lot.

   - Main bahut kuch seekh paya hoon.
4. He wasn't able to attend the meeting.

   - Wo meeting attend nahi kar paya.
5. Will you be able to help me?

   - Kya tum meri madad kar paoge?
