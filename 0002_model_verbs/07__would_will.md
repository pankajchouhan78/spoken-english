# Would & Will - Future, Hypothetical, Polite Requests

---

## 1. Will (Simple Future)

> **Usage:** Jab future action batani ho  
> **Structure:** Subject + will + V1 (base verb)

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I will call you tomorrow.
   - Main tumhe kal call karunga.

2. She will come at 5 PM.
   - Wo 5 baje aayegi.

3. We will go to the mall later.
   - Hum baad mein mall jayenge.

4. It will rain tonight.
   - Aaj raat baarish hogi.

5. He will help you.
   - Wo tumhari madad karega.

6. I will finish this by evening.
   - Main yeh shaam tak khatam kar dunga.

7. They will arrive soon.
   - Wo jaldi aa jayenge.

8. She will understand.
   - Wo samajh jayegi.

9. We will meet next week.
   - Hum agle hafte milenge.

10. I will do it.
    - Main kar lunga.

---

## 2. Will (Promises & Commitment)

> **Usage:** Jab promise ya commitment karna ho  
> **Structure:** Subject + will + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I will always support you.
   - Main hamesha tumhara saath dunga.

2. I will never forget this.
   - Main yeh kabhi nahi bhulunga.

3. I will be there for you.
   - Main tumhare liye wahan hounga.

4. I promise I will help you.
   - Main promise karta hoon main tumhari madad karunga.

5. I will make sure it happens.
   - Main pakka karunga yeh ho.

---

## 3. Will (Spontaneous Decision)

> **Usage:** Jab abhi decide karo future ke liye  
> **Structure:** Subject + will + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I will have coffee.
   - Main coffee lunga.

2. I will take this one.
   - Main yeh le lunga.

3. I will answer the door.
   - Main darwaza kholunga.

4. Wait, I will help you.
   - Ruko, main tumhari madad karta hoon.

5. I will join you.
   - Main tumhare saath aa raha hoon.

---

## 4. Won't (Negative - Refusal)

> **Usage:** Jab refuse karna ho ya nahi karega  
> **Structure:** Subject + won't + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I won't do this again.
   - Main yeh dobara nahi karunga.

2. He won't listen to me.
   - Wo meri nahi sunega.

3. She won't come today.
   - Wo aaj nahi aayegi.

4. I won't tell anyone.
   - Main kisi ko nahi bataunga.

5. They won't agree.
   - Wo maan nahi jayenge.

6. It won't work.
   - Yeh kaam nahi karega.

7. I won't let you down.
   - Main tumhe disappoint nahi karunga.

8. He won't give up.
   - Wo haar nahi manega.

9. She won't forget this.
   - Wo yeh nahi bhulegi.

10. We won't be late.
    - Hum late nahi honge.

---

## 5. Will Have (Future Perfect)

> **Usage:** Jab future mein kuch complete ho chuka hoga  
> **Structure:** Subject + will have + V3

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I will have finished by then.
   - Tab tak main khatam kar chuka hounga.

2. She will have reached by now.
   - Wo ab tak pahunch chuki hogi.

3. They will have completed the project.
   - Wo project complete kar chuke honge.

4. By tomorrow, I will have left.
   - Kal tak main ja chuka hounga.

5. We will have eaten by 8 PM.
   - 8 baje tak hum kha chuke honge.

---

## 6. Would (Polite Requests)

> **Usage:** Jab politely request karna ho
> **Structure:** Would you + V1? / Would you mind + V-ing?

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. Would you help me?
   - Kya aap meri madad karenge?

2. Would you please close the door?
   - Kya aap door band kar denge please?

3. Would you like some tea?
   - Kya aap chai lenge?

4. Would you mind waiting?
   - Kya aap wait kar sakte hain?

5. Would you pass the salt?
   - Kya aap namak denge?

6. Would you come with me?
   - Kya aap mere saath aayenge?

7. Would you please be quiet?
   - Kya aap please chup rahenge?

8. Would you like to join us?
   - Kya aap humse join karna chahenge?

9. Would you hold this for me?
   - Kya aap yeh pakad lenge?

10. Would you call me later?
    - Kya aap mujhe baad mein call karenge?

---

## 7. Would (Past Habits)

> **Usage:** Jab past ki aadat batani ho
> **Structure:** Subject + would + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. When I was young, I would play outside.
   - Jab main chhota tha, bahar khelta tha.

2. She would always help others.
   - Wo hamesha doosron ki madad karti thi.

3. He would visit us every Sunday.
   - Wo har Sunday humse milne aata tha.

4. We would go to the park daily.
   - Hum roz park jaate the.

5. My grandfather would tell us stories.
   - Mere dada humein kahaniyaan sunate the.

---

## 8. Would (Hypothetical/Conditional)

> **Usage:** Jab "agar-toh" situation batani ho
> **Structure:** If + past, Subject + would + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. If I had money, I would travel the world.
   - Agar mere paas paise hote, main duniya ghumta.

2. If she knew, she would tell me.
   - Agar usse pata hota, wo mujhe batati.

3. I would buy a car if I could.
   - Main car kharidta agar kar sakta.

4. If it rained, we would stay home.
   - Agar baarish hoti, hum ghar rehte.

5. He would help if you asked.
   - Wo madad karta agar tum poochte.

---

## 9. Would Have (Unreal Past - Regret)

> **Usage:** Jab past mein kuch ho sakta tha par nahi hua
> **Structure:** Subject + would have + V3

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I would have come, but I was busy.
   - Main aata, par main busy tha.

2. She would have helped you.
   - Wo tumhari madad karti.

3. We would have won if we tried.
   - Hum jeet jaate agar koshish karte.

4. He would have called if he knew.
   - Wo call karta agar usse pata hota.

5. I would have told you earlier.
   - Main tumhe pehle batata.

6. They would have finished by now.
   - Wo ab tak khatam kar chuke hote.

7. I would have attended the meeting.
   - Main meeting attend karta.

8. She would have understood.
   - Wo samajh jaati.

9. We would have gone if invited.
   - Hum jaate agar invite hote.

10. He would have agreed.
    - Wo maan jaata.

---

## 10. Wouldn't (Negative - Refusal/Hypothetical)

> **Usage:** Jab refuse karna ho ya hypothetical negative
> **Structure:** Subject + wouldn't + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I wouldn't do that.
   - Main aisa nahi karta.

2. She wouldn't agree.
   - Wo nahi maanti.

3. He wouldn't listen.
   - Wo nahi sunta.

4. I wouldn't mind.
   - Mujhe koi problem nahi hoti.

5. They wouldn't understand.
   - Wo nahi samajhte.
