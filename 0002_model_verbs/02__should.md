# Should - Advice, Expectation, Obligation

---

## 1. Should (Present/Future - Advice & Suggestion)

> **Usage:** Advice dena, suggestion dena, expectation batana
> **Structure:** Subject + should + V1 (base verb)

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. You should drink more water.
   - Tumhe zyada paani peena chahiye.

2. We should leave now.
   - Humein abhi nikalna chahiye.

3. You should take rest.
   - Tumhe aaram karna chahiye.

4. I should call my parents today.
   - Mujhe aaj apne parents ko call karna chahiye.

5. She should eat on time.
   - Usse samay par khana khana chahiye.

6. You should focus on your health.
   - Tumhe apni health par dhyan dena chahiye.

7. We should respect our elders.
   - Humein apne bado ka samman karna chahiye.

8. You should wake up early.
   - Tumhe subah jaldi uthna chahiye.

9. I should exercise daily.
   - Mujhe roz exercise karni chahiye.

10. You should save money for the future.
    - Tumhe future ke liye paisa bachana chahiye.

---

## 2. Should Have (Past - Regret / Missed Opportunity)

> **Usage:** Jab past mein kuch karna chahiye tha, par nahi kiya - regret ya missed opportunity
> **Structure:** Subject + should have + V3 (past participle)

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I should have called you earlier.
   - Mujhe tumhe pehle phone karna chahiye tha.

2. You should have studied harder.
   - Tumhe zyada mehnat se padhna chahiye tha.

3. We should have left earlier.
   - Humein pehle nikalna chahiye tha.

4. I should have listened to you.
   - Mujhe tumhari baat sunni chahiye thi.

5. She should have taken the medicine.
   - Usse dawai leni chahiye thi.

6. You should have told me the truth.
   - Tumhe mujhe sach batana chahiye tha.

7. I should have saved that file.
   - Mujhe wo file save karni chahiye thi.

8. We should have booked tickets in advance.
   - Humein advance mein tickets book karni chahiye thi.

9. He should have apologized.
   - Usse maafi maangni chahiye thi.

10. I should have slept early last night.
    - Mujhe kal raat jaldi sona chahiye tha.

---

## 3. Should Be (State/Condition - Expected State)

> **Usage:** Jab kisi cheez ki expected state ya condition batani ho
> **Structure:** Subject + should be + adjective/noun/V-ing

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. You should be more careful.
   - Tumhe zyada saavdhan rehna chahiye.

2. This should be ready by tomorrow.
   - Yeh kal tak ready hona chahiye.

3. The food should be hot.
   - Khana garam hona chahiye.

4. You should be studying right now.
   - Tumhe abhi padhai karni chahiye.

5. He should be here by 5 PM.
   - Wo 5 baje tak yahaan hona chahiye.

---

## 4. Should Have Been (Past State - Expected but didn't happen)

> **Usage:** Jab past mein koi state ya action expected thi par nahi hui
> **Structure:** Subject + should have been + adjective/V-ing

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. You should have been more careful.
   - Tumhe zyada saavdhan rehna chahiye tha.

2. I should have been studying, not watching TV.
   - Mujhe padhai karni chahiye thi, TV nahi dekhna chahiye tha.

3. They should have been here by now.
   - Unhe ab tak yahaan hona chahiye tha.

4. The work should have been completed yesterday.
   - Kaam kal tak complete hona chahiye tha.

5. She should have been at the meeting.
   - Usse meeting mein hona chahiye tha.

---

## 5. Shouldn't (Negative - Advice against something)

> **Usage:** Jab kisi kaam ko na karne ki advice deni ho
> **Structure:** Subject + shouldn't + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. You shouldn't eat junk food.
   - Tumhe junk food nahi khana chahiye.

2. We shouldn't waste time.
   - Humein time waste nahi karna chahiye.

3. You shouldn't stay up late.
   - Tumhe raat ko late tak nahi jaagna chahiye.

4. She shouldn't skip meals.
   - Usse khana skip nahi karna chahiye.

5. You shouldn't worry too much.
   - Tumhe zyada chinta nahi karni chahiye.

---

## 6. Shouldn't Have (Past - Something you regret doing)

> **Usage:** Jab past mein kuch kiya jo nahi karna chahiye tha
> **Structure:** Subject + shouldn't have + V3

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I shouldn't have said that.
   - Mujhe wo nahi bolna chahiye tha.

2. You shouldn't have eaten so much.
   - Tumhe itna nahi khana chahiye tha.

3. We shouldn't have ignored his advice.
   - Humein uski advice ignore nahi karni chahiye thi.

4. She shouldn't have trusted him.
   - Usse us par bharosa nahi karna chahiye tha.

5. I shouldn't have stayed up so late.
   - Mujhe itni raat tak nahi jaagna chahiye tha.

---

## 7. Ought To (Strong Moral Obligation)

> **Usage:** Moral duty ya strong recommendation - "Should" se thoda strong
> **Structure:** Subject + ought to + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. You ought to respect your parents.
   - Tumhe apne parents ka samman karna chahiye.

2. We ought to help the poor.
   - Humein garibon ki madad karni chahiye.

3. People ought to follow traffic rules.
   - Logon ko traffic rules follow karne chahiye.

4. You ought to tell the truth.
   - Tumhe sach bolna chahiye.

5. Students ought to attend classes regularly.
   - Students ko regularly classes attend karni chahiye.
