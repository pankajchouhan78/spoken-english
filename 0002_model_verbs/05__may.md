# May / Might - Permission, Possibility

---

## 1. May (Formal Permission)

> **Usage:** Jab formally permission maangni ho ya deni ho
> **Structure:** May + subject + V1? / Subject + may + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. May I come in?
   - Kya main andar aa sakta hoon?

2. May I use your phone?
   - Kya main aapka phone use kar sakta hoon?

3. May I ask you a question?
   - Kya main aapse ek sawaal pooch sakta hoon?

4. You may leave now.
   - Aap ab ja sakte hain.

5. May I sit here?
   - Kya main yahaan baith sakta hoon?

6. You may take one day off.
   - Aap ek din ki chutti le sakte hain.

7. May I speak to the manager?
   - Kya main manager se baat kar sakta hoon?

8. Students may leave after the exam.
   - Students exam ke baad ja sakte hain.

9. May I have a glass of water?
   - Kya mujhe ek glass paani mil sakta hai?

10. You may start your work.
    - Aap apna kaam shuru kar sakte hain.

---

## 2. May (Possibility - Present/Future)

> **Usage:** Jab koi possibility batani ho (50% chance)
> **Structure:** Subject + may + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. It may rain today.
   - Aaj baarish ho sakti hai.

2. I may go to the gym later.
   - Ho sakta hai main baad mein gym jaaun.

3. She may come to the party.
   - Ho sakta hai wo party mein aaye.

4. He may be late today.
   - Wo aaj late ho sakta hai.

5. We may visit them tomorrow.
   - Ho sakta hai hum kal unse milein.

6. The train may be delayed.
   - Train late ho sakti hai.

7. I may not come tomorrow.
   - Ho sakta hai main kal na aaun.

8. They may change the plan.
   - Wo plan badal sakte hain.

9. She may call you back.
   - Wo tumhe wapas call kar sakti hai.

10. This medicine may help you.
    - Yeh dawai tumhari madad kar sakti hai.

---

## 3. May Have (Past Possibility)

> **Usage:** Jab past mein kuch hua ho sakta hai (uncertain)
> **Structure:** Subject + may have + V3

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. He may have missed the train.
   - Ho sakta hai usne train miss kar di ho.

2. She may have forgotten about it.
   - Ho sakta hai wo bhool gayi ho.

3. They may have already left.
   - Ho sakta hai wo ja chuke hon.

4. I may have made a mistake.
   - Ho sakta hai maine galti ki ho.

5. He may have taken the wrong bus.
   - Ho sakta hai usne galat bus li ho.

6. She may have lost her keys.
   - Ho sakta hai uski keys kho gayi hon.

7. They may have finished the work.
   - Ho sakta hai unhone kaam khatam kar liya ho.

8. I may have left my wallet at home.
   - Ho sakta hai maine wallet ghar chhod diya ho.

9. He may have already eaten.
   - Ho sakta hai wo kha chuka ho.

10. We may have met before.
    - Ho sakta hai hum pehle mile hon.

---

## 4. May Be (Present State - Possibility)

> **Usage:** Jab present mein kisi state ki possibility ho
> **Structure:** Subject + may be + adjective/noun/V-ing

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. He may be busy right now.
   - Wo abhi busy ho sakta hai.

2. She may be sleeping.
   - Wo so rahi ho sakti hai.

3. They may be waiting for us.
   - Wo humara wait kar rahe ho sakte hain.

4. It may be true.
   - Yeh sach ho sakta hai.

5. He may be at home.
   - Wo ghar par ho sakta hai.

---

## 5. May Have Been (Past State - Possibility)

> **Usage:** Jab past mein koi state ho sakti thi
> **Structure:** Subject + may have been + adjective/V-ing

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. She may have been busy when you called.
   - Jab tumne call kiya wo busy ho sakti thi.

2. He may have been sleeping.
   - Wo so raha ho sakta tha.

3. They may have been working late.
   - Wo late tak kaam kar rahe ho sakte the.

4. I may have been wrong.
   - Ho sakta hai main galat tha.

5. It may have been a mistake.
   - Yeh galti ho sakti thi.

---

## 6. Might (Less Certain Possibility)

> **Usage:** May se kam possibility (30% chance)
> **Structure:** Subject + might + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. It might rain tomorrow.
   - Kal baarish ho sakti hai (shayad).

2. I might go to the party.
   - Shayad main party jaaun.

3. She might call you later.
   - Shayad wo baad mein call kare.

4. He might not come today.
   - Shayad wo aaj na aaye.

5. We might visit next week.
   - Shayad hum agle hafte aayein.

---

## 7. Might Have (Past - Less Certain)

> **Usage:** Past mein kuch hua ho sakta tha (less sure)
> **Structure:** Subject + might have + V3

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. He might have forgotten.
   - Shayad wo bhool gaya ho.

2. She might have already left.
   - Shayad wo ja chuki ho.

3. They might have changed the plan.
   - Shayad unhone plan badal diya ho.

4. I might have seen him somewhere.
   - Shayad maine use kahin dekha ho.

5. It might have been a joke.
   - Shayad yeh mazaak tha.

---

## 8. May Not / Might Not (Negative)

> **Usage:** Jab negative possibility batani ho
> **Structure:** Subject + may not / might not + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I may not come tomorrow.
   - Ho sakta hai main kal na aaun.

2. She might not agree.
   - Shayad wo maan na jaaye.

3. It may not work.
   - Yeh kaam na kare.

4. He might not know about it.
   - Shayad usse pata na ho.

5. They may not be available.
   - Wo available na hon.
