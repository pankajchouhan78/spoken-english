# Could - Past Ability, Polite Requests, Possibility

---

## 1. Could (Past Ability)

> **Usage:** Jab past mein kuch karne ki ability thi  
> **Structure:** Subject + could + V1 (base verb)

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I could run very fast when I was young.
   - Main bachpan mein bahut tez daur sakta tha.

2. She could speak French fluently.
   - Wo French fluently bol sakti thi.

3. He could play guitar very well.
   - Wo guitar bahut accha baja sakta tha.

4. We could stay up late during holidays.
   - Hum holidays mein der tak jaag sakte the.

5. They could finish the work quickly.
   - Wo kaam jaldi khatam kar sakte the.

---

## 2. Could (Polite Requests)

> **Usage:** Jab politely kuch maangna ho ya request karni ho  
> **Structure:** Could + subject + V1?

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. Could you please help me?
   - Kya aap meri madad kar sakte hain?

2. Could you pass me the salt?
   - Kya aap mujhe namak de sakte hain?

3. Could I use your phone?
   - Kya main aapka phone use kar sakta hoon?

4. Could you please repeat that?
   - Kya aap please wo dobara bol sakte hain?

5. Could you wait for a moment?
   - Kya aap ek minute ruk sakte hain?

6. Could I have a glass of water?
   - Kya mujhe ek glass paani mil sakta hai?

7. Could you send me the file?
   - Kya aap mujhe file bhej sakte hain?

8. Could you please speak slowly?
   - Kya aap please dheere bol sakte hain?

9. Could I ask you something?
   - Kya main aapse kuch pooch sakta hoon?

10. Could you tell me the time?
    - Kya aap mujhe time bata sakte hain?

---

## 3. Could (Possibility/Suggestion)

> **Usage:** Jab koi possibility ya suggestion deni ho  
> **Structure:** Subject + could + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. It could rain today.
   - Aaj baarish ho sakti hai.

2. We could go to a movie tonight.
   - Hum aaj raat movie dekh sakte hain.

3. You could try this restaurant.
   - Tum is restaurant ko try kar sakte ho.

4. She could be at home.
   - Wo ghar par ho sakti hai.

5. This could be the right answer.
   - Yeh sahi jawab ho sakta hai.

---

## 4. Could Have (Past Possibility - Didn't Happen)

> **Usage:** Jab past mein kuch ho sakta tha par nahi hua  
> **Structure:** Subject + could have + V3 (past participle)

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I could have helped you, but you didn't ask.
   - Main tumhari madad kar sakta tha, par tumne poocha nahi.

2. You could have called me.
   - Tum mujhe call kar sakte the.

3. We could have won the match.
   - Hum match jeet sakte the.

4. She could have passed if she studied.
   - Agar wo padhti toh pass ho sakti thi.

5. He could have caught the train.
   - Wo train pakad sakta tha.

6. They could have finished earlier.
   - Wo pehle khatam kar sakte the.

7. I could have bought that phone.
   - Main wo phone kharid sakta tha.

8. You could have told me the truth.
   - Tum mujhe sach bata sakte the.

9. We could have avoided this problem.
   - Hum is problem se bach sakte the.

10. She could have been more careful.
    - Wo zyada careful ho sakti thi.

---

## 5. Could Be (Present Possibility)

> **Usage:** Jab present mein koi possibility ho  
> **Structure:** Subject + could be + adjective/noun/V-ing

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. He could be sleeping right now.
   - Wo abhi so raha ho sakta hai.

2. She could be busy.
   - Wo busy ho sakti hai.

3. This could be a mistake.
   - Yeh galti ho sakti hai.

4. They could be waiting for us.
   - Wo humara intezaar kar rahe ho sakte hain.

5. It could be true.
   - Yeh sach ho sakta hai.

---

## 6. Could Have Been (Past State - Didn't Happen)

> **Usage:** Jab past mein koi state ho sakti thi par nahi thi  
> **Structure:** Subject + could have been + adjective/V-ing

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I could have been a doctor.
   - Main doctor ban sakta tha.

2. It could have been worse.
   - Yeh aur bura ho sakta tha.

3. She could have been here by now.
   - Wo ab tak yahaan ho sakti thi.

4. We could have been friends.
   - Hum dost ho sakte the.

5. He could have been more successful.
   - Wo zyada successful ho sakta tha.

---

## 7. Couldn't (Negative - Past Inability)

> **Usage:** Jab past mein kuch nahi kar sake
> **Structure:** Subject + couldn't + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I couldn't sleep last night.
   - Main kal raat so nahi paya.

2. She couldn't understand the question.
   - Wo sawaal samajh nahi paayi.

3. We couldn't find the address.
   - Hum address nahi dhoond paaye.

4. He couldn't come to the party.
   - Wo party nahi aa paya.

5. They couldn't finish on time.
   - Wo time par khatam nahi kar paaye.

---

## 8. Couldn't Have (Past Impossibility)

> **Usage:** Jab past mein kuch possible hi nahi tha
> **Structure:** Subject + couldn't have + V3

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. I couldn't have done it without you.
   - Main tumhare bina yeh nahi kar sakta tha.

2. She couldn't have known about it.
   - Wo is baare mein nahi jaan sakti thi.

3. He couldn't have been there.
   - Wo wahan nahi ho sakta tha.

4. We couldn't have predicted this.
   - Hum iska andaza nahi laga sakte the.

5. They couldn't have arrived so early.
   - Wo itni jaldi nahi pahunch sakte the.
