# Must - Necessity, Obligation, Strong Deduction

---

## 1. Must (Necessity/Obligation)

> **Usage:** Jab kuch karna zaroori ho, obligation ho
> **Structure:** Subject + must + V1 (base verb)

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. You must complete your work on time.
   - Tumhe apna kaam time par khatam karna padega.

2. I must go now.
   - Mujhe abhi jaana padega.

3. You must wear a seatbelt.
   - Tumhe seatbelt lagani padegi.

4. We must follow the rules.
   - Humein rules follow karne padenge.

5. You must take your medicine.
   - Tumhe apni dawai leni padegi.

6. I must call my parents today.
   - Mujhe aaj apne parents ko call karna padega.

7. Students must attend the class.
   - Students ko class attend karni padegi.

8. You must submit the report by Friday.
   - Tumhe Friday tak report submit karni padegi.

9. We must leave early tomorrow.
   - Humein kal jaldi nikalna padega.

10. You must be careful.
    - Tumhe careful rehna padega.

---

## 2. Must (Strong Recommendation)

> **Usage:** Jab strongly recommend karna ho
> **Structure:** Subject + must + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. You must try this restaurant.
   - Tumhe yeh restaurant zaroor try karna chahiye.

2. You must watch this movie.
   - Tumhe yeh movie zaroor dekhni chahiye.

3. You must read this book.
   - Tumhe yeh book zaroor padhni chahiye.

4. You must visit Kashmir once.
   - Tumhe Kashmir ek baar zaroor jaana chahiye.

5. You must taste this food.
   - Tumhe yeh khana zaroor taste karna chahiye.

---

## 3. Must (Strong Deduction - Present)

> **Usage:** Jab kisi cheez ke baare mein pura yakeen ho (95% sure)
> **Structure:** Subject + must + V1 / must be

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. She must be tired after the long journey.
   - Lambe safar ke baad wo thaki hui hogi zaroor.

2. He must be at home now.
   - Wo abhi ghar par hoga zaroor.

3. You must be hungry.
   - Tum bhuke hoge zaroor.

4. It must be very cold outside.
   - Bahar bahut thand hogi zaroor.

5. She must know the answer.
   - Usse jawab pata hoga zaroor.

6. He must be joking.
   - Wo mazaak kar raha hoga zaroor.

7. They must be waiting for us.
   - Wo humara wait kar rahe honge zaroor.

8. This must be the right place.
   - Yeh sahi jagah hogi zaroor.

9. She must be very happy.
   - Wo bahut khush hogi zaroor.

10. He must be sleeping now.
    - Wo abhi so raha hoga zaroor.

---

## 4. Must Have (Past Deduction - Strong)

> **Usage:** Jab past ke baare mein pura yakeen ho
> **Structure:** Subject + must have + V3

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. He must have forgotten.
   - Wo zaroor bhool gaya hoga.

2. She must have missed the bus.
   - Usne zaroor bus miss kar di hogi.

3. They must have already left.
   - Wo zaroor ja chuke honge.

4. I must have left my phone at home.
   - Maine zaroor phone ghar chhod diya hoga.

5. He must have taken the wrong road.
   - Usne zaroor galat raasta liya hoga.

6. She must have heard the news.
   - Usne zaroor news suni hogi.

7. They must have finished by now.
   - Wo ab tak zaroor khatam kar chuke honge.

8. He must have worked very hard.
   - Usne zaroor bahut mehnat ki hogi.

9. You must have seen him somewhere.
   - Tumne use zaroor kahin dekha hoga.

10. She must have told him.
    - Usne use zaroor bataya hoga.

---

## 5. Must Have Been (Past State - Strong Deduction)

> **Usage:** Jab past ki state ke baare mein yakeen ho
> **Structure:** Subject + must have been + adjective/V-ing

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. It must have been difficult for you.
   - Tumhare liye zaroor mushkil raha hoga.

2. She must have been very tired.
   - Wo zaroor bahut thaki hui hogi.

3. He must have been sleeping when I called.
   - Jab maine call kiya wo zaroor so raha hoga.

4. They must have been busy.
   - Wo zaroor busy rahe honge.

5. It must have been a mistake.
   - Yeh zaroor galti rahi hogi.

---

## 6. Mustn't (Prohibition - Strong)

> **Usage:** Jab kuch karna bilkul mana ho
> **Structure:** Subject + mustn't + V1

**Daily Use Examples:**

1. You mustn't smoke here.
   - Tumhe yahaan smoke nahi karna chahiye.

2. You mustn't be late.
   - Tumhe late nahi hona chahiye.

3. You mustn't tell anyone.
   - Tumhe kisi ko nahi batana chahiye.

4. We mustn't waste water.
   - Humein paani waste nahi karna chahiye.

5. You mustn't touch that.
   - Tumhe wo nahi chhuna chahiye.

6. Students mustn't cheat in exams.
   - Students ko exam mein cheating nahi karni chahiye.

7. You mustn't forget your passport.
   - Tumhe apna passport nahi bhoolna chahiye.

8. We mustn't make noise here.
   - Humein yahaan shor nahi karna chahiye.

9. You mustn't drive without a license.
   - Tumhe bina license ke gaadi nahi chalani chahiye.

10. You mustn't give up.
    - Tumhe haar nahi maanni chahiye.

---

## 7. Must vs Have To (Difference)

> **Must:** Internal obligation (apni taraf se)
> **Have To:** External obligation (bahar se majboori)

**Examples:**

| Must (Internal) | Have To (External) |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| I must study hard. (Main khud chahta hoon) | I have to study hard. (Exam hai isliye) |
| I must lose weight. (Main chahta hoon) | I have to lose weight. (Doctor ne kaha) |
| I must call her. (Mujhe lagta hai) | I have to call her. (Boss ne kaha) |
