**Modal Verbs** ek tarah se hamare jazbaat, soch, aur intention ko express karne ka tareeka hai. Yeh humari soch ko ek structure dete hain. So, agar hum bolte hain, "I **can** do it," toh yeh ek **ability** ko express kar raha hai — "Mujhe yeh karne ki kshamata hai." Agar hum bolte hain, "You **should** try harder," toh yeh ek **suggestion** ya **advice** deta hai — "Tumhe zyada mehnat karni chahiye."

**Modal verbs** wo helping verbs hote hain jo humari baat ko thoda aur strong bana dete hain. Jaise hum kisi kaam ko karne ki **ability** (can), **permission** (may), **suggestion** (should), **obligation** (must) ya **possibility** (might) dikhana chahte hain, toh hum modal verbs ka use karte hain.

---

### **Primary Modal Verbs:**

1. **Can**

   - **Ability**: Main swimming kar sakta hoon.
   - **Permission**: Kya main jaana chahoon?
   - **Possibility**: Kal barish ho sakti hai.
2. **Could**

   - **Past Ability**: Jab main chhota tha, main bhaag sakta tha.
   - **Polite Permission**: Kya main aapka pen le sakta hoon?
   - **Possibility**: Woh kal aa sakti hai.
3. **May**

   - **Permission**: Kya main yeh kar sakta hoon?
   - **Possibility**: Woh shaayad aaj aaye.
4. **Might**

   - **Possibility**: Woh shaayad aaj nahi aayegi.
   - **Suggestion**: Tumhe yeh try karna chahiye.
5. **Shall**

   - **Future Intention**: Main tumse milne aunga.
   - **Offer**: Kya hum park chalna chahenge?
6. **Should**

   - **Advice**: Tumhe padhai karni chahiye.
   - **Expectation**: Tumhe 7 baje tak aa jaana chahiye.
7. **Will**

   - **Future Action**: Main tumse baat karunga.
   - **Promise**: Main tumhari madad karunga.
8. **Would**

   - **Polite Request**: Kya aap mujhe madad de sakte hain?
   - **Past Habit**: Main pehle har weekend cricket khelta tha.
9. **Must**

   - **Necessity**: Tumhe helmet pehna hoga.
   - **Strong Suggestion**: Tumhe yeh cake zaroor try karna chahiye, yeh zabardast hai!

---

### **Semi-Modals (Jise thoda zyada formal maana jaata hai)**

10. **Ought to**

- **Advice**: Tumhe unhe call karna chahiye.
- **Expectation**: Woh ab tak yahaan honi chahiye thi.

11. **Need**

- **Necessity (Without "to")**: Tumhe chinta karne ki zarurat nahi hai.
- **Requirement (With "to")**: Tumhe paani peene ki zarurat hai.

12. **Dare**

- **Challenge**: Main tumse keh raha hoon ke tum yahaan se kudne ki himmat rakhte ho.
- **Negative**: Woh mujhse aise baat karne ki himmat nahi rakhta.

13. **Used to**

- **Past Habit**: Main har din cricket khelta tha.
- **No longer happening**: Woh yahan rehti thi, ab nahi rehti.
