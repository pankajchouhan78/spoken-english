# Sentence Structure

> Understanding how sentences are built.
> The foundation of speaking and writing correctly.

---

## What is a Sentence?

A **sentence** is a group of words that expresses a complete thought.

**Must have:**
1. Subject (who/what)
2. Predicate (what about it)

---

## Basic Sentence Pattern: SVO

| S | V | O |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | Verb | Object |
| Kaun/Kya | Kya karta hai | Kisko/Kya |

**Examples:**
- **I** (S) **eat** (V) **rice** (O).
- **She** (S) **reads** (V) **books** (O).
- **They** (S) **play** (V) **cricket** (O).

---

## Sentence Components

| Component | Question | Example |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| **Subject** | Who/What does? | **The boy** plays. |
| **Verb** | What action? | The boy **plays**. |
| **Object** | What/Whom? | He plays **football**. |
| **Complement** | More info about S/O | She is **a teacher**. |
| **Adverbial** | When/Where/How? | He runs **fast**. |

## 5 Basic Sentence Patterns

### Pattern 1: S + V (Subject + Verb)
| Subject | Verb |
|---------|------|
| Birds | fly. |
| The sun | rises. |
| Children | play. |

### Pattern 2: S + V + O (Subject + Verb + Object)
| Subject | Verb | Object |
|---------|------|--------|
| I | eat | food. |
| She | reads | books. |
| We | watch | TV. |

### Pattern 3: S + V + C (Subject + Verb + Complement)
| Subject | Verb | Complement |
|---------|------|------------|
| He | is | a doctor. |
| They | are | happy. |
| It | seems | difficult. |

### Pattern 4: S + V + O + O (Subject + Verb + Indirect Object + Direct Object)
| Subject | Verb | IO | DO |
|---------|------|-----|-----|
| She | gave | me | a book. |
| He | sent | her | a letter. |
| Mom | made | us | dinner. |

### Pattern 5: S + V + O + C (Subject + Verb + Object + Complement)
| Subject | Verb | Object | Complement |
|---------|------|--------|------------|
| They | made | him | captain. |
| We | found | the movie | boring. |
| She | painted | the wall | blue. |

## Types of Sentences by Purpose

### 1. Declarative (Statement)
> Tells something. Ends with period (.)

- I am a student.
- The weather is nice.
- She works in a bank.

### 2. Interrogative (Question)
> Asks something. Ends with question mark (?)

- Are you a student?
- Where do you live?
- What time is it?

### 3. Imperative (Command/Request)
> Gives order or request. Ends with period (.) or (!)

- Close the door.
- Please sit down.
- Don't talk!

### 4. Exclamatory (Exclamation)
> Shows strong emotion. Ends with exclamation mark (!)

- What a beautiful day!
- How wonderful!
- I can't believe it!

---

## Types of Sentences by Structure

### 1. Simple Sentence
> One independent clause (one subject + one predicate)

- I like coffee.
- The dog barked loudly.
- She is a teacher.

### 2. Compound Sentence
> Two independent clauses joined by coordinating conjunction (FANBOYS)

- I like coffee, **but** she likes tea.
- He studied hard, **so** he passed.
- It rained, **and** we got wet.

### 3. Complex Sentence
> One independent + one dependent clause

- **When it rains**, I stay home.
- I eat **because I am hungry**.
- **If you study**, you will pass.

### 4. Compound-Complex Sentence
> Two independent + one dependent clause

- **When I arrived**, he was cooking, **and** she was cleaning.
- I will go **if you come**, **but** I won't wait long.

## Dependent (Subordinate) Clauses

| Type | Starts with | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Noun Clause | that, what, who | I know **that he is honest**. |
| Adjective Clause | who, which, that | The boy **who came** is my friend. |
| Adverb Clause | when, if, because | I'll go **when you come**. |

## Word Order Rules

### Basic Order: SVOMPT
| S | V | O | M | P | T |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Subject | Verb | Object | Manner | Place | Time |
| I | play | football | well | in the park | every day. |

### Question Word Order
**Statement:** You are happy.
**Question:** Are you happy?

**Statement:** She can swim.
**Question:** Can she swim?

### Negative Word Order
**Positive:** I like coffee.
**Negative:** I **do not** like coffee.

**Positive:** She is happy.
**Negative:** She **is not** happy.

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| He good plays. | He plays well. |
| I yesterday went. | I went yesterday. |
| Very I am happy. | I am very happy. |
| She beautiful is. | She is beautiful. |
