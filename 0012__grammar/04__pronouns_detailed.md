# Pronouns - Detailed Guide

> Pronouns replace nouns to avoid repetition.
> "Rahul is smart. Rahul works hard." → "Rahul is smart. He works hard."

---

## Types of Pronouns

1. Personal Pronouns
2. Possessive Pronouns
3. Reflexive Pronouns
4. Demonstrative Pronouns
5. Interrogative Pronouns
6. Relative Pronouns
7. Indefinite Pronouns

---

## 1. Personal Pronouns

### Subject Pronouns (Before verb)

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|--------|----------|--------|
| 1st | I | we |
| 2nd | you | you |
| 3rd | he, she, it | they |

**Examples:**
- **I** am a student.
- **She** is my sister.
- **They** are playing.

### Object Pronouns (After verb/preposition)

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|--------|----------|--------|
| 1st | me | us |
| 2nd | you | you |
| 3rd | him, her, it | them |

**Examples:**
- Give **me** the book.
- I saw **him** yesterday.
- Call **them** now.

### Complete Table

| Subject | Object | Use |
|---------|--------|-----|
| I | me | I see him. He sees me. |
| you | you | You help me. I help you. |
| he | him | He loves her. She loves him. |
| she | her | She knows me. I know her. |
| it | it | It is big. I like it. |
| we | us | We play. They call us. |
| they | them | They work. I thank them. |

## 2. Possessive Pronouns

### Possessive Adjectives (Before noun)
| Pronoun | Possessive Adj | Example |
|---------|----------------|---------|
| I | my | **my** book |
| you | your | **your** pen |
| he | his | **his** car |
| she | her | **her** bag |
| it | its | **its** tail |
| we | our | **our** house |
| they | their | **their** school |

### Possessive Pronouns (Replace noun)
| Possessive Adj | Possessive Pronoun | Example |
|----------------|-------------------|---------|
| my | mine | This book is **mine**. |
| your | yours | Is this pen **yours**? |
| his | his | That car is **his**. |
| her | hers | This bag is **hers**. |
| its | its | - |
| our | ours | The house is **ours**. |
| their | theirs | The idea was **theirs**. |

> **Note:** No apostrophe! It's "yours" not "your's" ❌

---

## 3. Reflexive Pronouns

> When subject and object are the same person.

| Subject | Reflexive |
|---------|-----------|
| I | myself |
| you | yourself |
| he | himself |
| she | herself |
| it | itself |
| we | ourselves |
| you (pl) | yourselves |
| they | themselves |

**Examples:**
- I cut **myself**. (I cut me)
- She hurt **herself**.
- They enjoyed **themselves**.
- The machine turns **itself** off.

**Emphatic Use:**
- I **myself** did it. (emphasis)
- She **herself** cooked. (emphasis)

---

## 4. Demonstrative Pronouns

> Point to specific things.

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|----------|--------|
| Near | this | these |
| Far | that | those |

**Examples:**
- **This** is my book. (near)
- **That** is your pen. (far)
- **These** are apples. (near, plural)
- **Those** are oranges. (far, plural)

---

## 5. Interrogative Pronouns

> Used to ask questions.

| Pronoun | Asks about | Example |
|---------|------------|---------|
| Who | Person (subject) | **Who** is he? |
| Whom | Person (object) | **Whom** did you see? |
| Whose | Possession | **Whose** is this? |
| What | Thing/Info | **What** is this? |
| Which | Choice | **Which** do you want? |

---

## 6. Relative Pronouns

> Connect clauses and refer back to a noun.

| Pronoun | Use | Example |
|---------|-----|---------|
| who | People (subject) | The man **who** came is my uncle. |
| whom | People (object) | The person **whom** I met was nice. |
| whose | Possession | The girl **whose** bag was lost cried. |
| which | Things | The book **which** I read was good. |
| that | People/Things | The car **that** I bought is new. |

**Examples:**
- The teacher **who** teaches us is kind.
- The book **which/that** I bought is interesting.
- The man **whose** car was stolen reported it.

---

## 7. Indefinite Pronouns

> Refer to non-specific people or things.

| Category | Pronouns |
|----------|----------|
| Some- | someone, somebody, something |
| Any- | anyone, anybody, anything |
| No- | no one, nobody, nothing |
| Every- | everyone, everybody, everything |
| Other | all, both, each, either, neither, one, other, another, few, many, much, several, some, any, none |

**Examples:**
- **Someone** is at the door.
- **Nobody** knows the answer.
- **Everyone** is here.
- I don't want **anything**.
- **Each** student has a book.
- **Some** are good, **some** are bad.

## Common Pronoun Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Rule |
|---------|---------|------|
| Me and him went. | He and I went. | Subject pronoun |
| Between you and I | Between you and me | Object after preposition |
| Give it to myself. | Give it to me. | Reflexive only when S=O |
| Theirselves | Themselves | Correct form |
| Hisself | Himself | Correct form |
| This books | These books | Plural demonstrative |
| It's tail | Its tail | Possessive has no apostrophe |
| Your's | Yours | No apostrophe |

---

## Subject vs Object Pronoun Quick Test

**Trick:** Remove other words and see what sounds right.

- "Ram and (I/me) went to the market."
  - Remove "Ram and": "(I/me) went" → **I** went ✅
  
- "She gave it to Ram and (I/me)."
  - Remove "Ram and": "She gave it to (I/me)" → to **me** ✅
