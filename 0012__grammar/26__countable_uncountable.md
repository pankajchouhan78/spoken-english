# Countable and Uncountable Nouns

> Countable: Can be counted (one, two, three...)
> Uncountable: Cannot be counted

---

## Countable Nouns

> Things you can count individually.

**Examples:**
- one book, two books
- one apple, three apples
- one car, five cars

### Characteristics:
- Have singular and plural forms
- Can use a/an with singular
- Can use numbers

| Singular | Plural |
|----------|--------|
| a book | books |
| an apple | apples |
| a car | cars |
| a child | children |

---

## Uncountable Nouns

> Things you cannot count individually.

**Examples:**
- water (not one water, two waters)
- rice (not one rice, two rices)
- information (not one information)

### Characteristics:
- No plural form
- Cannot use a/an
- Cannot use numbers directly
- Always use singular verb

### Common Uncountable Nouns

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Liquids | water, milk, tea, coffee, oil, juice |
| Food | rice, bread, sugar, salt, butter, flour, meat |
| Materials | wood, plastic, glass, paper, gold, iron |
| Abstract | love, happiness, anger, time, money, knowledge |
| Others | information, advice, news, furniture, luggage, equipment |

---

## Quantifiers

### Only with Countable

| Quantifier | Example |
|------------|---------|
| many | many books |
| few | few people |
| a few | a few apples |
| several | several students |
| a number of | a number of cars |

### Only with Uncountable

| Quantifier | Example |
|------------|---------|
| much | much water |
| little | little time |
| a little | a little money |
| a great deal of | a great deal of effort |
| an amount of | an amount of work |

### With Both

| Quantifier | Countable | Uncountable |
|------------|-----------|-------------|
| some | some books | some water |
| any | any books | any water |
| a lot of | a lot of books | a lot of water |
| lots of | lots of books | lots of water |
| plenty of | plenty of books | plenty of water |
| enough | enough books | enough water |
| no | no books | no water |
| all | all books | all water |
| most | most books | most water |

---

## Few vs A Few, Little vs A Little

| Word | Meaning | Tone | Example |
|------|---------|------|---------|
| few | almost none | negative | Few people came. (very few) |
| a few | some | positive | A few people came. (some) |
| little | almost none | negative | Little time is left. (very little) |
| a little | some | positive | A little time is left. (some) |

## Making Uncountable Countable

> Use counting words/containers

| Uncountable | Countable Form |
|-------------|----------------|
| water | a glass of water, two bottles of water |
| bread | a loaf of bread, two slices of bread |
| rice | a bowl of rice, a grain of rice |
| sugar | a spoon of sugar, a bag of sugar |
| coffee | a cup of coffee, two cups of coffee |
| information | a piece of information |
| advice | a piece of advice |
| news | a piece of news |
| furniture | a piece of furniture |
| luggage | a piece of luggage |
| paper | a sheet of paper, a piece of paper |
| music | a piece of music |
| work | a piece of work |

## Tricky Nouns

### Some nouns that look plural but are singular

| Noun | Verb | Example |
|------|------|---------|
| news | is | The news **is** bad. |
| mathematics | is | Mathematics **is** hard. |
| physics | is | Physics **is** interesting. |
| economics | is | Economics **is** useful. |
| politics | is | Politics **is** complex. |

### Some nouns that are always plural

| Noun | Verb | Example |
|------|------|---------|
| scissors | are | The scissors **are** sharp. |
| trousers | are | My trousers **are** new. |
| glasses | are | His glasses **are** broken. |
| pants | are | These pants **are** tight. |
| jeans | are | My jeans **are** blue. |

> Use "a pair of" to make them singular
> - a pair of scissors **is**
> - a pair of trousers **is**

### Nouns with same singular and plural

| Noun | Example |
|------|---------|
| sheep | one sheep, two sheep |
| fish | one fish, two fish |
| deer | one deer, two deer |
| series | one series, two series |
| species | one species, two species |

## Different Meaning When Countable

| Uncountable | Countable |
|-------------|-----------|
| glass (material) | a glass (drinking container) |
| paper (material) | a paper (newspaper/document) |
| hair (general) | a hair (single strand) |
| chicken (meat) | a chicken (bird) |
| coffee (drink) | a coffee (a cup of coffee) |
| room (space) | a room (in a house) |
| work (general) | a work (artwork) |
| experience (general) | an experience (event) |
| time (general) | a time (occasion) |

**Examples:**
- I don't have **time**. (uncountable - general)
- I've been there many **times**. (countable - occasions)
- I have long **hair**. (uncountable - general)
- There's a **hair** in my soup! (countable - single strand)

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| I need an information. | I need **information** / **some information**. |
| He gave me an advice. | He gave me **advice** / **a piece of advice**. |
| I have many works. | I have **a lot of work**. |
| The news are good. | The news **is** good. |
| I bought a furniture. | I bought **furniture** / **a piece of furniture**. |
| How much books? | How **many** books? |
| How many water? | How **much** water? |
| Less people came. | **Fewer** people came. |

---

## Less vs Fewer

| Less | Fewer |
|------|-------|
| Uncountable | Countable |
| less water | fewer bottles |
| less time | fewer hours |
| less money | fewer coins |
| less sugar | fewer spoons of sugar |
