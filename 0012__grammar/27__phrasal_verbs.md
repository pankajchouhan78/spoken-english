# Phrasal Verbs

> Verb + particle (preposition/adverb) = new meaning
> Essential for natural English!

---

## What is a Phrasal Verb?

**Verb + Particle = New meaning**

| Parts | Phrasal Verb | Meaning |
|-------|--------------|---------|
| look + up | look up | search (in dictionary) |
| give + up | give up | quit/stop trying |
| turn + on | turn on | start (machine) |

---

## Types of Phrasal Verbs

### 1. Intransitive (No Object)
| Phrasal Verb | Meaning | Example |
|--------------|---------|---------|
| wake up | jaagna | I **wake up** at 6. |
| get up | uthna | I **get up** early. |
| sit down | baithna | Please **sit down**. |
| stand up | khada hona | **Stand up** please. |
| come back | wapas aana | I'll **come back** soon. |
| go away | chale jaana | **Go away**! |
| break down | kharab ho jaana | My car **broke down**. |
| show up | pahunchna | He finally **showed up**. |

### 2. Separable (Object can go between)
| Phrasal Verb | Meaning | Example |
|--------------|---------|---------|
| turn on | chalana | **Turn on** the light. / Turn the light **on**. |
| turn off | band karna | **Turn off** the TV. / Turn the TV **off**. |
| pick up | uthana | **Pick up** the book. / Pick the book **up**. |
| put down | rakhna | **Put down** the phone. / Put the phone **down**. |
| fill out | bharna | **Fill out** the form. / Fill the form **out**. |
| take off | utaarna | **Take off** your shoes. / Take your shoes **off**. |
| throw away | fekna | **Throw away** the trash. / Throw the trash **away**. |
| give back | wapas dena | **Give back** my book. / Give my book **back**. |

> **Pronoun rule:** Must separate with pronouns!
> - Turn it **on**. ✅
> - Turn on it. ❌

### 3. Inseparable (Cannot separate)
| Phrasal Verb | Meaning | Example |
|--------------|---------|---------|
| look after | dekhbhal karna | **Look after** the baby. |
| look for | dhundhna | I'm **looking for** my keys. |
| get over | recover | He **got over** his illness. |
| run into | milna | I **ran into** an old friend. |
| come across | milna | I **came across** an old photo. |
| go through | experience | She's **going through** a hard time. |
| look into | investigate | Police will **look into** it. |
| take after | similar to | He **takes after** his father. |

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| Look after it. ❌ | Look after the baby. ✅ |
| Turn on it. | Turn **it on**. |
| I'm looking my keys for. | I'm **looking for** my keys. |
| He takes his father after. | He **takes after** his father. |
| Pick up it. | Pick **it up**. |
