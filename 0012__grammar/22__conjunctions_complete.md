# Conjunctions - Complete Guide

> Words that join words, phrases, or sentences.
> Essential for making complex sentences.

---

## Types of Conjunctions

1. Coordinating Conjunctions (FANBOYS)
2. Subordinating Conjunctions
3. Correlative Conjunctions
4. Conjunctive Adverbs

---

## 1. Coordinating Conjunctions (FANBOYS)

> Join equal parts (word + word, sentence + sentence)

| Letter | Conjunction | Use | Example |
|--------|-------------|-----|---------|
| F | For | Reason | I'm tired, **for** I worked all day. |
| A | And | Addition | Tea **and** coffee |
| N | Nor | Negative addition | He didn't call, **nor** did he text. |
| B | But | Contrast | Smart **but** lazy |
| O | Or | Choice | Tea **or** coffee? |
| Y | Yet | Contrast | Poor **yet** happy |
| S | So | Result | I was tired, **so** I slept. |

### Usage Rules

**Use comma before FANBOYS when joining sentences:**
- I was tired, **but** I kept working.
- She called, **and** I answered.

**No comma when joining words/phrases:**
- Tea **and** coffee (no comma)
- Smart **but** lazy (no comma)

## 2. Subordinating Conjunctions

> Join dependent clause to independent clause.

### Time

| Conjunction | Meaning | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| when | jab | **When** I arrived, he left. |
| while | jab tak | **While** I slept, she worked. |
| before | pehle | **Before** you go, call me. |
| after | baad mein | **After** he left, I cried. |
| until | jab tak nahi | Wait **until** I come. |
| as soon as | jaise hi | **As soon as** I saw him, I ran. |
| since | jab se | **Since** I met you, I'm happy. |
| whenever | jab bhi | **Whenever** you want. |

### Cause/Reason

| Conjunction | Meaning | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| because | kyunki | I left **because** I was tired. |
| since | kyunki | **Since** it's late, I'll go. |
| as | kyunki | **As** you know, I'm busy. |
| now that | ab jab | **Now that** you're here, let's start. |

### Condition

| Conjunction | Meaning | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| if | agar | **If** you come, I'll be happy. |
| unless | agar nahi | **Unless** you study, you'll fail. |
| provided | agar | I'll go **provided** you come. |
| as long as | jab tak | Stay **as long as** you want. |
| in case | agar | Take umbrella **in case** it rains. |

### Contrast

| Conjunction | Meaning | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| although | halanki | **Although** tired, I worked. |
| though | halanki | **Though** poor, he's happy. |
| even though | halanki | **Even though** it rained, we went. |
| while | jabki | **While** I like tea, he likes coffee. |
| whereas | jabki | He's tall, **whereas** I'm short. |

### Purpose

| Conjunction | Meaning | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| so that | taaki | Study **so that** you pass. |
| in order that | taaki | Work hard **in order that** you succeed. |

### Comparison

| Conjunction | Meaning | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| than | se zyada | He's taller **than** me. |
| as...as | jitna...utna | **As** tall **as** me. |
| as if | jaise | He talks **as if** he knows everything. |
| as though | jaise | She acts **as though** nothing happened. |

## 3. Correlative Conjunctions

> Pairs of conjunctions that work together.

| Pair | Use | Example |
|------|-----|---------|
| both...and | Addition | **Both** Ram **and** Shyam came. |
| either...or | Choice | **Either** tea **or** coffee. |
| neither...nor | Negative | **Neither** he **nor** I went. |
| not only...but also | Emphasis | She's **not only** smart **but also** kind. |
| whether...or | Choice | I don't know **whether** to go **or** stay. |
| rather...than | Preference | I'd **rather** stay **than** go. |
| no sooner...than | Time | **No sooner** had I arrived **than** he left. |
| hardly...when | Time | **Hardly** had I sat **when** the phone rang. |
| as...as | Comparison | She's **as** tall **as** me. |
| so...that | Result | **So** tired **that** I slept. |

## 4. Conjunctive Adverbs

> Connect sentences; show relationship.

### Addition
| Word | Example |
|------|---------|
| also | I'm tired. I'm **also** hungry. |
| besides | It's late. **Besides**, I'm tired. |
| furthermore | It's cheap. **Furthermore**, it's good. |
| moreover | He's smart. **Moreover**, he's hardworking. |
| in addition | She sings. **In addition**, she dances. |

### Contrast
| Word | Example |
|------|---------|
| however | I tried. **However**, I failed. |
| nevertheless | It rained. **Nevertheless**, we went. |
| on the other hand | Tea is good. **On the other hand**, coffee is better. |
| instead | I won't go. **Instead**, I'll stay. |
| otherwise | Study. **Otherwise**, you'll fail. |

### Result
| Word | Example |
|------|---------|
| therefore | I'm tired. **Therefore**, I'll rest. |
| consequently | He lied. **Consequently**, I left. |
| thus | He worked hard. **Thus**, he succeeded. |
| hence | I'm busy. **Hence**, I can't come. |
| as a result | It rained. **As a result**, we stayed home. |

### Time
| Word | Example |
|------|---------|
| then | I ate. **Then** I slept. |
| meanwhile | I cooked. **Meanwhile**, he cleaned. |
| afterwards | We ate. **Afterwards**, we talked. |
| finally | We waited. **Finally**, he came. |

## Punctuation with Conjunctions

### Coordinating (FANBOYS)
- Sentence**, conjunction** sentence.
- I was tired**, but** I worked.

### Subordinating
- **Subordinate clause, main clause.** (comma needed)
- **Because** I was tired**, I slept.**

- Main clause **subordinate clause.** (no comma)
- I slept **because I was tired.**

### Conjunctive Adverbs
- Sentence**. Adverb,** sentence.
- I was tired**. However,** I worked.

- Sentence**; adverb,** sentence.
- I was tired**; however,** I worked.

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| Although he was tired but he worked. | Although he was tired, he worked. |
| Because I was tired so I slept. | Because I was tired, I slept. |
| I like not only tea but coffee. | I like not only tea but also coffee. |
| Neither he or I went. | Neither he nor I went. |
| I will go weather you come or not. | I will go whether you come or not. |
