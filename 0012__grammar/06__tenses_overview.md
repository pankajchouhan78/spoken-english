# Tenses - Complete Overview

> Tenses tell us WHEN an action happens.
> 3 Times × 4 Aspects = 12 Tenses

---

## The 12 Tenses at a Glance

| | Simple | Continuous | Perfect | Perfect Continuous |
|---|--------|------------|---------|-------------------|
| **Present** | I eat | I am eating | I have eaten | I have been eating |
| **Past** | I ate | I was eating | I had eaten | I had been eating |
| **Future** | I will eat | I will be eating | I will have eaten | I will have been eating |

---

## PRESENT TENSES

### 1. Simple Present
**Form:** V1 (add s/es for he/she/it)

| Subject | Form | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| I/You/We/They | V1 | I **eat** rice. |
| He/She/It | V1 + s/es | She **eats** rice. |

**Uses:**
- Habits: I **wake up** at 6 AM.
- Facts: Water **boils** at 100°C.
- Schedule: The train **leaves** at 9 PM.

### 2. Present Continuous
**Form:** am/is/are + V-ing

| Subject | Form | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| I | am + V-ing | I **am eating**. |
| He/She/It | is + V-ing | She **is eating**. |
| You/We/They | are + V-ing | They **are eating**. |

**Uses:**
- Now: I **am studying** now.
- Temporary: He **is staying** here this week.
- Future plan: I **am meeting** him tomorrow.

### 3. Present Perfect
**Form:** have/has + V3

| Subject | Form | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| I/You/We/They | have + V3 | I **have eaten**. |
| He/She/It | has + V3 | She **has eaten**. |

**Uses:**
- Past to now: I **have lived** here for 5 years.
- Experience: I **have visited** Paris.
- Just finished: I **have just finished** work.

### 4. Present Perfect Continuous
**Form:** have/has + been + V-ing

| Subject | Form | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| I/You/We/They | have been + V-ing | I **have been eating**. |
| He/She/It | has been + V-ing | She **has been eating**. |

**Uses:**
- Duration from past: I **have been waiting** for 2 hours.
- Recent continuous: It **has been raining**.

---

## PAST TENSES

### 5. Simple Past
**Form:** V2

| All Subjects | Form | Example |
|--------------|------|---------|
| I/He/She/You/They | V2 | I **ate** rice. |

**Uses:**
- Completed action: I **went** to Delhi yesterday.
- Past habits: I **played** cricket as a child.

### 6. Past Continuous
**Form:** was/were + V-ing

| Subject | Form | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| I/He/She/It | was + V-ing | I **was eating**. |
| You/We/They | were + V-ing | They **were eating**. |

**Uses:**
- Past ongoing: I **was sleeping** at 10 PM.
- Interrupted: I **was cooking** when he came.

### 7. Past Perfect
**Form:** had + V3

| All Subjects | Form | Example |
|--------------|------|---------|
| All | had + V3 | I **had eaten** before he came. |

**Uses:**
- Before another past: I **had finished** when he arrived.
- Reported speech: He said he **had seen** the movie.

### 8. Past Perfect Continuous
**Form:** had + been + V-ing

| All Subjects | Form | Example |
|--------------|------|---------|
| All | had been + V-ing | I **had been waiting** for 2 hours. |

**Uses:**
- Duration before past event: I **had been studying** for 3 hours before the exam.

---

## FUTURE TENSES

### 9. Simple Future
**Form:** will + V1

| All Subjects | Form | Example |
|--------------|------|---------|
| All | will + V1 | I **will eat** later. |

**Uses:**
- Future action: I **will go** tomorrow.
- Promise: I **will help** you.
- Prediction: It **will rain** today.

### 10. Future Continuous
**Form:** will + be + V-ing

| All Subjects | Form | Example |
|--------------|------|---------|
| All | will be + V-ing | I **will be eating** at 8 PM. |

**Uses:**
- Ongoing in future: I **will be working** tomorrow.

### 11. Future Perfect
**Form:** will + have + V3

| All Subjects | Form | Example |
|--------------|------|---------|
| All | will have + V3 | I **will have eaten** by then. |

**Uses:**
- Completed before future time: I **will have finished** by 5 PM.

### 12. Future Perfect Continuous
**Form:** will + have + been + V-ing

| All Subjects | Form | Example |
|--------------|------|---------|
| All | will have been + V-ing | I **will have been working** for 5 years. |

**Uses:**
- Duration until future time: By 2025, I **will have been teaching** for 10 years.

---

## Quick Summary Table

| Tense | Formula | Key Word |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Simple Present | V1 / V1+s | always, usually |
| Present Continuous | am/is/are + V-ing | now, currently |
| Present Perfect | have/has + V3 | just, already, ever |
| Present Perfect Cont. | have/has been + V-ing | for, since |
| Simple Past | V2 | yesterday, ago |
| Past Continuous | was/were + V-ing | while, when |
| Past Perfect | had + V3 | before, after |
| Past Perfect Cont. | had been + V-ing | for (before past) |
| Simple Future | will + V1 | tomorrow, next |
| Future Continuous | will be + V-ing | at this time tomorrow |
| Future Perfect | will have + V3 | by (future time) |
| Future Perfect Cont. | will have been + V-ing | for (until future) |
