# Articles - A, An, The

> Articles are small words that come before nouns.
> A/An = Indefinite (any one)
> The = Definite (specific one)

---

## The Three Articles

| Article | Type | Use |
|---------|------|-----|
| a | Indefinite | Before consonant sounds |
| an | Indefinite | Before vowel sounds |
| the | Definite | Specific/known noun |

---

## A vs AN

### Rule: Based on SOUND, not spelling!

**A** - before consonant SOUNDS
- a book
- a cat
- a university (sounds like "yoo")
- a one-way street (sounds like "wun")
- a European country (sounds like "yoo")

**AN** - before vowel SOUNDS
- an apple
- an orange
- an hour (silent h, sounds like "our")
- an honest man (silent h)
- an umbrella
- an MBA (sounds like "em")
- an X-ray (sounds like "ex")

### Tricky Examples

| Word | Article | Why |
|------|---------|-----|
| university | a | sounds like "yoo" (consonant) |
| umbrella | an | sounds like "uh" (vowel) |
| hour | an | "h" is silent |
| hotel | a | "h" is pronounced |
| honest | an | "h" is silent |
| European | a | sounds like "yoo" |
| FBI agent | an | "F" sounds like "ef" |

## When to Use A/AN

### 1. First mention of something
- I saw **a** dog. **The** dog was big.
- She has **a** car.

### 2. One of many (non-specific)
- I need **a** pen. (any pen)
- She is **a** teacher. (one of many teachers)

### 3. With jobs/professions
- He is **a** doctor.
- She is **an** engineer.

### 4. With "what" exclamations
- What **a** beautiful day!
- What **an** amazing story!

### 5. Per/each meaning
- once **a** week (per week)
- Rs. 100 **a** kilo

---

## When to Use THE

### 1. Already mentioned
- I saw a dog. **The** dog was brown.
- I bought a book. **The** book is interesting.

### 2. Only one exists
- **The** sun rises in **the** east.
- **The** moon is bright.
- **The** President of India

### 3. Specific/known to listener
- Please close **the** door. (we both know which door)
- Where is **the** bathroom?

### 4. Superlatives
- **The** best student
- **The** tallest building
- **The** most beautiful

### 5. Ordinal numbers
- **The** first time
- **The** second chapter
- **The** last page

### 6. Musical instruments
- play **the** piano
- play **the** guitar

### 7. Geographical names

**USE THE:**
| Type | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Rivers | the Ganga, the Nile |
| Oceans/Seas | the Pacific, the Indian Ocean |
| Mountain ranges | the Himalayas, the Alps |
| Deserts | the Sahara, the Thar |
| Groups of islands | the Maldives, the Philippines |
| Countries with Republic/Kingdom | the UK, the USA, the UAE |

**NO THE:**
| Type | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Continents | Asia, Europe |
| Most countries | India, China, France |
| Cities | Delhi, Mumbai, London |
| Single mountains | Mount Everest |
| Single islands | Sri Lanka |
| Lakes | Lake Victoria |

---

## When NOT to Use Articles (Zero Article)

### 1. Plural/Uncountable nouns (general)
- **Dogs** are loyal. (dogs in general)
- **Water** is essential.
- I like **music**.
- **Honesty** is important.

### 2. Meals (general)
- I had **breakfast** at 8.
- Let's have **lunch**.

### 3. Languages
- I speak **English**.
- She knows **Hindi**.

### 4. Sports
- I play **cricket**.
- She likes **tennis**.

### 5. Days/Months (general)
- See you on **Monday**.
- I was born in **January**.

### 6. Academic subjects
- I study **mathematics**.
- She teaches **history**.

### 7. Certain places (purpose)
| Place | Purpose | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| school | to study | go to school |
| church | to pray | go to church |
| hospital | treatment | go to hospital |
| prison | as prisoner | go to prison |
| bed | to sleep | go to bed |
| work | to work | go to work |

> BUT: "the school" when referring to building
> - The school is big. (building)
> - He goes to school. (purpose: study)

## Articles with Proper Nouns

| With THE | Without THE |
|----------|-------------|
| the Taj Mahal | India |
| the Great Wall | Mount Everest |
| the White House | London |
| the Ganges | Lake Superior |
| the Times of India | Time Magazine |
| the Himalayas | K2 |

---

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| I have headache. | I have **a** headache. |
| She is doctor. | She is **a** doctor. |
| I like the music. | I like music. (general) |
| I play the cricket. | I play cricket. |
| He is an European. | He is **a** European. |
| I need a advice. | I need advice. (uncountable) |
| Sun is hot. | **The** sun is hot. |
| I go to the school daily. | I go to school daily. |
