# Nouns - Detailed Guide

> Nouns are naming words - they name people, places, things, and ideas.
> The most basic and essential part of speech.

---

## Types of Nouns

### 1. Common vs Proper Nouns

| Common Noun | Proper Noun |
|-------------|-------------|
| boy | Rahul |
| city | Mumbai |
| country | India |
| river | Ganga |
| day | Monday |
| month | January |
| book | Harry Potter |

> **Rule:** Proper nouns start with CAPITAL letter.

---

### 2. Concrete vs Abstract Nouns

| Concrete (Can sense) | Abstract (Cannot sense) |
|---------------------|------------------------|
| table | love |
| water | happiness |
| music | freedom |
| perfume | courage |
| ice cream | honesty |

---

### 3. Countable vs Uncountable Nouns

| Countable | Uncountable |
|-----------|-------------|
| one apple, two apples | rice ✓, rices ✗ |
| a book, three books | water ✓, waters ✗ |
| a chair, many chairs | information ✓ |
| an idea, several ideas | advice ✓ |

**Common Uncountable Nouns:**
- Food: rice, bread, sugar, salt, flour, meat
- Liquids: water, milk, oil, juice, coffee, tea
- Materials: wood, glass, paper, plastic, gold
- Abstract: information, advice, news, knowledge, work
- Weather: rain, snow, sunshine, thunder

> **Tip:** Use "some," "much," "a lot of" with uncountable nouns.
> Wrong: Give me an advice ❌
> Right: Give me some advice ✅

## Singular and Plural

### Regular Plurals

| Rule | Singular | Plural |
|------|----------|--------|
| Add -s | book | books |
| Add -es (s, x, ch, sh, o) | bus, box, church | buses, boxes, churches |
| y → ies (consonant + y) | baby, city | babies, cities |
| y → ys (vowel + y) | boy, day | boys, days |
| f/fe → ves | wife, knife, leaf | wives, knives, leaves |

### Irregular Plurals

| Singular | Plural |
|----------|--------|
| man | men |
| woman | women |
| child | children |
| tooth | teeth |
| foot | feet |
| mouse | mice |
| goose | geese |
| person | people |
| ox | oxen |

### Same Singular & Plural

| Word | Example |
|------|---------|
| sheep | one sheep, many sheep |
| fish | one fish, many fish |
| deer | one deer, many deer |
| aircraft | one aircraft, many aircraft |
| species | one species, many species |

### Always Plural (No Singular)

scissors, trousers, pants, glasses, clothes, stairs, news

> The **scissors are** on the table. ✅
> The scissor is... ❌

## Possessive Nouns

> Shows ownership - add 's or '

### Singular Possessive: Add 's
| Noun | Possessive | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| boy | boy's | The **boy's** book |
| Rahul | Rahul's | **Rahul's** car |
| teacher | teacher's | The **teacher's** desk |

### Plural Possessive: Add ' after s
| Noun | Possessive | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| boys | boys' | The **boys'** books |
| teachers | teachers' | The **teachers'** room |
| students | students' | The **students'** bags |

### Irregular Plural Possessive: Add 's
| Noun | Possessive | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| children | children's | **Children's** toys |
| men | men's | **Men's** clothing |
| women | women's | **Women's** section |

---

## Noun as Subject vs Object

| Position | Role | Example |
|----------|------|---------|
| Before verb | Subject | **The dog** barks. |
| After verb | Object | I love **the dog**. |
| After preposition | Object | Give it to **the dog**. |

---

## Compound Nouns

> Two words combined to make new meaning.

| Type | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Noun + Noun | toothpaste, sunlight, football |
| Adjective + Noun | blackboard, greenhouse, software |
| Verb + Noun | swimming pool, washing machine |
| Noun + Verb | sunrise, haircut, rainfall |
| Preposition + Noun | underground, overcoat, input |
