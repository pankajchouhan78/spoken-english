# Passive Voice

> Active: The subject does the action.
> Passive: The subject receives the action.

---

## Active vs Passive

| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| Ram eats rice. | Rice is eaten by Ram. |
| She wrote a letter. | A letter was written by her. |
| They will help you. | You will be helped by them. |

**Active:** Subject → does → Object
**Passive:** Object → receives → by Subject

---

## When to Use Passive

1. **Unknown doer:** My bike was stolen. (Who stole? Unknown)
2. **Doer not important:** The bridge was built in 1950.
3. **Formal writing:** The report was submitted.
4. **Focus on action:** English is spoken here.

---

## Passive Voice Formula

### Active to Passive Conversion

| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| Subject + Verb + Object | Object + BE + V3 + by Subject |

### Step-by-step:
1. Object becomes Subject
2. Add correct form of BE
3. Change verb to V3 (past participle)
4. Add "by" + original subject (optional)

---

## Passive in All Tenses

| Tense | Active | Passive (BE + V3) |
|-------|--------|-------------------|
| Simple Present | He **writes** letters. | Letters **are written** by him. |
| Present Continuous | He **is writing** a letter. | A letter **is being written** by him. |
| Present Perfect | He **has written** a letter. | A letter **has been written** by him. |
| Simple Past | He **wrote** a letter. | A letter **was written** by him. |
| Past Continuous | He **was writing** a letter. | A letter **was being written** by him. |
| Past Perfect | He **had written** a letter. | A letter **had been written** by him. |
| Simple Future | He **will write** a letter. | A letter **will be written** by him. |
| Future Perfect | He **will have written** a letter. | A letter **will have been written** by him. |

---

## BE Forms in Passive

| Tense | BE Form |
|-------|---------|
| Simple Present | am/is/are |
| Present Continuous | am/is/are + being |
| Present Perfect | have/has + been |
| Simple Past | was/were |
| Past Continuous | was/were + being |
| Past Perfect | had + been |
| Simple Future | will + be |
| Future Perfect | will + have + been |

---

## Modal Verbs in Passive

**Formula:** Modal + be + V3

| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| He **can do** it. | It **can be done** by him. |
| She **must finish** it. | It **must be finished** by her. |
| They **should help** us. | We **should be helped** by them. |
| You **may use** this. | This **may be used** by you. |
| I **have to complete** it. | It **has to be completed** by me. |

---

## Pronoun Changes

| Active (Subject) | Passive (by + Object) |
|------------------|----------------------|
| I | by me |
| we | by us |
| you | by you |
| he | by him |
| she | by her |
| it | by it |
| they | by them |

**Example:**
- Active: **She** loves **him**.
- Passive: **He** is loved by **her**.

---

## Questions in Passive

### Yes/No Questions
| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| Did he write the letter? | Was the letter written by him? |
| Will they help you? | Will you be helped by them? |

### WH Questions
| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| Who wrote this book? | By whom was this book written? |
| What did she make? | What was made by her? |
| When will they announce results? | When will results be announced? |

---

## Negative Passive

**Formula:** Subject + BE + not + V3

| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| He doesn't write letters. | Letters are not written by him. |
| She didn't make tea. | Tea was not made by her. |
| They won't help us. | We won't be helped by them. |

---

## Verbs That Cannot Be Passive

**Intransitive verbs** (no object) cannot be made passive:
- happen, occur, exist, sleep, die, arrive, appear

| Wrong ❌ | Why |
|---------|-----|
| The accident was happened. | "happen" is intransitive |
| The baby was slept. | "sleep" is intransitive |

---

## GET Passive (Informal)

> More common in spoken English.

| BE Passive | GET Passive |
|------------|-------------|
| He was hurt. | He **got** hurt. |
| They were married. | They **got** married. |
| I was fired. | I **got** fired. |
| She was paid. | She **got** paid. |

---

## Common Passive Expressions

| Expression | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| I was born in 1990. | Mera janam 1990 mein hua. |
| It is said that... | Kaha jaata hai ki... |
| He is known as... | Woh jaana jaata hai... |
| It is believed that... | Mana jaata hai ki... |
| The work is done. | Kaam ho gaya. |
| English is spoken here. | Yahan English boli jaati hai. |
| Smoking is not allowed. | Smoking allowed nahi hai. |
| You are invited. | Aapko invite kiya gaya hai. |

## Practice Conversions

| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| The dog bit the boy. | The boy was bitten by the dog. |

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| The letter written by him. | The letter **was** written by him. |
| Rice is eat by me. | Rice is **eaten** by me. |
| The work is being do. | The work is being **done**. |
| He was gave a gift. | He was **given** a gift. |
