## Prepositions of TIME

### AT, ON, IN

| Preposition | Used for | Examples |
|-------------|----------|----------|
| AT | Specific time | at 5 o'clock, at noon, at midnight, at night |
| ON | Days/Dates | on Monday, on 15th August, on my birthday |
| IN | Longer periods | in January, in 2024, in summer, in the morning |

### Time Prepositions Table

| Preposition | Use | Example |
|-------------|-----|---------|
| at | Clock time | at 3 PM, at 10:30 |
| at | Mealtimes | at breakfast, at lunch |
| at | Specific times | at night, at noon, at midnight |
| at | Holidays | at Diwali, at Christmas |
| on | Days | on Sunday, on Friday |
| on | Dates | on July 4th, on 26th January |
| on | Special days | on my birthday, on New Year's Day |
| in | Months | in March, in December |
| in | Years | in 2020, in 1990 |
| in | Seasons | in summer, in winter |
| in | Parts of day | in the morning, in the evening |
| in | Centuries | in the 21st century |

### Other Time Prepositions

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| before | pehle | before 5 PM |
| after | baad | after lunch |
| during | dauraan | during the meeting |
| for | ke liye | for 2 hours |
| since | se (past to now) | since 2020 |
| until/till | tak | until tomorrow |
| by | tak (deadline) | by Monday |
| from...to | se...tak | from 9 to 5 |
| within | ke andar | within a week |

**For vs Since:**
- **for** + duration: for 2 hours, for 5 years
- **since** + point in time: since morning, since 2020

## Prepositions of PLACE

### AT, ON, IN

| Preposition | Used for | Examples |
|-------------|----------|----------|
| AT | Specific point | at the door, at the bus stop, at home |
| ON | Surface | on the table, on the wall, on the floor |
| IN | Enclosed space | in the box, in the room, in Delhi |

## No Preposition Needed

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| discuss about | discuss (something) |
| enter into | enter (a place) |
| reach at | reach (a place) |
| return back | return |
| repeat again | repeat |
| go to home | go home |
| explain me | explain to me |

| on night | at night |
| in the morning of Sunday | on Sunday morning |
