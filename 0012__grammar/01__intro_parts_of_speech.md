# Introduction to Grammar & Parts of Speech

> Grammar is the set of rules that govern how we use language.
> Understanding parts of speech is the foundation of grammar.

---

## What is Grammar?

**Grammar** = Rules for correct language use

| Aspect | What it Covers |
|--------|----------------|
| Syntax | Word order in sentences |
| Morphology | Word forms and changes |
| Semantics | Meaning of words |
| Punctuation | Marks like . , ? ! |

---

## The 8 Parts of Speech

Every word in English belongs to one of these categories:

| # | Part of Speech | Hindi | Function | Example |
|---|----------------|-------|----------|---------|
| 1 | Noun | Sangya | Names things | book, Delhi, happiness |
| 2 | Pronoun | Sarvanaam | Replaces nouns | he, she, it, they |
| 3 | Verb | Kriya | Shows action/state | run, eat, is, have |
| 4 | Adjective | Visheshan | Describes nouns | big, beautiful, red |
| 5 | Adverb | Kriya Visheshan | Modifies verbs/adj | quickly, very, always |
| 6 | Preposition | Sambandh Suchak | Shows relationship | in, on, at, by |
| 7 | Conjunction | Sanyojak | Joins words/sentences | and, but, because |
| 8 | Interjection | Vismayadi Bodhak | Expresses emotion | Wow! Oh! Ouch! |

---

## 1. NOUN (Sangya)

> Names a person, place, thing, or idea.

| Type | Description | Examples |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Common | General names | boy, city, book |
| Proper | Specific names | Rahul, Mumbai, India |
| Abstract | Ideas/feelings | love, happiness, freedom |
| Collective | Groups | team, family, crowd |
| Countable | Can be counted | apple, chair, student |
| Uncountable | Cannot be counted | water, rice, information |

---

## 2. PRONOUN (Sarvanaam)

> Replaces a noun to avoid repetition.

| Type | Examples | Use |
|------|----------|-----|
| Personal | I, you, he, she, it, we, they | Replace people/things |
| Possessive | mine, yours, his, hers, ours | Show ownership |
| Reflexive | myself, yourself, himself | Refers back to subject |
| Demonstrative | this, that, these, those | Point to things |
| Interrogative | who, what, which | Ask questions |
| Relative | who, which, that | Connect clauses |
| Indefinite | someone, anyone, nobody | Unspecified |

---

## 3. VERB (Kriya)

> Shows action or state of being.

| Type | Description | Examples |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Action | Physical/mental action | run, think, eat, write |
| Linking | Connects subject to info | is, am, are, was, were |
| Helping | Assists main verb | do, does, did, have, will |
| Modal | Shows possibility/ability | can, could, may, must |

---

## 4. ADJECTIVE (Visheshan)

> Describes or modifies a noun.

| Type | Description | Examples |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Descriptive | Describes quality | beautiful, tall, old |
| Quantitative | Shows amount | many, few, some |
| Demonstrative | Points out | this, that, these |
| Possessive | Shows ownership | my, your, his, her |
| Interrogative | Asks questions | which, what, whose |

---

## 5. ADVERB (Kriya Visheshan)

> Modifies verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs.

| Type | Question | Examples |
|------|----------|----------|
| Manner | How? | slowly, quickly, carefully |
| Time | When? | now, today, yesterday |
| Place | Where? | here, there, everywhere |
| Frequency | How often? | always, never, sometimes |
| Degree | How much? | very, quite, extremely |

---

## 6. PREPOSITION (Sambandh Suchak)

> Shows relationship between words.

| Type | Examples | Use |
|------|----------|-----|
| Time | at, on, in, during | Shows when |
| Place | at, on, in, under | Shows where |
| Direction | to, from, towards | Shows movement |
| Agent | by, with | Shows doer |

---

## 7. CONJUNCTION (Sanyojak)

> Joins words, phrases, or sentences.

| Type | Examples | Use |
|------|----------|-----|
| Coordinating | and, but, or, so | Equal parts |
| Subordinating | because, although, if | Dependent clause |
| Correlative | both...and, either...or | Pairs |

---

## 8. INTERJECTION (Vismayadi Bodhak)

> Expresses sudden emotions.

| Emotion | Interjections |
|---------|---------------|
| Surprise | Wow! Oh! |
| Pain | Ouch! Ow! |
| Joy | Hurray! Yay! |
| Disgust | Yuck! Ew! |
| Agreement | Yes! Okay! |
| Attention | Hey! Hello! |

---

## Quick Memory Trick

**N P V A A P C I** = Noun, Pronoun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb, Preposition, Conjunction, Interjection

> "**N**ever **P**ut **V**egetables **A**nd **A**pples **P**ast **C**losing **I**nterval"

## Same Word, Different Part of Speech

| Word | As Noun | As Verb | As Adjective |
|------|---------|---------|--------------|
| run | a run | I run | run time |
| light | the light | light the candle | light blue |
| work | hard work | I work | work clothes |
| fast | a fast | to fast | fast car |
| water | the water | water the plants | water bottle |
