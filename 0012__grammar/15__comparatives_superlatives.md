# Comparatives and Superlatives

> Comparing things using adjectives and adverbs.
> Comparative: Between two things
> Superlative: Among three or more

---

## Three Degrees of Comparison

| Degree | Use | Example |
|--------|-----|---------|
| Positive | No comparison | She is **tall**. |
| Comparative | Two things | She is **taller** than me. |
| Superlative | Three+ things | She is the **tallest** in class. |

---

## Forming Comparatives and Superlatives

### Short Adjectives (1 syllable): Add -er / -est

| Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| tall | taller | tallest |
| fast | faster | fastest |
| old | older | oldest |
| young | younger | youngest |
| long | longer | longest |
| short | shorter | shortest |
| cheap | cheaper | cheapest |
| small | smaller | smallest |

### Spelling Rules

| Rule | Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| Ends in -e: add -r/-st | large | larger | largest |
| CVC: double consonant | big | bigger | biggest |
| CVC: double consonant | hot | hotter | hottest |
| CVC: double consonant | thin | thinner | thinnest |
| Ends in -y: change to -ier/-iest | happy | happier | happiest |
| Ends in -y: change to -ier/-iest | easy | easier | easiest |

### Long Adjectives (2+ syllables): Use more / most

| Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| beautiful | more beautiful | most beautiful |
| expensive | more expensive | most expensive |
| interesting | more interesting | most interesting |
| difficult | more difficult | most difficult |
| comfortable | more comfortable | most comfortable |
| important | more important | most important |

### Two-Syllable Adjectives (Both ways possible)

| Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| clever | cleverer / more clever | cleverest / most clever |
| simple | simpler / more simple | simplest / most simple |
| narrow | narrower / more narrow | narrowest / most narrow |
| quiet | quieter / more quiet | quietest / most quiet |

## Irregular Comparatives and Superlatives

| Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| good | better | best |
| bad | worse | worst |
| far | farther/further | farthest/furthest |
| little | less | least |
| much/many | more | most |
| old | older/elder | oldest/eldest |

**Examples:**
- This is **better** than that.
- She is the **best** student.
- The situation is getting **worse**.
- This is the **worst** day ever.

---

## Using Comparatives

### Structure: Comparative + than

| Sentence |
|----------|
| She is **taller than** me. |
| This book is **more interesting than** that one. |
| He runs **faster than** I do. |
| My car is **cheaper than** yours. |

### Less + Adjective + than (Opposite comparison)

| Sentence |
|----------|
| This is **less expensive than** that. |
| He is **less intelligent than** her. |
| It's **less difficult than** I thought. |

---

## Using Superlatives

### Structure: the + Superlative (+ in/of)

| Sentence |
|----------|
| She is **the tallest** in the class. |
| This is **the most beautiful** place I've seen. |
| He is **the best** player on the team. |
| It was **the worst** day of my life. |

**in** = places, groups
**of** = a number/set

- the tallest **in** the class
- the best **of** all

---

## Comparing Equals: As...As

### Positive: as + adjective + as

| Sentence | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| She is **as tall as** me. | Same height |
| This is **as good as** that. | Same quality |
| He runs **as fast as** I do. | Same speed |

### Negative: not as + adjective + as

| Sentence | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| She is **not as tall as** me. | She is shorter |
| This is **not as good as** that. | This is worse |
| He doesn't run **as fast as** me. | He is slower |

---

## Double Comparatives

### Repeating change: Comparative + and + Comparative

| Sentence | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| It's getting **hotter and hotter**. | Temperature rising |
| She's becoming **more and more beautiful**. | Increasing beauty |
| The exam is getting **harder and harder**. | Increasing difficulty |

### Cause and effect: The + Comparative, the + Comparative

| Sentence | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **The more** you practice, **the better** you get. | More practice = better results |
| **The harder** you work, **the more** you earn. | More work = more money |
| **The sooner**, **the better**. | Early is good |
| **The bigger**, **the better**. | Bigger is preferred |

## Comparing Adverbs

| Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| fast | faster | fastest |
| hard | harder | hardest |
| early | earlier | earliest |
| carefully | more carefully | most carefully |
| quickly | more quickly | most quickly |
| well | better | best |
| badly | worse | worst |

**Examples:**
- She works **harder than** me.
- He drives **more carefully than** his brother.
- She sings **the best** of all.

---

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| more better | **better** |
| more taller | **taller** |
| most biggest | **biggest** |
| gooder | **better** |
| badder | **worse** |
| more easy | **easier** |
| the most tall | **the tallest** |
| taller than me | taller than **I am** (formal) |
| She is taller than. | She is taller than **me/him**. |

---

## Modifiers with Comparatives

| Modifier | Example |
|----------|---------|
| much | He is **much taller** than me. |
| a lot | This is **a lot better**. |
| far | It's **far more expensive**. |
| a little | She's **a little older**. |
| slightly | It's **slightly cheaper**. |
| even | This is **even worse**. |
