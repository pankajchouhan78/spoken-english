# When to Use "s" or "es" with Verbs

> **Usage:** Present Simple Tense mein He/She/It ke saath verb mein "s" ya "es" lagta hai.

---

## Quick Rule Summary

| Subject          | Verb Form      | Example            |
| ---------------- | -------------- | ------------------ |
| I, You, We, They | V1 (no change) | I play, They eat   |
| He, She, It      | V1 + s/es      | He plays, She eats |

---

## Rule 1: No "s/es" with I, You, We, They

Inke saath verb ka original base form use hota hai.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I go to office every day.

   - Main roz office jaata hoon.
2. You speak English well.

   - Tum acchi English bolte ho.
3. We eat dinner at 8 PM.

   - Hum 8 baje dinner karte hain.
4. They play cricket on weekends.

   - Wo weekend par cricket khelte hain.
5. I like this movie.

   - Mujhe yeh movie pasand hai.
6. You understand everything.

   - Tum sab samajhte ho.
7. We need more time.

   - Humein aur time chahiye.
8. They know the answer.

   - Unhe jawab pata hai.
9. I work from home.

   - Main ghar se kaam karta hoon.
10. You look great today.

    - Tum aaj bahut acche lag rahe ho.

---

## Rule 2: Add "s" with He, She, It (Normal Verbs)

Jab verb kisi special letter par end nahi hota.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. He works in a bank.

   - Wo bank mein kaam karta hai.
2. She reads books daily.

   - Wo roz kitaabein padhti hai.
3. He plays football.

   - Wo football khelta hai.
4. She drinks tea every morning.

   - Wo har subah chai peeti hai.
5. It rains a lot here.

   - Yahaan bahut baarish hoti hai.
6. He eats healthy food.

   - Wo healthy khana khata hai.
7. She writes very well.

   - Wo bahut accha likhti hai.
8. He drives carefully.

   - Wo dhyan se drive karta hai.
9. She speaks three languages.

   - Wo teen bhashaein bolti hai.
10. It costs 500 rupees.

    - Iska daam 500 rupaye hai.
11. He lives in Delhi.

    - Wo Delhi mein rehta hai.
12. She cooks delicious food.

    - Wo mazedaar khana banati hai.
13. He earns a good salary.

    - Wo acchi salary kamata hai.
14. She sings beautifully.

    - Wo bahut sundar gaati hai.
15. It takes 2 hours to reach.

    - Pahunchne mein 2 ghante lagte hain.

---

## Rule 3: Add "es" (Special Endings)

### When verb ends in: -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -z, -o

| Ending | Verb  | With "es" |
| ------ | ----- | --------- |
| -s     | pass  | passes    |
| -ss    | miss  | misses    |
| -sh    | wash  | washes    |
| -ch    | watch | watches   |
| -x     | fix   | fixes     |
| -z     | buzz  | buzzes    |
| -o     | go    | goes      |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. He watches TV every night.

   - Wo har raat TV dekhta hai.
2. She washes her hands before eating.

   - Wo khane se pehle haath dhoti hai.
3. He goes to gym daily.

   - Wo roz gym jaata hai.
4. She teaches English at school.

   - Wo school mein English padhati hai.
5. He finishes work by 6 PM.

   - Wo 6 baje tak kaam khatam karta hai.
6. She catches the bus at 8 AM.

   - Wo 8 baje bus pakadti hai.
7. He does his homework.

   - Wo apna homework karta hai.
8. She fixes computers.

   - Wo computers theek karti hai.
9. He misses his family.

   - Wo apni family ko miss karta hai.
10. She passes this road daily.

    - Wo roz is raaste se guzarti hai.
11. He brushes his teeth twice.

    - Wo din mein do baar brush karta hai.
12. She pushes the door open.

    - Wo darwaza dhakka dekar kholti hai.
13. He touches his phone too much.

    - Wo apna phone bahut baar chhoota hai.
14. She relaxes on weekends.

    - Wo weekends par aaraam karti hai.
15. He mixes everything together.

    - Wo sab kuch mila deta hai.

---

## Rule 4: Consonant + "y" → Change to "ies"

When verb ends in consonant + y, change "y" to "ies".

| Verb  | With He/She/It |
| ----- | -------------- |
| study | studies        |
| carry | carries        |
| try   | tries          |
| cry   | cries          |
| fly   | flies          |
| worry | worries        |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. He studies every night.

   - Wo har raat padhta hai.
2. She carries her bag everywhere.

   - Wo apna bag har jagah le jaati hai.
3. He tries his best.

   - Wo apni puri koshish karta hai.
4. She worries too much.

   - Wo bahut zyada tension leti hai.
5. The baby cries at night.

   - Bachcha raat ko rota hai.
6. Time flies quickly.

   - Waqt jaldi guzar jaata hai.
7. She copies my style.

   - Wo meri style copy karti hai.
8. He hurries to office.

   - Wo jaldi jaldi office jaata hai.
9. She applies for new jobs.

   - Wo nayi jobs ke liye apply karti hai.
10. He replies to all messages.

    - Wo saari messages ka reply karta hai.

---

## Rule 5: Vowel + "y" → Just Add "s"

When verb ends in vowel (a, e, i, o, u) + y, just add "s".

| Verb  | With He/She/It |
| ----- | -------------- |
| play  | plays          |
| stay  | stays          |
| enjoy | enjoys         |
| say   | says           |
| pay   | pays           |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. He plays cricket well.

   - Wo accha cricket khelta hai.
2. She stays at home on Sundays.

   - Wo Sunday ko ghar par rehti hai.
3. He enjoys his work.

   - Wo apne kaam mein maza leta hai.
4. She says the truth always.

   - Wo hamesha sach bolti hai.
5. He pays his bills on time.

   - Wo apne bills time par bharta hai.
6. She displays good behavior.

   - Wo accha behavior dikhati hai.
7. He obeys his parents.

   - Wo apne parents ki baat maanta hai.
8. She prays every morning.

   - Wo har subah prayer karti hai.
9. He surveys the market.

   - Wo market ka survey karta hai.
10. She conveys the message.

    - Wo message pahunchati hai.

## Quick Reference Table

| Verb Ending                   | Rule              | Example                      |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Normal verbs                  | Add "s"           | work → works                |
| -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -z, -o | Add "es"          | go → goes, watch → watches |
| Consonant + y                 | Change y to "ies" | study → studies             |
| Vowel + y                     | Just add "s"      | play → plays                |
