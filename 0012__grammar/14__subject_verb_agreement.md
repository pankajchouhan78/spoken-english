# Subject-Verb Agreement

> The verb must agree with the subject in number.
> Singular subject → Singular verb
> Plural subject → Plural verb

---

## Basic Rule

| Subject | Verb | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Singular (he/she/it) | adds s/es | He **plays**. |
| Plural (they/we) | no s | They **play**. |
| I | no s | I **play**. |
| You | no s | You **play**. |

---

## With BE Verbs

| Subject | Present | Past |
|---------|---------|------|
| I | am | was |
| He/She/It | is | was |
| You/We/They | are | were |

---

## With HAS/HAVE

| Subject | Verb |
|---------|------|
| I/You/We/They | have |
| He/She/It | has |

- I **have** a car.
- She **has** a car.

---

## With DOES/DO

| Subject | Verb |
|---------|------|
| I/You/We/They | do |
| He/She/It | does |

- **Do** you like coffee?
- **Does** she like coffee?

---

## Tricky Cases

### 1. Words Between Subject and Verb

> Ignore words between subject and verb.

| Sentence | Subject | Verb |
|----------|---------|------|
| The list of items **is** long. | list (singular) | is |
| The boys in the class **are** smart. | boys (plural) | are |
| One of the students **is** absent. | one (singular) | is |
| The quality of the products **has** improved. | quality | has |

### 2. Either...Or / Neither...Nor

> Verb agrees with the NEAREST subject.

| Sentence | Nearest Subject | Verb |
|----------|-----------------|------|
| Either he or I **am** going. | I | am |
| Either I or he **is** going. | he | is |
| Neither the teacher nor the students **were** there. | students | were |
| Neither the students nor the teacher **was** there. | teacher | was |

### 3. Collective Nouns

> Usually singular (the group as one unit).

| Subject | Verb | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| team | is/was | The team **is** playing. |
| family | is/was | My family **is** big. |
| class | is/was | The class **is** noisy. |
| audience | is/was | The audience **is** clapping. |
| government | is/was | The government **has** decided. |
| committee | is/was | The committee **has** approved. |

> Can be plural if members act individually:
> The team **are** arguing among themselves.

### 4. Indefinite Pronouns

**Singular (use singular verb):**
- everyone, everybody, everything
- someone, somebody, something
- anyone, anybody, anything
- no one, nobody, nothing
- each, every, either, neither

| Example |
|---------|
| Everyone **is** here. |
| Nobody **knows** the answer. |
| Each student **has** a book. |
| Either **is** fine. |

**Plural (use plural verb):**
- both, few, many, several, others

| Example |
|---------|
| Both **are** correct. |
| Many **have** tried. |
| Few **know** the truth. |

**Singular or Plural (depends on noun):**
- all, any, most, some, none

| Example | Why |
|---------|-----|
| All the water **is** gone. | water = uncountable |
| All the books **are** here. | books = plural |
| Some of the cake **is** left. | cake = uncountable |
| Some of the cookies **are** left. | cookies = plural |

### 5. There is / There are

> Verb agrees with the noun AFTER it.

| Sentence | Subject | Verb |
|----------|---------|------|
| There **is** a book on the table. | book (singular) | is |
| There **are** books on the table. | books (plural) | are |
| There **was** a problem. | problem | was |
| There **were** problems. | problems | were |

### 6. Titles and Names

> Always singular (even if looks plural).

| Example |
|---------|
| "The Lord of the Rings" **is** a great book. |
| The United States **is** a big country. |
| Mathematics **is** difficult. |
| The news **is** shocking. |

### 7. Uncountable Nouns

> Always singular.

| Example |
|---------|
| Money **is** important. |
| Information **is** available. |
| Water **is** essential. |
| Furniture **is** expensive. |
| Advice **is** helpful. |
| News **is** good. |

### 8. Plural-Looking Singular Nouns

| Noun | Verb | Example |
|------|------|---------|
| news | singular | The news **is** good. |
| physics | singular | Physics **is** hard. |
| mathematics | singular | Math **is** easy. |
| economics | singular | Economics **is** interesting. |
| measles | singular | Measles **is** dangerous. |

### 9. Plural Form, Plural Meaning

| Noun | Verb | Example |
|------|------|---------|
| scissors | plural | The scissors **are** sharp. |
| trousers | plural | My trousers **are** new. |
| glasses | plural | His glasses **are** broken. |
| pants | plural | These pants **are** tight. |

### 10. Fractions and Percentages

> Depends on the noun after "of."

| Example | Why |
|---------|-----|
| Half of the cake **is** eaten. | cake = singular |
| Half of the cookies **are** eaten. | cookies = plural |
| 50% of the work **is** done. | work = uncountable |
| 50% of the students **are** present. | students = plural |

---

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| He don't know. | He **doesn't** know. |
| The news are good. | The news **is** good. |
| Everyone are here. | Everyone **is** here. |
| Mathematics are hard. | Mathematics **is** hard. |
| One of them are coming. | One of them **is** coming. |
| There is many books. | There **are** many books. |
| The team are winning. | The team **is** winning. |
| Neither of them are right. | Neither of them **is** right. |
