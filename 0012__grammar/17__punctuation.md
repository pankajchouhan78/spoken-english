# Punctuation

> Marks that help readers understand written text.
> Essential for clear writing.

---

## Common Punctuation Marks

| Mark | Name | Use |
|------|------|-----|
| . | Period/Full stop | End of statement |
| , | Comma | Separate items, pause |
| ? | Question mark | End of question |
| ! | Exclamation mark | Strong emotion |
| : | Colon | Introduce list/explanation |
| ; | Semicolon | Join related sentences |
| ' | Apostrophe | Possession, contractions |
| " " | Quotation marks | Direct speech |
| - | Hyphen | Compound words |
| — | Dash | Emphasis, interruption |
| ( ) | Parentheses | Extra information |
| ... | Ellipsis | Omission, trailing off |

---

## Period / Full Stop (.)

**Uses:**
- End of statement: I am a student.
- Abbreviations: Mr., Dr., etc.
- Decimals: 3.14, Rs. 99.99

---

## Comma (,)

**Uses:**
1. **Separate list items:** apples, oranges, and bananas
2. **After intro words:** However, I disagree.
3. **Before FANBOYS:** I was tired, but I worked.
4. **Non-essential info:** My brother, who lives in Mumbai, is a doctor.
5. **Direct address:** Rahul, please come here.
6. **Dates:** July 4, 2024

---

## Question Mark (?)

**Uses:**
- Direct questions: What is your name?
- NOT indirect: He asked where I lived. (no ?)

---

## Exclamation Mark (!)

**Uses:**
- Strong emotions: Wow! Amazing!
- Commands: Stop! Be quiet!

---

## Colon (:)

**Uses:**
- Introduce list: I need: pen, paper, pencil.
- Explanation: Remember this: practice makes perfect.
- Time: 10:30 AM

---

## Semicolon (;)

**Uses:**
- Join related sentences: I was tired; I went to bed.
- Complex lists: Mumbai, India; Tokyo, Japan; Paris, France.

---

## Apostrophe (')

### Possession
| Singular | Plural |
|----------|--------|
| Ram's book | The boys' books |
| The dog's tail | Children's toys |

### Contractions
| Full | Short |
|------|-------|
| I am | I'm |
| do not | don't |
| it is | it's |

### Common Mistakes
| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| it's tail | its tail (possession) |
| your coming | you're coming (you are) |

---

## Quotation Marks (" ")

**Uses:**
- Direct speech: He said, "I am happy."
- Titles: I read "How to Learn English."
- Special terms: The "expert" knew nothing.

---

## Hyphen (-)

**Uses:**
- Compound adjectives: a well-known actor, five-year-old child
- Numbers: twenty-one, ninety-nine
- Prefixes: self-confidence, ex-husband

---

## Capital Letters

| Use | Example |
|-----|---------|
| First word | The book is good. |
| Proper nouns | India, Rahul, Monday |
| Titles | Mr., Dr., Professor |
| I (pronoun) | He and I went. |
| Days/Months | Monday, January |
| Languages | English, Hindi |

---

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| its raining | it's raining |
| boys's books | boys' books |
| He asked where am I? | He asked where I was. |
| Dont | Don't |
