# Relative Clauses

> Clauses that give more information about a noun.
> Connected by relative pronouns: who, which, that, whose, where, when

---

## What is a Relative Clause?

**Two sentences:**
1. I know the man.
2. The man lives next door.

**Combined with relative clause:**
- I know the man **who lives next door**.

The relative clause "who lives next door" describes "the man."

---

## Relative Pronouns

| Pronoun | Used for | Example |
|---------|----------|---------|
| who | People | The man **who** called is my uncle. |
| whom | People (object) | The person **whom** I met was nice. |
| which | Things/Animals | The book **which** I read was good. |
| that | People/Things | The car **that** I bought is new. |
| whose | Possession | The girl **whose** bag was lost cried. |
| where | Places | The house **where** I was born is old. |
| when | Time | The day **when** we met was special. |

---

## WHO vs WHOM

### WHO - Subject of the clause
- The man **who** called me is my friend.
  - (who = the man; the man called me)

### WHOM - Object of the clause
- The man **whom** I called is my friend.
  - (whom = the man; I called the man)

> **Tip:** If you can replace with "him/her" → use whom
> If you can replace with "he/she" → use who

---

## WHICH vs THAT

Both can be used for things:
- The book **which** I read...
- The book **that** I read...

**BUT:**
- **That** is more common in spoken English
- **Which** is more formal
- Use **which** after commas (non-defining)

---

## Two Types of Relative Clauses

### 1. Defining (Essential)
> Necessary to identify which person/thing we mean.
> NO COMMAS

| Sentence | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| The man **who stole my phone** was caught. | Which man? The one who stole. |
| The book **that you gave me** is interesting. | Which book? The one you gave. |

### 2. Non-Defining (Extra Information)
> Extra information, not essential.
> USE COMMAS

| Sentence | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| My brother, **who lives in Delhi**, is a doctor. | I have one brother; extra info about him. |
| The Taj Mahal, **which is in Agra**, is beautiful. | We know Taj Mahal; extra info. |

> **Cannot use "that" in non-defining clauses!**
> My brother, that lives in Delhi ❌
> My brother, who lives in Delhi ✅

---

## Omitting Relative Pronouns

### Can omit when pronoun is OBJECT of the clause:

| Full | Shortened |
|------|-----------|
| The man **whom** I met was kind. | The man I met was kind. ✅ |
| The book **that** I read was good. | The book I read was good. ✅ |
| The movie **which** we watched was boring. | The movie we watched was boring. ✅ |

### Cannot omit when pronoun is SUBJECT:

| Sentence | Can omit? |
|----------|-----------|
| The man **who** called me... | No ❌ (who is subject) |
| The book **that** fell... | No ❌ (that is subject) |

---

## WHOSE (Possession)

> Shows something belongs to someone.

| Sentence | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| The boy **whose** father is a doctor... | The boy; his father is a doctor |
| The woman **whose** car was stolen... | The woman; her car was stolen |
| The house **whose** roof is red... | The house; its roof is red |

---

## WHERE (Place)

> Refers to places.

| Sentence | Same as |
|----------|---------|
| The city **where** I was born... | The city in which I was born... |
| The restaurant **where** we ate... | The restaurant at which we ate... |
| The house **where** I live... | The house in which I live... |

---

## WHEN (Time)

> Refers to time.

| Sentence | Same as |
|----------|---------|
| The day **when** we met... | The day on which we met... |
| The year **when** I graduated... | The year in which I graduated... |
| The moment **when** I saw her... | The moment at which I saw her... |

---

## Prepositions with Relative Clauses

### Informal (Preposition at end)
- The person **who** I spoke **to**...
- The hotel **which** we stayed **at**...
- The man **whom** I work **with**...

### Formal (Preposition before pronoun)
- The person **to whom** I spoke...
- The hotel **at which** we stayed...
- The man **with whom** I work...

> After preposition, use **whom** (not who) and **which** (not that)

---

## WHAT as Relative

> What = the thing(s) that

| Sentence | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| I don't know **what** he wants. | ...the thing that he wants |
| **What** you said was true. | The thing that you said... |
| I'll do **what** I can. | ...the things that I can do |

---

## Common Relative Clause Patterns

| Pattern | Example |
|---------|---------|
| The person who... | The person **who helped me** was kind. |
| The thing that... | The thing **that I need** is time. |
| The place where... | The place **where I work** is far. |
| The reason why... | The reason **why I'm late** is traffic. |
| The way (that)... | I don't like the way **(that) he talks**. |
| All (that)... | I did all **(that) I could**. |
| Everything (that)... | Everything **(that) he said** was true. |
| Anyone who... | Anyone **who comes** is welcome. |

---

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| The man which called... | The man **who** called... |
| My friend, that lives... | My friend, **who** lives... |
| The reason because... | The reason **why**... |
| The book who I read... | The book **which/that** I read... |
| The person which I met... | The person **whom/who** I met... |
