# Advanced Grammar Structures

> Complex structures for fluent English.

---

## Cleft Sentences

> Emphasize a specific part of the sentence.

### It-cleft: It + be + emphasized part + that/who

| Normal | Cleft (emphasis) |
|--------|------------------|
| Ram broke the window. | **It was Ram who** broke the window. |
| I need money. | **It is money that** I need. |
| She left yesterday. | **It was yesterday that** she left. |

### What-cleft: What + clause + be + emphasized part

| Normal | Cleft |
|--------|-------|
| I need help. | **What I need is** help. |
| She wants money. | **What she wants is** money. |
| He said something. | **What he said was** interesting. |

## Inversion

> Reverse subject-verb order for emphasis.

### After negative adverbs

| Normal | Inverted |
|--------|----------|
| I have never seen this. | **Never have I** seen this. |
| He rarely comes. | **Rarely does he** come. |
| She hardly knew. | **Hardly did she** know. |
| We not only won. | **Not only did we** win. |

### After "only"

| Normal | Inverted |
|--------|----------|
| I realized it only then. | **Only then did I** realize it. |
| We can succeed only by working hard. | **Only by working hard can we** succeed. |

### In conditionals (without "if")

| With if | Without if (inverted) |
|---------|----------------------|
| If I had known... | **Had I known**... |
| If you should need... | **Should you need**... |
| If it were not for... | **Were it not for**... |

## Participle Clauses

> Shorten sentences using -ing or -ed forms.

### Present Participle (-ing)

| Full | Shortened |
|------|-----------|
| When I arrived, I saw him. | **Arriving**, I saw him. |
| Because she was tired, she slept. | **Being tired**, she slept. |
| While he was walking, he fell. | **Walking**, he fell. |

### Past Participle (-ed/V3)

| Full | Shortened |
|------|-----------|
| Because it was written in Hindi, I understood it. | **Written in Hindi**, I understood it. |
| When it is seen from above, it looks small. | **Seen from above**, it looks small. |

### Perfect Participle (Having + V3)

| Full | Shortened |
|------|-----------|
| After I had finished work, I went home. | **Having finished** work, I went home. |
| Because he had eaten, he wasn't hungry. | **Having eaten**, he wasn't hungry. |

## Subjunctive Mood

> Expressing wishes, suggestions, demands.

### After suggest, recommend, insist, demand

| Structure | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| Verb + that + subject + base verb | I suggest that he **go**. (not goes) |
| | She demanded that he **leave**. |
| | It's important that she **be** there. |

### If I were (unreal)

- If I **were** you, I would go. (not was)
- I wish I **were** taller. (not was)

---

## Ellipsis (Omitting Words)

> Leaving out words to avoid repetition.

| Full | With Ellipsis |
|------|---------------|
| I can swim and she can swim too. | I can swim and she can too. |
| He will come if he wants to come. | He will come if he wants to. |
| A: Are you coming? B: Yes, I am coming. | A: Are you coming? B: Yes, I am. |

---

## Fronting

> Moving elements to the front for emphasis.

| Normal | Fronted |
|--------|---------|
| I really like this book. | **This book** I really like. |
| She's very talented. | **Talented** she certainly is. |
| I've never seen such beauty. | **Such beauty** I've never seen. |

---

## Complex Conditionals

### Mixed Conditionals

**Past condition → Present result:**
- If I **had studied** medicine, I **would be** a doctor now.

**Present condition → Past result:**
- If I **were** taller, I **would have played** basketball.

### Wish + Would (Complaints)

| Structure | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| Wish + would | I wish you **would stop** talking. |
| | I wish it **would stop** raining. |

---

## Emphasis Structures

### Do/Does/Did for emphasis

| Normal | Emphatic |
|--------|----------|
| I like this. | I **do** like this! |
| She came. | She **did** come! |
| He works hard. | He **does** work hard! |

### Own for emphasis

| Example |
|---------|
| I saw it with my **own** eyes. |
| She made it with her **own** hands. |

---

## Reported Questions (Complex)

| Direct | Indirect |
|--------|----------|
| "What would you do if you won?" | He asked what I would do if I won. |
| "Had you ever been there?" | She asked if I had ever been there. |

---

## Common Errors in Advanced Structures

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| I suggest that he goes. | I suggest that he **go**. |
| If I was you | If I **were** you |
| Never I have seen | Never **have I** seen |
| Hardly he knew | Hardly **did he** know |
