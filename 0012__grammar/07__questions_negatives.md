# Questions & Negatives

> How to ask questions and make negative sentences.
> Essential for everyday communication.

---

## Types of Questions

### 1. Yes/No Questions
> Answer: Yes or No

**Formula:** Helping Verb + Subject + Main Verb + ?

| Statement | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| You are happy. | **Are** you happy? |
| She can swim. | **Can** she swim? |
| They have finished. | **Have** they finished? |
| He will come. | **Will** he come? |

**With DO/DOES/DID:**
| Statement | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| You like coffee. | **Do** you like coffee? |
| She plays tennis. | **Does** she play tennis? |
| They went home. | **Did** they go home? |

### 2. WH Questions
> Need information, not yes/no.

| WH Word | Asks About | Example |
|---------|------------|---------|
| What | Thing/Action | **What** is this? |
| Who | Person | **Who** are you? |
| Where | Place | **Where** do you live? |
| When | Time | **When** is the meeting? |
| Why | Reason | **Why** are you late? |
| How | Manner | **How** are you? |
| Which | Choice | **Which** one do you want? |
| Whose | Possession | **Whose** book is this? |

**Formula:** WH + Helping Verb + Subject + Main Verb + ?

| Question |
|----------|
| **What** do you want? |
| **Where** does she live? |
| **When** did they arrive? |
| **Why** are you crying? |
| **How** will you go? |

### 3. Choice Questions
> Give options with "or"

- Do you want tea **or** coffee?
- Is it hot **or** cold?
- Will you come today **or** tomorrow?

### 4. Tag Questions
> Confirm what you think is true.

**Rule:** Positive statement → Negative tag
**Rule:** Negative statement → Positive tag

| Statement | Tag |
|-----------|-----|
| You are coming, | aren't you? |
| She is smart, | isn't she? |
| They can swim, | can't they? |
| He doesn't know, | does he? |
| You won't forget, | will you? |
| They haven't left, | have they? |

## Question Formation by Tense

| Tense | Statement | Question |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| Simple Present | You work here. | Do you work here? |
| Simple Present (he) | He works here. | Does he work here? |
| Present Continuous | She is sleeping. | Is she sleeping? |
| Present Perfect | They have eaten. | Have they eaten? |
| Simple Past | You went there. | Did you go there? |
| Past Continuous | He was reading. | Was he reading? |
| Past Perfect | She had left. | Had she left? |
| Simple Future | They will come. | Will they come? |

## Making Negative Sentences

### With BE (is, am, are, was, were)
**Add NOT after BE:**

| Positive | Negative |
|----------|----------|
| I am happy. | I am **not** happy. |
| She is here. | She is **not** here. |
| They are ready. | They are **not** ready. |
| He was late. | He was **not** late. |
| We were there. | We were **not** there. |

### With Helping Verbs
**Add NOT after helping verb:**

| Positive | Negative |
|----------|----------|
| I can swim. | I can**not** swim. |
| She will come. | She will **not** come. |
| They have finished. | They have **not** finished. |
| He should go. | He should **not** go. |
| We must leave. | We must **not** leave. |

### With DO/DOES/DID
**Use DO/DOES/DID + NOT + V1:**

| Positive | Negative |
|----------|----------|
| I like coffee. | I **do not** like coffee. |
| She plays tennis. | She **does not** play tennis. |
| They went home. | They **did not** go home. |
| He eats meat. | He **does not** eat meat. |
| We studied. | We **did not** study. |

## Negative Words

| Word | Example |
|------|---------|
| no | I have **no** money. |
| not | I do **not** know. |
| never | I **never** lie. |
| nothing | I saw **nothing**. |
| nobody/no one | **Nobody** came. |
| nowhere | I went **nowhere**. |
| neither | **Neither** is correct. |
| none | **None** of them came. |

> **Double Negative Rule:** Don't use two negatives in one sentence!
> Wrong: I don't have no money. ❌
> Right: I don't have any money. ✅

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| Does she likes? | Does she like? |
| Did you went? | Did you go? |
| Why you are late? | Why are you late? |
| What means this? | What does this mean? |
| He don't know. | He doesn't know. |
| She not is here. | She is not here. |
| I amn't sure. | I'm not sure. |

## Short Answers

| Question | Yes Answer | No Answer |
|----------|------------|-----------|
| Are you ready? | Yes, I am. | No, I'm not. |
| Does she know? | Yes, she does. | No, she doesn't. |
| Did they come? | Yes, they did. | No, they didn't. |
| Can you swim? | Yes, I can. | No, I can't. |
| Will he come? | Yes, he will. | No, he won't. |
| Have you eaten? | Yes, I have. | No, I haven't. |
