# Reported Speech (Indirect Speech)

> Direct: He said, "I am happy."
> Reported: He said that he was happy.

---

## Direct vs Reported Speech

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Exact words in quotes | Reported without quotes |
| "I am tired." | He said that he was tired. |
| Uses quotation marks | No quotation marks |

---

## Basic Structure

**Direct:** He said, "I am busy."
**Reported:** He said (that) he was busy.

> "That" is optional but commonly used.

---

## Tense Changes (Backshift)

When reporting verb is in PAST (said, told, asked):

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Present Simple → | Past Simple |
| "I **eat** rice." | He said he **ate** rice. |
| Present Continuous → | Past Continuous |
| "I **am eating**." | He said he **was eating**. |
| Present Perfect → | Past Perfect |
| "I **have eaten**." | He said he **had eaten**. |
| Past Simple → | Past Perfect |
| "I **ate** rice." | He said he **had eaten** rice. |
| Past Continuous → | Past Perfect Continuous |
| "I **was eating**." | He said he **had been eating**. |
| Will → | Would |
| "I **will** go." | He said he **would** go. |
| Can → | Could |
| "I **can** swim." | He said he **could** swim. |
| May → | Might |
| "I **may** come." | He said he **might** come. |

---

## No Change Cases

### 1. Universal truths
- Direct: He said, "The sun rises in the east."
- Reported: He said that the sun **rises** in the east. ✅

### 2. Reporting verb in present
- Direct: He says, "I am happy."
- Reported: He says that he **is** happy. ✅

### 3. Past Perfect stays same
- Direct: "I had finished."
- Reported: He said he **had finished**. ✅

### 4. Would, could, should, might stay same
- Direct: "I would help."
- Reported: He said he **would** help. ✅

## Pronoun Changes

| Direct | Reported |
|--------|----------|
| I | he/she |
| me | him/her |
| my | his/her |
| we | they |
| us | them |
| our | their |
| you (to me) | me/I |

**Example:**
- Direct: He said, "**I** love **my** job."
- Reported: He said that **he** loved **his** job.

---

## Time/Place Word Changes

| Direct | Reported |
|--------|----------|
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| this week | that week |
| last week | the week before |
| next week | the following week |
| ago | before |
| here | there |
| this | that |
| these | those |

**Example:**
- Direct: "I'll come **tomorrow**."
- Reported: He said he would come **the next day**.

## Reporting Statements

| Reporting Verb | Direct | Reported |
|----------------|--------|----------|
| said | "I am tired." | He said he was tired. |
| told | "I am tired." | He told me he was tired. |
| mentioned | "I am busy." | He mentioned that he was busy. |
| explained | "I was late." | He explained that he had been late. |

> **said** - no object needed
> **told** - needs object (told me/him/her)

---

## Reporting Questions

### Yes/No Questions: Use if/whether

| Direct | Reported |
|--------|----------|
| "Are you happy?" | He asked **if/whether** I was happy. |
| "Do you like coffee?" | He asked **if** I liked coffee. |
| "Can you swim?" | He asked **whether** I could swim. |

### WH Questions: Keep WH word

| Direct | Reported |
|--------|----------|
| "Where do you live?" | He asked where I lived. |
| "What is your name?" | He asked what my name was. |
| "Why did you come?" | He asked why I had come. |
| "When will you return?" | He asked when I would return. |

> **Note:** Question becomes statement word order.
> Wrong: He asked where did I live. ❌
> Right: He asked where I lived. ✅

---

## Reporting Commands/Requests

### Commands: Use tell + to infinitive

| Direct | Reported |
|--------|----------|
| "Sit down." | He told me **to sit** down. |
| "Don't talk." | He told me **not to talk**. |
| "Open the door." | He told me **to open** the door. |

### Requests: Use ask + to infinitive

| Direct | Reported |
|--------|----------|
| "Please help me." | He asked me **to help** him. |
| "Please don't go." | He asked me **not to go**. |
| "Could you wait?" | He asked me **to wait**. |

---

## Reporting Suggestions

| Direct | Reported |
|--------|----------|
| "Let's go." | He suggested **going**. / He suggested that we **(should) go**. |
| "Why don't we eat?" | He suggested **eating**. |
| "How about watching TV?" | He suggested **watching** TV. |

---

## Other Reporting Verbs

| Verb | Example |
|------|---------|
| admit | He admitted that he was wrong. |
| agree | She agreed to help. |
| apologize | He apologized for being late. |
| complain | She complained that it was cold. |
| deny | He denied stealing the money. |
| insist | She insisted on paying. |
| promise | He promised to come. |
| refuse | She refused to go. |
| warn | He warned me not to go there. |
| advise | She advised me to study. |

---

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| He said me that... | He **told** me that... |
| He asked that where... | He asked **where**... |
| He asked where do I live. | He asked where I **lived**. |
| He told to sit. | He told **me** to sit. |
