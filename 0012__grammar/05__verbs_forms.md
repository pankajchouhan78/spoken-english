# Verbs - Forms and Types

> Verbs are action words - they tell what someone/something does.
> The most important part of any sentence.

---

## Types of Verbs

### 1. Action Verbs
> Show physical or mental action.

| Physical | Mental |
|----------|--------|
| run, walk, eat | think, believe, know |
| write, dance, play | understand, remember, forget |
| cook, drive, swim | love, hate, want |

### 2. Linking Verbs (State of Being)
> Connect subject to information about it.

**Common Linking Verbs:**
- be (is, am, are, was, were)
- become, seem, appear
- look, sound, smell, taste, feel

**Examples:**
- She **is** a teacher. (is = she = teacher)
- The food **smells** delicious.
- He **became** famous.

### 3. Helping (Auxiliary) Verbs
> Help the main verb.

| Type | Verbs | Example |
|------|-------|---------|
| Primary | be, have, do | I **am** eating. |
| Modal | can, could, will, would, may, might, must, shall, should | I **can** swim. |

## Three Principal Forms of Verbs

| V1 (Base) | V2 (Past) | V3 (Past Participle) |
|-----------|-----------|---------------------|
| Present | Simple Past | With have/has/had |
| go | went | gone |
| eat | ate | eaten |
| write | wrote | written |
| play | played | played |

| Rule | Example |

## -ing Form (Present Participle)

**Used for:** Continuous tenses, gerunds, adjectives

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Add -ing | play → playing |
| Drop -e, add -ing | make → making |
| Double consonant + -ing | run → running |
| ie → ying | lie → lying |

## Subject-Verb Agreement

### Rule: Singular subject = Singular verb

| Subject | Verb | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| I | am/was/have | I **am** here. |
| He/She/It | is/was/has | She **is** here. |
| You/We/They | are/were/have | They **are** here. |

### Adding -s/-es to Verbs

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Add -s | He play**s** |
| Add -es (s, x, ch, sh, o) | She watch**es** |
| consonant + y → ies | He stud**ies** |
| vowel + y → ys | She play**s** |

**Examples:**
- I play. He play**s**.
- I go. She go**es**.
- I study. He stud**ies**.
- I watch. She watch**es**.

## Transitive vs Intransitive Verbs

### Transitive: Needs an object
- I eat **rice**. (rice = object)
- She reads **books**.
- He broke **the glass**.

### Intransitive: No object needed
- The baby sleeps.
- Birds fly.
- He laughed.

### Some verbs can be both:
- I **eat** rice. (transitive)
- I **eat** at 8 PM. (intransitive)

---

## Stative vs Dynamic Verbs

### Stative (No continuous form)
> Describe states, not actions.

- I **know** the answer. ✅
- I **am knowing** the answer. ❌

**Common stative verbs:** know, believe, understand, want, need, like, love, hate, prefer, own, belong, see, hear, seem, appear

### Dynamic (Can use continuous)
> Describe actions.

- I **am eating** now. ✅
- I **am running**. ✅
