# Negative Sentences

> How to make sentences negative in English.
> Adding NOT or negative words to sentences.

---

## Basic Negative Formation

### With BE verb: Add NOT after BE

| Positive | Negative |
|----------|----------|
| I **am** happy. | I **am not** happy. |
| She **is** here. | She **is not** here. |
| They **are** coming. | They **are not** coming. |
| He **was** late. | He **was not** late. |
| We **were** there. | We **were not** there. |

### With Auxiliary Verbs: Add NOT after auxiliary

| Positive | Negative |
|----------|----------|
| I **have** finished. | I **have not** finished. |
| She **has** eaten. | She **has not** eaten. |
| He **will** come. | He **will not** come. |
| They **can** swim. | They **can not** swim. |
| She **should** go. | She **should not** go. |

### With DO/DOES/DID (Simple Tenses)

| Tense | Positive | Negative |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Simple Present (I/you/we/they) | I like coffee. | I **do not** like coffee. |
| Simple Present (he/she/it) | She likes coffee. | She **does not** like coffee. |
| Simple Past | He went there. | He **did not** go there. |

> **Note:** After do not / does not / did not, use base verb (V1)
> She does not likes ❌ → She does not **like** ✅

## Negative Words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| no | koi nahi | I have **no** money. |
| not | nahi | I am **not** happy. |
| never | kabhi nahi | I **never** lie. |
| nothing | kuch nahi | I saw **nothing**. |
| nobody/no one | koi nahi | **Nobody** came. |
| nowhere | kahin nahi | I went **nowhere**. |
| neither | dono nahi | **Neither** is good. |
| none | koi bhi nahi | **None** of them came. |
| hardly | mushkil se | I **hardly** know him. |
| barely | mushkil se | I **barely** passed. |
| seldom | bahut kam | I **seldom** go there. |
| rarely | bahut kam | I **rarely** eat out. |

## Double Negatives - AVOID!

> In English, two negatives make a positive!

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| I don't have **no** money. | I don't have **any** money. |
| I didn't see **nobody**. | I didn't see **anybody**. |
| I can't find it **nowhere**. | I can't find it **anywhere**. |
| I don't want **nothing**. | I don't want **anything**. |

---

## NO vs NOT

### NO + Noun
- I have **no** money. (= I don't have any money)
- There is **no** time. (= There isn't any time)
- **No** problem! (= Not a problem)

### NOT + Verb
- I do **not** have money.
- I am **not** going.
- She did **not** come.

## Negative Prefixes

| Prefix | Examples |
|--------|----------|
| un- | unhappy, unable, unfair |
| in- | incorrect, incomplete, inactive |
| im- | impossible, impatient, impolite |
| il- | illegal, illiterate |
| ir- | irregular, irresponsible |
| dis- | disagree, disappear, dishonest |
| non- | non-stop, non-violent |
| mis- | misunderstand, misuse |

**Examples:**
- He is **unhappy**. (= He is not happy)
- It's **impossible**. (= It's not possible)
- I **disagree**. (= I don't agree)

## Neither...Nor

> Both things are negative.

| Structure | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| Neither + noun + nor + noun | **Neither** Ram **nor** Shyam came. |
| Neither of | **Neither of** them is here. |

## Negative Questions

| Question | Implication |
|----------|-------------|
| **Don't** you like it? | I thought you'd like it! |
| **Isn't** he coming? | I expected him to come. |
| **Haven't** you finished? | You should have finished! |
| **Can't** you help? | Please help! |

---

## Short Negative Answers

| Question | Negative Answer |
|----------|-----------------|
| Are you coming? | No, I'm **not**. |
| Do you like it? | No, I **don't**. |
| Can you swim? | No, I **can't**. |
| Have you eaten? | No, I **haven't**. |
| Will you come? | No, I **won't**. |

---

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| She not is here. | She **is not** here. |
| I no like it. | I **don't** like it. |
| He doesn't likes. | He doesn't **like**. |
| I didn't went. | I didn't **go**. |
| I don't have no money. | I don't have **any** money. |
| I am not have money. | I **don't** have money. |
