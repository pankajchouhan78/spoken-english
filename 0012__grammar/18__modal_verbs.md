# Modal Verbs

> Special helping verbs that show ability, possibility, permission, etc.
> Can, Could, May, Might, Will, Would, Shall, Should, Must

---

## What are Modal Verbs?

**Characteristics:**
- Don't change form (no -s, -ed, -ing)
- Followed by base verb (V1)
- No "do/does/did" in questions/negatives
- No "to" after modals

---

## Quick Overview

| Modal | Main Use |
|-------|----------|
| can | ability, permission |
| could | past ability, polite request |
| may | permission, possibility |
| might | possibility (less sure) |
| will | future, willingness |
| would | conditional, polite |
| shall | suggestion, future (formal) |
| should | advice, expectation |
| must | obligation, strong probability |

---

## CAN

### Uses:

| Use | Example | Hindi |
|-----|---------|-------|
| Ability | I **can** swim. | Main tair sakta hoon. |
| Permission (informal) | **Can** I go? | Kya main ja sakta hoon? |
| Possibility | It **can** be cold in winter. | Thand ho sakti hai. |
| Request | **Can** you help me? | Kya tum madad kar sakte ho? |

**Negative:** can't / cannot
**Question:** Can you...?

---

## COULD

### Uses:

| Use | Example | Hindi |
|-----|---------|-------|
| Past ability | I **could** swim when I was 5. | Main 5 saal mein tair sakta tha. |
| Polite request | **Could** you help me? | Kya aap madad kar sakte hain? |
| Possibility | It **could** rain. | Baarish ho sakti hai. |
| Suggestion | We **could** go tomorrow. | Hum kal ja sakte hain. |

**Negative:** couldn't / could not

---

## MAY

### Uses:

| Use | Example | Hindi |
|-----|---------|-------|
| Permission (formal) | **May** I come in? | Kya main andar aa sakta hoon? |
| Possibility | It **may** rain today. | Aaj baarish ho sakti hai. |
| Wish | **May** you live long! | Tum lambi umar jiyo! |

**Negative:** may not

---

## MIGHT

### Uses:

| Use | Example | Hindi |
|-----|---------|-------|
| Possibility (less sure) | It **might** rain. | Shayad baarish ho. |
| Suggestion (tentative) | You **might** want to check. | Shayad tum check karna chaho. |

**Negative:** might not

**May vs Might:**
- may = more likely (50%)
- might = less likely (30%)

---

## WILL

### Uses:

| Use | Example | Hindi |
|-----|---------|-------|
| Future | I **will** go tomorrow. | Main kal jaunga. |
| Promise | I **will** help you. | Main tumhari madad karunga. |
| Willingness | I **will** do it. | Main karunga. |
| Request | **Will** you help me? | Kya tum madad karoge? |
| Prediction | It **will** rain. | Baarish hogi. |

**Negative:** won't / will not
**Contraction:** I'll, you'll, he'll, she'll, we'll, they'll

---

## WOULD

### Uses:

| Use | Example | Hindi |
|-----|---------|-------|
| Conditional | I **would** go if I had time. | Agar time hota toh jaata. |
| Polite request | **Would** you help me? | Kya aap madad karenge? |
| Past habit | He **would** always come late. | Woh hamesha late aata tha. |
| Preference | I **would** like coffee. | Mujhe coffee chahiye. |

**Negative:** wouldn't / would not
**Contraction:** I'd, you'd, he'd, she'd, we'd, they'd

---

## SHALL

### Uses:

| Use | Example | Hindi |
|-----|---------|-------|
| Suggestion | **Shall** we go? | Chalein? |
| Offer | **Shall** I help you? | Kya main madad karoon? |
| Future (formal) | I **shall** return. | Main wapas aaunga. |

**Negative:** shall not / shan't

---

## SHOULD

### Uses:

| Use | Example | Hindi |
|-----|---------|-------|
| Advice | You **should** study more. | Tumhe zyada padhna chahiye. |
| Expectation | He **should** be here by now. | Ab tak aa jaana chahiye tha. |
| Obligation (mild) | You **should** respect elders. | Badon ki izzat karni chahiye. |

**Negative:** shouldn't / should not

---

## MUST

### Uses:

| Use | Example | Hindi |
|-----|---------|-------|
| Strong obligation | You **must** wear a helmet. | Helmet pehnna zaroori hai. |
| Strong probability | He **must** be tired. | Woh thaka hoga zaroor. |
| Necessity | I **must** go now. | Mujhe ab jaana hai. |

**Negative:** mustn't / must not (prohibition)

**Must vs Have to:**
- must = personal obligation
- have to = external obligation
- I must lose weight. (my decision)
- I have to work tomorrow. (boss says)

---

## MUST NOT vs DON'T HAVE TO

| Modal | Meaning | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| must not | Prohibited | You **mustn't** smoke here. (Not allowed!) |
| don't have to | Not necessary | You **don't have to** come. (Optional) |

---

## Modal + Have + V3 (Past)

| Modal | Past Form | Example |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| can | could have + V3 | I could have helped. (but didn't) |
| should | should have + V3 | You should have called. (but didn't) |
| would | would have + V3 | I would have gone. (but didn't) |
| must | must have + V3 | He must have left. (probably did) |
| may | may have + V3 | She may have forgotten. (possibly) |
| might | might have + V3 | They might have won. (possibly) |

**Examples:**
- You **should have studied**. (You didn't, regret)
- He **must have slept** late. (I'm sure he did)
- I **could have helped** but I didn't.

---

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| He cans swim. | He **can** swim. |
| I must to go. | I **must** go. |
| She should studies. | She should **study**. |
| Do you can swim? | **Can** you swim? |
| I don't can swim. | I **can't** swim. |
| He musted go. | He **had to** go. |
