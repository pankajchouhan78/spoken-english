# Word Order in English

> English follows strict word order rules.
> Unlike Hindi, word order changes meaning!

---

## Basic Word Order: SVO

**S**ubject + **V**erb + **O**bject

| S | V | O |
|---|---|---|
| I | eat | rice. |
| She | reads | books. |
| They | play | cricket. |

**Hindi:** Main chawal khata hoon. (S-O-V)
**English:** I eat rice. (S-V-O)

---

## Complete Sentence Order: SVOMPT

| S | V | O | M | P | T |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Subject | Verb | Object | Manner | Place | Time |
| I | play | football | well | in the park | every day. |
| She | works | - | hard | at the office | daily. |

---

## Adverb Placement

### Adverbs of Frequency

**Position:** Before main verb, after BE/auxiliary

| With Main Verb | With BE | With Auxiliary |
|----------------|---------|----------------|
| I **always** go. | I am **always** late. | I can **never** swim. |
| She **often** reads. | She is **often** busy. | She has **always** helped. |
| They **never** eat. | They are **never** here. | They will **never** know. |

**Frequency adverbs:** always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never, seldom

### Adverbs of Manner (How?)

**Position:** After the verb/object

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| He quickly ate. | He ate **quickly**. |
| She carefully opened it. | She opened it **carefully**. |
| They loudly sang. | They sang **loudly**. |

### Adverbs of Time (When?)

**Position:** Usually at the end

| Example |
|---------|
| I went there **yesterday**. |
| She will come **tomorrow**. |
| We met **last week**. |

Can also come at the beginning for emphasis:
- **Yesterday**, I went there.

### Adverbs of Place (Where?)

**Position:** After verb/object

| Example |
|---------|
| She lives **here**. |
| They went **outside**. |
| I looked **everywhere**. |

### Adverbs of Degree

**Position:** Before adjective/adverb they modify

| Example |
|---------|
| She is **very** tall. |
| He runs **quite** fast. |
| It's **extremely** hot. |

---

## Multiple Adverbs Order: MPT

**M**anner → **P**lace → **T**ime

| Manner | Place | Time |
|--------|-------|------|
| well | in the park | yesterday |
| carefully | at home | today |
| quickly | to school | this morning |

**Example:** She sang **beautifully** **at the concert** **last night**.

---

## Adjective Order

When using multiple adjectives, follow this order:

**OSASCOMP**

| Order | Type | Example |
|-------|------|---------|
| O | Opinion | beautiful, ugly, nice |
| S | Size | big, small, tall |
| A | Age | old, new, young |
| S | Shape | round, square, flat |
| C | Color | red, blue, green |
| O | Origin | Indian, Chinese, French |
| M | Material | wooden, plastic, cotton |
| P | Purpose | cooking (pot), sleeping (bag) |

**Examples:**
- a **beautiful big old** house ✅
- a big beautiful old house ❌
- a **small red plastic** ball ✅
- a red small plastic ball ❌
- a **lovely old Italian** car ✅

## Question Word Order

### Yes/No Questions: Auxiliary + Subject + Verb

| Statement | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| You are happy. | **Are you** happy? |
| She can swim. | **Can she** swim? |
| They have finished. | **Have they** finished? |

### With DO/DOES/DID

| Statement | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| You like coffee. | **Do you** like coffee? |
| She plays tennis. | **Does she** play tennis? |
| They went home. | **Did they** go home? |

### WH Questions: WH + Auxiliary + Subject + Verb

| WH | Aux | Subject | Verb |
|----|-----|---------|------|
| Where | do | you | live? |
| What | is | she | doing? |
| When | did | they | arrive? |

---

## Negative Word Order

### With BE: Subject + BE + not

| Positive | Negative |
|----------|----------|
| I am happy. | I am **not** happy. |
| She is here. | She is **not** here. |

### With Auxiliary: Subject + Aux + not + Verb

| Positive | Negative |
|----------|----------|
| I can swim. | I can**not** swim. |
| She has finished. | She has **not** finished. |

### With DO/DOES/DID

| Positive | Negative |
|----------|----------|
| I like coffee. | I **do not** like coffee. |
| She plays tennis. | She **does not** play tennis. |
| They went home. | They **did not** go home. |

---

## Indirect Object Order

### Two options:

**Option 1:** V + IO + DO (no preposition)
- Give **me** the book.
- She told **him** a story.

**Option 2:** V + DO + to/for + IO
- Give the book **to me**.
- She told a story **to him**.

---

## Phrasal Verb Order

### Separable: Object can go in between

| Together | Separated |
|----------|-----------|
| Turn off the light. | Turn the light off. |
| Pick up the book. | Pick the book up. |

**With pronouns:** Must separate!
- Turn **it** off. ✅
- Turn off it. ❌

### Inseparable: Object after particle

| Correct |
|---------|
| Look after the baby. ✅ |
| Look the baby after. ❌ |

---

## Reported Speech Order

**Direct:** He said, "Where do you live?"
**Reported:** He asked where I **lived**. (Statement order)

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| He asked where did I live. | He asked where I lived. |
| She asked what is my name. | She asked what my name was. |

---

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| I yesterday went. | I went yesterday. |
| He plays well football. | He plays football well. |
| Very I am happy. | I am very happy. |
| A red big car | A big red car |
| He always is late. | He is always late. |
| Where you are going? | Where are you going? |
| I don't know what is it. | I don't know what it is. |
