# Common Grammar Errors

> Mistakes that learners frequently make.
> Learn these to improve your English quickly!

---

## Subject-Verb Errors

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Rule |
|---------|---------|------|
| He don't know. | He **doesn't** know. | He/She/It + doesn't |
| She have a car. | She **has** a car. | He/She/It + has |
| They was there. | They **were** there. | They + were |
| The news are bad. | The news **is** bad. | News = singular |
| Everyone are here. | Everyone **is** here. | Everyone = singular |
| Mathematics are hard. | Mathematics **is** hard. | Subject = singular |

---

## Tense Errors

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Rule |
|---------|---------|------|
| I am study now. | I **am studying** now. | Present continuous |
| He plays yesterday. | He **played** yesterday. | Past for past time |
| I have seen him yesterday. | I **saw** him yesterday. | Past simple with yesterday |
| When I will come... | When I **come**... | Present after "when" |
| If I will go... | If I **go**... | Present after "if" |
| I am knowing the answer. | I **know** the answer. | Stative verb |

---

## Article Errors

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Rule |
|---------|---------|------|
| I have headache. | I have **a** headache. | Need article |
| She is doctor. | She is **a** doctor. | Need article for jobs |
| I like the music. | I like music. | General = no article |
| I play the cricket. | I play cricket. | Sports = no article |
| He is an European. | He is **a** European. | Sound, not spelling |
| I need a advice. | I need advice. | Uncountable = no a/an |
| Sun is hot. | **The** sun is hot. | Unique things = the |

---

## Preposition Errors

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Rule |
|---------|---------|------|
| in Monday | **on** Monday | Days = on |
| at January | **in** January | Months = in |
| in 5 o'clock | **at** 5 o'clock | Time = at |
| on night | **at** night | Exception |
| married with | married **to** | Correct preposition |
| different than | different **from** | Correct preposition |
| good in | good **at** | Correct preposition |
| listen music | listen **to** music | Verb + preposition |

---

## Pronoun Errors

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Rule |
|---------|---------|------|
| Me and him went. | **He and I** went. | Subject pronouns |
| Between you and I | Between you and **me** | Object after preposition |
| Give it to myself. | Give it to **me**. | Reflexive only S=O |
| Theirselves | **Themselves** | Correct form |
| It's tail | **Its** tail | Possessive = no apostrophe |
| Your welcome | **You're** welcome | You're = You are |

---

## Word Order Errors

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Rule |
|---------|---------|------|
| He plays well cricket. | He plays cricket **well**. | Adverb after object |
| I yesterday went. | I went **yesterday**. | Time at end |
| Very I am happy. | I am **very** happy. | Adverb before adjective |
| Always he comes late. | He **always** comes late. | Adverb position |
| Where you are going? | Where **are you** going? | Inversion in questions |

---

## Question Formation Errors

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Rule |
|---------|---------|------|
| You are coming? | **Are you** coming? | Invert for questions |
| Where you live? | Where **do you** live? | Need auxiliary |
| Does she likes? | Does she **like**? | Base form after does |
| What did happened? | What **happened**? | No did when who/what is subject |
| Why did he went? | Why did he **go**? | Base form after did |

---

## Double Negative Errors

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Rule |
|---------|---------|------|
| I don't have no money. | I don't have **any** money. | One negative only |
| I didn't see nobody. | I didn't see **anybody**. | One negative only |
| I can't find it nowhere. | I can't find it **anywhere**. | One negative only |

---

## Comparative/Superlative Errors

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Rule |
|---------|---------|------|
| more better | **better** | No double comparative |
| more taller | **taller** | No double comparative |
| most biggest | **biggest** | No double superlative |
| gooder | **better** | Irregular form |
| badder | **worse** | Irregular form |
| more easy | **easier** | Short word = -er |

---

## Modal Verb Errors

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Rule |
|---------|---------|------|
| He cans swim. | He **can** swim. | No -s on modals |
| I must to go. | I **must** go. | No "to" after modals |
| She should studies. | She should **study**. | Base form after modals |
| Do you can swim? | **Can** you swim? | No do with modals |
| He musted go. | He **had to** go. | Must has no past |

---

## Gerund/Infinitive Errors

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Rule |
|---------|---------|------|
| I enjoy to swim. | I enjoy **swimming**. | Enjoy + gerund |
| I want swimming. | I want **to swim**. | Want + infinitive |
| I'm good at to cook. | I'm good at **cooking**. | Preposition + gerund |
| I look forward to meet you. | I look forward to **meeting** you. | "to" is preposition here |

---

## Confusing Words

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Difference |
|---------|---------|------------|
| Their coming. | **They're** coming. | They're = They are |
| Its raining. | **It's** raining. | It's = It is |
| Your right. | **You're** right. | You're = You are |
| I loose my keys. | I **lose** my keys. | lose = verb |
| I feel good. | I feel **well**. | well = health |
| He did good. | He did **well**. | well = adverb |

---

## Missing Words

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Missing |
|---------|---------|---------|
| I am agree. | I agree. | Remove "am" |
| He suggested me to go. | He suggested **that I** go. | Missing "that I" |
| I am not have money. | I **don't** have money. | Wrong auxiliary |
| Despite of rain | Despite **the** rain / Despite rain | Wrong form |

---

## Redundancy Errors

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Problem |
|---------|---------|---------|
| return back | **return** | back is redundant |
| repeat again | **repeat** | again is redundant |
| advance forward | **advance** | forward is redundant |
| revert back | **revert** | back is redundant |
| free gift | **gift** | free is redundant |
| true fact | **fact** | true is redundant |

---

## Quick Fixes

| If you write... | Change to... |
|-----------------|--------------|
| I am agree | I agree |
| discuss about | discuss |
| return back | return |
| reach at | reach |
| enter into | enter |
| explain me | explain to me |
| different than | different from |
| cope up with | cope with |
