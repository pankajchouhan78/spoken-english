# Question Tags

> Short questions at the end of statements.
> Used to confirm or check information.

---

## What is a Question Tag?

**Statement + mini question**
- You are coming, **aren't you?**
- She likes tea, **doesn't she?**

---

## Basic Rules

### Rule 1: Positive statement → Negative tag
### Rule 2: Negative statement → Positive tag

| Statement | Tag |
|-----------|-----|
| You are happy, | **aren't you?** |
| You aren't happy, | **are you?** |
| She can swim, | **can't she?** |
| She can't swim, | **can she?** |

---

## Forming Question Tags

### With BE Verbs

| Statement | Tag |
|-----------|-----|
| I am late, | **aren't I?** |
| You are coming, | **aren't you?** |
| He is a doctor, | **isn't he?** |
| They are here, | **aren't they?** |
| She was there, | **wasn't she?** |
| We were wrong, | **weren't we?** |

> Note: "I am" → "aren't I" (not "amn't I")

### With Auxiliary Verbs

| Statement | Tag |
|-----------|-----|
| You have finished, | **haven't you?** |
| She has left, | **hasn't she?** |
| They will come, | **won't they?** |
| He would help, | **wouldn't he?** |
| You can swim, | **can't you?** |
| She could drive, | **couldn't she?** |
| They should go, | **shouldn't they?** |
| He must work, | **mustn't he?** |

### With DO/DOES/DID

> When no auxiliary in statement, use DO/DOES/DID

| Statement | Tag |
|-----------|-----|
| You like coffee, | **don't you?** |
| She plays tennis, | **doesn't she?** |
| They went home, | **didn't they?** |
| He works hard, | **doesn't he?** |
| We arrived late, | **didn't we?** |

## Special Cases

### Imperatives (Commands)

| Type | Tag |
|------|-----|
| Close the door, | **will you?** |
| Don't be late, | **will you?** |
| Let's go, | **shall we?** |

### "There is/are"

| Statement | Tag |
|-----------|-----|
| There is a problem, | **isn't there?** |
| There are many people, | **aren't there?** |
| There was no time, | **was there?** |

### "I am"

| Statement | Tag |
|-----------|-----|
| I am right, | **aren't I?** |
| I am not late, | **am I?** |
| I am your friend, | **aren't I?** |

### Negative Words = Negative Statement

> Words like: never, hardly, rarely, seldom, barely, nothing, nobody, no one

| Statement | Tag |
|-----------|-----|
| He never comes, | **does he?** |
| She hardly knows, | **does she?** |
| They rarely visit, | **do they?** |
| You seldom eat, | **do you?** |
| Nothing happened, | **did it?** |
| Nobody came, | **did they?** |

### Everyone/Someone/Nobody

| Statement | Tag |
|-----------|-----|
| Everyone is here, | **aren't they?** |
| Someone called, | **didn't they?** |
| Nobody knows, | **do they?** |
| Everybody left, | **didn't they?** |

### This/That

| Statement | Tag |
|-----------|-----|
| This is yours, | **isn't it?** |
| That was nice, | **wasn't it?** |

---

## Question Tag Intonation

### Rising Intonation ↗ (Real question)
> You're not sure, asking for information.
- You're coming tomorrow, **aren't you?** ↗
- (I don't know, I'm asking)

### Falling Intonation ↘ (Expecting agreement)
> You expect the person to agree.
- Nice weather, **isn't it?** ↘
- (I expect you to agree)

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| You are coming, are you? | You are coming, **aren't you?** |
| I am late, amn't I? | I am late, **aren't I?** |
| He likes tea, isn't he? | He likes tea, **doesn't he?** |
| Let's go, don't we? | Let's go, **shall we?** |
| Nothing happened, didn't it? | Nothing happened, **did it?** |
| Someone called, didn't someone? | Someone called, **didn't they?** |
| He never comes, doesn't he? | He never comes, **does he?** |
