# Gerunds and Infinitives

> Two ways to use verbs as nouns.
> Gerund: Verb + ing (swimming)
> Infinitive: to + Verb (to swim)

---

## What is a Gerund?

> Verb + ing used as a noun.

| As Subject | As Object |
|------------|-----------|
| **Swimming** is fun. | I enjoy **swimming**. |
| **Reading** is my hobby. | She loves **reading**. |
| **Cooking** takes time. | He finished **cooking**. |

---

## What is an Infinitive?

> "to" + base verb used as a noun.

| As Subject | As Object |
|------------|-----------|
| **To swim** is fun. | I want **to swim**. |
| **To learn** is important. | She decided **to learn**. |
| **To travel** is exciting. | He hopes **to travel**. |

---

## Verbs Followed by GERUND Only

> These verbs need verb+ing after them.

| Verb | Example |
|------|---------|
| enjoy | I enjoy **reading**. |
| finish | He finished **eating**. |
| avoid | Avoid **making** mistakes. |
| consider | Consider **changing** jobs. |
| keep | Keep **trying**. |
| mind | Do you mind **waiting**? |
| miss | I miss **seeing** you. |
| practice | Practice **speaking**. |
| quit/stop | He quit **smoking**. |
| suggest | I suggest **going** now. |
| admit | He admitted **stealing**. |
| deny | She denied **lying**. |
| imagine | Imagine **living** there. |
| recommend | I recommend **reading** it. |
| risk | Don't risk **losing** it. |
| can't help | I can't help **laughing**. |
| feel like | I feel like **eating**. |
| give up | He gave up **trying**. |
| put off | Don't put off **studying**. |

---

## Verbs Followed by INFINITIVE Only

> These verbs need "to + verb" after them.

| Verb | Example |
|------|---------|
| want | I want **to go**. |
| need | I need **to sleep**. |
| decide | She decided **to stay**. |
| hope | I hope **to see** you. |
| plan | We plan **to travel**. |
| expect | I expect **to win**. |
| promise | He promised **to help**. |
| agree | She agreed **to come**. |
| refuse | He refused **to pay**. |
| offer | She offered **to help**. |
| learn | I'm learning **to cook**. |
| seem | He seems **to be** happy. |
| appear | It appears **to be** true. |
| pretend | He pretended **to sleep**. |
| afford | I can't afford **to buy**. |
| manage | She managed **to finish**. |
| fail | He failed **to pass**. |
| choose | I chose **to leave**. |
| wish | I wish **to speak**. |
| would like | I'd like **to go**. |

---

## Verbs That Take BOTH (Same Meaning)

> Either gerund or infinitive - no change in meaning.

| Verb | With Gerund | With Infinitive |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| like | I like **swimming**. | I like **to swim**. |
| love | She loves **dancing**. | She loves **to dance**. |
| hate | I hate **waiting**. | I hate **to wait**. |
| prefer | I prefer **walking**. | I prefer **to walk**. |
| start | It started **raining**. | It started **to rain**. |
| begin | He began **laughing**. | He began **to laugh**. |
| continue | She continued **talking**. | She continued **to talk**. |

---

## Verbs That Take BOTH (Different Meaning)

### STOP
| Form | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| stop + gerund | Quit doing | I stopped **smoking**. (I quit) |
| stop + infinitive | Pause to do | I stopped **to smoke**. (I paused) |

### REMEMBER
| Form | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| remember + gerund | Memory of past | I remember **meeting** him. (I met him before) |
| remember + infinitive | Don't forget to | Remember **to call** me. (Call me later) |

### FORGET
| Form | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| forget + gerund | Memory of past | I'll never forget **meeting** you. |
| forget + infinitive | Fail to do | I forgot **to lock** the door. |

### TRY
| Form | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| try + gerund | Experiment | Try **adding** more salt. |
| try + infinitive | Attempt | I tried **to open** the door. |

### REGRET
| Form | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| regret + gerund | Regret past action | I regret **telling** him. |
| regret + infinitive | Sorry to say | I regret **to inform** you... |

## After Prepositions → GERUND

> Always use gerund after prepositions.

| Preposition + Gerund | Example |
|---------------------|---------|
| good at | She's good at **singing**. |
| interested in | I'm interested in **learning**. |
| tired of | I'm tired of **waiting**. |
| afraid of | He's afraid of **flying**. |
| think about | Think about **going**. |
| before | Before **leaving**, call me. |
| after | After **eating**, I slept. |
| instead of | Instead of **walking**, take a taxi. |
| without | He left without **saying** goodbye. |
| by | Learn by **doing**. |
| for | Thank you for **helping**. |

## Common Expressions with Gerund

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| It's no use | It's no use **crying**. |
| It's worth | It's worth **trying**. |
| There's no point | There's no point **arguing**. |
| Have trouble | I have trouble **sleeping**. |
| Have difficulty | I have difficulty **understanding**. |
| Spend time | I spend time **reading**. |
| Go + activity | Let's go **shopping**. |
| Be busy | I'm busy **working**. |
| Can't stand | I can't stand **waiting**. |
| Look forward to | I look forward to **meeting** you. |

---

## Infinitive of Purpose

> To explain WHY someone does something.

| Sentence | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| I went to the shop **to buy** milk. | Purpose: buy milk |
| She studies hard **to pass**. | Purpose: pass |
| He called **to say** hello. | Purpose: say hello |

---

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| I enjoy to swim. | I enjoy **swimming**. |
| I want swimming. | I want **to swim**. |
| I'm good at to cook. | I'm good at **cooking**. |
| Thank you for to help. | Thank you for **helping**. |
| I look forward to meet you. | I look forward to **meeting** you. |
| I stopped to smoke. (quit) | I stopped **smoking**. |
