# English Grammar - Quick Summary

> One-page reference for essential grammar rules.

---

## Sentence Structure: SVO

**S**ubject + **V**erb + **O**bject
- I eat rice. | She reads books.

---

## Tenses Overview

| Tense | Form | Example |
|-------|------|---------|
| Present Simple | V1/V1+s | I go / She goes |
| Present Continuous | am/is/are + V-ing | I am going |
| Present Perfect | have/has + V3 | I have gone |
| Past Simple | V2 | I went |
| Past Continuous | was/were + V-ing | I was going |
| Past Perfect | had + V3 | I had gone |
| Future Simple | will + V1 | I will go |

---

## Articles

| Article | Use | Example |
|---------|-----|---------|
| a | Consonant sound | a book, a university |
| an | Vowel sound | an apple, an hour |
| the | Specific/known | the sun, the book I bought |
| (none) | General | Dogs are loyal. |

---

## Conditionals

| Type | If Clause | Main Clause |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| 0 | Present | Present |
| 1 | Present | will + V1 |
| 2 | Past | would + V1 |
| 3 | Past Perfect | would have + V3 |

---

## Modal Verbs

| Modal | Use |
|-------|-----|
| can/could | ability, permission |
| may/might | possibility |
| will/would | future, conditional |
| should | advice |
| must | obligation |

---

## Passive Voice

**Formula:** BE + V3
- Active: He writes a letter.
- Passive: A letter is written.

---

## Comparatives & Superlatives

| Type | Short words | Long words |
|------|-------------|------------|
| Comparative | -er (taller) | more (more beautiful) |
| Superlative | -est (tallest) | most (most beautiful) |

**Irregular:** good → better → best | bad → worse → worst

---

## Countable vs Uncountable

| Countable | Uncountable |
|-----------|-------------|
| many, few | much, little |
| a/an | (no a/an) |
| plural possible | no plural |

---

## Key Rules

1. **He/She/It** → V1 + s
2. **No double comparatives** → better (not more better)
3. **Present after if/when** → If I go (not if I will go)
4. **Modals + V1** → can swim (not can to swim)
5. **Preposition + gerund** → good at swimming

---

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| He don't know. | He doesn't know. |
| I am agree. | I agree. |
| discuss about | discuss |
| more better | better |
| I enjoy to swim. | I enjoy swimming. |
