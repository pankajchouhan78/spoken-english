# Present Tense - All 4 Types

---

## 1. Present Simple Tense

> **Usage:** Daily habits, routines, facts, general truths  
> **Structure:** Subject + V1 (he/she/it ke saath V1+s/es)

### When to Use:
- Daily routine (I wake up at 7 AM)
- Habits (She drinks coffee every morning)
- Facts (The sun rises in the east)
- Permanent situations (I live in Delhi)

---

### Affirmative (+)

**Structure:** S + V1 (+ s/es for he/she/it)

| Subject | Example | Hindi |
|---------|---------|-------|
| I | I go to office. | Main office jaata hoon. |
| You | You work hard. | Tum mehnat karte ho. |
| He | He eats breakfast. | Wo nashta karta hai. |
| She | She reads books. | Wo kitaabein padhti hai. |
| It | It rains a lot here. | Yahaan bahut baarish hoti hai. |
| We | We play cricket. | Hum cricket khelte hain. |
| They | They live in Mumbai. | Wo Mumbai mein rehte hain. |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I wake up at 6 AM every day.
   - Main roz subah 6 baje uthta hoon.

2. She goes to gym daily.
   - Wo roz gym jaati hai.

3. He works in a bank.
   - Wo bank mein kaam karta hai.

4. We eat dinner at 8 PM.
   - Hum 8 baje dinner karte hain.

5. The train arrives at 9 AM.
   - Train 9 baje aati hai.

6. I drink tea in the morning.
   - Main subah chai peeta hoon.

7. She speaks English fluently.
   - Wo fluent English bolti hai.

8. He watches news every night.
   - Wo har raat news dekhta hai.

9. Water boils at 100 degrees.
   - Paani 100 degree par ubalta hai.

10. I love my family.
    - Main apne family se pyaar karta hoon.

11. She takes the bus to work.
    - Wo kaam ke liye bus leti hai.

12. He always arrives on time.
    - Wo hamesha time par aata hai.

13. I check my phone every morning.
    - Main roz subah apna phone check karta hoon.

14. The shop opens at 10 AM.
    - Dukaan 10 baje khulti hai.

15. She cooks dinner for her family.
    - Wo apne family ke liye dinner banati hai.

16. I take a shower before breakfast.
    - Main nashte se pehle shower leta hoon.

17. He reads the newspaper daily.
    - Wo roz akhbaar padhta hai.

18. We go to temple every Sunday.
    - Hum har Sunday mandir jaate hain.

19. She teaches English at a school.
    - Wo school mein English padhati hai.

20. I brush my teeth twice a day.
    - Main din mein do baar brush karta hoon.

21. He earns a good salary.
    - Wo acchi salary kamata hai.

22. The earth revolves around the sun.
    - Dharti suraj ke chakkar lagati hai.

23. I prefer coffee over tea.
    - Mujhe chai se zyada coffee pasand hai.

24. She knows how to cook.
    - Usse khana banana aata hai.

25. We meet every weekend.
    - Hum har weekend milte hain.

### Negative (-)

**Structure:** S + do/does not + V1

| Subject | Example | Hindi |
|---------|---------|-------|
| I | I do not (don't) eat meat. | Main meat nahi khata. |
| You | You do not (don't) understand. | Tum nahi samajhte. |
| He | He does not (doesn't) smoke. | Wo smoke nahi karta. |
| She | She does not (doesn't) like coffee. | Usse coffee pasand nahi. |
| We | We do not (don't) watch TV. | Hum TV nahi dekhte. |
| They | They do not (don't) work here. | Wo yahaan kaam nahi karte. |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I don't eat spicy food.
   - Main teekha khana nahi khata.

2. She doesn't drink alcohol.
   - Wo alcohol nahi peeti.

3. He doesn't like waking up early.
   - Usse subah jaldi uthna pasand nahi.

4. We don't go out on weekdays.
   - Hum weekdays mein bahar nahi jaate.

5. They don't speak Hindi.
   - Wo Hindi nahi bolte.

6. I don't have time for this.
   - Mere paas iske liye time nahi hai.

7. She doesn't eat after 8 PM.
   - Wo 8 baje ke baad nahi khati.

8. He doesn't know the answer.
   - Usse jawab nahi pata.

9. We don't work on Sundays.
   - Hum Sunday ko kaam nahi karte.

10. I don't trust him.
    - Mujhe us par bharosa nahi hai.

11. She doesn't watch TV much.
    - Wo zyada TV nahi dekhti.

12. He doesn't talk much.
    - Wo zyada baat nahi karta.

13. They don't live here anymore.
    - Wo ab yahaan nahi rehte.

14. I don't feel well today.
    - Aaj meri tabiyat theek nahi hai.

15. She doesn't need help.
    - Usse madad ki zaroorat nahi hai.

### Interrogative (?)

**Structure:** Do/Does + S + V1?

| Question | Hindi |
|----------|-------|
| Do you work here? | Kya tum yahaan kaam karte ho? |
| Does he play cricket? | Kya wo cricket khelta hai? |
| Do they live in Delhi? | Kya wo Delhi mein rehte hain? |
| Does she speak English? | Kya wo English bolti hai? |
| Do we have time? | Kya humare paas time hai? |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Do you eat non-veg?
   - Kya tum non-veg khate ho?

2. Does she work on Sundays?
   - Kya wo Sunday ko kaam karti hai?

3. Do they know about this?
   - Kya unhe is baare mein pata hai?

4. Does he drive a car?
   - Kya wo car chalata hai?

5. Do you understand Hindi?
   - Kya tum Hindi samajhte ho?

6. Do you like coffee?
   - Kya tumhe coffee pasand hai?

7. Does she live alone?
   - Kya wo akeli rehti hai?

8. Do you have a car?
   - Kya tumhare paas car hai?

9. Does he know about the meeting?
   - Kya usse meeting ke baare mein pata hai?

10. Do we need to go now?
    - Kya humein abhi jaana chahiye?

11. Does this bus go to the station?
    - Kya yeh bus station jaati hai?

12. Do you want some water?
    - Kya tumhe paani chahiye?

13. Does she speak French?
    - Kya wo French bolti hai?

14. Do they accept credit cards?
    - Kya wo credit card lete hain?

15. What time does the shop close?
    - Dukaan kitne baje band hoti hai?

## 2. Present Continuous Tense

> **Usage:** Action happening right now, temporary situations  
> **Structure:** Subject + am/is/are + V-ing

### When to Use:
- Action happening now (I am eating)
- Temporary situations (I am staying at hotel)
- Future plans (I am meeting him tomorrow)
- Annoying habits with "always" (He is always complaining)

---

### Affirmative (+)

**Structure:** S + am/is/are + V-ing

| Subject | am/is/are | Example | Hindi |
|---------|-----------|---------|-------|
| I | am | I am working. | Main kaam kar raha hoon. |
| You | are | You are reading. | Tum padh rahe ho. |
| He | is | He is sleeping. | Wo so raha hai. |
| She | is | She is cooking. | Wo khana bana rahi hai. |
| It | is | It is raining. | Baarish ho rahi hai. |
| We | are | We are watching TV. | Hum TV dekh rahe hain. |
| They | are | They are playing. | Wo khel rahe hain. |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I am eating lunch right now.
   - Main abhi lunch kha raha hoon.

2. She is talking on the phone.
   - Wo phone par baat kar rahi hai.

3. He is driving to work.
   - Wo office ja raha hai (drive karke).

4. We are waiting for you.
   - Hum tumhara wait kar rahe hain.

5. They are watching a movie.
   - Wo movie dekh rahe hain.

6. I am learning English.
   - Main English seekh raha hoon.

7. She is preparing for exam.
   - Wo exam ki tayyari kar rahi hai.

8. The baby is crying.
   - Bachcha ro raha hai.

9. I am coming right now.
   - Main abhi aa raha hoon.

10. It is getting dark.
    - Andhera ho raha hai.

11. I am working from home today.
    - Main aaj ghar se kaam kar raha hoon.

12. She is looking for a new job.
    - Wo nayi job dhoondh rahi hai.

13. He is making tea.
    - Wo chai bana raha hai.

14. We are planning a trip.
    - Hum trip ki planning kar rahe hain.

15. They are moving to a new house.
    - Wo naye ghar mein shift ho rahe hain.

16. I am trying to understand.
    - Main samajhne ki koshish kar raha hoon.

17. She is wearing a red dress.
    - Usne red dress pehni hai.

18. The kids are playing outside.
    - Bachche bahar khel rahe hain.

19. I am reading a good book these days.
    - Main aaj kal ek acchi book padh raha hoon.

20. He is always complaining about something.
    - Wo hamesha kisi cheez ki complaint karta rehta hai.

---

## 3. Present Perfect Tense

> **Usage:** Action completed (recently), experience, result visible now
> **Structure:** Subject + have/has + V3 (past participle)

### When to Use:
- Just completed action (I have finished my work)
- Life experience (I have visited Paris)
- Result still matters (She has lost her keys)
- With: just, already, yet, ever, never

---

### Affirmative (+)

**Structure:** S + have/has + V3

| Subject | have/has | Example | Hindi |
|---------|----------|---------|-------|
| I | have | I have eaten. | Maine kha liya hai. |
| You | have | You have done well. | Tumne accha kiya hai. |
| He | has | He has left. | Wo ja chuka hai. |
| She | has | She has finished. | Usne khatam kar liya hai. |
| We | have | We have seen this. | Humne yeh dekha hai. |
| They | have | They have arrived. | Wo aa chuke hain. |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I have finished my work.
   - Maine apna kaam khatam kar liya hai.

2. She has already eaten.
   - Usne pehle hi kha liya hai.

3. He has gone to office.
   - Wo office ja chuka hai.

4. We have seen this movie before.
   - Humne yeh movie pehle dekhi hai.

5. They have just arrived.
   - Wo abhi abhi aaye hain.

6. I have never been to London.
   - Main kabhi London nahi gaya.

7. Have you ever tried sushi?
   - Kya tumne kabhi sushi khayi hai?

8. She has lost her phone.
   - Uska phone kho gaya hai.

9. I have completed the project.
   - Maine project complete kar liya hai.

10. He has lived here for 10 years.
    - Wo yahaan 10 saal se reh raha hai.

11. I have sent you the email.
    - Maine tumhe email bhej diya hai.

12. She has bought a new car.
    - Usne nayi car li hai.

13. We have known each other for years.
    - Hum ek dusre ko saalon se jaante hain.

14. He has already told me.
    - Usne mujhe pehle hi bata diya hai.

15. I have read this book twice.
    - Maine yeh book do baar padhi hai.

16. She has changed a lot.
    - Wo bahut badal gayi hai.

17. They have moved to a new city.
    - Wo nayi city mein shift ho gaye hain.

18. I have made my decision.
    - Maine apna decision le liya hai.

19. He has broken his leg.
    - Uski taang toot gayi hai.

20. We have booked the tickets.
    - Humne tickets book kar liye hain.

---

## 4. Present Perfect Continuous Tense

> **Usage:** Action started in past, still continuing
> **Structure:** Subject + have/has + been + V-ing

### When to Use:
- Action ongoing from past (I have been waiting for 2 hours)
- Duration with: for, since
- Recent continuous activity (Your eyes are red. Have you been crying?)

---

### Affirmative (+)

**Structure:** S + have/has + been + V-ing

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I have been waiting for 2 hours.
   - Main 2 ghante se wait kar raha hoon.

2. She has been working here since 2020.
   - Wo 2020 se yahaan kaam kar rahi hai.

8. She has been cooking since 4 PM.

9. He has been driving for 3 hours.
   - Wo 3 ghante se drive kar raha hai.

10. We have been watching TV all evening.
    - Hum puri shaam se TV dekh rahe hain.

11. I have been thinking about this for days.
    - Main kayi dino se is baare mein soch raha hoon.

    - Wo kayi mahino se job dhoondh rahi hai.

## Quick Reference Table

| Tense | Structure | Example | Hindi | When to Use |
|-------|-----------|---------|-------|-------------|
| Present Simple | S + V1 | I eat. | Main khata hoon. | Daily habits, facts |
| Present Continuous | S + am/is/are + V-ing | I am eating. | Main kha raha hoon. | Right now |
| Present Perfect | S + have/has + V3 | I have eaten. | Maine kha liya hai. | Just completed |
| Present Perfect Continuous | S + have/has been + V-ing | I have been eating. | Main kha raha hoon (kab se). | Duration from past |

## For vs Since (Time Words)

| For | Since |
|-----|-------|
| Duration (kitne time) | Starting point (kab se) |
| for 2 hours | since 10 AM |
| for 3 days | since Monday |
| for 5 years | since 2020 |
| for a long time | since childhood |
