# Past Tense - All 4 Types

---

## 1. Past Simple Tense

> **Usage:** Completed actions in the past  
> **Structure:** Subject + V2 (past form of verb)

### When to Use:
- Completed action (I went to school yesterday)
- Past habits (She always walked to work)
- Series of completed actions (I woke up, had tea, and left)
- Specific time in past (He called me at 5 PM)

---

### Affirmative (+)

**Structure:** S + V2

| Subject | Example | Hindi |
|---------|---------|-------|
| I | I went to office. | Main office gaya. |
| You | You worked hard. | Tumne mehnat ki. |
| He | He ate breakfast. | Usne nashta kiya. |
| She | She read the book. | Usne kitaab padhi. |
| It | It rained yesterday. | Kal baarish hui. |
| We | We played cricket. | Humne cricket khela. |
| They | They came late. | Wo late aaye. |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I went to the market yesterday.

    - Usne mujhe sach bataya.

## 2. Past Continuous Tense

> **Usage:** Action in progress at a specific time in the past
> **Structure:** Subject + was/were + V-ing

### When to Use:
- Action in progress (I was eating at 8 PM)
- Two actions happening together (While I was cooking, he was watching TV)
- Interrupted action (I was sleeping when he called)
- Background description (The sun was shining)

---

### Affirmative (+)

**Structure:** S + was/were + V-ing

| Subject | was/were | Example | Hindi |
|---------|----------|---------|-------|
| I | was | I was working. | Main kaam kar raha tha. |
| You | were | You were reading. | Tum padh rahe the. |
| He | was | He was sleeping. | Wo so raha tha. |
| She | was | She was cooking. | Wo khana bana rahi thi. |
| It | was | It was raining. | Baarish ho rahi thi. |
| We | were | We were watching TV. | Hum TV dekh rahe the. |
| They | were | They were playing. | Wo khel rahe the. |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I was working when you called.
   - Jab tumne call kiya, main kaam kar raha tha.

2. She was cooking dinner at 7 PM.
   - Wo 7 baje khana bana rahi thi.

3. He was driving when it started raining.
   - Jab baarish shuru hui, wo drive kar raha tha.

4. We were waiting for you.
   - Hum tumhara wait kar rahe the.

5. They were playing cricket in the park.
   - Wo park mein cricket khel rahe the.

6. I was sleeping when you arrived.
   - Jab tum aaye, main so raha tha.

7. She was talking on the phone for an hour.
   - Wo ek ghante tak phone par baat kar rahi thi.

8. He was studying all night.
   - Wo puri raat padh raha tha.

9. The kids were making noise.
   - Bachche shor macha rahe the.

10. I was thinking about you.
    - Main tumhare baare mein soch raha tha.

11. She was looking for her keys.
    - Wo apni chabi dhoondh rahi thi.

12. He was running late for the meeting.
    - Wo meeting ke liye late ho raha tha.

13. We were planning a surprise party.
    - Hum surprise party plan kar rahe the.

14. They were discussing the project.
    - Wo project discuss kar rahe the.

15. The sun was setting beautifully.
    - Suraj khoobsoorat tarike se doob raha tha.

16. I was watching TV when the power went off.
    - Jab light gayi, main TV dekh raha tha.

17. She was crying when I saw her.
    - Jab maine use dekha, wo ro rahi thi.

18. He was listening to music.
    - Wo music sun raha tha.

19. We were having dinner together.
    - Hum saath mein dinner kar rahe the.

20. It was snowing heavily last night.
    - Kal raat bahut barf gir rahi thi.

## 3. Past Perfect Tense

> **Usage:** Action completed before another past action
> **Structure:** Subject + had + V3 (past participle)

### When to Use:
- Action before another past action (I had eaten before he came)
- Experience until a point in past (She had never seen snow until that day)
- Reported speech (He said he had finished)
- Cause of a past situation (I was tired because I had worked all day)

---

### Affirmative (+)

**Structure:** S + had + V3

| Subject | Example | Hindi |
|---------|---------|-------|
| I | I had finished. | Maine khatam kar diya tha. |
| You | You had left. | Tum ja chuke the. |
| He | He had eaten. | Usne kha liya tha. |
| She | She had seen it. | Usne dekh liya tha. |
| We | We had decided. | Humne decide kar liya tha. |
| They | They had arrived. | Wo aa chuke the. |

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I had finished my work before he called.
   - Uske call karne se pehle maine kaam khatam kar liya tha.

2. She had already left when I reached.
   - Jab main pahuncha, wo ja chuki thi.

3. He had eaten before the party started.
   - Party shuru hone se pehle usne kha liya tha.

4. We had met before that day.
   - Hum us din se pehle mil chuke the.

5. They had seen this movie earlier.
   - Wo yeh movie pehle dekh chuke the.

6. I had never visited Delhi before 2020.
   - 2020 se pehle main kabhi Delhi nahi gaya tha.

7. She had studied hard, so she passed.
   - Usne mehnat ki thi, isliye pass ho gayi.

8. He had lost his phone before the trip.
   - Trip se pehle uska phone kho gaya tha.

9. We had booked the tickets in advance.
   - Humne tickets pehle se book kar liye the.

10. By 10 PM, I had gone to bed.
    - 10 baje tak main so gaya tha.

11. She had told me about it earlier.
    - Usne mujhe pehle bata diya tha.

12. He had completed his graduation by 2018.
    - 2018 tak usne graduation complete kar liya tha.

13. We had known each other for years.
    - Hum ek dusre ko saalon se jaante the.

14. They had prepared well for the interview.
    - Unhone interview ki acchi tayyari ki thi.

15. I had sent the email before the meeting.
    - Meeting se pehle maine email bhej diya tha.

16. She had never traveled abroad before.
    - Wo pehle kabhi videsh nahi gayi thi.

17. He had saved enough money for the car.
    - Usne car ke liye kaafi paise bacha liye the.

18. We had discussed this topic before.
    - Hum is topic par pehle baat kar chuke the.

19. They had finished the project on time.
    - Unhone project time par khatam kar liya tha.

20. I had already called him twice.
    - Maine use pehle se do baar call kar liya tha.

---

## 4. Past Perfect Continuous Tense

> **Usage:** Action that was ongoing before another past action
> **Structure:** Subject + had been + V-ing

### When to Use:
- Duration before a past point (I had been waiting for 2 hours when he came)
- Cause of past result (She was tired because she had been working all day)
- Recent continuous activity in past (His eyes were red. He had been crying.)

---

### Affirmative (+)

**Structure:** S + had been + V-ing

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I had been waiting for 2 hours when he arrived.
   - Jab wo aaya, main 2 ghante se wait kar raha tha.

2. She had been working there for 5 years.

3. He had been driving for 3 hours before he stopped.

8. He had been looking for a job for months.

9. We had been planning this trip for a year.

10. It had been raining since last night.
    - Kal raat se baarish ho rahi thi.

11. I had been trying to call you.
    - Main tumhe call karne ki koshish kar raha tha.

12. She had been learning French for 2 years.
    - Wo 2 saal se French seekh rahi thi.

13. He had been exercising regularly before the injury.

14. We had been saving money for this house.

15. They had been traveling for a week.

## Quick Reference Table

| Tense | Structure | Example | Hindi | When to Use |
|-------|-----------|---------|-------|-------------|
| Past Simple | S + V2 | I ate. | Maine khaya. | Completed action |
