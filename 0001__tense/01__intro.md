# Tense - Introduction

---

## What is Tense?

> **Tense = Time**  
> Tense batata hai ki action **kab** hua - abhi, pehle, ya baad mein.

**Simple Examples:**
- I eat. (Main khata hoon) - **Present**
- I ate. (Maine khaya) - **Past**
- I will eat. (Main khaunga) - **Future**

---

## 3 Main Tenses

| Tense | Meaning | Example | Hindi |
|-------|---------|---------|-------|
| Present | Abhi ho raha hai | I eat. | Main khata hoon. |
| Past | Pehle ho chuka | I ate. | Maine khaya. |
| Future | Baad mein hoga | I will eat. | Main khaunga. |

---

## 4 Types (Aspects) of Each Tense

| Type | Use | Present Example | Hindi |
|------|-----|-----------------|-------|
| Simple | General action | I eat. | Main khata hoon. |
| Continuous | Action in progress | I am eating. | Main kha raha hoon. |
| Perfect | Action completed | I have eaten. | Maine kha liya hai. |
| Perfect Continuous | Action duration | I have been eating. | Main kha raha hoon (kab se). |

---

## Total: 12 Tenses (3 × 4 = 12)

| | Simple | Continuous | Perfect | Perfect Continuous |
|--|--------|------------|---------|-------------------|
| **Present** | I eat | I am eating | I have eaten | I have been eating |
| **Past** | I ate | I was eating | I had eaten | I had been eating |
| **Future** | I will eat | I will be eating | I will have eaten | I will have been eating |

---

## Quick Reference - All 12 Tenses

### Present Tenses:

| Tense | Structure | Example | Hindi |
|-------|-----------|---------|-------|
| Present Simple | S + V1/V1s | I work. | Main kaam karta hoon. |
| Present Continuous | S + am/is/are + V-ing | I am working. | Main kaam kar raha hoon. |
| Present Perfect | S + have/has + V3 | I have worked. | Maine kaam kar liya hai. |
| Present Perfect Continuous | S + have/has been + V-ing | I have been working. | Main kaam kar raha hoon (kab se). |

### Past Tenses:

| Tense | Structure | Example | Hindi |
|-------|-----------|---------|-------|
| Past Simple | S + V2 | I worked. | Maine kaam kiya. |
| Past Continuous | S + was/were + V-ing | I was working. | Main kaam kar raha tha. |
| Past Perfect | S + had + V3 | I had worked. | Maine kaam kar liya tha. |
| Past Perfect Continuous | S + had been + V-ing | I had been working. | Main kaam kar raha tha (kab se). |

### Future Tenses:

| Tense | Structure | Example | Hindi |
|-------|-----------|---------|-------|
| Future Simple | S + will + V1 | I will work. | Main kaam karunga. |
| Future Continuous | S + will be + V-ing | I will be working. | Main kaam kar raha hounga. |
| Future Perfect | S + will have + V3 | I will have worked. | Main kaam kar chuka hounga. |
| Future Perfect Continuous | S + will have been + V-ing | I will have been working. | Main kaam kar raha hounga (kab se). |

## Helping Verbs (Auxiliary Verbs)

| Tense | Helping Verb |
|-------|--------------|
| Present Simple | do/does |
| Present Continuous | am/is/are |
| Present Perfect | have/has |
| Past Simple | did |
| Past Continuous | was/were |
| Past Perfect | had |
| Future (all) | will |

---

## Subject + Helping Verb Match

| Subject | am/is/are | was/were | have/has | do/does |
|---------|-----------|----------|----------|---------|
| I | am | was | have | do |
| You | are | were | have | do |
| He/She/It | is | was | has | does |
| We/They | are | were | have | do |

---

## Sentence Types

| Type | Structure | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Affirmative (+) | S + V | I eat rice. |
| Negative (-) | S + do not + V | I do not eat rice. |
| Interrogative (?) | Do + S + V? | Do you eat rice? |

---

## Time Words (Clue Words)

| Present | Past | Future |
|---------|------|--------|
| now, today | yesterday, last week | tomorrow, next week |
| always, usually | ago, in 2020 | soon, later |
| every day | when I was young | next year |
| at the moment | at that time | in the future |
