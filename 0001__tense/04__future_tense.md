# Future Tense - All 4 Types

---

## 1. Future Simple Tense

> **Usage:** Actions that will happen in the future  
> **Structure:** Subject + will + V1

### When to Use:
- Predictions (It will rain tomorrow)
- Promises (I will help you)
- Instant decisions (I'll take this one)
- Future facts (The sun will rise at 6 AM)

---

### Affirmative (+)

**Structure:** S + will + V1

| Subject | Example | Hindi |
|---------|---------|-------|
| I | I will go. | Main jaunga. |
| You | You will come. | Tum aaoge. |
| He | He will eat. | Wo khayega. |
| She | She will call. | Wo call karegi. |
| It | It will rain. | Baarish hogi. |
| We | We will play. | Hum khelenge. |
| They | They will help. | Wo madad karenge. |

## 2. Future Continuous Tense

> **Usage:** Action in progress at a specific time in the future
> **Structure:** Subject + will be + V-ing

### When to Use:
- Action in progress at a future time (I will be sleeping at 10 PM)
- Parallel actions in future (While you work, I will be cooking)
- Polite inquiry (Will you be using this chair?)
- Planned future action (I will be meeting him tomorrow)

---

### Affirmative (+)

|---------|---------|-------|

| She | She will be cooking. | Wo khana bana rahi hogi. |

| They | They will be playing. | Wo khel rahe honge. |

    - Kal is waqt main London ja raha hounga.

    - Wo competition ke liye practice kar rahi hogi.

    - Wo apna project present kar raha hoga.

## 3. Future Perfect Tense

> **Usage:** Action completed before a specific time in the future
> **Structure:** Subject + will have + V3

### When to Use:
- Completed before a future time (I will have finished by 5 PM)
- Duration until a future point (By 2025, I will have worked here for 10 years)
- Assumption about completion (She will have reached by now)

---

### Affirmative (+)

**Structure:** S + will have + V3

| Subject | Example | Hindi |
|---------|---------|-------|

| She | She will have arrived. | Wo aa chuki hogi. |
| We | We will have completed. | Hum complete kar chuke honge. |

## 4. Future Perfect Continuous Tense

> **Usage:** Action continuing until a specific time in the future
> **Structure:** Subject + will have been + V-ing

### When to Use:
- Duration up to a future point (By 5 PM, I will have been working for 8 hours)
- Cause of future result (She will be tired because she will have been traveling all day)
- Emphasis on duration (By next year, I will have been learning English for 3 years)

---

| Tense | Structure | Example | Hindi | When to Use |
|-------|-----------|---------|-------|-------------|
| Future Simple | S + will + V1 | I will go. | Main jaunga. | Future actions |
| Future Continuous | S + will be + V-ing | I will be going. | Main ja raha hounga. | In progress at future time |
| Future Perfect | S + will have + V3 | I will have gone. | Main ja chuka hounga. | Completed by future time |

## Will vs Going to

| Will | Going to |
|------|----------|
| Instant decision | Planned decision |
| "I'll take this one." | "I'm going to buy a car." |
| Prediction based on opinion | Prediction based on evidence |
| "I think it will rain." | "Look at the clouds. It's going to rain." |
