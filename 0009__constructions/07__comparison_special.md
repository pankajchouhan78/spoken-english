# Special Comparison Constructions

> The more...the more, As if/As though, No sooner...than
> Express comparisons and sequences.

---

## THE MORE...THE MORE (Jitna...Utna)

> Two things increase together.

**Structure:** The + comparative + clause, the + comparative + clause

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. The more you practice, the better you get.
   - Jitna practice karoge, utne acche banoge.

2. The more I eat, the hungrier I feel.
   - Jitna khata hoon, utni bhukh lagti hai.

3. The harder you work, the more you earn.
   - Jitni mehnat karoge, utna kamayoge.

4. The more he explains, the more confused I get.
   - Jitna wo samjhata hai, utna confused hota hoon.

5. The sooner, the better.
   - Jitna jaldi, utna accha.

6. The older I get, the wiser I become.
   - Jitna bada hota hoon, utna samajhdaar banta hoon.

7. The more you read, the more you learn.
   - Jitna padhoge, utna seekhoge.

8. The longer you wait, the worse it gets.
   - Jitna wait karoge, utna bura hoga.

9. The less you speak, the more you hear.
   - Jitna kam bologe, utna zyada sunoge.

10. The more expensive, the better quality.
    - Jitna mehenga, utni acchi quality.

11. The more sleep I get, the better I feel.
    - Jitni neend milti hai, utna accha lagta hai.

12. The faster you drive, the more dangerous it is.
    - Jitna tez drive karoge, utna khatarnak hai.

13. The more we talk, the closer we become.
    - Jitna baat karte hain, utne kareeb aate hain.

14. The higher you climb, the harder you fall.
    - Jitna upar chadho, utna mushkil girna.

15. The more I think, the more worried I become.
    - Jitna sochta hoon, utni chinta hoti hai.

## AS IF / AS THOUGH (Jaise Ki)

> Something SEEMS true but may NOT be true.
> Used for imaginary comparisons.

**Structure:** Subject + verb + as if/as though + clause

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. He talks as if he knows everything.
   - Wo aise baat karta hai jaise sab jaanta ho.

2. She acts as if she's the boss.
   - Wo aise behave karti hai jaise boss ho.

3. He looked at me as though I was a stranger.
   - Usne mujhe aise dekha jaise ajnabi hoon.

4. They treat me as if I'm invisible.
   - Wo mujhse aise behave karte hain jaise main hoon hi nahi.

5. She cried as if the world had ended.
   - Wo aise royi jaise duniya khatam ho gayi.

6. He spends money as if he's a millionaire.
   - Wo paise aise kharch karta hai jaise crorepati ho.

7. I feel as if I've known you forever.
   - Aisa lagta hai jaise tujhe sadiyoon se jaanta hoon.

8. She acts as though nothing happened.
   - Wo aise behave karti hai jaise kuch hua hi nahi.

9. He works as if there's no tomorrow.
   - Wo aise kaam karta hai jaise kal hi nahi hai.

10. It looks as if it's going to rain.
    - Lagta hai jaise baarish hone wali hai.

11. She walks as though she's on a ramp.
    - Wo aise chalti hai jaise ramp par ho.

12. He eats as if he hasn't eaten for days.
    - Wo aise khata hai jaise dino se khaya hi nahi.

13. They stared as if they'd seen a ghost.
    - Unhone aise dekha jaise bhoot dekh liya.

14. I feel as though I'm dreaming.
    - Aisa lagta hai jaise sapna dekh raha hoon.

15. She smiled as if she knew a secret.
    - Wo aise muskurayi jaise koi raaz jaanti ho.

## NO SOONER...THAN (Jaise Hi...Waise Hi)

> One thing happens IMMEDIATELY after another.

**Structure:** No sooner + had + subject + V3 + than + clause

**Note:** "No sooner" starts with inverted word order!

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. No sooner had I arrived than it started raining.
   - Jaise hi main pahuncha, baarish shuru ho gayi.

2. No sooner had she left than he called.
   - Jaise hi wo gayi, usne call kiya.

3. No sooner had I sat down than the phone rang.
   - Jaise hi baitha, phone baja.

4. No sooner had we reached than the train left.
   - Jaise hi pahunche, train chal di.

5. No sooner had I slept than I woke up.
   - Jaise hi soya, jaag gaya.

6. No sooner had he started than he stopped.
   - Jaise hi shuru kiya, ruk gaya.

7. No sooner had she spoken than he interrupted.
   - Jaise hi boli, usne tok diya.

8. No sooner had I eaten than I felt sick.
   - Jaise hi khaya, tabiyat kharab ho gayi.

9. No sooner had the match begun than it rained.
   - Jaise hi match shuru hua, baarish aa gayi.

10. No sooner had I opened the door than the dog ran out.
    - Jaise hi darwaza khola, kutta bhaag gaya.

---

## HARDLY/SCARCELY...WHEN (Mushkil Se...Tabhi)

> Same meaning as "no sooner...than"

**Structure:** Hardly/Scarcely + had + subject + V3 + when + clause

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. Hardly had I reached when it started raining.
   - Mushkil se pahuncha tha ki baarish shuru ho gayi.

2. Scarcely had she finished when he called.
   - Mushkil se khatam kiya tha ki usne call kiya.

3. Hardly had I sat down when the boss arrived.
   - Mushkil se baitha tha ki boss aa gaye.

4. Scarcely had we begun when the power went off.
   - Mushkil se shuru kiya tha ki bijli gayi.

5. Hardly had I slept when the alarm rang.
   - Mushkil se soya tha ki alarm baj gaya.
