# Used To Structures

> Three different "used to" patterns with different meanings.
> Very commonly confused!

---

## Overview

| Pattern | Meaning | Hindi |
|---------|---------|-------|
| **used to** + V1 | Past habit (no more) | Pehle karta tha |
| **be used to** + V-ing | Accustomed to | Aadat hai |
| **get used to** + V-ing | Becoming accustomed | Aadat pad rahi hai |

---

## USED TO + V1 (Past Habit)

> Something you DID REGULARLY in the past but DON'T DO anymore.

**Structure:** Subject + used to + base verb (V1)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I used to play cricket every day.
   - Main roz cricket khelta tha.

2. She used to live in Delhi.
   - Wo Delhi mein rehti thi.

3. We used to go there often.
   - Hum wahaan aksar jaate the.

4. He used to smoke, but he quit.
   - Wo cigarette peeta tha, par chhod diya.

5. I used to wake up early.
   - Main jaldi uthta tha.

6. They used to be friends.
   - Wo dost hua karte the.

7. She used to cook every day.
   - Wo roz khana banati thi.

8. We used to watch this show together.
   - Hum ye show saath mein dekhte the.

9. I used to be very shy.
   - Main bahut sharmila tha.

10. He used to work here.
    - Wo yahaan kaam karta tha.

11. I used to hate vegetables.
    - Mujhe sabzi se nafrat thi.

12. She used to sing beautifully.
    - Wo sundar gaati thi.

13. We used to fight a lot.
    - Hum bahut ladte the.

14. I used to study all night.
    - Main poori raat padhta tha.

15. They used to live next door.
    - Wo bagal mein rehte the.

---

### Negative: didn't use to

1. I didn't use to like coffee.
   - Mujhe coffee pasand nahi thi.

2. She didn't use to exercise.
   - Wo exercise nahi karti thi.

3. We didn't use to travel much.
   - Hum zyada travel nahi karte the.

4. He didn't use to be so angry.
   - Wo itna gussa nahi karta tha.

5. I didn't use to wake up early.
   - Main jaldi nahi uthta tha.

---

### Question: Did you use to...?

1. Did you use to play sports?
   - Kya tum sports khelte the?

2. Did she use to work here?
   - Kya wo yahaan kaam karti thi?

3. Did they use to live together?
   - Kya wo saath rehte the?

4. What did you use to do in summer?
   - Garmiyon mein kya karte the?

5. Where did you use to study?
   - Tum kahaan padhte the?

---

## BE USED TO + V-ing (Accustomed)

> Something you are COMFORTABLE with now (habituated).

**Structure:** Subject + am/is/are + used to + V-ing/noun

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I am used to waking up early.
   - Mujhe jaldi uthne ki aadat hai.

2. She is used to living alone.
   - Use akele rehne ki aadat hai.

3. He is used to hard work.
   - Use mehnat ki aadat hai.

4. We are used to this weather.
   - Humein is mausam ki aadat hai.

5. I am used to spicy food.
   - Mujhe teekha khana khane ki aadat hai.

6. They are used to noise.
   - Unhein shor ki aadat hai.

7. I am not used to this cold.
   - Mujhe itni thandi ki aadat nahi.

8. She is used to working late.
   - Use late kaam karne ki aadat hai.

9. He is used to traveling by bus.
   - Use bus se travel ki aadat hai.

10. Are you used to this routine?
    - Kya tumhe is routine ki aadat hai?

11. I am used to criticism now.
    - Ab mujhe aalochna ki aadat ho gayi.

12. She is used to Indian food.
    - Use Indian khane ki aadat hai.

13. We are used to his jokes.
    - Humein uske jokes ki aadat hai.

14. I am not used to getting up so early.
    - Mujhe itni jaldi uthne ki aadat nahi.

15. They are used to the traffic.
    - Unhein traffic ki aadat hai.

---

## GET USED TO + V-ing (Becoming Accustomed)

> Something you are IN THE PROCESS of getting comfortable with.

**Structure:** Subject + get/got/getting + used to + V-ing/noun

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I am getting used to this job.
   - Mujhe is job ki aadat pad rahi hai.

2. She got used to the new city.
   - Use naye sheher ki aadat pad gayi.

3. It takes time to get used to it.
   - Aadat padne mein time lagta hai.

4. I can't get used to this weather.
   - Mujhe is mausam ki aadat nahi pad rahi.

5. He is getting used to living alone.
   - Use akele rehne ki aadat pad rahi hai.

6. I'll get used to it soon.
   - Mujhe jaldi aadat pad jayegi.

7. She got used to working from home.
   - Use ghar se kaam karne ki aadat pad gayi.

8. Are you getting used to the new school?
   - Kya tumhe naye school ki aadat pad rahi hai?

9. I never got used to that noise.
   - Mujhe us shor ki kabhi aadat nahi padi.

10. They got used to each other.
    - Unhein ek doosre ki aadat pad gayi.

11. I'm slowly getting used to the routine.
    - Dheere dheere routine ki aadat pad rahi hai.

12. You'll get used to it eventually.
    - Aakhir mein tumhe aadat pad jayegi.

13. She couldn't get used to the food.
    - Use khane ki aadat nahi padi.

14. He got used to the new system quickly.
    - Use naye system ki jaldi aadat pad gayi.

15. I'm still getting used to my new phone.
    - Mujhe abhi naye phone ki aadat pad rahi hai.

## Comparison Table

| Structure | Time | Meaning | Example |
|-----------|------|---------|---------|
| used to + V1 | Past | Did before, not now | I **used to** smoke. |
| be used to + V-ing | Present | Comfortable now | I **am used to** waking early. |
| get used to + V-ing | Process | Becoming comfortable | I'm **getting used to** it. |

---

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ |
|---------|---------|
| I used to waking up early. | I **am used to** waking up early. |
| I am used to wake up early. | I **used to** wake up early. |
| I am used to work hard. | I am used to **working** hard. |
| He use to play cricket. | He **used to** play cricket. |
| I'm use to it. | I'm **used to** it. |

## Quick Practice

**Fill in the blanks:**

1. I _____ (play) football when I was young. → used to play
2. I _____ (work) late now. → am used to working
3. She _____ (adjust) to the new job. → is getting used to

---

## Summary

```
USED TO + V1           → Past habit (pehle karta tha)
BE USED TO + V-ing     → Comfortable now (aadat hai)
GET USED TO + V-ing    → Becoming comfortable (aadat pad rahi hai)
```
