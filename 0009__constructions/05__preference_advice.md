# Preference & Advice Constructions

> Would rather, Had better, Prefer, Would prefer
> Expressing what you like more or strong advice.

---

## WOULD RATHER (Pasand karunga)

> Express PREFERENCE between options.
> More common in speech than "prefer."

**Structure:** Subject + would rather + V1 (+ than + V1)

### Positive Sentences

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I would rather stay home.
   - Main ghar rehna pasand karunga.

2. She would rather walk than drive.
   - Wo drive karne se zyada chalna pasand karegi.

3. I'd rather have tea than coffee.
   - Mujhe coffee se zyada chai pasand hai.

4. He would rather not go.
   - Wo jaana nahi chahega.

5. We would rather eat at home.
   - Hum ghar par khana pasand karenge.

6. I'd rather wait here.
   - Main yahaan wait karna pasand karunga.

7. She'd rather take a taxi.
   - Wo taxi lena pasand karegi.

8. I would rather not talk about it.
   - Main iske baare mein baat nahi karna chahta.

9. He'd rather work alone.
   - Wo akele kaam karna pasand karta hai.

10. I'd rather die than lie.
    - Main jhooth bolne se marna pasand karunga.

11. They would rather save money.
    - Wo paise bachana pasand karenge.

12. I'd rather you didn't tell anyone.
    - Main chahta hoon tum kisi ko na batao.

13. She'd rather be honest.
    - Wo imaandar rehna pasand karegi.

14. I would rather study now.
    - Main abhi padhna pasand karunga.

15. We'd rather order online.
    - Hum online order karna pasand karenge.

---

### Question Form

1. Would you rather have tea or coffee?
   - Chai pasand karoge ya coffee?

2. Would you rather stay or leave?
   - Rukna pasand karoge ya jaana?

3. Would you rather eat here or take away?
   - Yahaan khana hai ya pack karna hai?

4. Where would you rather go?
   - Kahaan jaana pasand karoge?

5. Which one would you rather buy?
   - Kaun sa kharidna pasand karoge?

## HAD BETTER (Behtar Hoga)

> Strong ADVICE or WARNING.
> Suggests something bad will happen if you don't.

**Structure:** Subject + had better (+ not) + V1

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. You had better leave now.
   - Tumhe abhi nikalna chahiye.

2. You'd better study hard.
   - Tumhe mehnat se padhna chahiye.

3. We had better hurry.
   - Humein jaldi karna chahiye.

4. You had better not be late.
   - Tumhe late nahi hona chahiye.

5. She had better apologize.
   - Use maafi maangni chahiye.

6. You'd better take an umbrella.
   - Chhata le lo.

7. I had better go now.
   - Mujhe ab jaana chahiye.

8. You'd better not tell him.
   - Use mat batana.

9. We had better book tickets early.
   - Humein jaldi ticket book karna chahiye.

10. You'd better listen to me.
    - Meri baat sun lo.

11. He had better finish the work.
    - Use kaam khatam karna chahiye.

12. You had better see a doctor.
    - Tumhe doctor ko dikhana chahiye.

13. We'd better leave before it rains.
    - Baarish se pehle nikalna chahiye.

14. You had better not forget.
    - Bhoolna mat.

15. She had better explain herself.
    - Use apni safai deni chahiye.

## PREFER (Zyada Pasand)

> Like one thing MORE than another.

### PREFER + noun/V-ing

**Structure:** Subject + prefer + noun/V-ing + to + noun/V-ing

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I prefer tea to coffee.
   - Mujhe coffee se zyada chai pasand hai.

2. She prefers reading to watching TV.
   - Use TV se zyada padhna pasand hai.

3. I prefer walking to driving.
   - Mujhe drive se zyada chalna pasand hai.

4. He prefers summer to winter.
   - Use sardiyon se garmiyan pasand hain.

5. We prefer Indian food.
   - Humein Indian khana zyada pasand hai.

6. I prefer morning workouts.
   - Mujhe subah ki exercise pasand hai.

7. She prefers working alone.
   - Use akele kaam karna pasand hai.

8. They prefer quality over quantity.
   - Unhe quantity se quality pasand hai.

9. I prefer staying home on weekends.
   - Mujhe weekend mein ghar rehna pasand hai.

10. He prefers action movies.
    - Use action movies pasand hain.

---

### WOULD PREFER + to-infinitive

**Structure:** Subject + would prefer + to + V1 (+ rather than + V1)

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. I would prefer to stay home.
   - Main ghar rehna pasand karunga.

2. She would prefer to take a taxi.
   - Wo taxi lena pasand karegi.

3. I'd prefer to eat later.
   - Main baad mein khana pasand karunga.

4. He would prefer not to go.
   - Wo jaana pasand nahi karega.

5. We would prefer to meet tomorrow.
   - Hum kal milna pasand karenge.

6. I'd prefer to discuss this privately.
   - Main private mein baat karna pasand karunga.

7. She'd prefer to work from home.
   - Wo ghar se kaam karna pasand karegi.

8. I would prefer to wait.
   - Main wait karna pasand karunga.

9. They'd prefer to order online.
   - Wo online order karna pasand karenge.

10. I would prefer not to comment.
    - Main comment nahi karna chahta.

---

## Comparison Table

| Structure | Meaning | Strength |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| prefer | Like more | Mild |
| would prefer | Like more (specific situation) | Mild |
| would rather | Prefer to do (choice) | Moderate |
| had better | Strong advice/warning | Strong |

---

## Would Rather vs Had Better

| Would Rather | Had Better |
|--------------|------------|
| Personal preference | Advice/Warning |
| No bad consequence | Bad consequence if not done |
| "I'd rather stay" (my choice) | "You'd better stay" (advice) |

**Examples:**

1. I'd rather stay home. (preference)
   - Main ghar rehna chahta hoon.

2. You'd better stay home. (advice - or something bad)
   - Tumhe ghar rehna chahiye (warna...)

3. She'd rather not go. (her choice)
   - Wo jaana nahi chahti.

4. She'd better not go. (warning)
   - Use nahi jaana chahiye (khatranak hai).

---

## Common Contractions

| Full Form | Contraction |
|-----------|-------------|
| I would rather | I'd rather |
| You had better | You'd better |
| She would prefer | She'd prefer |
| We would rather | We'd rather |

**Note:** "I'd" can mean "I would" or "I had" - context tells us which!

---

## Summary

| Pattern | Structure | Example |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Would rather | S + would rather + V1 | I'd rather stay. |
| Would rather...than | S + would rather + V1 + than + V1 | I'd rather walk than drive. |
| Had better | S + had better + V1 | You'd better hurry. |
| Had better not | S + had better not + V1 | You'd better not be late. |
| Prefer | S + prefer + N/V-ing + to | I prefer tea to coffee. |
| Would prefer | S + would prefer + to + V1 | I'd prefer to wait. |
