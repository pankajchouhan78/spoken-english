# Degree & Result Constructions

> So...that, Such...that, Too...to, Enough...to
> Express degree and its result.

---

## SO...THAT (Itna...ki)

> Adjective/Adverb + Result

**Structure:** So + adjective/adverb + that + clause

### With Adjectives

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. He is so tall that he can touch the ceiling.
   - Wo itna lamba hai ki ceiling choo sakta hai.

2. She is so beautiful that everyone stares.
   - Wo itni sundar hai ki sab dekhte hain.

3. The movie was so boring that I fell asleep.
   - Film itni boring thi ki main so gaya.

4. It is so hot that I can't go outside.
   - Itni garmi hai ki main bahar nahi ja sakta.

5. The food was so spicy that I couldn't eat it.
   - Khana itna teekha tha ki kha nahi saka.

6. I was so tired that I slept immediately.
   - Main itna thaka tha ki turant so gaya.

7. She is so smart that she solved it easily.
   - Wo itni smart hai ki aasani se solve kar liya.

8. The noise was so loud that I woke up.
   - Shor itna tez tha ki main jaag gaya.

9. He is so honest that everyone trusts him.
   - Wo itna imaandar hai ki sab bharosa karte hain.

10. The exam was so easy that everyone passed.
    - Exam itna aasaan tha ki sab pass ho gaye.

11. I was so happy that I cried.
    - Main itna khush tha ki ro diya.

12. The bag is so heavy that I can't lift it.
    - Bag itna bhaari hai ki utha nahi sakta.

13. She spoke so softly that I couldn't hear.
    - Wo itna dheere boli ki main sun nahi saka.

14. He was so angry that he left.
    - Wo itna gussa tha ki chala gaya.

15. The story was so sad that I cried.
    - Kahani itni dukhi thi ki main ro diya.

---

### With Adverbs

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. He runs so fast that no one can catch him.
   - Wo itna tez daudta hai ki koi pakad nahi sakta.

2. She spoke so clearly that everyone understood.
   - Wo itna saaf boli ki sabko samajh aa gaya.

3. He works so hard that he never fails.
   - Wo itni mehnat karta hai ki kabhi fail nahi hota.

4. They arrived so late that the show was over.
   - Wo itni der se aaye ki show khatam ho gaya.

5. She sang so beautifully that everyone clapped.
   - Wo itna sundar gayi ki sabne taali bajayi.

## SUCH...THAT (Aisa...ki)

> Noun + Result

**Structure:** Such + (a/an) + adjective + noun + that + clause

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. It was such a hot day that we stayed inside.
   - Itna garam din tha ki hum andar rahe.

2. She is such a kind person that everyone loves her.
   - Wo itni dayalu hai ki sab pyaar karte hain.

3. It was such a difficult question that no one answered.
   - Itna mushkil sawaal tha ki kisi ne jawab nahi diya.

4. He told such a funny joke that everyone laughed.
   - Usne itna funny joke sunaya ki sab hase.

5. They are such good friends that they share everything.
   - Wo itne acche dost hain ki sab share karte hain.

6. It was such heavy rain that we got drenched.
   - Itni tez baarish thi ki hum bheeg gaye.

7. He made such a big mistake that he got fired.
   - Usne itni badi galti ki ki nikaal diya gaya.

8. She has such a sweet voice that everyone listens.
   - Uski awaaz itni meethi hai ki sab sunte hain.

9. It was such a boring movie that I walked out.
   - Itni boring film thi ki main nikal gaya.

10. He showed such courage that he was praised.
    - Usne itna sahas dikhaya ki tareef hui.

11. We had such fun that we forgot the time.
    - Itna maza aaya ki time bhool gaye.

12. She wore such a beautiful dress that everyone noticed.
    - Usne itni sundar dress pehni ki sabne dekha.

13. He is such a liar that no one believes him.
    - Wo itna jhootha hai ki koi nahi maanta.

14. It was such bad weather that flights were cancelled.
    - Mausam itna kharab tha ki flights cancel ho gayi.

15. She cooks such delicious food that guests love coming.
    - Wo itna swaadist khana banati hai ki mehman aana pasand karte hain.

---

## SO vs SUCH

| SO | SUCH |
|----|------|
| Before adjective/adverb | Before noun (phrase) |
| so + adjective + that | such + (a/an) + adj + noun + that |
| He is **so** tall that... | He is **such** a tall man that... |
| It was **so** hot that... | It was **such** a hot day that... |

**Same Meaning, Different Structure:**

1. She is so beautiful that... = She is such a beautiful woman that...
2. The exam was so easy that... = It was such an easy exam that...
3. He was so angry that... = He was such an angry person that...

## TOO...TO (Bahut Zyada...Isliye Nahi)

> Excessive degree → Negative result

**Structure:** Too + adjective + (for someone) + to + verb

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. It is too hot to go outside.
   - Bahar jaane ke liye bahut garam hai.

2. She is too young to drive.
   - Wo drive karne ke liye bahut chhoti hai.

3. The bag is too heavy to carry.
   - Bag uthane ke liye bahut bhaari hai.

4. He is too proud to apologize.
   - Wo maafi maangne ke liye bahut ghamandi hai.

5. The coffee is too hot to drink.
   - Coffee peene ke liye bahut garam hai.

6. I'm too tired to work.
   - Main kaam karne ke liye bahut thaka hoon.

7. It's too late to change now.
   - Ab badalne ke liye bahut der hai.

8. The problem is too complex to solve easily.
   - Problem itni complex hai ki aasani se solve nahi hogi.

9. He is too shy to speak in public.
   - Wo public mein bolne ke liye bahut sharmila hai.

10. The music is too loud to talk.
    - Music itna loud hai ki baat nahi ho sakti.

11. This is too good to be true.
    - Ye sach hone ke liye bahut accha hai.

12. She is too busy to meet.
    - Wo milne ke liye bahut busy hai.

13. The movie is too scary for kids.
    - Film bachon ke liye bahut darawni hai.

14. It's too early to decide.
    - Decide karne ke liye bahut jaldi hai.

15. He is too weak to walk.
    - Wo chalne ke liye bahut kamzor hai.

---

## ENOUGH...TO (Kaafi...Isliye Ho Sakta)

> Sufficient degree → Positive result

**Structure:** Adjective + enough + (for someone) + to + verb

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. She is old enough to drive.
   - Wo drive karne ke liye kaafi badi hai.

2. The water is warm enough to swim.
   - Paani tairne ke liye kaafi garam hai.

3. He is strong enough to lift it.
   - Wo uthane ke liye kaafi takatwar hai.

4. I'm not rich enough to buy that.
   - Main wo kharidne ke liye itna ameer nahi.

5. The room is big enough for ten people.
   - Kamra das logon ke liye kaafi bada hai.

6. She is smart enough to understand.
   - Wo samajhne ke liye kaafi smart hai.

7. Is it safe enough to swim?
   - Kya tairne ke liye kaafi safe hai?

8. He is tall enough to reach the shelf.
   - Wo shelf tak pahunchne ke liye kaafi lamba hai.

9. The coffee is cool enough to drink now.
   - Coffee ab peene laayak thandi ho gayi.

10. She is brave enough to speak up.
    - Wo bolne ke liye kaafi bahadur hai.

11. I'm not good enough to compete.
    - Main competition ke liye itna accha nahi.

12. The food is hot enough to eat.
    - Khana khane ke liye kaafi garam hai.

13. He is fast enough to win.
    - Wo jeetne ke liye kaafi tez hai.

14. Is this loud enough to hear?
    - Kya ye sunne ke liye kaafi loud hai?

15. We have enough time to finish.
    - Humare paas khatam karne ke liye kaafi time hai.

---

## TOO vs ENOUGH

| TOO | ENOUGH |
|-----|--------|
| Excessive (negative) | Sufficient (positive) |
| Before adjective | After adjective |
| **Too** hot to drink | Hot **enough** to drink |
| Can't do | Can do |

**Examples:**

| Too (Can't) | Enough (Can) |
|-------------|--------------|
| Too young to drive | Old enough to drive |
| Too tired to work | Rested enough to work |
| Too weak to lift | Strong enough to lift |
| Too short to reach | Tall enough to reach |

| Pattern | Structure | Meaning |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| so...that | so + adj + that | Itna...ki |
| such...that | such + noun + that | Aisa...ki |
