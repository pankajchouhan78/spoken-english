# It Is - Dummy Subject Constructions

> "It" is used as a DUMMY SUBJECT when the real subject comes later.
> Very common in English for weather, time, distance, and opinions.

---

## Types of IT Constructions

| Type | Pattern | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| Weather | It is + weather | It is raining. |
| Time | It is + time | It is 5 o'clock. |
| Distance | It is + distance | It is 10 km from here. |
| Opinion | It is + adj + to | It is easy to learn. |
| Duration | It takes + time | It takes 2 hours. |

---

## IT + Weather

> Describe weather conditions.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. It is raining outside.
   - Bahar baarish ho rahi hai.

2. It is very hot today.
   - Aaj bahut garam hai.

3. It is cold in the morning.
   - Subah thanda hai.

4. It was sunny yesterday.
   - Kal dhoop thi.

5. It will be cloudy tomorrow.
   - Kal badal honge.

6. It is snowing in the mountains.
   - Pahadon mein baraf gir rahi hai.

7. It is windy today.
   - Aaj hawa chal rahi hai.

8. It was foggy last night.
   - Kal raat kohra tha.

9. It is getting warmer.
   - Garam ho raha hai.

10. It looks like it will rain.
    - Lagta hai baarish hogi.

## IT + Time

> Tell the time.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. It is 10 o'clock.
   - Das baje hain.

2. It is time to go.
   - Jaane ka time ho gaya.

3. It is getting late.
   - Der ho rahi hai.

4. It was midnight when I woke up.
   - Jab utha tab aadhi raat thi.

5. It is early in the morning.
   - Abhi subah jaldi hai.

6. It is almost noon.
   - Lagbhag dopahar hai.

7. What time is it?
   - Kya time hua?

8. It is past 6 PM.
   - 6 baj gaye hain.

9. It is time for lunch.
   - Lunch ka time hai.

10. It was exactly 5 when he arrived.
    - Jab wo aaya bilkul 5 baje the.

## IT + Distance

> Express how far something is.

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. It is 5 km from here.
   - Yahaan se 5 km hai.

2. It is a long way to the station.
   - Station bahut door hai.

3. It is just around the corner.
   - Bilkul corner par hai.

4. It is 200 meters from my house.
   - Mere ghar se 200 meter hai.

5. It is not far from here.
   - Yahaan se door nahi.

6. It is quite close.
   - Kaafi paas hai.

7. How far is it to the airport?
   - Airport kitni door hai?

8. It is an hour's drive.
   - Ek ghante ki drive hai.

9. It is within walking distance.
   - Paidal jaane layak hai.

10. It is only 10 minutes away.
    - Sirf 10 minute door hai.

## IT IS + Adjective + TO (Opinion/Difficulty)

> Express how easy, hard, important something is.

**Structure:** It is + adjective + (for someone) + to + verb

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. It is easy to learn English.
   - English seekhna aasaan hai.

2. It is difficult to understand him.
   - Use samajhna mushkil hai.

3. It is important to exercise daily.
   - Roz exercise karna zaroori hai.

4. It is hard to wake up early.
   - Jaldi uthna mushkil hai.

5. It is good to help others.
   - Dusron ki madad karna accha hai.

6. It is nice to meet you.
   - Aapse milkar accha laga.

7. It is wrong to lie.
   - Jhooth bolna galat hai.

8. It is dangerous to drive fast.
   - Tez drive karna khatarnak hai.

9. It is possible to finish today.
   - Aaj khatam karna possible hai.

10. It is impossible to please everyone.
    - Sabko khush karna namumkin hai.

11. It is necessary to work hard.
    - Mehnat karna zaroori hai.

12. It is better to be honest.
    - Imaandar rehna behtar hai.

13. It is fun to travel.
    - Travel karna mazedaar hai.

14. It is boring to wait.
    - Wait karna boring hai.

15. It is rude to interrupt.
    - Beech mein bolna badtameezi hai.

## IT IS + Adjective + FOR someone + TO

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. It is easy for me to understand.
   - Mere liye samajhna aasaan hai.

2. It is difficult for her to walk.
   - Uske liye chalna mushkil hai.

3. It is hard for him to accept.
   - Uske liye maanna mushkil hai.

4. It is important for you to study.
   - Tumhare liye padhna zaroori hai.

5. It is impossible for us to finish.
   - Humare liye khatam karna namumkin hai.

6. It is good for children to play.
   - Bachon ke liye khelna accha hai.

7. It is necessary for everyone to follow rules.
   - Sabke liye niyam follow karna zaroori hai.

8. It is not safe for kids to go alone.
   - Bachon ke liye akele jaana safe nahi.

9. It is easier for you than for me.
   - Tumhare liye mere se aasaan hai.

10. It is possible for them to win.
    - Unke liye jeetna possible hai.

## IT TAKES (Time Required)

> How much time is needed for something.

**Structure:** It takes + time + to + verb

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. It takes 2 hours to reach there.
   - Wahaan pahunchne mein 2 ghante lagte hain.

2. It takes time to learn a skill.
   - Skill seekhne mein time lagta hai.

3. It took me 10 minutes to find it.
   - Mujhe dhundhne mein 10 minute lage.

4. It will take a week to complete.
   - Complete hone mein ek hafta lagega.

5. It doesn't take long to cook.
   - Banana mein zyada time nahi lagta.

6. How long does it take to get there?
   - Wahaan pahunchne mein kitna time lagta hai?

7. It takes 30 minutes by bus.
   - Bus se 30 minute lagte hain.

8. It took years to build this.
   - Ise banane mein saal lage.

9. It takes patience to succeed.
   - Safal hone ke liye sabr chahiye.

10. It takes a lot of effort.
    - Bahut mehnat lagti hai.

11. It takes courage to speak the truth.
    - Sach bolne ke liye himmat chahiye.

12. It won't take long.
    - Zyada time nahi lagega.

13. It took me a while to understand.
    - Samajhne mein thoda time laga.

14. It takes practice to improve.
    - Sudhar ke liye practice chahiye.

15. It took forever to download.
    - Download hone mein bahut time laga.

## IT + Seems / Appears / Looks

**Daily Use Sentences:**

1. It seems like he's angry.
   - Lagta hai wo gussa hai.

2. It appears that she left.
   - Lagta hai wo chali gayi.

3. It looks like rain.
   - Lagta hai baarish hogi.

4. It seems impossible.
   - Ye namumkin lagta hai.

5. It doesn't seem right.
   - Ye sahi nahi lagta.
