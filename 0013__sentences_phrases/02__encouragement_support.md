# Encouragement & Support

> Phrases to encourage others and respond to encouragement.

---

## Ways to Encourage Someone

| English | Hindi |
|---------|-------|
| You can do it! | Tum kar sakte ho! |
| Don't give up! | Himmat mat haaro! |
| I believe in you! | Mujhe tum par vishwas hai! |
| Keep going, you're doing great! | Chalte raho, tum bohot accha kar rahe ho! |
| You've got this! | Tum yeh kar loge! |
| Stay positive, everything will be fine. | Positive raho, sab theek ho jayega. |
| Don't worry, you'll figure it out! | Chinta mat karo, tum samajh jaoge! |
| Take it one step at a time. | Ek ek kadam badhayein. |

---

## Responses to Encouragement

| English | Hindi |
|---------|-------|
| Thank you, I'll try my best! | Shukriya, main apni poori koshish karoonga! |
| I appreciate your support! | Main aapke samarthan ka aabhaar karta hoon! |
| That means a lot to me! | Yeh mere liye bohot maayne rakhta hai! |
| I feel more confident now, thanks! | Ab mujhe zyada vishwas ho raha hai, dhanyavaad! |
| With your support, I can do it! | Aapke samarthan ke saath, main yeh kar sakta hoon! |
| Thanks for believing in me! | Mujh par vishwas karne ke liye shukriya! |
| I needed to hear that! | Mujhe yeh sunne ki zaroorat thi! |
| I'll keep pushing forward! | Main aage badhta rahunga! |

---

## Phrases to Offer Support and Guidance

| English | Hindi |
|---------|-------|
| Take your time, there's no rush. | Apna samay lo, koi jaldi nahi hai. |
| Trust your instincts, you know what's best. | Apni soojh par vishwas karo, tumhe pata hai kya sahi hai. |
| Whatever you choose, I'll support you. | Jo bhi tum chuno, main tumhara saath doonga. |
| Think about what matters most to you. | Jo tumhare liye sabse mahatvapurn hai, uske baare mein socho. |
| There's no right or wrong answer. | Koi sahi ya galat jawab nahi hai. |
| It's okay to be unsure. | Pareshan hona theek hai. |
| You've handled tough situations before. | Tumne pehle bhi mushkil haalaat sambhale hain. |
| Whatever decision you make, you can handle it. | Jo bhi faisla tum loge, tum uska samna kar sakte ho. |

---

## Expressions of Disappointment or Sadness

| English | Hindi |
|---------|-------|
| I'm really disappointed. | Main sach mein niraash hoon. |
| That's so sad. | Yeh bohot udas hai. |
| I didn't expect this to happen. | Maine iski ummeed nahi ki thi. |
| I feel let down. | Mujhe niraasha mehsoos ho rahi hai. |
| This is disappointing news. | Yeh niraashajanak khabar hai. |
| I'm feeling really low right now. | Main abhi bohot udas mehsoos kar raha hoon. |
| It's hard to accept this. | Isse svikar karna mushkil hai. |
| This has really affected me. | Isne mujhe sach mein prabhavit kiya hai. |
| Oh shit, I can't believe this happened. | Oh shit, mujhe nahi pata tha yeh hoga. |
