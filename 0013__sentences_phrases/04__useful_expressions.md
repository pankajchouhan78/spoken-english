# Useful Expressions

> Common expressions with examples and Hindi meanings.

---

## 1. Money doesn't grow on trees

**Meaning:** Paise asaani se nahi milte, sambhal ke kharch karo.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Can you buy me a new phone? | Sorry, money doesn't grow on trees. |
| Why don't you spend freely? | Because money doesn't grow on trees. |
| Can we go on another trip? | No, money doesn't grow on trees. |

---

## 2. Not my cup of tea

**Meaning:** Jo mujhe pasand nahi hai.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Do you like horror movies? | No, they're not my cup of tea. |
| Will you come dancing? | Sorry, not my cup of tea. |
| Do you enjoy camping? | It's really not my cup of tea. |

---

## 3. Habitual with

**Meaning:** Jo aadat mein hota hai ya regular hota hai.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Do you drink tea every day? | Yes, I'm habitual with tea in the morning. |
| Why do you wake up so early? | I'm habitual with early mornings. |
| Do you always exercise? | Yes, I'm habitual with daily workouts. |

---

## 4. There is a

**Meaning:** Koi cheez hai ya maujood hai batane ke liye.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| What's in the box? | There is a gift inside. |
| Is there a problem? | Yes, there is a small issue. |
| What's on the table? | There is a book on the table. |

---

## 5. I have to

**Meaning:** Kuch zaroori kaam jo karna hi hai.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Why are you leaving now? | I have to meet someone. |
| Are you busy? | Yes, I have to finish this work. |
| Why can't you stay? | I have to go home. |

---

## 6. I wish

**Meaning:** Koi tamanna ya afsos batane ke liye.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Do you want to travel? | I wish I could go abroad. |
| Why are you sad? | I wish I hadn't said that. |
| Do you miss home? | I wish I were there right now. |

---

## 7. I want such a

**Meaning:** Mujhe aisi hi koi cheez chahiye.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| You liked that phone? | Yes, I want such a phone. |
| That's a beautiful dress! | I want such a dress for my birthday. |
| Was the vacation good? | Amazing! I want such a trip too. |

---

## 8. No need to

**Meaning:** Iski koi zarurat nahi hai.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Should I bring my ID? | No need to, it's not required here. |
| Do I have to explain again? | No need to, I got it. |
| Should I apologize again? | No need to, they've moved on. |

---

## 9. Nothing can be

**Meaning:** Isse behtar ya bura kuch nahi ho sakta.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| How's this feeling? | Nothing can be more peaceful than this. |
| Is this your favorite dish? | Yes, nothing can be tastier! |
| Was the view great? | Nothing can be more beautiful. |

---

## 10. I admit

**Meaning:** Main maanta hoon / Main accept karta hoon.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Was it your mistake? | Yes, I admit I was wrong. |
| Did you like the movie? | I admit, it was better than I expected. |
| Were you nervous? | I admit, I was a little scared. |

---

## 11. I did such a

**Meaning:** Maine itna accha/zyada/strong kaam kiya.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| How was your performance? | I did such a good job! |
| Why are you tired? | I did such a hard workout. |
| Why are you proud? | I did such a difficult task alone. |

---

## 12. Would you like to

**Meaning:** Tumhe ye karna pasand hoga kya?

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| What can we do now? | Would you like to go for a walk? |
| How about dinner? | Would you like to join us? |
| Want to see a movie? | Would you like to watch it tonight? |

---

## 13. Do you know how to

**Meaning:** Tumhe ye kaam karna aata hai kya?

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Can you fix this? | Do you know how to repair it? |
| Need help with cooking? | Do you know how to make pasta? |
| Can you use this app? | Do you know how to install it? |

---

## 14. I don't have time to

**Meaning:** Mere paas ye karne ka time nahi hai.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Why didn't you call? | I don't have time to chat. |
| Can you help me today? | Sorry, I don't have time to spare. |
| Why didn't you cook? | I don't have time to cook today. |

---

## 15. As a matter of fact

**Meaning:** Sach kahoon to / Haqeeqat mein.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did you see him? | As a matter of fact, I met him yesterday. |
| Do you know her? | As a matter of fact, we're friends. |
| Is that true? | As a matter of fact, yes. |

---

## 16. To make a long story short

**Meaning:** Seedhe point par aane ke liye.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Why did you quit? | To make a long story short, I wanted change. |
| What happened? | To make a long story short, we broke up. |
| Why are you late? | To make a long story short, traffic was terrible. |
